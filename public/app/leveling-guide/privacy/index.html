WoW Forever Leveling Guide: Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

WoW Forever Leveling Guide does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your progress

Your characters and the steps you tick are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, they are also stored in your own private iCloud database, so they sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Only you can see that data; we cannot.

Purchases

The in-app purchase is handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or your name.

Links

The app links to wowforever.be, YouTube and Twitch. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@wowforever.be
