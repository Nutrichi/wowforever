WoW Forever Leveling Guide: Support

Last updated: 19 November 2026

WoW Forever Leveling Guide is a step-by-step leveling route for World of Warcraft: Forever, from level 1 to 60. It is a manual checklist: the app does not connect to the game. You tick each step yourself as you play.

Free and full route

Levels 1 to 12 are free. One purchase unlocks the full route from 12 to 60 on all your devices that use the same Apple Account, with every content update included.

Restore your purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling the app, open the More tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought the route with.

Sync with iCloud

Your characters and ticked steps sync through your own iCloud. Sign in to iCloud on each device and keep iCloud turned on for the app. The More tab shows whether sync is on. Without iCloud, your progress stays on that one device.

Contact

Found a mistake in the route, or need help? Mail contact@wowforever.be with your race, class and the step.
