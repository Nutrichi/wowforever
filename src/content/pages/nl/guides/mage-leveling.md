De Mage levelt in WoW Forever snel en alleen, met eindeloos eten en drinken en veel controle. Frost is veruit de snelste spec, dankzij de nieuwe <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> en Fingers of Frost. Deze gids behandelt de specs, de rotatie, de belangrijke levels, classquests en de nieuwe scrolls.

| | |
|---|---|
| Beste spec om te levelen | Frost |
| Belangrijke talents | Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost, Frostbite, Shatter, Ice Shards |
| Sterk in | Schade, crowd control, gratis eten en drinken |
| Zwak in | Mana, threat in dungeons, weinig health |
| Talent per level | [Levelbuild Mage](/nl/leveling/mage/) |
| Builds tot level 60 | Na de launch op 4 november |

## Met welke spec level je

**Frost** was altijd de veilige keuze: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> vertraagt elk doelwit, en <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> en <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11185"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_icestorm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Blizzard</a> bevriezen of vertragen hele groepen. In Forever is het ook de snelste. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> doet 200% meer schade aan Frozen doelwitten, en <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> behandelt je volgende twee spells alsof het doelwit Frozen is. Samen met <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11170"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shatter</a> en <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11207"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_iceshard.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Shards</a> geeft dat enorme crits. Eerder was die bonus 300%.

**Fire** verbruikt de minste mana, maar schittert pas met veel critical strike chance. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400624"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_firebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heating Up</a> maakt <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pyroblast</a> sneller na crits, dus op lage levels hangt het van geluk af.

**Arcane** kan goed zijn, maar hangt af van <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12536"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_manaburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Clearcasting</a>- en <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400588"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_missilebarrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Missile Barrage</a>-procs, zuinig omgaan met mana en gear. Fire en Arcane doen het beter in dungeons dan in de open wereld.

## De Frost-build

De [levelbuild van de Mage](/nl/leveling/mage/) geeft het talent voor elk level van 10 tot 35. In het kort: eerst <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11070"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Frostbolt</a> en Elemental Precision, dan <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> op 17 tot 19, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> op 20, Ice Shards, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11170"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shatter</a> op 25 tot 27, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11958"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frost.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Block</a> op 28, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> op 30 en 31, Missile Barrage uit de Arcane-boom op 32 en 33, en Piercing Ice op 34 en 35.

| Talent | Wat het doet in Forever |
|---|---|
| **Ice Lance** | Instant, 45 mana, 200% meer schade aan Frozen doelwitten |
| **Fingers of Frost** | Chill-effecten hebben 15% kans om je volgende 2 spells te behandelen alsof het doelwit Frozen is |
| **Frostbite** | Chill-effecten hebben 15% kans om het doelwit 5 seconden te bevriezen |
| **Shatter** | 50% meer critical strike chance tegen Frozen doelwitten |
| **Ice Shards** | Verdubbelt de critical strike-bonus van je Frost-spells |
| **Frost Channeling** | Frost-spells 15% goedkoper en 30% minder threat |

Met punten in <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225474"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_marksmanship.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Talented</a> uit het Legacy-systeem haal je elk talent tot vijf levels eerder. De [Frost Mage-gids](/nl/guides/frost-mage/) geeft de build voor level 30 met Missile Barrage, plus kiten, mana, Shatter en gear. De [Arcane Mage-gids](/nl/guides/arcane-mage/) geeft twee builds voor level 30 met Arcane Blast, plus burnfases en mana. De [Fire Mage-gids](/nl/guides/fire-mage/) geeft twee builds voor level 30 met Heating Up, plus Fire Power en mana.

## Rotatie

**Level 1 tot 10.** Je begint met <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=133"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flamebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fireball</a> en <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=168"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Armor</a>. Gebruik Fireball tot level 4, dan <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> als hoofdspell, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> op cooldown en je wand om doelwitten af te maken. Maak eten en drinken tussen de pulls.

## Classquests

De classquests van de Mage leren geen spells. Ze geven staves, off-hands, robes en wands.
