Van de bazen van The Drowned City is nog geen buit bekend: de speldata koppelt tot nu toe geen items aan de bazen. De eerste questbeloning is wel bekend: bij The Only Good Brinescale... van de Horde kies je <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279888"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pants_13.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tidesoaked Leggings</a> of <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279889"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shoulder_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Naga Priestess's Mantle</a>.

De trash laat wel twee questitems vallen: <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=270305"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_115.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Brinescale Trident</a> van <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=262397">Brinescale Skirmisher</a> en <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=270333"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_troll_tablet.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Voodoo-Infused Artifact</a> van Saltseer Manhunters. Voor welke quests ze dienen, is nog niet bekend.
