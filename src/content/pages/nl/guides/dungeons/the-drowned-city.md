De eerste bekende drop van een baas is <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274615"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_elemental_primal_water.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heart of the Primeval</a> van de Primeval Elemental: een trinket die 30 seconden tot 19 spell damage en healing geeft. De rest van de buit van de bazen is nog niet bekend.

Vier van de acht quests hebben een bekende beloning. Voor de Alliance leidt Take Two With Water naar Findings for the Explorer's League in Ironforge, met <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279880"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_10_green.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ancient Voodoo Pouch</a> en <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279881"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_chain.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Intrepid Explorer's Chainmail</a>. Voor de Horde kies je bij The Only Good Brinescale... <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279888"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pants_13.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tidesoaked Leggings</a> of <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279889"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shoulder_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Naga Priestess's Mantle</a>. Beide kanten halen <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279930"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_helmet_15.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Saltseer's Helm</a> met Shady Saltseers in Booty Bay.
