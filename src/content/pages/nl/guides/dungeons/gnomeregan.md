Gnomeregan is de dungeon van de ingenieurs: Thermaplugg alleen laat vier schematics vallen, waaronder de <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7742"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Schematic: Gnomish Cloaking Device</a>, en de monsters nog een paar meer. Verschillende items voor casters kregen in Forever spell damage en healing, en <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9446"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_30.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electrocutioner Leg</a> doet dubbele schade aan Mechanical-doelwitten. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thermaplugg's Left Arm</a> en de <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9492"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator</a> vallen nog altijd bij Thermaplugg.

Electrocutioner 6000 laat de <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6893"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Workshop Key</a> vallen, die de achterdeur bij het Train Depot opent.
