El primer botín de jefe conocido es <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274615"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_elemental_primal_water.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heart of the Primeval</a> del Primeval Elemental: un abalorio que da hasta 19 de daño y sanación de hechizos durante 30 segundos. El resto del botín de los jefes aún no se conoce.

Cuatro de las ocho misiones tienen una recompensa conocida. Para la Alianza, Take Two With Water lleva a Findings for the Explorer's League en Ironforge, con <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279880"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_10_green.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ancient Voodoo Pouch</a> y <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279881"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_chain.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Intrepid Explorer's Chainmail</a>. Para la Horda, The Only Good Brinescale... te deja elegir <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279888"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pants_13.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tidesoaked Leggings</a> o <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279889"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shoulder_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Naga Priestess's Mantle</a>. Las dos facciones consiguen <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279930"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_helmet_15.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Saltseer's Helm</a> con Shady Saltseers en Booty Bay.
