## Cómo conseguirla

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256400">Okamache</a> la vende en The Barrens por 6000 Merchant's Favor.

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256736">Huey Sunnydale</a> la vende en Redridge Mountains por 6000 Merchant's Favor.

Comprarla o usarla requiere Exalted con Durotar Supply and Logistics.

Comprarla o usarla requiere Exalted con Azeroth Commerce Authority.

Montarla requiere Journeyman Riding.
