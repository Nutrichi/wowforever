Gnomeregan est le donjon des ingénieurs : Thermaplugg à lui seul laisse quatre schémas, dont le <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7742"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Schematic: Gnomish Cloaking Device</a>, et les monstres en laissent quelques autres. Plusieurs objets de lanceurs de sorts ont reçu des dégâts et soins des sorts dans Forever, et <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9446"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_30.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electrocutioner Leg</a> inflige des dégâts doublés aux cibles Mechanical. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thermaplugg's Left Arm</a> et l’<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9492"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator</a> tombent toujours sur Thermaplugg.

Electrocutioner 6000 laisse la <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6893"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Workshop Key</a>, qui ouvre la porte arrière au Train Depot.
