Le premier butin de boss connu est <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274615"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_elemental_primal_water.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heart of the Primeval</a> du Primeval Elemental : un bijou qui donne jusqu'à 19 de dégâts et de soins des sorts pendant 30 secondes. Le reste du butin des boss n'est pas encore connu.

Quatre des huit quêtes ont une récompense connue. Pour l'Alliance, Take Two With Water mène à Findings for the Explorer's League à Ironforge, avec <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279880"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_10_green.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ancient Voodoo Pouch</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279881"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_chain.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Intrepid Explorer's Chainmail</a>. Pour la Horde, The Only Good Brinescale... laisse choisir <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279888"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pants_13.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tidesoaked Leggings</a> ou <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279889"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shoulder_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Naga Priestess's Mantle</a>. Les deux factions obtiennent <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279930"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_helmet_15.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Saltseer's Helm</a> avec Shady Saltseers à Booty Bay.
