Le Mage monte vite et seul dans WoW Forever, avec de la nourriture et de l'eau à l'infini et beaucoup de contrôle. Frost est de loin la spécialisation la plus rapide, grâce au nouveau <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> et à Fingers of Frost. Ce guide couvre les spécialisations, la rotation, les niveaux clés et les parchemins.

| | |
|---|---|
| Meilleure spé pour monter | Frost |
| Talents clés | Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost, Frostbite, Shatter, Ice Shards |
| Points forts | Dégâts, contrôle, nourriture et eau gratuites |
| Points faibles | Mana, menace en donjon, peu de points de vie |
| Talent par niveau | [Build de leveling du Mage](/fr/leveling/mage/) |
| Builds jusqu'au niveau 60 | Après la sortie du 4 novembre |

## Quelle spécialisation pour monter

**Frost** a toujours été le choix sûr : <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> ralentit chaque cible, et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11185"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_icestorm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Blizzard</a> gèlent ou ralentissent des groupes entiers. Dans Forever, c'est aussi la plus rapide. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> inflige 200 % de dégâts en plus aux cibles Frozen, et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> traite vos deux sorts suivants comme si la cible était Frozen. Avec <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11170"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shatter</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11207"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_iceshard.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Shards</a>, cela donne d'énormes critiques. Le build bêta du 15 octobre a ramené ce bonus de 300 % à 200 %.

**Fire** dépense le moins de mana, mais ne brille qu'avec beaucoup de chances de critique. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400624"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_firebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heating Up</a> accélère <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pyroblast</a> après des critiques, donc à bas niveau tout repose sur la chance.

**Arcane** peut être bon, mais dépend des procs de <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12536"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_manaburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Clearcasting</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400588"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_missilebarrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Missile Barrage</a>, d'une bonne gestion de la mana et de l'équipement. Fire et Arcane s'en sortent mieux en donjon qu'en monde ouvert.

## Le build Frost

Le [build de leveling du Mage](/fr/leveling/mage/) donne le talent de chaque niveau de 10 à 35. En bref : d'abord <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11070"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Frostbolt</a> et Elemental Precision, puis <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> de 15 à 17, Ice Shards, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> au 20, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11170"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shatter</a> de 25 à 27, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11958"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frost.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Block</a> au 28 et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> aux niveaux 30 et 31.

| Talent | Ce qu'il fait dans Forever |
|---|---|
| **Ice Lance** | Instantané, 45 de mana, 200 % de dégâts en plus aux cibles Frozen |
| **Fingers of Frost** | Les effets Chill ont 15 % de chances de traiter vos 2 sorts suivants comme si la cible était Frozen |
| **Frostbite** | Les effets Chill ont 15 % de chances de geler la cible pendant 5 secondes |
| **Shatter** | 50 % de chances de critique en plus contre les cibles Frozen |
| **Ice Shards** | Double le bonus de critique de vos sorts Frost |
| **Frost Channeling** | Sorts Frost 15 % moins chers et 30 % de menace en moins |

Avec des points dans <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225474"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_marksmanship.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Talented</a> du système Legacy, vous atteignez chaque talent jusqu'à cinq niveaux plus tôt.

## Rotation

**Niveaux 1 à 10.** Vous commencez avec <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=133"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flamebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fireball</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=168"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Armor</a>. Utilisez Fireball jusqu'au niveau 4, puis <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> comme sort principal, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> dès qu'il est prêt et votre baguette pour achever les cibles. Invoquez de la nourriture et de l'eau entre les pulls.

**Frost**
- Gardez <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_magicalsentry.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Intellect</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=168"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Armor</a> actifs.
- Lancez <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> comme sort principal.
- Lancez <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> quand <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> se déclenche, ou quand <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> gèle la cible.
- Quand une cible s'approche : <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a> et Ice Lance jusqu'à ce que le gel se brise.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_glacier.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cone of Cold</a> si Frost Nova est en recharge, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> pendant que vous vous replacez.
- Contre un groupe : Frost Nova, puis <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_selfdestruct.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Flamestrike</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_wispsplode.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Explosion</a>. En groupe, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_icestorm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blizzard</a> à bonne distance.

**Fire**
- Pré-lancez <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pyroblast</a> sur un boss, ouvrez avec <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=133"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flamebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fireball</a> sur les monstres.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2948"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_soulburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scorch</a> jusqu'à 5 cumuls de <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11095"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_windsofwoe.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Scorch</a>.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> dès qu'il est prêt.
- Pyroblast à 3 cumuls de <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400624"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_firebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heating Up</a>, sinon Fireball.

**Arcane**
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400574"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Blast</a> pour accumuler des cumuls et déclencher <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400588"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_missilebarrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Missile Barrage</a>.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5143"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_starfall.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Missiles</a> avec 1 ou 2 cumuls d'Arcane Blast.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> ou <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_wispsplode.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Explosion</a> pour consommer les cumuls si Missile Barrage ne se déclenche pas.

Dans chaque spé : <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a> et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blink.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blink</a> pour fuir, Frost Blast dès le niveau 24 pour ralentir, et <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=118"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a> sur une cible en trop dangereuse.

Le build bêta du 15 octobre retravaille Arcane Blast, qui ajoute désormais de la puissance des sorts par cumul pendant 15 secondes, et place Missile Barrage en première rangée. La rotation Arcane ci-dessus suit le build précédent. Tous les changements sont dans [cet article](/fr/news/battle-shout-and-totems-now-buff-the-whole-raid/).

## Quêtes de classe

Les quêtes de classe du Mage n'apprennent pas de sorts. Elles donnent des bâtons, des main gauche, des robes et des baguettes.
