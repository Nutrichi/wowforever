Gnomeregan ist der Dungeon der Ingenieure: Thermaplugg allein lässt vier Schematics fallen, darunter die <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7742"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Schematic: Gnomish Cloaking Device</a>, und die Monster noch ein paar weitere. Einige Gegenstände für Zauberer bekamen in Forever Zauberschaden und Heilung, und <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9446"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_30.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electrocutioner Leg</a> verursacht doppelten Schaden an Mechanical-Zielen. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thermaplugg's Left Arm</a> und der <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9492"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator</a> droppen weiterhin bei Thermaplugg.

Electrocutioner 6000 lässt den <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6893"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Workshop Key</a> fallen, der die Hintertür am Train Depot öffnet.
