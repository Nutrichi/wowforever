## Come ottenerla

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256400">Okamache</a> la vende a The Barrens per 6000 Merchant's Favor.

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256736">Huey Sunnydale</a> la vende a Redridge Mountains per 6000 Merchant's Favor.

Comprarla o usarla richiede Exalted con Durotar Supply and Logistics.

Comprarla o usarla richiede Exalted con Azeroth Commerce Authority.

Cavalcarla richiede Journeyman Riding.
