Gnomeregan è il dungeon degli ingegneri: solo Thermaplugg lascia quattro schemi, tra cui il <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7742"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Schematic: Gnomish Cloaking Device</a>, e i mostri ne lasciano qualcun altro. Diversi oggetti da incantatore hanno ottenuto danni e cure magiche in Forever, e <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9446"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_30.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electrocutioner Leg</a> infligge danni doppi ai bersagli Mechanical. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thermaplugg's Left Arm</a> e l’<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9492"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator</a> cadono ancora da Thermaplugg.

Electrocutioner 6000 lascia la <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6893"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Workshop Key</a>, che apre la porta sul retro al Train Depot.
