La Cathedral chiude le due grandi missioni del monastero: Into The Scarlet Monastery per la Horde e In the Name of the Light per l’Alliance. Entrambe chiedono Mograine e Whitemane qui, Herod nell’[Armory](/it/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-armory/) e Houndmaster Loksey nella [Library](/it/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-library/).
