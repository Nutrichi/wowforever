Leveling from 85 to 90 in MoP Classic takes you through Pandaria, one zone per level. You stay on the ground until level 90, and each zone has a main story chain that gives much more experience than its side hubs. Following that chain and leaving a zone on time is most of the route.

## Before you start

- Take the quest to Pandaria from the Hero's Call Board (Alliance) or the Warchief's Command Board (Horde). It leads into The Jade Forest.
- Heirlooms stop scaling at 85, so plan to replace them with the first quest rewards.
- Log out in an inn to build rested experience: kills give a lot of experience in Pandaria.
- Monks can pick up Enlightenment at the Peak of Serenity, a bonus to experience that makes their climb even faster.

## Zones per level range

| Levels | Zone | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 85-86 | The Jade Forest | Follow the story from Paw'Don Village (Alliance) or Honeydew Village (Horde). Skip the coastal side hubs. |
| 86-87 | Valley of the Four Winds | Follow Chen Stormstout toward Stormstout Brewery and do the defence of Stoneplow. Krasarang Wilds fills a gap if needed. |
| 87-88 | Kun-Lai Summit | Binan Village first, then the grummle caravan up the mountain. |
| 88-89 | Townlong Steppes | The chains of the Shado-Pan against the mantid, with many enemies close together. |
| 89-90 | Dread Wastes | The Klaxxi chains from Klaxxi'vess, which also build reputation that matters at 90. |

Grab every flight path you pass: they are the only fast way to travel until level 90.

## Dungeons worth a run

| Levels | Dungeons |
|---|---|
| 85-87 | Temple of the Jade Serpent, Stormstout Brewery |
| 87-90 | Mogu'shan Palace, Shado-Pan Monastery |

Gate of the Setting Sun and Siege of Niuzao Temple are dungeons for level 90 and are not part of the route.

## At level 90

Train Wisdom of the Four Winds at a riding trainer in Pandaria, then head to the Timeless Isle, where gear for every slot drops. Start the legendary cloak questline right away, since it takes several weeks.

## Habits that save time

- Do the quests that move the main story of a zone first; take side quests only when they share an area with it.
- Queue for dungeons while questing, and keep questing until the queue fires.
- Leave a zone as soon as the next one opens.
- Skip the Tillers farm at Halfhill while leveling; it is worth more at level 90.
