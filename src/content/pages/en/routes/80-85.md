Leveling from 80 to 85 takes you through the zones of Cataclysm, in Cata Classic and in MoP Classic. The zones are linear: each quest hub sends you to the next one, and the world changes as the story moves on. You fly from the start, so the route is short.

## Before you start

- Buy the Flight Master's License from a riding trainer: it allows flying in the old world and the Cataclysm zones.
- Heirloom shoulders and an heirloom chest each give 10% more experience and scale up to level 85.
- Take the zone intros from the Hero's Call Board (Alliance) or the Warchief's Command Board (Horde) in Stormwind City or Orgrimmar. They lead straight into each zone.
- Log out in an inn from level 79 on: rested experience covers a large part of these five levels.

## Zones per level range

| Levels | Zone | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 80-82 | Mount Hyjal | Start at Nordrassil and follow the chain. Leave when you reach 82, even with quests left. |
| 82-83 | Deepholm | Almost the whole zone, around the Temple of Earth. The Stonecore entrance is in the zone. |
| 83-84 | Uldum | The Ramkahen and Neferset chains. Leave at 84; the later chains have many cutscenes. |
| 84-85 | Twilight Highlands | Start with the intro from your capital city and follow the faction chains. |

Vashj'ir can replace Mount Hyjal for levels 80 to 82. The zones are the same for both factions, apart from the first hubs in Twilight Highlands: Highbank for the Alliance and Dragonmaw Port for the Horde.

## Dungeons worth a run

| Levels | Dungeons |
|---|---|
| 80-82 | Blackrock Caverns, Throne of the Tides |
| 81-83 | The Stonecore |
| 82-84 | The Vortex Pinnacle |
| 84-85 | Grim Batol, Halls of Origination, Lost City of the Tol'vir |

One run per dungeon is enough while leveling. Pick up the dungeon quests first, inside the dungeon or in the nearby hub.

## Habits that save time

- Fly straight onto the quest objective instead of travelling on the ground.
- Take the zone intro from the board in your capital city when a zone is done; that is faster than flying to the next zone.
- Take herbs and ore on your flight line for extra experience.
- Leave a zone at the level where the next one starts: the story of a zone is not worth staying for while leveling.
