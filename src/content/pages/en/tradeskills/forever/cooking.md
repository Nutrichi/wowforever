Cooking is a secondary tradeskill, so it never costs you one of your two main slots. In WoW Forever it matters from level 1: most dishes add bonus experience, and every camp starts with a campfire from Cooking.

![A player cooking at a small campfire in a dry valley at dusk, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-cooking-at-a-campfire.jpg)

## What changes in Forever

- **Experience from food.** Most cooked food gives 5% more experience from kills while you are Well Fed, writes Wowhead.
- **One stat per dish.** Recipes you know are tweaked to favour a single primary stat, according to Warcraft Tavern.
- **New recipes**, such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=286152"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_fish_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Plated Armorfish</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=251525"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_fish_69.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Savory Whimsyfin Delight</a>.
- **Campfires.** Cooking builds the fire of every camp; see the [camping guide](/guides/camping/).
- **More than 100 recipes**, of which the trainers teach only about a dozen. The rest come from vendors, drops, quests and the [Azeroth Commerce Authority](/reputations/azeroth-commerce-authority/).
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5198"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cookie's Stirring Rod</a>** from Cookie in The Deadmines now cooks 25% faster.

## Ranks and where to learn them

| Rank | Skill | How |
|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | 1 to 75 | A Cooking trainer |
| Journeyman | 75 to 150 | A Cooking trainer |
| Expert | 150 to 225 | The book <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16072"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_book_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Expert Cookbook</a>, 10 gold |
| Artisan | 225 to 300 | The quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6610">Clamlette Surprise</a>, from character level 35 |

**The Expert book** is sold by <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3955">Shandrina</a>, northeast of Talondeep Path in Ashenvale (/way 49.6 67.0), for the Alliance, and by <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=12033">Wulan</a> in Shadowprey Village, Desolace (/way 26.2 69.8), for the Horde. It can be traded, so it also shows up at the Auction House.

## How it works

The Cooking window sorts the recipes by the stat they raise, with Camping on top. The only workstation is a fire, and your own campfire counts. Meat comes from beasts, fish from [Fishing](/tradeskills/forever/fishing/), spices from the Cooking vendors.
