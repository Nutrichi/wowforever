First Aid is a secondary tradeskill, so it never takes one of your two main slots. In WoW Forever it does far more than bandages: it now crafts the healing potions, and curatives against poison, disease and bleeding.

![Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen, Trauma Surgeon, in his infirmary in Theramore, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-doctor-gustaf-vanhowzen.jpg)

## What changes in Forever

- **Healing potions** moved from Alchemy to First Aid. They work in combat, so First Aid pays off for healers too.
- **Curatives** against disease and bleeding join the anti-venoms. The new ones cannot be used in combat, writes Warcraft Tavern.
- **Camp objects:** a First Aid Kit for Stamina, and two workstations with potions at a campsite.
- **The bandages** of Classic are all still there.

## Ranks and where to learn them

| Rank | Skill | How |
|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | 1 to 75 | A First Aid trainer |
| Journeyman | 75 to 150 | A First Aid trainer |
| Expert | 150 to 225 | The book <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16084"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_book_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Expert First Aid - Under Wraps</a> |
| Artisan | 225 to 300 | The quest Triage, from character level 35 |

**The Expert book** is sold by <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=2805">Deneb Walker</a> in Stromgarde Keep, Arathi Highlands (/way 27.0 58.8), for the Alliance, and by <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=13476">Balai Lok'Wein</a> in Brackenwall Village, Dustwallow Marsh (/way 36.4 30.4), for the Horde. It is not soulbound, so it also shows up at the Auction House.

**Artisan** comes from the quest Triage. The Alliance gets <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6624">Triage</a> from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=12939">Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen</a> on Theramore Isle (/way 67.6 48.8), the Horde <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6622">Triage</a> from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=12920">Doctor Gregory Victor</a> in Hammerfall (/way 73.4 36.8). The breadcrumbs <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6625">Alliance Trauma</a> and <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6623">Horde Trauma</a> start with the trainers in Ironforge and Orgrimmar, but you can skip them.
