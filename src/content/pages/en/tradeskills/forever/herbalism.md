Herbalism is a gathering tradeskill: you pick herbs from plants all over the world. Alchemists always need them, and in WoW Forever more tradeskills use herbs than before. On the way to level 60 you pass herbs everywhere, so it earns gold while you level.

## What changes in Forever

- **Camp objects.** Herbalists build the <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1229705"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_candle_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Incense Candle</a>, which gives everyone sitting at the camp Intellect like <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10157"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_magicalsentry.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Intellect</a>, the <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1262980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_herb_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Greenhouse</a> and the <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1263070"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_farm_herbseed.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seed Hybridizer</a>. How a campsite works is in the [camping guide](/guides/camping/).
- **Tauren** get more out of <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20552"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_flower_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cultivation</a>: it now duplicates a nearby herb instead of adding a flat bonus to the skill. Since 27 September it needs a minimum level, see the [post about it](/news/cultivation-gets-a-level-requirement-to-stop-herb-bots/).
- **Extras from herbs.** Besides the herb you sometimes get a seed, <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=268415"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_farm_pumpkinseed.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Commonplace Herb Seeds</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=268416"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_farm_pumpkinseed_yellow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Uncommon Herb Seeds</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=268417"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_farm_pumpkinseed_green.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Unusual Herb Seeds</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=268418"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_farm_pumpkinseed_blue.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scarce Herb Seeds</a>, or a Scarce material: <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249399"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_food_109_honeylichen.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frilled Lichen</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249424"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_herb_talandrasrose.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Rosecap</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249274"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_herbalism_70_nightmaredreamleaf.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Stranglevine</a>. Which herb gives which is not known yet.
- **New herbs** for the endgame: <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=248823"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/item_herbc.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Marefoil</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=248821"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_herb_heartblossom.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Demonsage</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=248822"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_nulllotus_void.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Death Lotus</a>. Where they grow and which skill they need is not known yet.
- **More herbs early.** Even starting areas such as Northshire have spawns of Peacebloom and Silverleaf.
- **A free start.** An early quest in every starting zone lets you choose <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=247841"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_10_specialization_professionbook_herbalism_color1.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wild Harvest</a>, which teaches Apprentice Herbalism.

**A good pair:** Mining, because you pass veins on the same routes, or Alchemy, which uses almost every herb. Druids avoid fights with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=9913"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_ambush.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Prowl</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=783"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_travelform.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Travel Form</a>; Hunters and Warlocks let the pet hold a monster while they pick, and a Hunter drops combat with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5384"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_feigndeath.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Feign Death</a>.
