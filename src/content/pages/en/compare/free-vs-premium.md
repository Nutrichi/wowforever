Leveling help for WoW Classic comes in two kinds. Free addons show quests and objectives, or follow routes that other players wrote. Paid guides give a complete route, step by step, and keep it up to date. The right choice depends on how much planning you want to do yourself.

## Free help

- **Questie** shows every available quest, its givers and its objectives on the map and the minimap. It is free and widely used. It does not choose an order: you decide which quests to do and where to go.
- **Guidelime** shows routes step by step inside the game. The addon is free, and the routes come from the community, so their quality and coverage vary.

## Paid help

- **RestedXP** gives routes built for fast leveling, written by speedrunners, with separate routes for Hardcore. See [RestedXP and Zygor compared](/compare/restedxp-vs-zygor/).
- **Zygor** combines leveling guides with advice on gear and talents and guides for dungeons, reputations and more.

## What the difference comes down to

| | Free addons | Paid guides |
|---|---|---|
| Quest markers on the map | Yes | Yes |
| A complete route in order | Only with a good community route | Yes |
| Arrow to the next step | Guidelime, with a route | Yes |
| Updates after patches | Depends on volunteers | Maintained by the company |
| Cost | None | A purchase or a subscription |

There are no independent measurements of how much time a paid guide saves. The gain is mainly less planning: the route decides the order, so you spend less time on the map.

## When free is enough

- You level one character and enjoy finding your way.
- You already know the zones well from earlier versions.
- A good Guidelime route exists for your faction and version.

## When paid help makes more sense

- You level several characters, where the same planning would repeat every time.
- You want to reach the level cap quickly, for example before a new phase opens.
- You play on a Hardcore realm and want a route that avoids risky areas.
