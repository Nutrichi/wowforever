RestedXP and Zygor are paid addons that show a leveling route inside the game, step by step. Both point an arrow to the next objective and follow your quest log. They differ in focus: RestedXP is built around fast leveling routes, and Zygor is a broader assistant that also helps with things beyond leveling.

## What RestedXP is

- Routes written by speedrunners, with the order of quests, grinding spots and dungeon stops worked out for speed.
- A compact window with the current step, an arrow and options to accept and hand in quests automatically.
- Separate routes for Hardcore that leave out risky pulls.
- Guides for the versions of WoW Classic and for modern World of Warcraft, sold per version on the RestedXP store.

## What Zygor is

- One of the oldest guide addons, in use since the original World of Warcraft.
- Leveling guides together with advice on gear and talents, and guides for dungeons, achievements, reputations and gold, depending on the version.
- A larger interface with more windows and settings.
- Sold as a subscription.

## Side by side

| | RestedXP | Zygor |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Fast leveling routes | Leveling plus other guides |
| Route | Built for speed, with Hardcore variants | Complete zone guides |
| Extras | Hardcore routes | Gear and talent advice, dungeons, achievements, gold |
| Interface | Compact | More windows and options |
| Payment | Per game version | Subscription |

Neither company publishes independent measurements of how fast its routes are, so claims about hours saved are best treated with care.

## Which one fits whom

- **Players who mainly want to reach the level cap quickly** get the most out of RestedXP, with its speed-focused order and compact window.
- **Players who want one addon for gear, talents and dungeons as well** find more of that in Zygor.
- **Players who level many alts** save the most planning with either addon, since the route is the same work every time.
- **Players who first want to try a free option** can start with Questie or Guidelime; the [comparison of free and paid help](/compare/free-vs-premium/) explains the difference.
