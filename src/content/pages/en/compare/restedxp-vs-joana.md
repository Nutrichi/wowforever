RestedXP and Joana's guide both give a fixed order of quests and zones to level quickly. The difference is how you follow it. RestedXP is an addon that shows each step inside the game, with an arrow. Joana's guide became famous as a written guide that you read and follow yourself.

## Where they come from

Joana's guide is one of the best-known leveling guides of original World of Warcraft. It was written by the speedrunner Joana, who became known for leveling a Horde character to 60 very quickly and turned that route into a guide. For many players it was the first time a leveling route was written down step by step.

RestedXP was started much later by speedrunners of WoW Classic. It puts the route inside the game as an addon and covers the versions of WoW Classic as well as modern World of Warcraft.

## An addon or a guide you read

- **RestedXP** shows the current step in a window, points an arrow to the objective and can accept and hand in quests. You look at the game, not at a second screen.
- **A written guide** gives the same kind of steps as text. You check the next step, find the place on the map and keep track of where you are. That costs attention, but it works without any addon.

## Side by side

| | RestedXP | Joana's guide |
|---|---|---|
| Form | Addon inside the game | Written guide |
| Following the route | Arrow and current step on screen | Reading and finding the way yourself |
| Origin | Speedrunners of WoW Classic | Speedrunner of original World of Warcraft |
| Hardcore | Separate Hardcore routes | No separate routes known |
| Versions | WoW Classic versions and modern WoW | Check the current version of the guide |

Neither guide publishes independent measurements of its speed. How fast a character levels depends as much on the player as on the route.

## Which one fits whom

- **Players who want to look at the game, not at a text,** get more out of RestedXP.
- **Players who like to plan and learn the world** can enjoy a written route, and it works without addons.
- **Players on Hardcore realms** have separate routes in RestedXP that avoid risky areas.
