World of Warcraft: Forever launches on 4 November 2026, and three paid leveling addons will be ready for it: RestedXP, Journey and Zygor. All three show the next step of a route in the game, with an arrow to the next objective. They differ in price, in how much of the route is ready at launch and in what they do besides leveling.

Prices are in dollars, plus tax. None of the three publishes independent measurements of its speed, so claims about hours saved are best treated with care.

## RestedXP

RestedXP sells its Forever guide per faction in its store, as a one-time purchase. The addon itself is free; the routes are the paid part. Its routes are written by speedrunners and are known for a compact window that accepts and hands in quests for you. How far the Forever route goes on launch day is not published yet. The comparison outside Forever is in [RestedXP and Zygor compared](/compare/restedxp-vs-zygor/).

## Journey

Journey is new and built for Forever. Levels 1 to 30 are free for everyone and already have Forever's new quests and the Skyborne start on Zephras Isle. On 4 November, levels 30 to 60 start on the classic route; about a week later Forever's new quests are added, around five levels every four to five days. Everything else is in [the post about Journey](/news/leveling-addon-journey-is-free-up-to-level-30/).

## Zygor

Zygor updates its 1 to 60 guides for the new zones, the Skyborne start and the new race and class combinations, and adds guides for the nine new dungeons. Its travel system and talent advisor get Forever updates too. Zygor warns that the beta's level cap limits what it can test, so the route may be partly updated at launch and completed afterwards. All of it comes with a Zygor Elite subscription.

## Why a Classic route will not do

Forever is not Classic Era with extras bolted on. Three changes break an existing route:

- **New zones and ports.** Mount Hyjal, Shen'Dralas, Riverglades and Zephras Isle, with new boat routes. See [the four new zones](/news/four-new-zones-and-three-ports-in-wow-forever/).
- **Over 1,000 new quests** on top of the roughly 3,000 that exist, which changes where the experience sits.
- **Dungeon experience moved.** Killing inside a dungeon gives less and the dungeon quests give much more. See [the dungeon experience change](/news/wow-forever-dungeon-experience-changes/).

## The free options

- **Questie** shows quest objectives on the map and works on every version soon after a patch. It gives no route.
- **Guidelime** plays routes written by the community, as soon as someone writes one for Forever.

Free and paid are not exclusive. The trade-offs are in [free and paid leveling help compared](/compare/free-vs-premium/).

## Which one to pick

- **You want the fastest route and pay once.** RestedXP is built for speed, and its Forever guide is on sale now.
- **You want to try before you buy.** Journey is free up to level 30, so you see its route before you spend anything.
- **You want one addon for more than leveling** and do not mind a subscription. Zygor also covers dungeons, gear and talents.
- **You are not in a hurry.** Questie plus your own reading is enough, and a free Guidelime route may turn up in the first weeks.
