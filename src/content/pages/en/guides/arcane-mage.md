The Arcane Mage in WoW Forever finally has damage spells of its own. Arcane Blast stacks spell damage, Missile Barrage makes Arcane Missiles free and fast, and Arcane Wrath is a heavy nuke that grows with your mana. The spec hits hard on one target, but it burns mana fast and depends on procs. This guide covers the builds, rotation, mana, survival and gear.

## What changes for Arcane

- **Arcane Blast is new.** A talent at level 20 with a 1.5 second cast. Each cast gives 15% of your Intellect as spell damage, up to 4 stacks, and raises the mana cost of the next Arcane Blast by 175%.
- **Missile Barrage is new.** At 2/2, Arcane Blast has a 40% chance, and Arcane Wrath, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=133"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flamebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fireball</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> and Frostfire Bolt a 20% chance, to make your next Arcane Missiles free and twice as fast. Arcane Missiles hits have a 10% chance too.
- **Arcane Wrath is new.** A talent at level 25: 3.5 second cast, 6 second cooldown, with extra spell damage equal to 5% of your maximum mana.
- **Arcane Meditation is stronger.** At 3/3, 50% of your mana regeneration continues while casting. With <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6117"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_magearmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mage Armor</a> at level 34 that becomes 100%.
- **The old support talents remain.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11213"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_manaburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Concentration</a>, Arcane Mind, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15058"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_teleport.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Instability</a> and Arcane Power still do what they did, now for a spec with real damage.

## Rotation

**One enemy**
1. Cast Arcane Blast to build stacks and damage the target.
2. Spend every Clearcasting proc on Arcane Blast.
3. Cast Arcane Missiles as soon as Missile Barrage procs.
4. Cast Arcane Wrath at 3 or 4 Arcane Blast stacks.
5. Can you not afford another Arcane Blast and Arcane Wrath is on cooldown? Cast Fireball or Frostbolt to fish for Missile Barrage, or Arcane Missiles without a proc.
6. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> while you move.
7. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1326053"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Blast</a> to slow a target from a distance, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a> rank 1 to lock down a dangerous monster, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_glacier.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cone of Cold</a> when it reaches you.
8. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2139"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_iceshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Counterspell</a> on a dangerous cast, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=118"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a> on a dangerous extra enemy.

**A pack of enemies**

## Arcane Blast stacks and procs

The spec plays in burn phases. The first Arcane Blast starts a 15 second buff that stacks to 4. Four Arcane Blasts in a row and then everything else would be ideal, but the mana cost rarely allows that, especially at low levels. So you spread your Arcane Blasts out and lean on two procs.

1. **Clearcasting first.** Use it on Arcane Blast for a free stack, especially once you are at 4 stacks.
2. **Missile Barrage next.** Arcane Missiles can trigger another Missile Barrage or Clearcasting by itself.
3. **Arcane Wrath twice per window.** Once at 3 stacks and once at 4, before the buff runs out. More Haste makes the long cast easier.

No procs at all? Save mana for the next window. Cast Arcane Wrath on cooldown until the buff ends, then switch to Fireball or Frostbolt to fish for Missile Barrage. From level 40, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=401502"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_frostfirebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostfire Bolt</a> works for that too.

## Mana

Everything about Arcane revolves around mana. Out of combat <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5504"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_drink_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Conjure Water</a> gives you endless water. In combat you control your mana in three ways: hold back Arcane Blast when you run low, time your procs with your most expensive spells, and use direct mana.

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12051"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_purge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Evocation</a>** from level 20 raises your mana regeneration by 1500% for 8 seconds, with an 8 minute cooldown. Save it for long boss fights. A High Order Skyborne can combine it with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259705"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_incantersabsorbtion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Read Leyline</a>.
- **Mana gems** such as <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=759"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gem_emerald_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Conjure Mana Agate</a> from level 28 restore 375 to 425 mana, one charge per gem. Make a new one before every long fight.
- **Mana potions** restore a lot, depending on their level.

From the third Arcane Blast stack, casting without thinking is the fastest way to run dry. Arcane Meditation and later Mage Armor carry you, but only if you pick your moments.

## Survival

Arcane has less survival than Frost and no Ice Block. Kill a target before it kills you, and pick your fights. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blink.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blink</a> and Frost Nova get you out of most trouble. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1463"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_detectlesserinvisibility.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mana Shield</a> costs too much mana to use all the time, but Mana Shield with Evocation can save you as a last resort.

Use Frost Blast or rank 1 Frostbolt to slow a target from a distance, Cone of Cold as an instant slow, and Polymorph to take a target out of the fight. Fences, hills, buildings and trees break line of sight: an enemy has to walk around them, which buys you casts. Against a caster, break line of sight when Counterspell is on cooldown.

## Stats and gear

The exact priority at level 60 follows after the launch. These stats help an Arcane Mage:

- **Spell damage and Arcane spell damage** first. Arcane spell damage only helps Arcane spells.
- **Intellect** matters more for Arcane than for the other Mage specs: a bigger mana pool, 10% extra from Arcane Mind, and Arcane Blast turns 15% of it into spell damage per stack.
- **Critical strike chance.** Arcane Impact and Arcane Mind make crits stronger. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flameshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Incineration</a> in the Fire tree adds 6% critical strike chance to Arcane Blast.
- **Haste** means faster casts and more procs. At low levels only Troll (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20554"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/racial_troll_berserk.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Berserking</a>) and High Order Skyborne (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259710"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_volatileair.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wind Blessed</a>) get it.
- **Spirit and mana per 5 seconds** are worth more for Arcane than for other Mages, thanks to Arcane Meditation and later Mage Armor.
- **Stamina** comes on most gear. You do not need to look for more.

**Armor.** Cloth with spell damage, Intellect and Stamina. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273044"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_cloth_17.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Violet Sorcerer's Robes</a> at level 27 is a new robe with 15 Intellect and spell damage.

**Weapons.** Weapons got a lot more spell damage in Forever. Orc is the strongest pick in PvE, Troll the second. For the Alliance, High Order Skyborne comes first and Human second. Gnome took nerfs on the beta and mainly stays interesting with Engineering. None of it is strong enough to skip a race you like more.

**Professions.** Tailoring makes cloth sets with spell damage and Intellect, and pairs with Enchanting for your own wands and enchants. Engineering is the best partner for Tailoring if you want the most damage. Herbalism feeds Alchemy, which makes elixirs, potions and flasks.
