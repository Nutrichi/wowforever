Mages in World of Warcraft: Forever can teleport themselves to the capitals of their faction and open portals for their group. This guide lists every spell with its level and cost, the runes you need and where each Portal Trainer stands.

| | |
|---|---|
| Teleports | From level 20, 20 silver per spell |
| Portals | From level 40, 1 gold 50 silver per spell |

## How you learn them

Each capital has its own Portal Trainer, who only teaches the teleport and portal to that city. To get every destination, you visit all three capitals of your faction. Every cast needs a rune, sold by every reagent vendor. With the Legacy talent <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225503"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_candle_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Reagent Economy</a> you no longer need those runes; see the [Legacy calculator](/talents/legacy/).
