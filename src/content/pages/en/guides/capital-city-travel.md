Travel between the capitals is easier in WoW Forever than in Classic. A new boat from Stormwind Harbor sails to Darnassus without the long Wetlands run. This guide shows the fastest way between the capitals of both factions, step by step.

## At a glance

| From | To | How |
|---|---|---|
| Stormwind | Ironforge | Deeprun Tram, later a direct flight |
| Stormwind | Darnassus | Boat to Auberdine, boat to Rut'theran Village, portal |
| Orgrimmar | Undercity | Zeppelin from the tower outside Orgrimmar |
| Orgrimmar | Thunder Bluff | On foot through The Barrens, later a direct flight |

All routes also work the other way round. Every flight point and boat is in the [flight path guide](/guides/flight-paths/), and a Mage can do it faster with [portals](/classes/mage/portals/).

## Stormwind to Ironforge

The Deeprun Tram starts in the Dwarven District of Stormwind. Walk through the portal into the tram station and board either train: both go to the same station in Ironforge. Do not jump around on the train, you can fall off. Then you have to walk the long tunnel. In Ironforge, the portal at the station takes you into Tinker Town.

The flight is faster, but only once you have both flight points. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=352">Dungar Longdrink</a> stands in the southwest of the Trade District in Stormwind (/way 66.2 62.4), <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=1573">Gryth Thurden</a> at the Great Forge in Ironforge (/way 55.8 48.0). So take the tram once, pick up the flight point, and fly from then on.

## Stormwind to Darnassus

![The harbor of Stormwind with a ship at the docks and the city behind it, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-12-wow-forever-stormwind-harbor.jpg)

1. **Stormwind Harbor to Auberdine.** The harbor is new in Forever. Take the boat on the left side: its dock has Night Elf ballistas and symbols, so it is easy to spot.
2. **Auberdine to Rut'theran Village.** Auberdine has three docks: the one you arrive at, one on the left to Rut'theran Village and one on the right to Menethil Harbor. The boat to Rut'theran docks on the north side of Auberdine. Pick up the flight point of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3841">Caylais Moonfeather</a> (/way 36.4 45.6) first if you do not have it yet.
3. **Rut'theran Village to Darnassus.** Walk through the glowing portal in the tree. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3838">Vesprystus</a> at Rut'theran (/way 58.4 94.0) flies to Auberdine.

Night Elves know the flight path between Rut'theran Village and Auberdine from the start, even if they have never been to Auberdine. They never have to wait for the boat on the way to Stormwind.

![A stone path through tall trees to the temple ruins of Darnassus under a pink sky, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-19-wow-forever-darnassus-temple-path.jpg)

## Orgrimmar to Undercity

The zeppelin is quick and has not changed. The tower stands just outside the gate of Orgrimmar, on the right. Climb it and board the zeppelin that docks next to the zeppelin master: it flies to Tirisfal Glades, right next to Undercity.

On the way back, the tower in Tirisfal stands between Brill and Undercity. Both towers also have a zeppelin to Grom'gol in Stranglethorn Vale, so check which one you board.

![Orgrimmar at sunset with a zeppelin above the city and a campfire on the sand, a screenshot of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-30-wow-forever-orgrimmar-sunset.jpg)

## Orgrimmar to Thunder Bluff

This one is still a long walk the first time. Pick up every flight point on the way, and you never have to walk it again.

1. Take the flight point of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3310">Doras</a> on the tower in the Valley of Strength (/way 45.2 63.8).
2. Leave Orgrimmar to Razor Hill and follow the road east to Far Watch Post at the edge of Durotar.
3. Go on into The Barrens and south to the Crossroads. Take the flight point of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3615">Devrak</a> (/way 51.4 30.2).
4. Follow the Gold Road south to Camp Taurajo and take the flight point of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=10378">Omusa Thunderhorn</a> (/way 44.4 59.0). Stay on the road at a low level.
5. Walk west into Mulgore, follow the road to Bloodhoof Village and on to the north, to the foot of Thunder Bluff. The elevators take you up.
6. Take the flight point of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=2995">Tal</a>, in the large totem in the middle of the city (/way 46.8 50.0).

From then on, Orgrimmar and Thunder Bluff are one flight apart.
