Camping is a new system in WoW Forever. Someone lights a campfire, every tradeskill adds an object around it, and anyone who sits there for a minute gets the buffs. Higher tiers add workstations, a vendor and a repair bot.

![A campfire, a tent, a first aid kit and candles under the trees of Teldrassil, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-camp-in-teldrassil.jpg)

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Campfire | Cooking, used once, burns about 10 minutes |
| Campfire cooldown | 5 minutes |
| Objects per camp | 3, 5 or 10, depending on the campfire |
| Objects per character | 1, with a shared cooldown of 1 hour |
| Getting the buffs | Sit (/sit) near the fire for 1 minute |
| Tier 1 objects | Skill 20, from the trainer or the Camping 101 quest |
| Tier 2 and 3 | Skill 140 and 300, from blueprints |

![The quest window of Camping 101: Mining, which teaches the Lodestone, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-camping-101-mining.jpg)

## The campfire

Every camp starts with a campfire from Cooking. You need <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4471"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_stone_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Flint and Tinder</a> and one piece of wood. Right-click the kit, pick a spot, and the fire burns for about 10 minutes.

## How the buffs work

Near a fire you get <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1283391"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_camelot_camping_campfirenearby.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Campfire Nearby</a>. Type /sit, and after a minute <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1289723"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Welcoming Campfire</a> turns into <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1229741"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_camelot_camping_campbenefits.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Camp Benefits</a>, one buff that holds every effect of the camp. Hover over it to see which ones you have. Sitting down again adds the effects of objects placed later.

Each camp buff is a weaker copy of a class buff and does not stack with it. The Camp Tent is the only one with a cooldown of its own: after it you get <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1229451"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_sleep.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Boosted Rest</a> for an hour.
