The Black Market Auction House, BMAH for short, sells rare items for a lot of gold. It first appeared in Mists of Pandaria, in patch 5.0.4. WoW Forever brings it to the old world, in the new zone The Riverglades, and Wowhead found it on the beta.

![Mister Graphed and Madam Swyndle, two goblins with the title Agent of the Black Market, on a purple carpet in a tent, with a Blood Elf in front of them, a screenshot from the game](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-27-wow-forever-black-market-agents.jpg)

## Where to find the BMAH

| | Where |
|---|---|
| Zone | The Riverglades, levels 30 to 45 |
| Place | Powderfuse Port, a small goblin outpost on the river |
| Coordinates | /way 78.1 51.9 |
| Not at | Farholde Keep, the new stronghold of the zone |

The Riverglades lie north of Redridge Mountains. A fork in Redridge that used to be a dead end now leads there, and a boat links the zone to Steamwheedle Port. The zone is described on [the Forever page](/forever/).

![The map of The Riverglades zoomed in on Powderfuse Port, a goblin outpost on the west bank of a river, with the player arrow on it](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-27-wow-forever-powderfuse-port-map.jpg)

## The two agents

| NPC | What they sell |
|---|---|
| [Madam Swyndle](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=263326) | The Black Market itself: gear, pets and collectibles |
| [Mister Graphed](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=263384) | Crafting reagents and other goods |

Both are level 60 goblins with the title Agent of the Black Market. Wowhead writes that Forever has "even more agents" of the Black Market, but not where the others stand.

## Gear: Dungeon Set 1

Most of what Madam Swyndle offers is gear from Dungeon Set 1, the blue sets from the dungeons of original Classic. Each piece costs 100 gold or more and binds when picked up. These four pieces were found on the beta.

### How the sets differ from Classic

The set names stayed, the bonuses changed. This comes from the tooltips of Wowhead for Forever and for Classic Era.

| Pieces | Classic Era | WoW Forever |
|---|---|---|
| each piece | no spell power of its own | 10 to 14 spell power |
| 2 | +200 Armor | +8 All Resistances |
| 3 | nothing | up to 18 spell power |
| 4 | up to 23 spell power | an effect when struck, 2% or 5% chance, 1 min cooldown |
| 5 | nothing | an effect on spellcast |
| 6 | an effect of the set | 8 mana per 5 sec |
| 8 | +8 All Resistances | no longer a bonus |

The effects per set in Forever:

| Set | 4 pieces | 5 pieces |
|---|---|---|
| Magister's Regalia | 2% chance to freeze the attacker for 3 sec | 5% chance on spellcast to restore 200 mana |
| Dreadmist Raiment | 2% chance to make the attacker flee for 2 sec | 5% chance on spellcast to heal for 270 to 330 |
| The Elements | 5% chance to disarm a melee attacker for 4 sec | a chance to gain up to 65 spell power for 10 sec |

Some stats went down to pay for this. Magister's Robes have 28 Intellect instead of 31. Dreadmist Mantle lost its 9 Spirit, and Magister's Crown lost 5 Spirit and 1 Stamina. Boots of Elements kept their stats.

## What is not known yet

- Whether players bid on items as on the retail Black Market, or pay a fixed price.
- How often the stock changes, and whether other Dungeon Set 1 pieces or mounts show up.
- Where the other agents of the Black Market stand.
- What Mister Graphed sells exactly.
- Whether the prices hold at launch on 4 November.
