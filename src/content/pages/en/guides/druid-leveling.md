The Druid can do almost anything in WoW Forever: tank as a bear, deal damage as a cat or from range, and heal. It also moves faster through the world than any other class. You level as Feral, with one build from level 10 to 60. This guide covers the build, the rotation, the key levels, the class quests, weapons and professions.

| | |
|---|---|

| Strength | Mobility, Stealth, self healing, can tank and heal |

## Which spec to level with

The first 20 levels change a lot. You start with Wrath, get Bear Form at 10 and Cat Form at 20. The start is a little slow, but from Cat Form on it goes fast.

**Level as Feral.** A cat hardly needs to stop for mana or healing, and Prowl, Dash and Feral Swiftness bring you from one quest to the next. The same build also tanks in Bear Form. Balance and Restoration are for groups and for level 60; they have their own guides for [Balance](/guides/balance-druid/) and [Restoration](/guides/restoration-druid/).

The build below gives a talent for every level from 10 to 60. With points in Talented from the Legacy system, you reach every talent up to five levels sooner. Skyborne Druids also have a step-by-step route through their starting area, from [level 1 to 6](/guides/leveling/skyborne-druid-1-6/).

## Levels 1 to 10

You have no talents and no forms yet, only <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5176"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_abolishmagic.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wrath</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5185"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_healingtouch.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Healing Touch</a>. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8921"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_starfall.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Moonfire</a> follows at 4, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=467"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_thorns.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thorns</a> at 6 and Entangling Roots at 8.

1. Keep Thorns on yourself.
2. Pull with Wrath.
3. Put Moonfire on the monster.
4. Cast Wrath until the monster is almost dead or your mana runs low.
5. Finish it with your weapon.

To get away, use <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16689"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_natureswrath.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nature's Grasp</a>, which every Druid learns at level 10 in Forever. Use Healing Touch to heal yourself when needed.

Stats at these levels: Intellect and spell damage, then Stamina and Spirit, then armor.

At level 30 the build looks like [this in the calculator](/talents/druid/?t=-5023003212110001).

**Up to 30** the build lowers the cost of your attacks, makes you faster and harder to spot in Prowl, and gives Feral Charge, Primal Bite and Shifting Power. **From 30** Leader of the Pack, Natural Instinct and Blood Frenzy raise your critical strikes and what they give back. **At 40** Berserk lets Primal Bite hit up to three monsters without a cooldown. **In Restoration** Furor gives back your Energy when you return to Cat Form, Natural Shapeshifter makes every shift 30% cheaper, and Naturalist adds 5% to all your damage.

**As a cat:**

1. Keep Thorns on yourself.
2. Use Faerie Fire for the armor debuff, or to pull from range.
3. Build Combo Points with Claw, or with Shred if you stand behind the monster.
4. Use Shifting Power below 50 Energy.
5. Use Rip at 4 or 5 Combo Points.
6. Put Rake on the monster. Skip the bleeds if it is about to die.
7. Finish it with Shred or Claw.

**As a bear:**

1. Keep Thorns on yourself.
2. Use Faerie Fire for the debuff, or to pull from range. In Bear Form it now causes threat.
3. Use Demoralizing Roar.
4. Use Primal Bite on cooldown.
5. Use Swipe against more monsters and Maul against one.

Stats from here: Strength, Agility and Attack Power, then Stamina, Intellect and Spirit, then armor.

## Weapons

In an animal form your auto attacks deal the damage per second of your weapon in Forever. A better weapon therefore raises all your melee damage, also as a cat or a bear. Polearms are new for the Druid and can be learned from level 20. A new weapon type starts at a low skill, so train it before you rely on it.

## Idols and gear

**Idols** go in the relic slot and give the Druid a bonus of its own. The quest for Aquatic Form rewards <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=263411"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_relics_idolofrejuvenation.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Idol of Shifting Tides</a>, which lowers the mana cost of every shapeshift by 40. Keep it until you find an idol that raises your damage. Horde Druids can get a similar idol at level 5 on Zephras Isle, through the quest Meddlesome Mages; more about it in [this post](/news/horde-druids-and-shamans-get-a-relic-at-level-5/).

The **Embrace of the Viper** set from [Wailing Caverns](/guides/dungeons/wailing-caverns/) is strong for a Feral Druid and lasts a while. With all five pieces, your Cat Form turns into a [snake](/news/a-hidden-set-bonus-turns-druids-into-a-snake/).

Two famous items are back for Feral Druids. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_mace_14.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Manual Crowd Pummeler</a> drops in [Gnomeregan](/guides/dungeons/gnomeregan/) and requires level 29. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=8345"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_helmet_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wolfshead Helm</a> comes from Leatherworking at level 40 and now gives 5 extra Energy from Shifting Power. Neither is likely to stay the best choice all the way to 60.

## Professions

Level your professions alongside your Druid, so you gather and craft at the right level. Camps give buffs to every profession.

- **Cooking** is a must: buff food gives extra experience, and you need it for a campfire.
- **Herbalism** is the classic choice: you pick herbs without leaving Travel Form. Its camp gives Intellect.
- **Alchemy** goes well with Herbalism and gives potions and a camp for mana regeneration.
- **Leatherworking** has strong soulbound leveling gear that you cannot get anywhere else, and the Camp Tent for rested experience.
- **Skinning** delivers the leather and is easy in Travel Form. Its Camp Chair gives the same kind of critical strike bonus as Leader of the Pack and Moonkin Aura, so Feral and Balance Druids gain little from it.

## Consumables

In Forever you can use potions and almost every similar consumable without leaving your form. That makes them much more useful for a Druid than before.

- **Food and water.** Stay Well Fed for the extra experience; any buff food is better than none. Even a Feral Druid sometimes needs mana, so carry water of your level.
- **Potions.** Healing potions for emergencies, now also from First Aid. The First Aid cures for diseases and bleeds help, because a Druid cannot remove those otherwise. Mana, Energy or Rage potions depending on your build.
- **Elixirs** with the right stats for your build help against a hard monster or quest.
- **Rebirth** needs a reagent for every cast, which one depends on the rank. Check the tooltip.

## Shapeshifting

Your forms are the core of the class. Bear Form tanks, Cat Form deals melee damage and gives Prowl, Aquatic Form swims faster and breathes under water, Travel Form runs fast without a mount, and Moonkin Form at level 40 is the form of Balance.

Forever takes away much of the friction. Potions, weapon enchants and procs work in form, most doors and objects open in form, and racials such as Shadowmeld and War Stomp work too. Fewer effects knock you out of a form, and Cat Form no longer lowers your maximum health. Each form has its own action bar, so set them up as you learn new abilities.

Shifting itself still breaks roots and slows. Do that instead of waiting until a root runs out. Leave your form to heal yourself or to use a spell that only works outside a form.

**Powershifting is gone.** At five points, Furor gives back the Energy you had when you last left Cat Form, plus 10 Energy for every second outside Bear Form, up to 100. A quick shift out and back therefore adds nothing. The talent Shifting Power turns mana into 40 Energy instead, with a cooldown of 16 seconds, or 8 with Improved Shifting Power. More about that in [this post](/news/shifting-power-replaces-powershifting-for-ferals/).

## Stealth and movement

No class moves through the world as easily as a Druid.

- **Prowl** makes you one of two classes with Stealth. You choose when and how a fight starts.
- **Dash** and **Feral Swiftness** make Cat Form faster; **Feral Charge** brings you to a monster. Pounce opens from Prowl from level 36.
- **Aquatic Form** crosses water and lets you collect items from the bottom of a lake without a breath bar.
- **Travel Form** at level 30 runs almost as fast as a mount and needs no summoning. You can pick up quest items and gather herbs in it. Other classes wait until level 40 for their mount.
- **Teleport: Moonglade** is a second Hearthstone. Moonglade has a flight master per faction to Darnassus or Thunder Bluff, and a regular one in the south of the zone, which you have to visit once first.

## Mana, Energy and Rage

Every Druid needs mana, also as a cat or a bear: shapeshifting and utility spells cost mana. A cat uses Energy and a bear Rage, which comes from dealing and taking damage. Keep something in reserve: enough mana to shift, or a little Rage to Bash right away when a monster starts casting.

## Healing

Even without Restoration you heal yourself and your group.

- **Healing Touch** is the plain heal you will use most.
- **Rejuvenation** tops you up on the move, or before expected damage.
- **Regrowth** gives a heal plus a small heal over time.
- **Tranquility** heals the group, but only at full channel and with a long cooldown. Not much use alone.
- **Cure Poison**, **Abolish Poison** and **Remove Curse** remove the cause before it hurts.

Restoration adds Swiftmend and Wild Growth through its talents.

## Crowd control

**Entangling Roots** holds a monster in place, and Nature's Grasp roots whoever hits you. Druids are good at kiting: root a monster, walk away while your damage over time ticks, cast again, and switch to Cat Form when you need more distance. Even as Feral you can keep one monster rooted while you drag the other one away.

**Hibernate** puts a beast or dragonkin to sleep, and breaks on damage. **Soothe Animal** helps others sneak past beasts. **Faerie Fire** lowers the armor of a monster and pulls it from range without leaving your form, also to separate it from a group.

## Buffs and cooldowns

**Mark of the Wild** raises stats and armor for every class; keep it up. **Thorns** damages whoever attacks you and speeds up your kills, but lasts only 10 minutes. Leader of the Pack and Moonkin Aura now reach the [whole raid](/news/battle-shout-and-totems-now-buff-the-whole-raid/).

Use your cooldowns in the open world too, not only on bosses. **Innervate** gives a lot of mana back, so you can keep pulling. **Barkskin** lowers physical damage taken, and attacks no longer delay your casts while it lasts. **Rebirth** brings a group member back in combat.

More about food, camps and rested XP is in the [experience guide](/guides/experience/).
