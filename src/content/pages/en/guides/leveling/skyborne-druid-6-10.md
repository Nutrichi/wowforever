This guide shows level 6 to 10 as a Skyborne Druid of the High Order, the Alliance side of the new race. Everything happens on Zephras Isle, the starting zone of the Skyborne. The video is raw gameplay from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever, without commentary, and follows the leveling route of [RestedXP](https://shop.restedxp.com/ref/nutri/).
