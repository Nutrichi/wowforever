## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=15078">Jang</a> sells it in Stranglethorn Vale for 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274026"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_coin_16.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Extravagant Extravaganza Coin</a>.

The coin is the prize for winning the Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza.

## Good to know

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
