## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=281167"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_zerglingpet_c60.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Zergling Leash</a> comes with the Skyborne Epic Pack and the Warcraft Forever Collection, two of the digital editions of WoW Forever in the Battle.net Shop. It works only in Forever. What each edition contains is on the [WoW Forever page](/forever/).

The old item of the Collector's Edition from 2004, <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=13582"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonfelhunter.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Zergling Leash</a>, is still in the files too. In Classic it was the reward of the quest Welcome! for owners of that edition.

## What changed since Classic

The item now teaches the pet: it becomes a spell and no longer takes a spot in your bags. Summoning it is instant instead of a 1 second cast, with a cooldown of 1.5 seconds.

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
