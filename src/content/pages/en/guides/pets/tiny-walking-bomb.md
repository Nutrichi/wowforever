## How to get it

Engineers make it with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15628"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bomb_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pet Bombling</a>, from 205 skill in Engineering. It needs 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4394"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bomb_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Big Iron Bomb</a>, 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7077"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_lavaspawn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heart of Fire</a>, 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7191"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gear_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fused Wiring</a> and 6 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3860"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ingot_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mithril Bar</a>.

## What changed since Classic

The item now teaches the pet: it becomes a spell and no longer takes a spot in your bags. Summoning it is instant instead of a 1 second cast, with a cooldown of 1.5 seconds.

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
