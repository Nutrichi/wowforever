## How to get it

Engineers make it with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19793"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_monsterhead_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lifelike Mechanical Toad</a>, from 265 skill in Engineering. It needs 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=12803"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_abolishmagic.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Living Essence</a>, 4 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15994"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thorium Widget</a>, 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=10558"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_battery_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gold Power Core</a> and 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=8170"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_leatherscrap_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Rugged Leather</a>.

## What changed since Classic

The item now teaches the pet: it becomes a spell and no longer takes a spot in your bags. Summoning it is instant instead of a 1 second cast, with a cooldown of 1.5 seconds.

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
