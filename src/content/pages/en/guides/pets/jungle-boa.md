## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=8404">Xan'tish</a> sells it in Orgrimmar for 52 silver 63 copper. The vendor is friendly to the Horde.

## Good to know

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
