## How to get it

It sits on Zephras Isle, the starting zone of the Skyborne, at /way 51.5 72.3. Even a level 1 character gets there, often without a fight.

1. **The way.** Follow the road south from Thendal Village and cut across a short field to the waterfalls. Do not run down the path below them.
2. **The frogs.** At the top of the waterfall, a group of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=259006">Musical Gustjumper</a> sits on a rock. Jump on the log to reach them.
3. **Sing.** Target a frog and use /sing. It sings back and the group disappears.
4. **Click.** A frog you can click appears on the log next to you. Click it to learn the pet.

## Good to know

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
