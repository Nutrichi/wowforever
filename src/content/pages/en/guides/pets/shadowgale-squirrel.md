## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=254151">Vayn Moongaze</a> sells it in Zephras Isle for 52 silver 63 copper.

The squirrel needs Revered with the Nightclaw Druids before the vendor sells it.

## Good to know

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
