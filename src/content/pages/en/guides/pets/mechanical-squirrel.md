## How to get it

Engineers make it with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=3928"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_crate_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mechanical Squirrel</a>, from 75 skill in Engineering. It needs 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4363"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Copper Modulator</a>, 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4359"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gear_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Handful of Copper Bolts</a>, 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2840"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ingot_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Copper Bar</a> and 2 <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=774"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gem_emerald_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Malachite</a>.

## What changed since Classic

The item now teaches the pet: it becomes a spell and no longer takes a spot in your bags. Summoning it is instant instead of a 1 second cast, with a cooldown of 1.5 seconds.

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
