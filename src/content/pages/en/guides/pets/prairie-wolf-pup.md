## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=268745">Mayhoa Skyhoof</a> sells it in Orgrimmar for 63 silver 15 copper. The vendor is friendly to the Horde.

## Good to know

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
