## How to get it

It is the reward of the quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=4298">Becoming a Parent</a>, from level 37, for the Alliance.

The Sprite Darter Egg is also sold at the Black Market Auction House, by Madam Swyndle at Powderfuse Port in The Riverglades. More in the [BMAH guide](/guides/bmah/).

## What changed since Classic

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
