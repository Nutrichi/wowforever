## How to get it

It is the reward of the quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=97583">WRIGGLE.</a>, which an <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=269452">Excitable Slime</a> in the canal of Undercity gives after a dance. No group is needed:

1. **Buy the food.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4612">Boyle</a>, the Sludge Seller in Undercity, sells <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=278265"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_food_legion_gooslime_bottle.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nutritious Slime Sludge</a> at /way 56.2 64.6.
2. **Find the slime.** Jump into the canal and look for an Excitable Slime that patrols there.
3. **Dance.** Target it and use /dance until it dances back. That takes several tries.
4. **Take the quest.** The slime then offers WRIGGLE. If it seems tired, dance with it again.
5. **Feed it.** Hand over the Nutritious Slime Sludge. The pet is yours, with 10 experience on top.

Use /dance at the pet later on, and it dances along.

## Good to know

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
