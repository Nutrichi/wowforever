## How to get it

It is the reward of the quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93176">My Little Friends</a>, from level 25, for both factions.

## Good to know

The game data marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
