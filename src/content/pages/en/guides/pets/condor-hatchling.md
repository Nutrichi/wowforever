## How to get it

It drops from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14268">Lord Condar</a> in Loch Modan. In the loot data it drops in about 1.4% of kills.

## Good to know

The game data marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
