## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=280614"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pachimarapet.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pachimari</a> comes with the Skyborne Epic Pack and the Warcraft Forever Collection, two of the digital editions of WoW Forever in the Battle.net Shop. It works only in Forever. What each edition contains is on the [WoW Forever page](/forever/).

## Good to know

The tooltip on the beta still shows placeholder text ([PH] Pachimara), so this pet is not finished yet.

Wowhead's database marks the pet as account-wide: learned once, it would be there for every character. Blizzard has not confirmed that itself.
