In WoW Forever you can change your look at any time in a barbershop. Every capital has one, and you recognize it by the red and white barber pole outside. It is free, so change as often as you like.

## What you can change

Sit in a barbershop chair and you get the character customization screen:

- **Every appearance option** from character creation, including your body type.
- **The model:** switch between the high definition and the classic character models.
- **Druid forms:** Druids can also change the look of their forms.

Your race and class stay the same. To change the look of your gear, visit a Transmogrifier in your capital.
