PvP in WoW Forever rests on two numbers: Rank Points (RP) and Honor. Rank Points take you up the 14 ranks of the original game, Honor buys the gear. This guide explains both, lists every rank with its reward, and shows where to queue and what the battlegrounds give.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rank Points (RP)** | Decide your rank, 1 to 14. Not spent. |
| **Honor** | Currency for PvP gear. |
| **Marks of Honor** | 3 for a win, 1 for a loss. Gear, experience, reputation. |
| **Cap** | Weekly, rising to 24,750 RP for Rank 14 |
| **Arena or rated** | None |
| **Battlegrounds** | Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, Darkspear Islands, Alterac Valley |

## Rank Points (RP)

You earn Rank Points in battlegrounds and PvP quests, with a bonus for the battleground of the holiday week. They move you through the ranks, up to Grand Marshal for the Alliance and High Warlord for the Horde. Rank 1 asks for 750 points.

There is a cap. It rises each week, so nobody can race to the top in the first days. The last step, Rank 14, sits at 24,750 Rank Points. You see your rank and points in the character pane: press C and open Player vs. Player on the right.

PvP runs in seasons. Each season has weekly tasks that give Honor and Rank Points, and three of them also give a [Legacy Point](/news/legacy-points-one-per-challenge/). The ranking is expected to reset every few months.

## Honor

Honor is what you spend. You get it for defeating players, finishing battlegrounds and PvP quests, again with extra during the holiday week. Blizzard has evened out the Honor per battleground, so Alterac Valley is no longer the only map worth playing.

The rare (blue) PvP set needs no rank at all, only Honor. To make it epic, you buy a Premier Emboldened Seal for that slot, and the seals do ask for a rank (see the table). Specs that never had PvP gear, like healing or melee Shaman, now have their own set.

## Battlegrounds and brackets

Forever has four battlegrounds. You queue alone or as a group, but [premades only meet other premades](/news/solo-players-never-face-a-premade/). A win gives three Marks of Honor and bonus Honor, a loss one Mark. Battlegrounds can mix players from several [rulesets](/guides/rulesets/), but never Hardcore.

| Battleground | Brackets |
|---|---|
| **Warsong Gulch** | 10-19, 20-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60 |
| **Arathi Basin** | 20-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60 |
| **Darkspear Islands** | 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60 |
| **Alterac Valley** | 51-60 |

## Darkspear Islands

## World PvP and the PvP flag

You can only fight a player of the other faction when both of you are flagged. How you get flagged depends on your [ruleset](/guides/rulesets/):

| Territory | PvP and RP-PvP | Normal, Roleplay, Hardcore |
|---|---|---|
| **Friendly** | Flagged only by /pvp, attacking or helping a flagged player | Same |
| **Contested** | Flagged on entry | Same as friendly |
| **Enemy** | Flagged on entry | Flagged on entry |

While flagged, guards of the other faction attack you when you come close. Neutral guards only attack when you fight near them. To lose the flag, stay 5 minutes in friendly territory. If you turned it on with /pvp, type /pvp again to start the 5-minute timer.
