Engineering in World of Warcraft: Forever has two specializations: Gnomish Engineering and Goblin Engineering. Each one teaches schematics the other cannot craft. This guide compares the two, lists both quests with every material and explains the membership card and how to switch.

| | |
|---|---|
| Requirement | Character level 30 and 200 Engineering |
| Gnomish | Gadgets, a teleport to Gadgetzan |
| Goblin | Explosives, a teleport to Everlook |
| Card | Renew every two weeks for 2 gold |
| Switch | Unlearn Engineering and level it again |

## Gnomish or Goblin

Gnomish Engineering is for gadgets, Goblin Engineering for things that blow up. Many players choose for the teleport: <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18986"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_12.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ultrasafe Transporter: Gadgetzan</a> for Gnomes, <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18984"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dimensional Ripper - Everlook</a> for Goblins.

Only three items need the specialization to use them: those two teleports and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18660"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">World Enlarger</a>. Bind on pickup items need the specialization to craft, not to use. Learn every schematic of one specialization, switch, and you keep using what you made. <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7148"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Goblin Jumper Cables</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18587"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Goblin Jumper Cables XL</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4397"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gnomish Cloaking Device</a> sound like specialization items, but every Engineer can make them.

## Gnomish Engineering

1. **Gnome Engineering.** Take the quest from an Engineering trainer: Tinkerwiz in Ratchet (`/way 62.7 36.3`), Lilliam Sparkspindle in Stormwind (`/way 54.8 7.6`), Springspindle Fizzlegear in Ironforge (`/way 68.5 43.5`) or Graham Van Talen in Undercity (`/way 75.6 74.6`).
2. **The Pledge of Secrecy.** The quest sends you to Tinkmaster Overspark in Tinker Town, Ironforge (`/way 69.6 50.3`), or Oglethorpe Obnoticus in Booty Bay (`/way 28.4 76.4`). Right-click the pledge in your bags and hand it in.
3. **Show Your Work.** Bring the items below to the same NPC for your <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=10790"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_letter_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gnome Engineer Membership Card</a>. You can also buy them from other Engineers.

| Item | Number | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| **Mithril Tube** | 6 | 18 Mithril Bar |
| **Accurate Scope** | 1 | Jade, Citrine, Bronze Tube; schematic <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=13310"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Schematic: Accurate Scope</a> from Mazk Snipeshot in Booty Bay (`/way 28.5 75.1`) |
| **Advanced Target Dummy** | 2 | 8 Heavy Leather, 2 Bronze Framework, 2 Iron Strut, 2 Gyrochronatom |

## Switching specialization

There is only one way: drop Engineering and level it again from 1. In the Professions tab of your character window, click the red X next to Engineering. You lose all your skill and every recipe, so be sure.

Back at 200, go to a small house north of Steamwheedle Port in Tanaris (`/way 65.4 18.6`). The book Soothsaying for Dummies on the table gives you the other specialization, without the quests. Bind on pickup items you made before stay usable, except the three that need the specialization.

The trainers for Engineering and the other professions are on the [professions page](/tradeskills/).
