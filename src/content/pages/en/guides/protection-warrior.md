The Protection Warrior in WoW Forever is the classic knight: heavy armor, a big shield and no magic, only shouts, stomps and blows. New talents give Rage for every block, dodge and parry, and Vanguard lets you Charge in Defensive Stance. This guide covers the level 30 build, threat rotation, debuffs, Rage, stats, gear and races.

## What changes for Protection

- **Shield Specialization** gives 5% more block chance and 5 Rage on every block at 5/5.
- **Master of Defense is new.** 5 Rage on every dodge and parry while you carry a shield.
- **Vanguard is new.** Charge works in Defensive Stance.
- **Focused Rage is new.** Your offensive abilities cost 3 Rage less at 3/3.
- **Defiance** gives 15% more threat in Defensive Stance with a shield at 3/3.
- **Last Stand** now gives 30% of your maximum health for 20 seconds.
- **Bastion** gives 10% more damage while you carry a shield at 5/5, and **Shield Slam** also dispels a Magic effect half of the time.
- **Improved Shield Wall** takes 11 minutes off the cooldown of Shield Wall at 2/2.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Shield Wall and Last Stand can win a fight | Weak when you are not the tank, and a two-handed weapon does not fix it |
| Shield Bash, Intercept, Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout set the pace | Needs Rage, and gets it mostly by being hit |
| Rage from blocks, dodges and parries | Against casters you quickly run short of Rage |

Because Protection does so little outside tanking, many Warriors play a damage spec with Dual Specialization, or take the role of main tank for good.

## Rotation

1. Spend extra Rage on Heroic Strike, or Cleave against several enemies, so it is never wasted.
2. Keep Battle Shout up.
3. Keep Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout up.
4. Stack Sunder Armor.
5. Revenge whenever it is ready.
6. Put Rend on the target and keep it there.
7. Shield Slam, once you have it.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Shield Wall** and **Retaliation** are very useful, but Retaliation needs Battle Stance.
- **Last Stand** is a strong defensive cooldown.
- **Berserker Rage** ignores Fear and Incapacitate effects, but only in Berserker Stance.
- **Recklessness** is the strongest damage cooldown in the game, but also needs Berserker Stance.
- **Bloodrage** freely, for extra Rage.
- **Challenging Shout** makes enemies attack you, unlike Taunt, so you keep tanking through immunities.

## Movement

Stay on the boss. Open with Charge for Rage and an instant start, in Defensive Stance thanks to Vanguard. In combat you have Intercept, but only in Berserker Stance, which costs your Rage and makes you take more damage.

## Debuffs and Rage

**Debuffs.** Keep Sunder Armor on the enemy for more damage and more threat. Keep Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout running all the time: if they drop, you take a lot more damage. Rend scales well and adds a fair amount of threat.

**Rage.** Plan ahead and keep enough for your important attacks. If Heroic Strike leaves Shield Slam or Revenge waiting for a second or two, that was a mistake. Keep your Rage moving: never full, never empty.

## Stats and gear

The exact order at level 60 follows after the launch. These stats help a Protection Warrior:

- **Hit chance.** A miss deals nothing and gives no Rage.
- **Stamina, Armor and avoidance.** More health is always better for a tank. Balance defense with enough offense to hold threat.
- **Strength and attack power.** 1 Strength gives 2 attack power.
- **Critical strike chance.** More damage and more Rage.
- **Agility.** Some critical strike chance and Armor.

**Armor.** Your armor matters a lot. Mail until level 40, Plate after that. A lighter armor type costs you so much mitigation that you struggle to survive. Blacksmiths make good leveling gear, and dungeons have plenty more.

**Weapons.** Your weapon still matters for threat. Always carry a shield, for Bastion and Shield Slam. A ranged weapon only counts for its stats.
