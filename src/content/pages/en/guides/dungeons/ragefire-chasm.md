Ragefire Chasm is a network of volcanic caves under Orgrimmar, where the Burning Blade cult hides from the Horde. For most Horde players it is the first dungeon of WoW Forever: short, straight and with simple fights. Four bosses and six quests wait inside.

![Taragaman the Hungerer on a platform surrounded by lava in Ragefire Chasm, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-ragefire-chasm-taragaman.jpg)

## Getting there

**Horde.** Walk into the Cleft of Shadow in Orgrimmar and look for the portal deep inside.

**Alliance.** Ragefire Chasm lies in the middle of a Horde capital. Take the boat to Ratchet and run to the northeastern tip of The Barrens, where the west entrance of Orgrimmar is quieter. Take off your gear and make a corpse run into the Cleft of Shadow until you can zone in.

After a wipe, the Spirit Healer stands in Durotar, outside the city.

The level is the lowest level at which the quest can be picked up, according to Wowhead.

- **Slaying the Beast.** Bring Taragaman the Hungerer's Heart from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11520">Taragaman the Hungerer</a>. He stands in the middle of the cross-shaped path before the last room.
- **The Power to Destroy...** Loot Spells of Shadow and Incantations from the Nether. Both drop from the Searing Blade Cultists and Searing Blade Warlocks in the second half of the dungeon.
- **Testing an Enemy's Strength.** Kill 8 Ragefire Troggs and 8 Ragefire Shamans, in the first big room and the tunnels around it.
- **Searching for the Lost Satchel.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11834">Maur Grimtotem</a> hides in a small cave to the right of the first trogg room, guarded by <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11517">Oggleflint</a> and two elite troggs. Maur gives you Returning the Lost Satchel, which goes back to Rahauro.
- **Hidden Enemies.** A chain of Thrall. First a Lieutenant's Insignia from the Burning Blade in Skull Rock, east of Orgrimmar, then a talk with Neeru Fireblade. The last step asks you to kill <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11519">Bazzalan</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11518">Jergosh the Invoker</a> inside.

## The bosses and their loot

The loot per boss comes from Wowhead, with the stats from the files of the beta.

### Oggleflint

A trogg chief with two troggs at his side. Crowd control one, kill the other first, and turn Oggleflint away from the group because of his Cleave. No special loot.

![Oggleflint between two troggs in a dark cave of Ragefire Chasm, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-ragefire-chasm-oggleflint.jpg)

### Taragaman the Hungerer

He stands on a platform surrounded by lava. Pull him away from the edge so his Uppercut does not knock anyone into the lava, and keep the ranged at maximum range for Fire Nova.

### Bazzalan

Up the right-hand ramp before Jergosh. The add on the right can be pulled without him. Bazzalan hits hard with Sinister Strike and Deadly Poison, so kill him quickly. No special loot.
