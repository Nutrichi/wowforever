All five quests are for the Horde and can be picked up from level 9. Two start in Thunder Bluff, two in Orgrimmar and one in the Undercity, so collect them before you go in.

Hidden Enemies is the end of a chain of Thrall: first a Lieutenant's Insignia from the Burning Blade in Skull Rock, east of Orgrimmar, then a talk with Neeru Fireblade. Searching for the Lost Satchel continues with <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5724">Returning the Lost Satchel</a>, which goes back to Rahauro.
