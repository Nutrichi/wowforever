Every boss of Alcaz Prison drops three items. The trinkets are the Fingerbreaker Stamp from The Questioner and Zarla's Snack Pouch from Zarla Ober. Tidemistress Ca'zass, The Questioner and Zarla also drop a quest item, and Overseer Wrex the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=263992"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Overseer's Keyring</a>.

Six more items can drop from any monster in the dungeon and are bind on equip, so you can sell them. The best quest rewards come at the end of the two story chains, see the [quests tab](/guides/dungeons/alcaz-prison/quests/).
