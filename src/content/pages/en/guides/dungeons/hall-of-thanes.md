The Hall of Thanes is the new dungeon beneath Old Ironforge, for levels 13 to 18, and for most Alliance players the first one of WoW Forever. It is the resting place of the great Dwarves of the Eastern Kingdoms, and the Dark Iron Dwarves have broken in. Four bosses and five quests wait inside.

![A long hall with red banners in Old Ironforge, leading to the blue portal of the Hall of Thanes, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-entrance.jpg)

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 13-18 according to Blizzard |
| Entrance | Old Ironforge, beneath the High Seat in Ironforge |
| Bosses | 4: Faldrim Anvilmar, Magmatus, Plunder, Durgen Dirgehammer |
| Quests | 5, all Alliance except An Ancient Grudge |
| Loot | Blue items that require level 13 |
| Spirit Healer | Just outside the city |

## Getting there

**Alliance.** Walk into the High Seat of Ironforge. The door to Old Ironforge is immediately on the left. Follow the path all the way down to the portal.

**Horde.** The dungeon is the Alliance counterpart of Ragefire Chasm, and the way in is long. From Tarren Mill it goes through the Arathi Highlands, the Wetlands and Loch Modan to Dun Morogh, and then past the guards of Ironforge. A faster route has not been found. Take off your gear before the guards, so it does not lose durability when you die.

**The exact way in.** Stand at the Great Forge and face the High Seat, where King Magni Bronzebeard sits. Inside his hall, a cobwebbed passage opens on the left, at /way 43.5 52.0. Follow it all the way down. The path runs above lava, and a fall is lethal.

## The quests at a glance

| Quest | Side | Where it starts | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|

| [The Restless Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96394) | Alliance | Afadra Dunwall, Old Ironforge | 3,550 |

| [An Ancient Grudge](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96395) | Both | A Ghostly Attendant, inside the dungeon | 3,550 |
| [The Treaty of Understanding](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98423) | Alliance | A tablet in a vault in the room of the final boss | 3,900 |

The quests are level 15 and 16 and can be picked up from level 9 or 10. Start with the chain in Dun Morogh: it is the only quest that does not begin at the dungeon, and it asks for the final boss.

## Before you go: the chain in Dun Morogh

Earthseer Farsen stands at /way 64.8 58.5 in Dun Morogh. He gives [Nip 'Em in the Bud](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96390): slay 10 Dark Iron Spies, found around /way 76.7 60.7. The road to Farsen starts with A Visitor to Dun Morogh, from Beldin Steelgrill at Steelgrill's Depot.

![Earthseer Farsen in the snow of Dun Morogh, with the quest window of Old Ironforge Incursion open](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-earthseer-farsen.jpg)

One of the spies drops a [Dark Iron Map](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274268). Use it from your bags to start Underground Map, which sends you back to Farsen. He then hands out Old Ironforge Incursion: enter the Hall of Thanes and bring back the head of Durgen Dirgehammer.

![The loot window of a Dark Iron Spy with the Dark Iron Map and its tooltip, This Item Begins a Quest](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-dark-iron-map.jpg)

## The quests one by one

**Important Heirlooms.** Thom Filch waits by the bridge that leads into the Hall of Thanes. He asks for 8 [Dwarven Heirlooms](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274289). They lie all over the dungeon from the first boss on, most of them on raised, candle-lit platforms. There is no need to fight the group for them: the vaults in the last room, the Reliquary of Kings, hold enough for five players. Reward: the Dwarven Tome or Tomb Robber's Gloves, plus 100 reputation with Gadgetzan.

**The Restless Dead.** Afadra Dunwall, a Lorekeeper of Ironforge, stands up a flight of stairs on the way to the entrance. She wants 15 Enraged Apparitions and 10 Tormented Souls dead, the ghosts in the first three rooms. Reward: the Dusty Belt or Cryptwalker Bracers, plus 100 reputation with Ironforge.

![Afadra Dunwall in a library full of books, up the stairs from the entrance to the Hall of Thanes](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-afadra-dunwall.jpg)

**An Ancient Grudge.** A Ghostly Attendant waits in the second room, Anvilmar's Rest. She asks you to put the spirit of Faldrim Anvilmar, the first boss, to rest. This is the one quest the Horde can do too. Whoever leaves without a Hearthstone passes her again on the way out, writes Warcraft Tavern. Reward: the Catacomb Cloak or Deepgrave Trousers.

![The Ghostly Attendant with the quest window of An Ancient Grudge open, between the ghosts of the first room](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-ghostly-attendant.jpg)

**Old Ironforge Incursion.** Loot [Durgen Dirgehammer's Head](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274286) from the final boss and take it to King Magni Bronzebeard in Ironforge. Reward: a choice of the Calibrated Blunderbuss, the Ironforge Greathammer or Deepblaze, plus 100 reputation with Ironforge and with Gnomeregan Exiles.

**The Treaty of Understanding.** Behind a vault door in the Reliquary of Kings lies a tablet with the sigils of Madoran Bronzebeard. It starts a quest without an item reward, for 3,900 experience and reputation with Ironforge and Gnomeregan Exiles: take the treaty to Magni Bronzebeard as well. It cannot be shared.

![The tablet of The Treaty of Understanding in a vault of the Reliquary of Kings, with the quest window open](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-treaty-of-understanding.jpg)

## The route through the dungeon

The Hall of Thanes is a straight line with one detour through a cave. From the portal:

1. **The first chamber.** Tormented Souls and Enraged Apparitions, easy pulls. Take the corridor on the left, to the west.
2. **The Ghostly Attendant.** In the next room she gives An Ancient Grudge. Then comes Anvilmar's Rest, a large room with a tomb in the middle and Faldrim Anvilmar walking around it. Clear the ghosts first.
3. **The Dark Iron side.** Leave Anvilmar's Rest through the southeast corridor. From here the Dark Iron Dwarves take over. Kegs of Seaforium Blasting Powder stand along the way: shoot them from range and they blow up the pack around them.
4. **The cave.** The tombs turn into a natural cavern, where Magmatus stands between Dark Iron Dwarves.
5. **Back into the halls.** Up the tunnel waits a pack of four with two kegs beside it. Shoot both kegs at the same time before the pull.
6. **Plunder** patrols the hallway right after.
7. **The Reliquary of Kings.** North over a bridge guarded by Lesser Stone Golems lies the last room, with Durgen Dirgehammer. Behind one of the vault doors lies the Treaty of Understanding.

On the way out, hand in An Ancient Grudge with the Ghostly Attendant.

## The four bosses

### Faldrim Anvilmar

A ghost who patrols the middle of Anvilmar's Rest. Clear one side of the room, wait until he comes your way and pull him there, away from the trash. He casts [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26048) and puts [Anvilmar's Curse](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1292602) on the tank for 10 minutes: attack and casting speed 20% slower, but immune to poison and bleed effects. A player who removes curses makes this easier.

### Magmatus

A fire elemental with a Dark Iron Summoner at his side. Let the tank drag Magmatus away from the group and kill the Summoner first, because the boss sets off a fire nova now and then. [Combust](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293228) sets a random player on fire for 12 seconds. A patrolling Summoner nearby is best taken out before the pull.

### Plunder

A golem that patrols a hallway, with one ability: [Knock Away](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10101), which throws a player far. Pull him to a spot where nobody lands in a pack of trash, with the group against a wall or in a corner.

### Durgen Dirgehammer

The final boss, in the Reliquary of Kings, with two Lesser Stone Golems at his side and Dark Iron Looters along the edges of the room. Clear the Looters first: his [Fear](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26070) sends players running into them. He also puts [Rend](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12054) on the tank. Kill the Golems one at a time; a Golem killed before a wipe does not come back, so every next try is easier. After the fight a switch in the back vault opens the locked doors, so the way out is short.

## Trash

The first rooms are easy; the Dark Iron side is not.

**Dark Iron Looters and Engineers.** Looters attack fast with daggers and drain the tank quickly when several come at once, so kill them first. Engineers throw bombs at a spot on the ground, and that damage can be dodged completely.

![A Dark Iron Looter and a Dark Iron Engineer in a dark hall of the Hall of Thanes, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-looter-and-engineer.jpg)

**Dark Iron Summoners.** They cast Fireball and call up a Fiery Assistant for 30 seconds. Pull them around a pillar or a wall, out of sight, so they have to come closer.

![A Dark Iron Summoner and a Dark Iron Looter next to a lantern, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-summoner.jpg)

**Lesser Stone Golems.** They hit hardest and move fast. Pull one at a time where possible, and focus one down before the next. The last pack before the final boss holds a Summoner, a Looter and a Golem together: [Polymorph](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=118) on one of them and stuns such as [Hammer of Justice](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=853) help. Healers draw a lot of threat at these levels.

![A Lesser Stone Golem, a grey stone giant with green runes, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-lesser-stone-golem.jpg)

**Bloodhound Runts.** Their bite leaves Infected Wounds for 5 minutes: more physical damage taken. Without a class that removes disease, a Simple Poultice from First Aid or the Dwarf racial [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20594) cures it.

**Seaforium kegs.** Kegs of Seaforium Blasting Powder stand next to several Dark Iron packs. A ranged player shoots them and the pack takes the blast.
