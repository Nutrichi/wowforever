The loot list follows the dungeon journal that Blizzard made for the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, and only items that exist in the Forever data appear as cards. 22 of the 25 items are in that data now. In the journal, the four dragons Weaver, Dreamscythe, Hazzas and Morphaz share one list as the Wardens of the Dream. Whether Blizzard changes the rest for Forever or those items simply are not in the data yet, is not known.

These drops from Classic are not in the Forever data yet:

| Boss | Loot in Classic |
|---|---|
| Wardens of the Dream | [Bloodfire Talons](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=12464), [Firebreather](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=10797) |
| Avatar of Hakkar | [Embrace of the Wind Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=12462) |

Atal'alarion and the six troll guardians on the balconies have no items in the journal.
