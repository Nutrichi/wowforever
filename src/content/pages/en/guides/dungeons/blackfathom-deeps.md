Almost every boss drop in Blackfathom Deeps is blue in Forever, and many caster items trade Intellect for spell damage and healing. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6909"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_42.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Strike of the Hydra</a> got a much stronger effect. Lorgus Jett has three items of his own now, and Gelihast carries the <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273141"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_garrison_blueprints3.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blueprint: Fishing Rack</a>, one of the [camping blueprints](/guides/camping/).

The blue items from the monsters, under Other drops, changed too: <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3416"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_chain.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Martyr's Chain</a> deals 420 Holy damage to nearby enemies when you die, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2567"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_shortblade_15.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Evocator's Blade</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3414"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_mace_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Crested Scepter</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3415"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_32.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Staff of the Friar</a> are caster and healer weapons now.
