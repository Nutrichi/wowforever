The loot list follows the dungeon journal that Blizzard made for Dire Maul in Classic, and only items that exist in the Forever data appear as cards. None of the 33 items of the five bosses is in that data yet. Whether Blizzard changes the loot for Forever or the items simply are not in the data yet, is not known.
