Seven of the eight quests are for both factions; Ramstein is for the Horde only. Most start at Light's Hope Chapel. The Flesh Does Not Lie and The Active Agent form a pair: turn in the first one halfway through, and you can do the second in the same run.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
