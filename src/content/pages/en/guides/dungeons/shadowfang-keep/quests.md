Three quests are for the Horde. None of them needs an earlier quest, so a Horde group can pick them all up at the Sepulcher and in the Undercity and share them. The other two are class quests: The Test of Righteousness for Paladins of the Alliance, and The Orb of Soran'ruk for Warlocks of both factions.
