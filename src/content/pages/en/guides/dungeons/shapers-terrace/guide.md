## Getting there

Shaper's Terrace is a new dungeon for World of Warcraft: Forever, for levels 58 to 60, in Un'Goro Crater. The entrance is on a hill above Marshal's Refuge in the north of the crater, at /way 48.1 6.1.

## The story

Shaper's Terrace is an old titan facility. Dinosaurs live there now, alongside a few titan constructs, between tar pits and crystals.

![A green jungle with a titan building between the trees and glowing crystals on the ground, an image from Blizzard of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-20-wow-forever-shapers-terrace-entrance.jpg)

## The layout

Most of the dungeon is one large open area. A path leads through a tar pit to the central structure, the Pillar of Assimilation. On the east side runs a cave tunnel full of crystals. A path along the north leads to a titan building and then to an area with waterfalls. There, behind a closed gate, a raid portal hides, found on the beta, see [this post](/news/hidden-instance-portals-hint-at-future-content/).

![A large stone arch with red crystals and a portal deep inside Shaper's Terrace, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-portal-shapers-terrace.jpg)

## The bosses

Four bosses are known by name, from the Legacy challenges of the dungeon. There are probably more.

- **Bolt**
- **Cinder**
- **Snowtalon**
- **Nanaya**
