All six quests on the Living side are for both factions. Four start at Light's Hope Chapel and Terrordale in the Eastern Plaguelands, one on Caer Darrow, and one inside the dungeon with Aurius. The Archivist and The Truth Comes Crashing Down form a pair: kill Archivist Galford first, turn in, and only then fight Balnazzar.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
