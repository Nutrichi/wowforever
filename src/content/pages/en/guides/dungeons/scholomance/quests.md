Barov Family Fortune has a version for each faction. Plagued Hatchlings and Dawn's Gambit start with Betina Bigglezink at Light's Hope Chapel. Eva Sarkhoff on Caer Darrow starts a long chain that runs through Scholomance three times, then Stratholme, and ends with The Lich, Ras Frostwhisper.

To open the door you need <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=13704"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Skeleton Key</a>. The chain The Key to Scholomance starts for the Alliance at Chillwind Camp and for the Horde at The Bulwark. It does not go inside the dungeon: you burn or clear a camp, light four towers in Andorhal, collect 15 Skeletal Fragments, pay 15 gold to Krinkle Goodsteel in Gadgetzan, forge the key at Fire Plume Ridge in Un'Goro Crater with 2 Thorium Bars, and kill Araj the Summoner in Andorhal with a group.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
