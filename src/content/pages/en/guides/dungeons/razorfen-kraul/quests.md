Blueleaf Tubers and Willix the Importer are open to both factions. Take the Crate with Holes, the Snufflenose Command Stick and the Snufflenose Owner's Manual from next to Mebok Mizzyrix in Ratchet before you go in. The escort of Willix passes many of the tubers.

Fire Hardened Mail and Brutal Armor are Warrior quests: both need the Vial of Phlogiston from Roogug. Going, Going, Guano! leads to Hearts of Zeal in [Scarlet Monastery](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-graveyard/), and the Small Scroll from Charlga Razorflank starts An Unholy Alliance, which continues in [Razorfen Downs](/guides/dungeons/razorfen-downs/).
