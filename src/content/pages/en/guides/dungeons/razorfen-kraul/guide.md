## Getting there

**Horde.** Fly to Camp Taurajo and ride south. Turn west just before the Great Lift to Thousand Needles; the entrance lies in the brambles at /way 42.3 89.9.

**Alliance.** Take the boat from Booty Bay to Ratchet, ride to the south of The Barrens and turn west before the Great Lift.

![The entrance of Razorfen Kraul, a portal in a tunnel of thorny roots, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-entrance.jpg)

## The route

A maze of thorny tunnels that runs from east to west. The numbers match the map above.

1. **The first caves.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=6168">Roogug</a> (1) waits close to the portal, with an Earth Rumbler at his side. He is optional unless you need his Vial of Phlogiston.
2. **The east.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4424">Aggem Thorncurse</a> (2) and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4428">Death Speaker Jargba</a> (3) stand in the caves to the east.
3. **The north.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4420">Overlord Ramtusk</a> (4) waits with two Razorfen Spearhides. The rare Earthcaller Halmgar can stand on a platform in the middle.
4. **The far west.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4422">Agathelos the Raging</a> (5), a giant boar, at the end of a long tunnel. The Blind Hunter, a rare bat, hides in the bat cave in the northwest.
5. **The end.** Willix the Importer and the body of Heralath Fallowbrook wait in a tent before the last boss. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4421">Charlga Razorflank</a> (6) sits in her hut.

## Important trash

**Razorfen Totemics** drop an Earthgrab Totem that roots everyone nearby for 30 seconds. Kill the totem first.

**Razorfen Dustweavers** cast Enveloping Winds, a 10-second stun. Pull them one at a time.

**Death's Head Adepts** cast Chains of Ice. Easy to kill, but dangerous with a second pack nearby.

## The bosses

### Roogug

Casts Lightning Bolt and calls an Earth Rumbler. Kill the elemental first, or banish it if a Warlock is in the group.

### Aggem Thorncurse

Battle Shout, Chain Heal and a Boar Spirit that he summons. Kill the boar first and interrupt every heal.

![Aggem Thorncurse under wooden beams in Razorfen Kraul, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-aggem-thorncurse.jpg)

### Death Speaker Jargba

Comes with two hard-hitting casters; crowd control at least one of them. Jargba casts Shadow Bolt and takes control of a player with Dominate Mind. Crowd control the controlled player and kill Jargba quickly.

![Death Speaker Jargba between two quilboar casters in Razorfen Kraul, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-death-speaker-jargba.jpg)

### Overlord Ramtusk

A level 32 boss in Battle Stance with Thunder Clap and Battle Shout, flanked by two Razorfen Spearhides whose Whirling Barrage is deadly. Crowd control both if you can.

![Overlord Ramtusk between two Razorfen Spearhides in Razorfen Kraul, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-overlord-ramtusk.jpg)

### Agathelos the Raging

A level 33 boar with heavy single-target damage. Rushing Charge, Enrage and Rampage make the fight about keeping the tank alive.

![Agathelos the Raging, a giant boar, in Razorfen Kraul, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-agathelos.jpg)

### Charlga Razorflank

The last boss, a caster with little armor in a raised hut. Pull her down to the platform, interrupt her and spread out against Chain Bolt. She heals herself with Renew and turns immune for a while with Purity.

![Charlga Razorflank on her throne in a hut in Razorfen Kraul, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-charlga-razorflank.jpg)

The Blind Hunter in the bat cave casts Sonic Burst, a silence on everyone nearby; stop casting when it starts. Hunters can tame him.

![Blind Hunter, a pink bat, in the bat cave of Razorfen Kraul, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-blind-hunter.jpg)
