All five quests start inside Dire Maul North and are open to both factions. Three of them belong to the Tribute Run: A Broken Trap for Guard Slip'kik, The Gordok Ogre Suit for Captain Kromcrush and The Gordok Taste Test after King Gordok. Do not hand in Free Knot! before the suit: Knot Thimblejack leaves afterwards.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
