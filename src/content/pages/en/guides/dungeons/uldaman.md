The loot of Uldaman in the Forever data is not complete yet. Revelosh, Ironaya, the Ancient Stone Keeper and Grimlok have blue items; the Lost Dwarves, the Obsidian Sentinel, Galgann Firehammer and Archaedas have none in the data so far. The blue drops of the monsters sit under Other drops.
