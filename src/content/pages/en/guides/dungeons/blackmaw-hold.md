Every boss of Blackmaw Hold drops three items, and four of them a trinket. Withermaw, Bonesnout the Unbroken, Elder Shaman Bloodmane and Varrox Bleakhoof also drop a quest item. The four rares drop two items each, so look around for them.

The best quest reward is a choice of four rings from Blackmaw Reclamation, see the [quests tab](/guides/dungeons/blackmaw-hold/quests/).
