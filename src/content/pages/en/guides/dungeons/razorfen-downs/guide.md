## Getting there

**Horde.** Fly to Camp Taurajo and ride south. Turn east just before the Great Lift to Thousand Needles; the entrance is at /way 50.8 92.8.

**Alliance.** Take the boat from Booty Bay to Ratchet, ride to the south of The Barrens and turn east before the Great Lift.

![The entrance of Razorfen Downs, a blue portal in a dark tunnel, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-downs-entrance.jpg)

## The route

The numbers match the map above.

1. **The Caller's Chamber.** Ring the gong for <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7355">Tuten'kash</a> (1).
2. **The Bone Pile.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7357">Mordresh Fire Eye</a> (3) sits on a pile of bones in the east, behind dancing skeletons.
3. **The Spiral of Thorns.** The path winds down around a hill. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=8567">Glutton</a> (5) waits on it, and the rare <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7354">Ragglesnout</a> (4) can stand on the lower ring.
4. **The idol.** Belnistrasz waits in the cells near Mordresh. His escort ends with <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7356">Plaguemaw the Rotting</a> (2).
5. **The top of the hill.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7358">Amnennar the Coldbringer</a> (6), the last boss.

## Important trash

**Freezing Spirits.** Ghosts that cast Frost Nova. Stack on the tank so nobody gets frozen out of position.

**Death's Head casters.** Cultists and Necromancers hit hard from range. Interrupt them or pull them out of sight.

## The bosses

### Tuten'kash

Summoned with the gong: clear the waves of spiders first, and ring the gong again after each wave. Turn him away from the group because of Web Spray, and remove the Curse of Tuten'kash and the Virulent Poison of his attacks.

![Tuten'kash, a giant spider, among webs in Razorfen Downs, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-downs-tutenkash.jpg)

### Plaguemaw the Rotting

The end of the escort of Belnistrasz. Kill the waves one at a time, ideally with an off-tank pulling them off Belnistrasz. Plaguemaw himself is a tank and spank with Putrid Stench and Withered Touch.

### Mordresh Fire Eye

A boss with little armor on a pile of bones. Clear the dancing skeletons first; the last pack pulls him, so kill it quickly with area damage. Interrupt his Fireball and step out of Fire Nova.

![Mordresh Fire Eye on a pile of bones in Razorfen Downs, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-downs-mordresh-fire-eye.jpg)

### Ragglesnout

A rare. His Dominate Mind is the danger: if the tank or healer is taken over, the hybrids fill in. Interrupt his Heal and Shadow Bolts.

### Glutton

A simple fight. Stay out of his Disease Cloud and heal the tank through his Enrage.

![Glutton, a green glowing abomination, in Razorfen Downs, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-downs-glutton.jpg)

### Amnennar the Coldbringer

The last boss. Tank him facing the tent where he stands to block Amnennar's Wrath, stack on the tank for Frost Nova, switch to the Frost Spectres when he summons them and interrupt Frostbolt.

![Amnennar the Coldbringer in front of a wall of thorns in Razorfen Downs, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-downs-amnennar.jpg)
