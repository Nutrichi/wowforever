Four quests for the Horde and eight for the Alliance, all from level 24. Below are the eight that start a chain; each follow-up is named under its quest. Two chains start from the Titan Relic that Relic Guardian drops, in a different version for each faction.

The Horde has the longer trip: Open the Maw starts with Dragonmaw Rumors from Zaruk in Hammerfall, and Changing Tastes starts in Orgrimmar. Collect both on the way to the Wetlands.
