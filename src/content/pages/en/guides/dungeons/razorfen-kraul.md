Most boss drops in Razorfen Kraul are blue in Forever, with new stats or effects: <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6681"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_shortblade_10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thornspike</a> hurls spikes that jump to a second enemy, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6693"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bone_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Agamaggan's Clutch</a> makes you faster in The Barrens, Thousand Needles and both Razorfen dungeons. Roogug has three items of his own now. The rares Blind Hunter and Earthcaller Halmgar keep their loot; it sits under Other drops with the blue items of the monsters.

Charlga Razorflank also drops the Small Scroll, which starts An Unholy Alliance for the Horde.
