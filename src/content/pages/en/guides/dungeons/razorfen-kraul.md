Razorfen Kraul grew where the blood of the demigod Agamaggan fell, ten thousand years ago: a maze of giant thorny vines in the south of The Barrens. The quilboar live there under Charlga Razorflank. Eight bosses, two of them rares, and seven quests wait inside.

![The entrance of Razorfen Kraul, a portal in a tunnel of thorny roots, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-razorfen-kraul-entrance.jpg)

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 25-34, bosses of level 28 to 33 |
| Entrance | The south of The Barrens, west of the Great Lift |
| Bosses | 8, of which Blind Hunter and Earthcaller Halmgar are rares |
| Quests | 7, for both factions |
| Loot | Most boss drops are blue in Forever |
| Short name | RFK |

## Getting there

**Horde.** Fly to Camp Taurajo and ride south. Turn west just before the Great Lift to Thousand Needles.

**Alliance.** Take the boat from Booty Bay to Ratchet, ride to the south of The Barrens and turn west before the Great Lift.

The level is the lowest level at which the quest can be picked up, according to Wowhead.

- **Blueleaf Tubers.** Take the Crate With Holes, the Snufflenose Owner's Manual and the Snufflenose Command Stick from next to Mebok. Inside, the crate summons a Snufflenose Gopher; the stick makes it dig up a tuber. Repeat until you have 6.
- **Willix the Importer.** An escort at the end of the dungeon. Its path passes many Blueleaf Tubers.
- **Mortality Wanes.** Treshala's Pendant drops from any quilboar inside.
- **The Crone of the Kraul and A Vengeful Fate.** Razorflank's Medallion and Razorflank's Heart, both from Charlga Razorflank. For the Alliance, Lonebrow's Journal lies on the body of a dwarf at the foot of the Great Lift.
- **Going, Going, Guano!** Kraul Guano from the Kraul Bats in the last part. It is needed for Hearts of Zeal in [Scarlet Monastery](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery/).
- **An Unholy Alliance.** The Small Scroll sends you to Varimathras in the Undercity; the chain continues in [Razorfen Downs](/guides/dungeons/razorfen-downs/).

## The quilboar casters

- **Razorfen Totemics** drop an Earthgrab Totem that roots everyone nearby for 30 seconds. Kill the totem first.
- **Razorfen Dustweavers** cast Enveloping Winds, a 10-second stun. Pull them one at a time.
- **Death's Head Adepts** cast Chains of Ice. Easy to kill, but dangerous with a second pack nearby.
