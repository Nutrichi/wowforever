The Excavation Site is new in WoW Forever, so all of its loot is new too. Saltspine, Shadetooth and Relic Guardian drop blue items, and Shadetooth and Relic Guardian also carry a blueprint. Relic Guardian drops the Titan Relic as well, which starts Lost Relic Carry for the Alliance and Elder Knowledge for the Horde. Highland Horror has no loot of its own: it only drops the Horrible Rootcore for Horrors in the Highland.
