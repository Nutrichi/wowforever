The Excavation Site is one of the new dungeons of WoW Forever. The Explorers' League found titan ruins southeast of Whelgar's Excavation Site in the Wetlands and set up a new camp there. Raptors and a titan guardian stand in the way. Four bosses and twelve quests wait inside.

![The portal of the Excavation Site at night, a blue swirl in the hills of the Wetlands, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-excavation-site-entrance.jpg)

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 26-31 according to Blizzard |
| Entrance | The Wetlands, on a hill southeast of Whelgar's Excavation Site |
| Bosses | 4: Saltspine, Shadetooth, Highland Horror, Relic Guardian |
| Quests | 4 for the Horde, 8 for the Alliance, all from level 24 |
| Loot | New blue items, the Titan Relic for two chains |
| New in Forever | The whole dungeon |

The beta of WoW Forever goes up to level 30 since 1 October. Wowhead updates its guide as testers clear the dungeon, so details can still change.

## Getting there

New roads lead up the hill to the entrance; in Classic this part of the Wetlands could not be reached.

**Alliance.** Menethil Harbor is close, and it now has a boat to Stormwind through Auberdine. Running through Loch Modan works too.

**Horde.** A longer trek on foot through Hillsbrad Foothills and the Arathi Highlands. Pick up the flight paths in Tarren Mill and Hammerfall on the way.

#### Songblade Search and Fallen in the Fen

Dorin Songblade, the Armorer in Lakeshire, wants you to find his brother Daewyn in what he calls Whelgar's Excavation Site. He means the dungeon, not the area of the same name in the Wetlands. Daewyn lies dead in a tent in the Dragonmaw camp, shortly after Shadetooth. Click his body to finish the quest and to pick up Fallen in the Fen, which you hand in with Dorin after the dungeon.

#### Horrors in the Highland

Rethiel the Greenwarden, a moss elemental in the middle of the Wetlands, only gives this quest after three others:

1. **Tramping Paws.** Kill 15 Mosshide Gnolls and 10 Mosshide Mongrels in the south of the Wetlands, along the central river.
2. **Fire Taboo.** Bring 9 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2611"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_stone_16.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Crude Flint</a>. ## The tall grass

The most dangerous part of the dungeon is the area before Saltspine and Shadetooth. **Thicket Lurkers** do not wait in stealth: they spawn when you walk through the tall grass long enough. **Thicket Matriarchs** call Lurkers to their aid when their health runs low.

Stay on the narrow path and out of the grass, and fight in open spots. When a Matriarch is in a pull, kill the other monsters first, so there are as few left as possible when the Lurkers arrive.

### Shadetooth

An unforgiving fight with two Thicket Hunters at his side. Their Infected Wound has to be dispelled quickly. Crowd control both adds for the whole fight, or one while you kill the other. Shadetooth uses Rend and Terrifying Roar, a fear of 5 seconds that cannot be dispelled and easily sends players into the grass. At 30% health he enrages; he can be slowed and stunned.

Clear the crocodile cove on the left first and fight there. The Thicket Hunters cannot swim, but do not drag the fight back to the area of the first boss, or he resets.

### Highland Horror

The third boss stands between Shadetooth and Relic Guardian. Alliance players need it for Horrors in the Highland: it drops the Horrible Rootcore. Its abilities and its loot are not known yet.

### Relic Guardian

Start the fight with the Archival Panel on the right of the inactive guardian. Relic Guardian is a check on gear and level: a big health pool and heavy hits on the tank. Thunderclap slows everyone within 10 yards; the tank is knocked back now and then and sometimes stunned for 5 seconds. The guardian can be stunned and slowed.
