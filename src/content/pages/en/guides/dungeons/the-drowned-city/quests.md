Five quests are known so far, two for the Alliance and three for the Horde, all from level 30. Both factions get the same task with the four Tablets of I'lalai under a different name. Who hands out most of them is not known yet, because the game data does not show it so far. The Only Good Brinescale... is the first with a known reward.
