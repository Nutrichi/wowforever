The Drowned City has eight quests. Two come from Loremaster Dibbs in Southshore for the Alliance, after the troll chain that starts with The Second Rebellion. Three are for the Horde, from Grom'gol and from Mazu'kon of the Earthen Ring. Three are open to both factions: Ancient Gurubashi Secrets from Archmage Timear in Dalaran, and two from Krazek in Booty Bay. Those three need level 34, the others level 30.
