Four quests start in or near Old Ironforge; Old Ironforge Incursion needs a short chain in Dun Morogh first. Pick that one up before you go, because it asks for the final boss.

The chain starts with A Visitor to Dun Morogh, from Beldin Steelgrill at Steelgrill's Depot. Earthseer Farsen at /way 64.8 58.5 then gives [Nip 'Em in the Bud](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96390): slay 10 Dark Iron Spies around /way 76.7 60.7. One of them drops a [Dark Iron Map](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274268), which starts Underground Map and sends you back to Farsen.
