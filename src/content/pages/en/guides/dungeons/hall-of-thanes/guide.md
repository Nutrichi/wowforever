## Getting there

**Alliance.** Walk into the High Seat of Ironforge. The door to Old Ironforge is immediately on the left. Follow the path all the way down to the portal.

**Horde.** The dungeon is the Alliance counterpart of Ragefire Chasm, and the way in is long. From Tarren Mill it goes through the Arathi Highlands, the Wetlands and Loch Modan to Dun Morogh, and then past the guards of Ironforge. A faster route has not been found. Take off your gear before the guards, so it does not lose durability when you die.

**The exact way in.** Stand at the Great Forge and face the High Seat, where King Magni Bronzebeard sits. Inside his hall, a cobwebbed passage opens on the left, at /way 43.5 52.0. Follow it all the way down. The path runs above lava, and a fall is lethal.

## The route

The Hall of Thanes is a straight line with one detour through a cave. From the portal:

1. **The first chamber.** Tormented Souls and Enraged Apparitions, easy pulls. Take the corridor on the left, to the west.
2. **The Ghostly Attendant.** In the next room she gives An Ancient Grudge. Then comes Anvilmar's Rest, a large room with a tomb in the middle and Faldrim Anvilmar walking around it. Clear the ghosts first.
3. **The Dark Iron side.** Leave Anvilmar's Rest through the southeast corridor. From here the Dark Iron Dwarves take over. Kegs of Seaforium Blasting Powder stand along the way: shoot them from range and they blow up the pack around them.
4. **The cave.** The tombs turn into a natural cavern, where Magmatus stands between Dark Iron Dwarves.
5. **Back into the halls.** Up the tunnel waits a pack of four with two kegs beside it. Shoot both kegs at the same time before the pull.
6. **Plunder** patrols the hallway right after.
7. **The Reliquary of Kings.** North over a bridge guarded by Lesser Stone Golems lies the last room, with Durgen Dirgehammer. Behind one of the vault doors lies the Treaty of Understanding.

On the way out, hand in [An Ancient Grudge](quests/#quest-96395) with the Ghostly Attendant.

## The four bosses

### Faldrim Anvilmar

A ghost who patrols the middle of Anvilmar's Rest. Clear one side of the room, wait until he comes your way and pull him there, away from the trash. He casts [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26048) and puts [Anvilmar's Curse](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1292602) on the tank for 10 minutes: attack and casting speed 20% slower, but immune to poison and bleed effects. A player who removes curses makes this easier. Killing him opens the gates deeper into the hall, says Blizzard's [dungeon video](/news/blizzard-shows-three-launch-dungeons-in-a-video/).

Faldrim does not pull the ghosts around him along, but give yourself room before the pull anyway.

### Magmatus

A fire elemental with a Dark Iron Summoner at his side. Let the tank drag Magmatus away from the group and kill the Summoner first, because the boss sets off a fire nova now and then. [Combust](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293228) sets a random player on fire for 12 seconds. A patrolling Summoner nearby is best taken out before the pull.

![The fire elemental Magmatus, level 16, towering over a Dwarf tank in the Hall of Thanes, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-24-wow-forever-magmatus.jpg)

### Plunder

A golem that patrols a hallway, with one ability: [Knock Away](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10101), which throws a player far. Pull him to a spot where nobody lands in a pack of trash, with the group against a wall or in a corner.

![Plunder, a dark bronze golem with huge fists, in a hall with a red carpet, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-plunder.jpg)

### Durgen Dirgehammer

The final boss, in the Reliquary of Kings, with two Lesser Stone Golems at his side and Dark Iron Looters along the edges of the room. Clear the Looters first: his [Fear](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26070) sends players running into them. He also puts [Rend](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12054) on the tank. Kill the Golems one at a time; a Golem killed before a wipe does not come back, so every next try is easier. After the fight a switch in the back vault opens the locked doors, so the way out is short.

A Shaman who drops a [Tremor Totem](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8143) before the pull makes the fear harmless.

## Trash

The first rooms are easy; the Dark Iron side is not.

**Dark Iron Looters and Engineers.** Looters attack fast with daggers and drain the tank quickly when several come at once, so kill them first. Engineers throw bombs at a spot on the ground, and that damage can be dodged completely.

![A Dark Iron Looter and a Dark Iron Engineer in a dark hall of the Hall of Thanes, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-looter-and-engineer.jpg)

**Dark Iron Summoners.** They cast Fireball and call up a Fiery Assistant for 30 seconds. Pull them around a pillar or a wall, out of sight, so they have to come closer.

![A Dark Iron Summoner and a Dark Iron Looter next to a lantern, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-summoner.jpg)

**Lesser Stone Golems.** They hit hardest and move fast. Pull one at a time where possible, and focus one down before the next. The last pack before the final boss holds a Summoner, a Looter and a Golem together: [Polymorph](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=118) on one of them and stuns such as [Hammer of Justice](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=853) help. Healers draw a lot of threat at these levels.

![A Lesser Stone Golem, a grey stone giant with green runes, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-28-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-lesser-stone-golem.jpg)

**Bloodhound Runts.** Their bite leaves Infected Wounds for 5 minutes: more physical damage taken. Without a class that removes disease, a Simple Poultice from First Aid or the Dwarf racial [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20594) cures it.

**Seaforium kegs.** Kegs of Seaforium Blasting Powder stand next to several Dark Iron packs. A ranged player shoots them and the pack takes the blast.
