Every boss of Krol'dok Stronghold drops three items and Overlord Mugg five, among them the trinket <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=275009"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_coin_10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Emblem of Slamming</a> and the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271721"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_16.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Krol'dok Ogre Key</a>. Krol'dok Battlegear has five pieces: Nar'gurk drops the helmet and the chest, while the gauntlets, legplates and pauldrons drop from the monsters in the dungeon and are bind on equip, just like <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=287008"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shoulder_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ogre Magi's Mantle</a>.
