<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7710"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Loksey's Training Stick</a> gives 28 Attack Power and a chance on hit to deal 91 Physical damage; Canines and Hyenas hit by it are stunned for 3 seconds. Loksey also drops the new <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274292"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_shortblade_10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Houndmaster Boomerang</a>, a thrown weapon that returns to your hand, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3456"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_beastcall.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dog Whistle</a> is a trinket now. Doan's <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7713"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Illusionary Rod</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7714"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_shortblade_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hypnotic Blade</a> give up to 44 spell damage and healing.

The pieces of the Chain of the Scarlet Crusade drop here too; the set bonuses are on the [Graveyard page](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-graveyard/).
