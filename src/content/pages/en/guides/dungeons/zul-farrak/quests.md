Six of the nine quests are open to both factions: three start in Gadgetzan, one in Steamwheedle Port, one in Dustwallow Marsh and one in Thousand Needles. Take them all before the run, because most can be shared. The Spider God (Horde) and Nekrum's Medallion (Alliance) close long chains that start in The Hinterlands. The Prophecy of Mosh'aru leads to the Sunken Temple.

<a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96775">Zum'rah's Head</a> is new in Forever: Mazu'kon of the Earthen Ring in Mulgore sends the Horde after Witch Doctor Zum'rah.

The levels and the rewards come from the Forever data.
