Six of the eight quests are open to both factions: three start in Gadgetzan, one in Steamwheedle Port, one in Dustwallow Marsh and one in Thousand Needles. Take them all before the run, because most can be shared. The Spider God (Horde) and Nekrum's Medallion (Alliance) close long chains that start in The Hinterlands. The Prophecy of Mosh'aru leads to the Sunken Temple.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
