The Armory has no quests of its own. Herod is one of the bosses for Into The Scarlet Monastery and In the Name of the Light, which also ask for Houndmaster Loksey in the [Library](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-library/) and Mograine and Whitemane in the [Cathedral](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-cathedral/).
