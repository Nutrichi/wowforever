## Getting there

The Scarlet Monastery stands at the eastern end of Tirisfal Glades, at /way 85.1 31.4. The door of the Armory needs The Scarlet Key from the end of the [Library](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-library/), or a Rogue with Lockpicking 175.

![The armory of the Scarlet Monastery, a round hall with statues and a red floor, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-scarlet-monastery-armory.jpg)

## The route

A straight line. The number matches the map above.

1. **The Training Grounds.** A large courtyard full of Scarlet soldiers and trainees.
2. **The Footman's Armory and the Crusader's Armory.** Halls of soldiers, chaplains and defenders. Pull one group at a time.
3. **The Hall of Champions.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3975">Herod</a> (1) waits in the round hall at the end.

## Important trash

**Scarlet Chaplains and Myrmidons.** The chaplains shield and heal, the Myrmidons enrage and hit hard. Interrupt the heals and kill the casters first.

**The trainees after Herod.** When Herod dies, a large pack of Scarlet Trainees rushes in from the Training Grounds. They are weak; group up and kill them with area damage.

## The bosses

### Herod

Cleave and Whirlwind, and he enrages at low health. While he whirls, everyone, the melee included, steps back until it ends. Save the tank's cooldowns for the enrage.
