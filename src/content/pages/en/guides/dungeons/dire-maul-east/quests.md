Four of the five quests start in Feralas or Moonglade; Arcane Refreshment is for Mages only and starts inside Dire Maul. Lethtendris's Web has a version for each faction, with a short breadcrumb from Ironforge or Orgrimmar. Shards of the Felvine first sends you to Silithus for the reliquary.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
