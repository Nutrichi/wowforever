The Horde quests start in Kargath in the Badlands, the Alliance quests at Morgan's Vigil in the Burning Steppes. Flame Crest, also in the Burning Steppes, has three quests for both factions. Seal of Ascension opens [Upper Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/upper-blackrock-spire/), and Warlord's Command starts the long Horde chain towards Onyxia's Lair.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
