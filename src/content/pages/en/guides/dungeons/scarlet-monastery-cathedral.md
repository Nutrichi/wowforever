The three items of High Inquisitor Fairbanks are blue in Forever. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7726"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shield_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Aegis of the Scarlet Commander</a> deals 13 to 15 Holy damage every time you block, <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7723"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_mace_13.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mograine's Might</a> trades Spirit for Strength and Intellect, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7721"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hand of Righteousness</a> is a healer's mace with up to 94 healing. Whitemane's Chapeau and the Triune Amulet are not in the Forever data yet.

The pieces of the Chain of the Scarlet Crusade drop here too; the set bonuses are on the [Graveyard page](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-graveyard/).
