Almost every boss drop in the Deadmines is blue in Forever, many with a new effect: <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=872"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_throwingaxe_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Rockslicer</a> mines ore without Mining, more in the [post about Rockslicer](/news/a-deadmines-axe-mines-without-the-tradeskill/). Miner Johnson, a rare past Rhahk'Zor, drops the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5443"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shield_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gold-plated Buckler</a> and the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5444"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_cape_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Miner's Cape</a>; they sit under Other drops.

| Pieces | Defias Leather |
|---|---|
| 2 | +5 Arcane Resistance |
| 3 | +15 Attack Power against Humanoids |
| 4 | 5% chance on a melee hit from behind to make the target bleed for 68 to 83 Physical damage over 5 seconds |
| 5 | Daggers +1 |

VanCleef and Greenskin each drop one piece; the other three drop from monsters in the dungeon.

**Partners in Crime.** <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=284715"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_ring_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">First Mate Band</a> from Mr. Smite is the first half of a set with <a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=284716"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_ring_54.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Band of the Better Half</a>, an epic ring for level 60. Both together give +22 Strength and a chance on a melee swing to gain 50 Strength for 10 seconds. Where the second ring drops is not known. More in the [post about the set](/news/a-set-pairs-a-deadmines-ring-with-a-level-60-ring/).
