Nine quests are for the Horde, mostly from Kargath in the Badlands. Seven are for the Alliance, from Morgan's Vigil in the Burning Steppes, Ironforge and Kharanos. Six are open to both. The two long chains end with Emperor Dagran Thaurissan: The Royal Rescue for the Horde and The Fate of the Kingdom for the Alliance. Attunement to the Core opens Molten Core.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
