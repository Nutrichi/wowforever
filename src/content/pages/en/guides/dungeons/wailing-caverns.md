Almost every boss drop in Wailing Caverns is blue in Forever. The four Lords of the Fang each carry a piece of Embrace of the Viper, the leather set of the Druids of the Fang.

| Pieces | Embrace of the Viper |
|---|---|
| 2 | +10 Intellect |
| 3 | +10 Attack Power |
| 4 | Taking damage at or below 25% health restores 100 health and mana over 5 seconds, once every 5 minutes |
| 5 | Melee attacks have a chance to stun the target for 1 second. A Druid in Cat Form also takes the shape of a serpent, a bonus the tooltip does not show. |

The fifth piece, <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=10413"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gauntlets_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gloves of the Fang</a>, is not tied to a boss and sits under Other drops.
