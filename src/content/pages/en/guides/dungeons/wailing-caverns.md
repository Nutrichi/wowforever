Wailing Caverns is a maze of caves under The Barrens, where the druid Naralex tried to restore the land through the Emerald Dream and got lost in a nightmare. His followers became the Druids of the Fang. It is the first dungeon that does not run in a straight line, and its Agility gear makes it worth a run.

![The entrance of Wailing Caverns, a cave mouth between ferns and green light, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-wailing-caverns-entrance.jpg)

| Quests | 7, five of them for both factions |

| Short name | WC |

## Getting there

**Horde.** Travel southwest from the Crossroads to the Lushwater Oasis. The entrance lies in a cave system in the hill.

**Alliance.** Travel east from Astranaar in Ashenvale, cross the river, take the road south into The Barrens and head south and west. The boat from Booty Bay to Ratchet works too; from Ratchet, ride west.

The level is the lowest level at which the quest can be picked up, according to Wowhead.

- **Deviate Eradication.** Kill a list of monsters all over the dungeon.
- **Deviate Hides.** Collect 20 Deviate Hides; most monsters inside drop them.
- **Smart Drinks.** 6 Wailing Essences from the Devouring, Evolving and Nightmare Ectoplasms.
- **Trouble at the Docks.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3655">Mad Magglish</a> carries the 99-Year-Old Port. He hides in a small cave outside the instance.
- **The Glowing Shard.** Only after Mutanus dies in the Naralex event. Bring the shard to Sputtervalve in Ratchet, next to the flight master. He sends you to Falla Sagewind on the hill above the entrance, who gives In Nightmares for Arch Druid Hamuul Runetotem in Thunder Bluff.
- **Leaders of the Fang.** The gems of the four Lords of the Fang: Lady Anacondra, Lord Cobrahn, Lord Pythas and Lord Serpentis.
- **Serpentbloom.** 10 Serpentbloom from the walls of the dungeon and the caves before the portal. There are few of them, so not everyone finishes in one run.

## The Druids of the Fang

The Druids of the Fang are the trash to watch. Their Druid's Slumber puts a player to sleep for up to 15 seconds, the healer included. Fight one at a time, crowd control the rest and interrupt the sleep. Once they take Serpent Form, they are silenced.

### The Naralex event: Mutanus the Devourer

Once all four Lords of the Fang are dead, the Disciple of Naralex asks for an escort to the stone where Naralex sleeps. During the ritual, waves of monsters attack the Disciple. After the second wave, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3654">Mutanus the Devourer</a> appears, a level 22 elite murloc. His Thundercrack hits everyone nearby hard, and he puts players to sleep and fears them. Bring ranged damage and a second healer if you can. If the Disciple dies or the group wipes, the event is over until the dungeon resets.
