Two quests are for the Horde and two for the Alliance; five are open to both. Most of them close long chains: the Mosh'aru tablets from Zul'Farrak lead to The God Hakkar, and the Atal'ai Haze chains start in Un'Goro Crater. Jammal'an the Prophet comes from the Atal'ai Exile in The Hinterlands.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
