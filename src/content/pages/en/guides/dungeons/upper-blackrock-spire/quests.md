Three quests are for the Horde, three for the Alliance and six for both. The long chains end here: For The Horde! takes the head of Rend, and the blood of General Drakkisath closes the attunement for Onyxia's Lair on both sides. Onyxia's Lair [keeps its attunement chain](/news/onyxias-lair-keeps-its-attunement-quest-chain/) in Forever. Blackhand's Command starts from an item and leads to Blackwing Lair in Classic.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
