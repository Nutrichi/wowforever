Five quests are open to both factions; Serpentbloom and Leaders of the Fang are for the Horde. Nalpak and Ebru stand in the cave above the entrance, so Deviate Hides and Deviate Eradication can be picked up on the way in. Mad Magglish for Trouble at the Docks hides in that same outer cave.
