The Horde has six quests, most of them from Je'neu Sancrea at Zoram'gar Outpost, a short way south of the entrance. The Essence of Aku'Mai only opens after Trouble in the Deeps from Tsunaman at Sunrock Retreat, and its sapphires grow on the walls of the cave before the portal, outside the dungeon.

The Alliance has five, gathered in Darnassus, Darkshore and Ironforge. Researching the Corruption follows The Corruption Abroad from Argos Nightwhisper in Stormwind; that one is optional.

Blackfathom Villainy starts inside, with Argent Guard Thaelrid in an alcove of the room of Ghamoo-ra, and exists in a version for each faction. Warlocks also come here for <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1740">The Orb of Soran'ruk</a>: the small fragments drop from the Twilight Acolytes.
