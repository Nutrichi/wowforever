Blackmaw Hold has nine quests, so seven for each character. Five are open to both factions and come from the Timbermaw in Azshara, most of them at Ursolan, north of the Ruins of Eldarath. They all give reputation with Timbermaw Hold. Each faction also gets Blackmaw Magic from Dalaran or Thunder Bluff and Get to the Roots of It from Darnassus or Thunder Bluff.

The best reward comes from Blackmaw Reclamation: a choice of four rings for the head of Varrox Bleakhoof. Gatekeeper Rageroar only gives it after his own chain, which needs Neutral with Timbermaw Hold and starts with The Legashi Threat from Ekkal. Do that chain before your first run.

A Lost Memento starts from the Feathered Charm Necklace, which random monsters inside drop. It is not a shared drop: only one player can loot it.

Bosses, trash and the entrance are in the [guide](/guides/dungeons/blackmaw-hold/guide/).
