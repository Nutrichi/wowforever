Most quests are for the Alliance and start in Tinker Town in Ironforge; they also give reputation with Gnomeregan Exiles. The Only Cure is More Green Glow follows Gnogaine from Ozzie Togglevolt in Kharanos: use the phial next to a living irradiated trogg, and later next to the irradiated slimes, lurkers and horrors. The second phial has to be back with Ozzie within 2 hours.

The Horde has Rig Wars from Nogg in Orgrimmar. Sovik, next to him, gives <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=2842">Chief Engineer Scooty</a>, which leads to the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9173"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_battery_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Goblin Transponder</a>: the way in for the Horde.

Three quests start inside and are open to both factions. For Data Rescue, the White Punch Card drops outside; the machines A to D, from Techbot to below Crowd Pummeler 9-60, turn it into the Prismatic Punch Card.
