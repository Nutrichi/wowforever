The Cathedral ends the two big quests of the monastery: Into The Scarlet Monastery for the Horde and In the Name of the Light for the Alliance. Both ask for Mograine and Whitemane here, Herod in the [Armory](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-armory/) and Houndmaster Loksey in the [Library](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-library/).
