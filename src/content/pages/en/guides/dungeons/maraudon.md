The loot list follows the dungeon journal that Blizzard made for Maraudon in Classic, and only items that exist in the Forever data are shown. That leaves out fourteen of the thirty-one items, more than in any other dungeon on this site: [Heart of Noxxion](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17744), [Noxxion's Shackles](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17746), [Phytoskin Spaulders](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17749), [Chloromesh Girdle](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17750), [Brusslehide Leggings](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17751), [Satyr's Lash](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17752), [Infernal Trickster Leggings](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17754), [Gizlock's Hypertech Buckler](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17718), [Helm of the Mountain](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17734), [Rockgrip Gauntlets](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17736), [Cloud Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17737), [Gemshard Heart](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17707), [Elemental Rockridge Leggings](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17711) and [Blade of Eternal Darkness](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17780).

Whether Blizzard dropped those items for Forever or they simply are not in the data yet is not known. Meshlok the Harvester, the rare at Poison Falls, is not in the list.
