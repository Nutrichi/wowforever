Alcaz Prison has seven quests. Each faction has a story chain about the prisoners: the Alliance looks for King Varian Wrynn for SI:7, starting with Our Man in Theramore from Master Mathias Shaw in Stormwind, and the Horde looks for the captured Kor'kron, starting with The Missing Kor'kron from Eitrigg in Orgrimmar. Both chains end with a choice between a shield, a necklace and a dagger.

Both factions also get a naga hunt for the head of Tidemistress Ca'zass, the Alliance from Sully McCleary and the Horde from Sergeant Ulka on Alcaz Island. The Alliance gets The Dragon's Journal from Dalaran, the Horde Blood Red Bandanas. The Man in the Iron Chains appears after the last boss for everyone, but only when The Questioner is still alive.

Bosses, trash and the entrance are in the [guide](/guides/dungeons/alcaz-prison/guide/).
