## Getting there

Alcaz Prison is a new dungeon for World of Warcraft: Forever, for levels 48 to 53, on Alcaz Island in the north-east of Dustwallow Marsh. You land on the west side of the island; the keep is on the north side. Take the path up the hill to the large building. The portal is in its basement, around /way 75 15. The spirit healer stands right outside, so a wipe costs only a short run.

- **Horde:** fly to Brackenwall Village. Without that flight path, come in from Ratchet and head south along the coast.
- **Alliance:** take the boat from Menethil Harbor to Theramore and head north.

## The story

The prison on Alcaz Island was long said to hold important prisoners, King Varian Wrynn among them, but nobody could get in. In Forever its doors open, and inside the Defias Brotherhood and the naga fight each other, according to [Blizzard](/news/blizzard-describes-all-nine-new-dungeons/). Traitors in Theramore work with the Defias, the Alliance looks for its lost king and the Horde for its captured Kor'kron. The dungeon is about as long as The Stockade, but some fights hit hard.

## Trash to watch out for

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255373">Strashaz Soldiers</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255995">Strashaz Enchantresses</a>** wait at the start. The Enchantresses cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=13323"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a> on a random player and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12737"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt Volley</a>, and the Soldiers hit anyone 8 yards or more away with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1270802"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_warrior_charge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Berserker Charge</a>. Stand close behind them so the Soldiers have nobody to hit, interrupt the Enchantresses and remove the Polymorph when one gets through.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255262">Alcaz Chaplains</a>** are the healers here and cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15586"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_heal.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heal</a> on themselves and their friends. Kill them first in every pack, or keep one out of the fight with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12825"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a> when there are two. The <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=271784">Alcaz Jailer</a> with them uses <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15496"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_warrior_cleave.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cleave</a>, so the tank turns it away from the group.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255271">Defias Truthfinders</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255364">Renegade Conjurers</a>** are the casters. Truthfinders cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=14032"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_shadowwordpain.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Word: Pain</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17165"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_siphonmana.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mind Flay</a>: remove both. Conjurers use mage spells like <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=14034"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flamebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fireball</a> and bring a <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255369">Arcane Servitor</a>. Packs of four or five are common: keep one out with crowd control and pull them around a corner so they come to you.

## The bosses

Five bosses. Do not kill The Questioner before the rest of the dungeon is clear, see below.

### 1. Tidemistress Ca'zass

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255997">Tidemistress Ca'zass</a> fights with two Strashaz Enchantresses: kill them first or keep them out with crowd control. Her Frostbolt Volley hits the whole group, Frost Nova roots players and she casts Blizzard on top of herself. Move her out of the Blizzard, and a Paladin or Priest removes the Frost Nova from anyone stuck in it. Her head is for A Bit of Wet Work and A Little Payback.

### 2. Overseer Wrex

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255391">Overseer Wrex</a> patrols the three cell blocks of his wing. If he sees you, every prisoner guard in those three rooms joins the fight. So sneak past him and clear all three rooms first, while he walks the other way. He only has Mortal Strike, a heavy hit on the tank that cuts healing by 50% for 5 seconds, and casts Battle Shout on himself and his friends when the fight starts. He drops the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=263992"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Overseer's Keyring</a>.

### 3. The Questioner

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256236">The Questioner</a> sits in his chamber with a few guards. Pull some of them before the boss and the fight is easy: he only uses Rend and Drain Life, and a Paladin or Priest can remove the Drain Life. **Leave him alive until the last boss is dead** if you want the toy: the quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96335">The Man in the Iron Chains</a> only appears when he is still alive.

### 4. Katerina the Cleaner

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=257016">Katerina the Cleaner</a> comes with two Alcaz Chaplains. Keep them out with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12825"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2070"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_sap.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sap</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6213"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_possession.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fear</a>, or kill them first so nobody heals her. She puts Grievous Garrote on a random player: a big hit that keeps draining health until that player is healed above 90%. Deadly Poison lands on the tank: remove it right away.

### 5. Zarla Ober and Blazeroar

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255707">Zarla Ober</a> is the last fight and hits a lot harder. As a human she casts Fireball and Flamestrike; melee players often have to stand in the Flamestrike. At 30% she shows her true form, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=255702">Blazeroar</a>, with full health. Searing Scales now burns the whole group all the time, Flame Breath hits the tank very hard, Thrash lands several hits at once and a fear sends everyone but the tank running. The tank saves a cooldown for this phase, and a Shaman puts down <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8143"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_tremortotem.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tremor Totem</a>. Zarla drops her journal for The Dragon's Journal.

## The secret disguise toy

A hidden chain starts here and ends with the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279471"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/achievement_boss_edwinvancleef.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Alcaz Operative Insignia</a>, a toy that disguises you as a Defias operative for 10 minutes. Clear the dungeon without killing The Questioner, take <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96335">The Man in the Iron Chains</a> at the end, then follow the trail to Moonbrook in Westfall and on to Stormwind (Alliance) or Booty Bay (Horde) for a <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279450"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_armorkit_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fractured Insignia</a>. Six of them make the toy. The steps are in [the post about the toy](/news/alcaz-prison-hides-a-defias-disguise-toy/).
