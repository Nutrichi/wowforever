Both factions get the same three quests from their own givers: Shadowshard Fragments, [Vyletongue Corruption](#quest-7029) and [Corruption of Earth and Seed](#quest-7064). Twisted Evils, The Pariah's Instructions and the two quests of Celebras are open to everyone. Shadowshard Fragments and The Pariah's Instructions are done in the caves outside the portals.

Legends of Maraudon leads to The Scepter of Celebras, which gives you the scepter that takes you straight to Earth Song Falls. After Princess Theradras, Zaetar's Spirit gives the Seed of Life, which goes to Keeper Remulos in Moonglade.

The levels come from the Forever data. Some differ from Classic: Twisted Evils asks for 25 Theradric Crystal Carvings now. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.
