The loot list follows the dungeon journal that Blizzard made for Blackrock Spire in Classic, and only items that exist in the Forever data appear as cards. Three of the 40 items are in that data: Gyth's Skull from Warchief Rend Blackhand, and Brigam Girdle and Draconian Deflector from General Drakkisath. Whether Blizzard changes the rest for Forever or those items simply are not in the data yet, is not known.
