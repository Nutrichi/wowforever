The City of Dalaran is new in WoW Forever, so all of its loot is new too. Every one of the eight bosses drops three items, the rare Lyn the Ignored included, and Unstable Sentinel and Shade of the Archmage also carry a blueprint. Lyn the Ignored only appears when summoned with the Tome of Dalaran; how that works is on the Guide tab.
