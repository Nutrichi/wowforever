The City of Dalaran is one of the new dungeons of WoW Forever. The violet dome around the rebuilt city of the Kirin Tor is gone, and the threats that overran it have to be cleared. In Classic the city could not be entered. Seven bosses wait inside, the Shade of the Archmage at the end.

![Dalaran at sunset, the violet towers of the city of mages above the Alterac Mountains, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-dalaran-at-sunset.jpg)

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 28-33 according to Blizzard |
| Entrance | The Dalaran Sewers for the Alliance, a tunnel outside the city for the Horde |
| Bosses | 7, of which Lyn the Ignored is a rare |
| Quests | Heart of Disruption, the last step of the Horde attunement |
| Watch out | Almost every monster is immune to Arcane |
| Short name | CoD |

The beta goes up to level 30 since 1 October. The last bosses are above that, so Wowhead does not know all their mechanics yet.

## Getting there

**Alliance.** The first time, it is a long ride from Hillsbrad Foothills to the city. Inside Dalaran, go to Cantrips & Crows in the Dalaran Sewers, open the gate and follow the pipe to the portal. More about the city in the [post about Dalaran on the beta](/news/dalaran-opens-on-the-beta-for-the-alliance-only/).

**Horde.** The Horde is not welcome in the city and enters through a tunnel outside it, at /way 8.8 59.6, where the <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=269127">Image of Archmage Modera</a> grants access. At least one player needs the <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=277507"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dalaran Sewer Key</a> to open the gate. From The Sepulcher in Silverpine Forest it is a few minutes on foot.

The Alliance only needs to speak to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=269127">Image of Archmage Modera</a> outside the city. ## The trash

- **Mana Phantoms** cast a nasty <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20817"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_manaburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mana Burn</a>. They are not elite: interrupt them and kill them first.
- **Saturated Remnants** leave a field behind when they die that restores mana to anyone standing in it.
- **Arcane immunity.** Almost every monster ignores Arcane damage, so Arcane Explosion and Arcane Shot do little and Counterspell does not interrupt.

## The bosses

### Atrexis the Grave Knight

Early in the dungeon and an easy fight. He summons non-elite skeletons that have to die and enrages now and then. Only heavy area damage before the tank has threat is dangerous.

### Arcane Anomaly

After the sewers. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1241501"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_arcanesurge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Focal Blast</a> is an arcane beam that sweeps across the room counterclockwise and kills anyone it hits. Stay inside the circle on the platform: small adds spawn at the edge of the room when the fight starts. The boss also casts Arcane Bolt.

### Arcanic Enigma

Two Mana Phantoms start the fight with him; kill them quickly. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1240869"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_wispsplode.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Manamorph</a> crowd controls a player for 8 seconds and must be dispelled as Magic. The healer stands far away, because of the area Silence around the boss. Arcane Manalings keep spawning and have to be cleaved down; no new Mana Phantoms appear.

### Unstable Sentinel

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1241329"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Malfunction</a> winds up and then hits everyone within 25 yards hard. Ranged players stay at maximum range, melee players run out at the wind-up. Kill the Angry Tomes that come out of the buildings before you pull him.

### Fel Ancient

Only reachable once the bosses around him are dead and his mana barrier breaks. His mechanics are not known yet.

### Lyn the Ignored, a rare

Her mechanics are not known yet.

### Shade of the Archmage

The final boss. He fights with his mana pool; when it runs dry, he casts Mass Polymorph and Evocation to fill it again. Arcane Bolt hits the tank steadily, Arcane Explosion everyone within 20 yards. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1241515"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_netherwindpresence.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bounding Mana</a> sends an arcane boomerang at one player that hurts anyone in its path. Spread out, keep the casters at range, and hide behind the wall during Mass Polymorph. Do not pull him out of his room: he resets.
