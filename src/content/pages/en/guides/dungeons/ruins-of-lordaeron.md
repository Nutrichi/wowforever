Every boss drops three blue items. Rotmender's Leggings from The Abandoned and Rotmender's Treads from Rath'mael belong to a new cloth set of five pieces. Two pieces give +10 Intellect, three reduce threat by 5%, four restore 600 mana when mana drops below 15%, and five give healing spells a chance to heal over time. The Robes, the Sash and the Gloves bind when equipped; where they drop is not known yet.
