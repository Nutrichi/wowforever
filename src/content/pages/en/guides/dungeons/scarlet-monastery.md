Scarlet Monastery is the stronghold of the Scarlet Crusade in Tirisfal Glades, a dungeon of four wings: the Graveyard, the Library, the Armory and the Cathedral. In WoW Forever several of its drops are reworked, and the Chain of the Scarlet Crusade set gets new bonuses. This guide holds what is known so far.

![The courtyard of the Scarlet Monastery with its pond, two statues and the cathedral, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-scarlet-monastery-cathedral.jpg)

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | Bosses of 32 to 42; the dungeon as a whole is hard at 26, at level at 37 and easy at 45 |
| Entrance | The Scarlet Monastery in the northeast of Tirisfal Glades |
| Wings | 4: Graveyard, Library, Armory, Cathedral |
| Bosses | 10, of which 3 are rares in the Graveyard |
| Quests | 8 |
| Key | The Scarlet Key for the Armory and the Cathedral |

The beta of WoW Forever goes up to level 30 since 1 October. Testers clear the Graveyard and the Library; the Armory and the Cathedral are above the cap, and what drops there in Forever is not confirmed yet.

## Graveyard

### Interrogator Vishas

<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7682"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_spear_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Torturing Poker</a> no longer deals a fixed amount of Fire damage. Every melee hit tortures the target for 1 Fire damage and adds 1 to that damage, up to 10 stacks, so at full stacks every attack deals 10 extra Fire damage. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7683"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gauntlets_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bloody Brass Knuckles</a> used to be a white fist weapon and is now blue, with 12 Attack Power and another 15 against Humanoids.

![The tooltips of Torturing Poker and Bloody Brass Knuckles on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-torturing-poker-and-bloody-brass-knuckles.jpg)

### Bloodmage Thalnos

<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7684"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shoulder_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bloodmage Mantle</a> goes from green to blue: 9 Intellect, up to 9 spell damage and healing, and 9 health every 5 seconds. Thalnos also drops <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7685"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_orb_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Orb of the Forgotten Seer</a>, an off-hand with up to 13 spell damage and healing.

![The tooltip of Bloodmage Mantle on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-bloodmage-mantle.jpg)

### Azshir the Sleepless, a rare

<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7731"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_necklace_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ghostshard Talisman</a> now gives 9 Stamina, 14 Attack Power and another 12 against Undead, where it used to give only Stamina and Spirit. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7708"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Necrotic Wand</a> becomes a healing wand, with up to 9 healing, 3 spell damage and 4 Shadow Resistance. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7709"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pants_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blighted Leggings</a> is the third blue item he drops on the beta.

![The tooltips of Ghostshard Talisman and Necrotic Wand on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-ghostshard-talisman-and-necrotic-wand.jpg)

The other two rares, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=6488">Fallen Champion</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=6489">Ironspine</a>, have no confirmed changes yet.

## Library

### Houndmaster Loksey

<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7710"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Loksey's Training Stick</a> used to be good only against Beasts. The staff now gives 28 Attack Power and a chance on hit to deal 91 Physical damage; Canines and Hyenas hit by it are stunned for 3 seconds and sit down. Loksey also drops a new Houndmaster Boomerang, a thrown weapon with 4 Stamina, 4 Attack Power and another 9 against Beasts that returns to your hand. The boomerang is not yet in the files of build 1.60.1.70178.

![The tooltips of Loksey's Training Stick and Houndmaster Boomerang on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-lokseys-training-stick-and-houndmaster-boomerang.jpg)

### Arcanist Doan

Doan is level 37, above the cap of the beta. Changes to his loot are not known yet.

## Armory and Cathedral

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3975">Herod</a> in the Armory and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4542">High Inquisitor Fairbanks</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3976">Scarlet Commander Mograine</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3977">High Inquisitor Whitemane</a> in the Cathedral are level 40 to 42. Nobody on the beta can reach them at level, so their loot in Forever is not confirmed.

## Chain of the Scarlet Crusade

The mail set of the Scarlet Crusade drops from monsters all over the dungeon. In the files of the beta it has two new bonuses, Enraging Light and Scarlet Guardian. No piece has dropped on the beta yet, as far as Icy Veins knows.

| Pieces | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 2 | +10 Shadow Resistance |
| 3 | +30 Attack Power against Undead |
| 4 | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293674"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unholyfrenzy.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Enraging Light</a>: taking damage has a 15% chance to make your melee attacks deal 20 Holy damage for 10 seconds, three times as much against Undead |
| 5 | +1% chance to hit |
| 6 | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293670"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_tabard_scarletcrusade_b_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scarlet Guardian</a>: taking damage at or below 20% health gives a shield that absorbs damage for 10 seconds, once every 4 minutes |
