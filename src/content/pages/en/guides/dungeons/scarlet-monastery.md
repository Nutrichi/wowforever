Scarlet Monastery is the stronghold of the Scarlet Crusade in Tirisfal Glades, a dungeon of four wings: the Graveyard, the Library, the Armory and the Cathedral. In WoW Forever several of its drops are reworked, and the Chain of the Scarlet Crusade set gets new bonuses. This guide holds what is known so far.

![The courtyard of the Scarlet Monastery with its pond, two statues and the cathedral, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-03-wow-forever-scarlet-monastery-cathedral.jpg)

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 26-45, bosses of level 32 to 42 |
| Entrance | The Scarlet Monastery in the northeast of Tirisfal Glades |
| Wings | 4: Graveyard, Library, Armory, Cathedral |
| Bosses | 10, of which 3 are rares in the Graveyard |
| Quests | 8 |
| Key | The Scarlet Key for the Armory and the Cathedral |

The beta of WoW Forever goes up to level 30 since 1 October. Testers clear the Graveyard and the Library; the Armory and the Cathedral are above the cap, and what drops there in Forever is not confirmed yet.

## Graveyard

### Interrogator Vishas

<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7682"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_spear_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Torturing Poker</a> no longer deals a fixed amount of Fire damage. Every melee hit tortures the target for 1 Fire damage and adds 1 to that damage, up to 10 stacks, so at full stacks every attack deals 10 extra Fire damage. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7683"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gauntlets_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bloody Brass Knuckles</a> used to be a white fist weapon and is now blue, with 12 Attack Power and another 15 against Humanoids.
