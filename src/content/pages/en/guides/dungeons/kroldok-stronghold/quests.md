Most quests for Krol'dok Stronghold open only after the story of the Riverglades. For the Alliance that is a chain of about ten quests at Farholde Keep, for the Horde a chain at Rog'mar. One quest starts inside the dungeon, and one comes from Dalaran for both factions.

## Alliance

The chain starts at Farholde Keep (/way 66.6 81.0) with <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94881">Curious Trinkets</a>, <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94879">Scraps of Knowledge</a> and <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94877">Saving Sunnyglade</a>, from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=257591">Ashe Amberhall</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=257558">Demar Brant</a>.

## Both factions

<a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96771">Vengeance of the Highborne</a>, level 45, comes from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=268425">Ranath Nightstride</a> in Dalaran in the Alterac Mountains (/way 17.8 66.0). Kill Zalfan Moonshadow in the stronghold.

## The key

Tunk drops the Krol'dok Moulding about half the time. Bring it to your faction's town, then hand in Key Ingredients for a key to a shortcut inside the dungeon. Buy the iron bars and leather before you go, so you do not have to come back. Which of the two keys in the game data, <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=267089"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Krol'dok Compound Key</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271721"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_16.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Krol'dok Ogre Key</a>, this is, is not confirmed.
