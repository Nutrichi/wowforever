## Getting there

Krol'dok Stronghold is a new dungeon for World of Warcraft: Forever, for levels 40 to 45, in the new zone the Riverglades. The ogre fortress stands on a hill above Sunnyglade on the north side of the zone. The entrance is a cave tucked away just west of the large gates, at /way 37.5 37.5.

Ogre outrunners kidnap travelers and plunder villages all over the Riverglades, and their trail leads here. After dark, strange chanting comes from the stronghold.

![An ogre guarding a stone gate with spiked towers in a forest, an image from Blizzard of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../../../assets/posts/2026-10-20-wow-forever-kroldok-stronghold-gate.jpg)

## The layout

A winding path leads uphill between crude buildings, workshops and camps, past guarded passages. A large wooden bridge crosses to the top, where the largest buildings stand. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=278310">Krol'dok Centurion</a> is one of the elite ogres you will meet.

## Trash to watch out for

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=258042">Twilight Grunts</a>** are the most dangerous monsters here. One is fine, but two or more hurt the melee players: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1307066"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_whirlwind.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bladestorm</a> hits everyone within 8 yards, and they <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15572"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_warrior_sunder.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sunder Armor</a> the tank. Stun them, and keep one out with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12825"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a> when a pack has more than two.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=258102">Twilight Outriders</a>** ride warhounds in groups of three along the long roads. They shoot like hunters and pin the tank down for 10 seconds with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=14030"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_ensnare.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hooked Net</a>. Pull them around a corner, away from other packs, and a Paladin frees the tank with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1044"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_sealofvalor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blessing of Freedom</a>.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=277611">Twilight Peons</a>** run very fast to warn other monsters. Stun or slow them before they get away.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=257888">Krol'dok Shamans</a>** heal and shield their friends, and **<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=257885">Krol'dok Centurions</a>** use Mortal Strike on the tank and Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout on the melee players. Kill the Shamans first, then the Centurions.

## The bosses

The map at the top shows the route and the six bosses in order. Q marks a quest spot along the way.

### 1. Slavecrusher Grox

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=258966">Slavecrusher Grox</a> patrols the mines in the first bend past the entrance. He uses Bloodlust (30% faster attacks for 30 seconds), knocks the tank down for 2 seconds and calls the prisoners nearby to help him. Clear the gnolls in his area first; the prisoners he calls are not elite and die fast.

### 2. Twilight Commander Zarmok

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=258972">Twilight Commander Zarmok</a> waits at the foot of the first climb with Twilight's Hammer guards around him. Kill the guards first, so you can pull him alone. His Rending Whirl hits everyone within 8 yards very hard, so only the tank and the melee players who can take it stay close.

### 3. War Handler Ashka

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=259281">War Handler Ashka</a> stands on the east side near the river, with her two wolves. Stun the wolves and kill them first: with Command: Kill she sends them after a random player for 10 seconds. Without the wolves the fight is easy.

### 4. Nar'gurk

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=258964">Nar'gurk</a> waits among the camps on the west side with three guards. Keep them out with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12825"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2070"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_sap.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sap</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6213"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_possession.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fear</a>, or kill them first. Nar'gurk casts Shadow Bolt Volley on the group and takes control of a player with Convert: bring that player down to 50% health to break it. He drops the helmet and chest of Krol'dok Battlegear.

### 5. Bossman Toof

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=264773">Bossman Toof</a> waits on the way up to the top. As soon as you attack the Centurions at the start of the bridge, he sends waves of two Brutes after you. A Rogue's <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1725"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_distract.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Distract</a> buys time for the first pack. Two Shamans stand with him: keep them out with crowd control, and let one more wave of Brutes come before you pull him. He only uses Mortal Strike, Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout, so on his own he is easy.

### 6. Overlord Mugg

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=258968">Overlord Mugg</a> waits at the top of the stronghold with two Centurions: keep them out or kill them first, the damage here is high. Crushing Slam lets him jump to a random player and knock everyone nearby back. Mugg's Fury makes him attack faster and faster, until he switches to two maces and at the end to two mauls with 50% more damage. So do not push him down while the Centurions are still alive. He is named in the Legacy challenge of the dungeon and drops <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=267086"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_eye_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Eye of Mugg</a>, a quest item.

## The Krol'dok Battlegear

The dungeon has a five-piece plate set for tanks. Nar'gurk drops the helmet and the chest, the other three pieces bind when equipped. Wear all five and the set can turn you into an ogre, see [the post about the set](/news/the-kroldok-set-turns-your-tank-into-an-ogre/).
