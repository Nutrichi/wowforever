Gnomeregan is the dungeon of engineers: Thermaplugg alone drops four schematics, the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7742"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Schematic: Gnomish Cloaking Device</a> among them, and the monsters drop several more. Several caster items got spell damage and healing in Forever, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9446"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_30.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electrocutioner Leg</a> deals double damage to Mechanical targets. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thermaplugg's Left Arm</a> and the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9492"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator</a> still drop from Thermaplugg.

Electrocutioner 6000 drops the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6893"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Workshop Key</a>, which opens the back door at the Train Depot.
