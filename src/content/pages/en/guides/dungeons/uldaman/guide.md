## Getting there

The main entrance lies at the northern edge of the Badlands, near the road to Loch Modan, at /way 35.2 10.7. The excavation tunnels before the portal are full of elite troggs, so go in as a group. A back door lies in another trogg cave in Dustwind Gulch, southeast of the main entrance.

## The route

A long string of tunnels and halls. The numbers match the maps above.

1. **The first halls.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=6910">Revelosh</a> (1), a trogg, waits with two non-elite troggs. He drops the Shaft of Tsol.
2. **Dig Two.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=6906">Baelog</a> and his brothers, the Lost Dwarves (2), guard a chest with the Gni'kiv Medallion. For the Alliance they are friendly.
3. **The Map Chamber.** Combine the Shaft of Tsol and the Gni'kiv Medallion into the Staff of Prehistoria and use it here to wake <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7228">Ironaya</a> (3).
4. **Echomok Cavern.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7023">Obsidian Sentinel</a> (4) stands among the Stone Stewards.
5. **The Temple Hall.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7206">Ancient Stone Keeper</a> (5) guards the hall.
6. **Dig Three.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7291">Galgann Firehammer</a> (6) with two Shadowforge Geologists.
7. **The Stonevault.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4854">Grimlok</a> (7), with a Geomancer, a Brawler and a Basilisk at his side.
8. **Khaz'Goroth's Seat.** Use the altar to wake <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=2748">Archaedas</a> (8).

## Important trash

**The stone constructs.** Stone Stewards and other earthen guardians have a lot of armor and health, and many are immune to damage over time.

**Troggs in the tunnels.** Geomancers and Brawlers come in large packs. Crowd control the casters and pull slowly.

**Dark Iron dwarves.** The Shadowforge dwarves deeper in the dungeon hit hard; Geologists cast fire spells, so interrupt them.

## The bosses

### Revelosh

Casts Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning. Kill the two adds first, spread out and interrupt Chain Lightning.

### The Lost Dwarves

Baelog, Eric "The Swift" and Olaf, fought together, and only by the Horde. Eric's Rushing Charge hits players at range, Olaf uses Shield Slam and blocks, Baelog shoots. Kill Eric first, then Olaf, then Baelog, and crowd control Baelog if you can.

### Ironaya

High armor, a lot of health and immune to damage over time. Knock Away throws the tank back, War Stomp stuns everyone nearby and Arcing Smash cleaves in front. Turn her away from the group and spread out.

### Obsidian Sentinel

A boss version of the Stone Stewards. He reflects spells, and at 66% and 33% health he calls Obsidian Shards. Kill the shards first, then return to the Sentinel.

### Ancient Stone Keeper

Sand Storms slow and silence everyone inside them. Ranged players and the healer stay at maximum range.

### Galgann Firehammer

A Dark Iron fire caster with two Shadowforge Geologists. Flame Lash, Flame Shock and Fire Nova hurt, and Amplify Flames makes the target take more Fire damage: remove it if you can. Kill the Geologists first, with a second player holding one of them.

### Grimlok

A trogg chief with a Stonevault Geomancer, a Stonevault Brawler and a Jadespine Basilisk. Crowd control the adds and burn Grimlok; without enough crowd control, kill the Basilisk first. He casts Shrink and speeds up with Bloodlust.

### Archaedas

The last boss wakes when the altar is used. Ground Tremor stuns everyone nearby, and he wakes the Earthen Guardians and Vault Warders around the room during the fight. Many groups pull him up the ramp into the previous hall, away from the statues. Kill the adds as they come and spread out from the boss.
