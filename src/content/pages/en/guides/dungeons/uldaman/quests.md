The level in the table is the lowest level at which the quest can be picked up. The rewards are not known yet in Forever.

- **The Lost Dwarves and The Hidden Chamber.** Find Baelog in the dungeon; his journal leads to the hidden chamber.
- **The Shattered Necklace and Necklace Recovery.** The Shattered Necklace drops from Shadowforge and Shadowvault mobs. The Alliance takes it to Talvash del Kissel in Ironforge, the Horde to Dran Droffers in Orgrimmar.
- **The Platinum Discs.** Talk to the stone watcher, then use the Discs of Norgannon.
