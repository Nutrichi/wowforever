The Forever data has no quests for Uldaman yet. These are the quests that lead here in Classic:

- **Alliance.** The Lost Dwarves from Prospector Stormpike, Reclaimed Treasures from Krom Stoutarm and The Lost Tablets of Will from Advisor Belgrum, all in Ironforge. Agmond's Fate from Prospector Ironband and Uldaman Reagent Run from Ghak Healtouch, both in Loch Modan. Amulet of Secrets from Hammertoe Grez in the Badlands.
- **Horde.** Reclaimed Treasures from Patrick Garrett in the Undercity, Necklace Recovery from Dran Droffers in Orgrimmar, and Uldaman Reagent Run from Jarkal Mossmeld in the Badlands.
- **Both factions.** Solution to Doom from Theldurin the Lost in the Badlands. The Shattered Necklace, The Hidden Chamber and The Platinum Discs start inside the dungeon.
