The Fel Steeds in the courtyard drop <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=932"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fel Steed Saddlebags</a>, a unique bag with 10 slots. Razorclaw the Butcher carries the blueprint for a Tanning Rack, one of the [camping blueprints](/guides/camping/).
