Shadowfang Keep was the castle of Baron Silverlaine in Silverpine Forest. Archmage Arugal summoned worgen there to defend Dalaran, and they overran the keep. Arugal now lives in its highest tower with the worgen as his pack. The dungeon is the same as in Classic; its loot is largely new in WoW Forever.

## Getting there

The keep stands in the mountains just north of Pyrewood Village in Silverpine Forest, at /way 44.5 68.0. For the Horde, the Sepulcher with all three quest givers is a short ride away.

For the Alliance, Silverpine Forest at this level is a long and dangerous trip. Skyborne characters have it easier: they reach the zone fairly quickly from Dalaran, writes Warcraft Tavern.

## The route

1. **The cells.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3914">Rethilgore</a> guards the prisoners at the start. Kill him to open the door to the courtyard; the prisoner of your faction, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3849">Deathstalker Adamant</a> for the Horde or <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3850">Sorcerer Ashcrombe</a> for the Alliance, opens it.
2. **The courtyard and the kitchen.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3886">Razorclaw the Butcher</a> stands in the kitchen with a few adds.
3. **The dining hall.** The ghost of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3887">Baron Silverlaine</a> haunts it, surrounded by a room full of adds.
4. **The upper hall.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4278">Commander Springvale</a> waits in a room of Wailing Guardsmen. Pull them in small groups. The rare <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3872">Deathsworn Captain</a> does not always spawn.
5. **The ramparts.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4279">Odo the Blindwatcher</a> and his Vile Bats, then <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4274">Fenrus the Devourer</a> in the room with The Book of Ur.
6. **The tower.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3927">Wolf Master Nandos</a> guards the door to Arugal with his wolves. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4275">Archmage Arugal</a> waits at the top with three worgen.

The level is the lowest level at which the quest can be picked up, according to Wowhead. None of the three needs an earlier quest, so a Horde group can pick them all up at once and share them. The rewards come from Wowhead; where it lists none, they are not known yet.

### Deathstalkers in Shadowfang

High Executor Hadrec sends you to find Deathstalker Adamant and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4444">Deathstalker Vincent</a>. Vincent's body lies just past Rethilgore, to the right of the stairs into the courtyard. The quest is finished and handed in inside the dungeon.

### The Book of Ur

Keeper Bel'dugur in The Apothecarium wants The Book of Ur. It stands on a shelf in the room of Fenrus the Devourer, near the end of the dungeon. The book is easy to forget in the middle of a fight, so look for it before you move on.

### Arugal Must Die

Dalar Dawnweaver at the Sepulcher wants the Head of Arugal, from the final boss.

Two class quests also lead here: <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1740">The Orb of Soran'ruk</a> for Warlocks of both factions, and <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1654">The Test of Righteousness</a> for Paladins of the Alliance. Both are in the [dungeon guide](/guides/dungeons/).

### Archmage Arugal

The final boss, a level 26 elite. Kill the three worgen in his room first. Arugal teleports through the room, takes control of players and casts Void Bolt, which cannot be interrupted. Arugal's Curse cannot be dispelled; crowd control a cursed player instead of attacking them. Shadow Resistance helps.
