The loot list follows the dungeon journal that Blizzard made for Zul'Farrak in Classic, and only items that exist in the Forever data are shown. Four items from that list are not in it yet: [Lifeblood Amulet](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=9641) of Antu'sul, [Jumanza Grips](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=18083) of Zum'rah, and [Ripsaw](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=9478) and [Embrace of the Lycan](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=9479) of Chief Ukorz Sandscalp.

Theka the Martyr and Hydromancer Velratha have no items of their own in the journal. Velratha drops the Tiara of the Deep and Theka the First Mosh'aru Tablet for the quests. The rares Zerillis, Dustwraith and Sandarr Dunereaver are not in the list.
