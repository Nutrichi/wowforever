Eight quests, all from level 24. Heart of Disruption is the last step of the attunement and gives the Dalaran Sewer Key; the Alliance and the Horde each have their own version, and the chain for the Horde is on the Guide tab. The other quests start in Stormwind, Undercity, Tarren Mill and outside the city, so collect them before you travel.
