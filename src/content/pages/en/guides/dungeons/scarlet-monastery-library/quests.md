Most quests here ask for a book from the shelves of the Library: Mythology of the Titans for the Alliance, the Compendium of the Fallen and The Beginnings of the Undead Threat for the Horde, and Rituals of Power for Mages. Past Due, from Magus Olvek in Dalaran, is new in Forever.

Into The Scarlet Monastery for the Horde and In the Name of the Light for the Alliance ask for Houndmaster Loksey here, Herod in the [Armory](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-armory/) and Mograine and Whitemane in the [Cathedral](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-cathedral/).
