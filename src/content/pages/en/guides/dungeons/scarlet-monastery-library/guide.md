## Getting there

The Scarlet Monastery stands at the eastern end of Tirisfal Glades, at /way 85.1 31.4. The Library is the second wing; its door is open to everyone. The [Graveyard page](/guides/dungeons/scarlet-monastery-graveyard/guide/) has the way there for both factions.

## The route

The numbers match the map above.

1. **The Huntsman's Cloister.** A long hallway of Scarlet soldiers leads to a garden. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3974">Houndmaster Loksey</a> (1) waits with his hounds in the room south of it.
2. **The Gallery of Treasures.** Halls full of Scarlet monks and Mythology of the Titans on the shelves.
3. **The Athenaeum.** The other books for the quests stand here. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=6487">Arcanist Doan</a> (2) waits at the end, with Doan's Strongbox and the Scarlet Key behind him.

## Important trash

**Scarlet casters.** Adepts, Conjurers and Monks hit hard and heal each other. Interrupt the heals and crowd control a second caster.

**Patrols.** Soldiers walk the long hallways. Wait for them before you pull, or they join the fight from behind.

## The bosses

### Houndmaster Loksey

Fights with his hounds, Bloodlust and Battle Shout. Kill the hounds first, or bring them down together with area damage.

### Arcanist Doan

Silence and Arcane Explosion. At half health he raises Arcane Bubble, polymorphs everyone near him and then casts Detonation: run far away as soon as you see the bubble.
