Blackrock Spire is the stronghold of the Dark Horde inside Blackrock Mountain. It is one large instance in two wings that count as separate dungeons: Lower Blackrock Spire for 5 players, and Upper Blackrock Spire above it, which needs a key.

| Wing | Levels | Group | Bosses | Quests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Lower Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/lower-blackrock-spire/) | 55-60 | 5 players | 9 | 12 |
| [Upper Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/upper-blackrock-spire/) | 58-60 | 10 players in Classic | 6 | 12 |

## Getting there

Both wings share one portal on the east side of Blackrock Mountain, the mountain between the Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes. From the main path through the mountain, two ways lead up:

- **The door next to the meeting stone** of Blackrock Spire. Follow the path upward; a few elites stand on the way.
- **The chain.** Climb the large chain, follow the wall to the balcony where the gnome Bodley stands and jump onto it. That puts you right in front of the portal without a single fight.

## Lower Blackrock Spire

The lower wing is a normal dungeon for 5 players. The bosses are Highlord Omokk, Shadow Hunter Vosh'gajin, War Master Voone, Mother Smolderweb, Urok Doomhowl, Quartermaster Zigris, Halycon, Gizrul the Slavener, Overlord Wyrmthalak. Rares that do not always show up: Burning Felguard, Spirestone Battle Lord, Spirestone Lord Magus, Bannok Grimaxe, Crystal Fang, Ghok Bashguud, Spirestone Butcher. Mor Grayhoof only appears for a quest.

Loot, quests and the route are in the guide to [Lower Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/lower-blackrock-spire/).

## Upper Blackrock Spire

The upper wing has to be unlocked: one player with the Seal of Ascension opens the door to the left of the entrance inside Lower Blackrock Spire. The bosses are Pyroguard Emberseer, Solakar Flamewreath, Goraluk Anvilcrack, Warchief Rend Blackhand, The Beast, General Drakkisath. Jed Runewatcher is a rare, and Lord Valthalak only appears for a quest.

Loot, quests and the route are in the guide to [Upper Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/upper-blackrock-spire/).

## What is not known yet

Blackrock Spire was closed on the beta, which stopped at level 30, so these pages follow the dungeon as it was in Classic. Whether Upper Blackrock Spire stays a dungeon for 10 players, and what else changes, Blizzard has not said. All dungeons are in the [dungeon overview](/guides/dungeons/).
