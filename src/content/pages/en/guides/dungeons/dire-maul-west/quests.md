Elven Legends has a version for each faction and starts in Feralas. The other two quests come from the Shen'dralar Ancient on the bridge in the Court of the Highborne: The Madness Within sends you to Immol'thar and Prince Tortheldrin, and The Treasure of the Shen'dralar follows.

The levels come from the Forever data. The rewards are not in that data yet, so the cards below say so; this page fills them in as soon as they are known.

**Weekly quest at level 60.** <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96076">In Demand - Ancient Elven Curiosities</a> from Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay asks for the Ancient Memento from Prince Tortheldrin here, plus one item from each of the other two wings. The reward is a satchel with an item worth up to 100 gold, more in [our post](/news/a-weekly-quest-at-level-60-pays-up-to-100-gold/).
