All six quests are for the Alliance. Four of them can be shared. Warden Thelwater, just outside the entrance, hands out two; the others start in Stormwind, Lakeshire, Darkshire and Dun Modr, so collect them on the way.

The Stockade Riots is part of a long chain: it starts with An Unsent Letter from Edwin VanCleef in [The Deadmines](/guides/dungeons/the-deadmines/), and Baros Alexston in Stormwind sends you on to the Warden with <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=389">Bazil Thredd</a>. After this quest the chain continues in Stormwind.
