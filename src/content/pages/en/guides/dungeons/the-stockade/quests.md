All six quests are for the Alliance. Four of them can be shared. Warden Thelwater, just outside the entrance, hands out two; the others start in Stormwind, Lakeshire, Darkshire and Dun Modr, so collect them on the way.

The Stockade Riots is part of a long chain: it starts with An Unsent Letter from Edwin VanCleef in [The Deadmines](/guides/dungeons/the-deadmines/), and Baros Alexston in Stormwind sends you on to the Warden with <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=389">Bazil Thredd</a>. After this quest the chain continues in Stormwind.

In Stormwind the chain passes Tyrion in the garden of Stormwind Keep, for <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=434">The Attack!</a>. Since the launch that event waits until your group is ready, and you can pick up and share the quest while another group is busy ([our post](/news/the-attack-now-waits-until-your-group-is-ready/)). At the end, <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=396">An Audience with the King</a> gives <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2933"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_ring_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Wrynn</a>.
