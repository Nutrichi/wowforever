The Demonology Warlock in WoW Forever lets the demon do the work. You brand the enemy with Searing Pain, your Succubus tanks it and deals most of your damage, and you fill the gaps with DoTs. This guide covers the build, rotation, threat, demons and stats.

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | Damage through your demon, the safest Warlock spec solo |
| Build at level 30 | 24 points in Demonology and 2 in Destruction, with the 5 extra points from Talented |
| Key talents | Unholy Power, Demonic Energies, Demonic Brand, Demonic Knowledge, Soul Link |
| Strength | Your demon tanks, strong pet damage, group utility |
| Weakness | Everything depends on your demon, the Brand needs constant upkeep |

## What changes for Demonology

- **Demonic Brand is new.** Searing Pain brands the target for 10 seconds. At 3/3 your demon's next 6 attacks against it deal extra Fire or Shadow damage and extra threat.
- **Demons scale with your gear.** Your Spell Power now makes your demon stronger too.
- **Demonic Knowledge is new.** It gives you and your demon Spell Power equal to 33% of your level per point while the demon lives.
- **Demonic Energies is new.** Your spells heal your demon, and Life Tap also restores its mana.
- **Banes are not Curses.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofsargeras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bane of Agony</a> is now a Bane, so you can add <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6205"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofmannoroth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Weakness</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1714"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseoftounges.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Tongues</a> on the same target.
- **Ritual of Summoning matters more.** Meeting Stones no longer summon, so <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=698"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_twilight.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ritual of Summoning</a> is the only way to summon players.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Your Succubus tanks almost every fight, Soul Link moves 30% of your damage taken to her | If your demon dies, you lose your tank and most of your damage at once |
| Your demon deals most of your damage while you cast DoTs and Searing Pain | The Brand runs out about every 6 seconds and Searing Pain is a 1.5 second cast |
| Soulstone, Healthstones, summons and a Succubus that seduces an extra enemy | One target at a time, your demon has no area damage |

This build uses 26 points: the 21 points of level 30 plus 5 from <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225474"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_marksmanship.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Talented</a> in the Legacy system. ## What the key talents do

| Talent | What it does in Forever |
|---|---|
| **Unholy Power** | 10% more damage for your demon at 5/5. Your demon deals most of your damage, so take all 5 |
| **Demonic Energies** | Your spells heal your demon, and Life Tap restores its mana |
| **Demonic Brand** | Searing Pain brands the target, your demon deals extra damage and threat on it. The most important talent in the build |
| **Demonic Knowledge** | Spell Power for you and your demon equal to your level at 3/3 |
| **Soul Link** | 30% of the damage you take goes to your demon, and you both deal 3% more damage |
| **Master Summoner** | A faster summon, and the way to Fel Domination for an almost instant new demon |
| **Demonic Sacrifice** | Only a requirement for Soul Link. Do not use it |

Improved Voidwalker makes the shield of Sacrifice stronger, a good pick for PvP. Improved Sayaad only raises the base damage of Lash of Pain, so the real gain is small.

## Rotation

**One enemy**
1. Send your demon in.
2. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5676"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_soulburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Searing Pain</a> to brand the target.
3. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofsargeras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bane of Agony</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=348"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_immolation.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Immolate</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=172"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_abominationexplosion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Corruption</a>.
4. Cast Searing Pain until the enemy dies. Every cast refreshes the Brand.
5. Low on mana? Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_burningspirit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Life Tap</a> if you have health to spare. Low on health? Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Drain Life</a>, and refresh the Brand with Searing Pain when it runs out.

**Two enemies.** Put Bane of Agony, Immolate and Corruption on the first and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5782"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_possession.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fear</a> it. Send your demon at the second, cast your DoTs on it and brand it with Searing Pain. Fear the first again when it comes back. When the second dies, send your demon to the first and brand it. With a focus target and a modifier key you can cast on the feared enemy without switching targets.

**Dungeons.** On bosses, cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=440892"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/warlock_curse_shadow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of the Elements</a> in a group with many casters, or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=704"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unholystrength.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Recklessness</a> for a group with mostly melee. Skip curses on trash. Send your demon in after the tank, cast your DoTs, and skip them on enemies that die within about 12 seconds. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=686"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_shadowbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Bolt</a> or Drain Life on the tank's target. In a group, Shadow Bolt is worth it, because your healer pays for part of your Life Taps.

## Demonic Brand and threat

At 3/3, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5676"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_soulburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Searing Pain</a> makes 50% less threat and brands the target for 10 seconds. Each rank adds 2 demon attacks to the Brand, so your demon's next 6 attacks deal extra Fire or Shadow damage, depending on the demon, and extra threat. The Brand ends after 10 seconds or after the last of those attacks, so in practice you recast about every 6 seconds.

Searing Pain has no travel time, and rank 2 arrives at level 26. Before you have Demonic Brand, do not cast it: it makes so much threat that it pulls enemies off your demon. From level 28, put a Firestone from <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gem_bloodstone_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Create Firestone</a> on your weapon for extra Fire damage and crit on Searing Pain.

## Demons

- **Succubus or Incubus** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=712"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonsuccubus.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Succubus</a>): the most damage of any demon, and with Demonic Brand it holds threat for almost the whole fight. Your default solo demon. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6358"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_mindsteal.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seduction</a> keeps a humanoid out of the fight in dungeons.
- **Voidwalker** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=697"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonvoidwalker.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Voidwalker</a>): your backup tank for enemies that hit too hard. Torment holds threat much better than in Classic, and rank 3 at level 30 fixes the threat problems of the late 20s.
- **Felhunter** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=691"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonfelhunter.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Felhunter</a>): almost the damage of the Succubus, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19505"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_purge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Devour Magic</a> removes magic effects. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19244"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_mindrot.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Spell Lock</a> only arrives at level 36.
- **Imp** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=688"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonimp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Imp</a>): little damage, but <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6307"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_bloodboil.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blood Pact</a> gives the group Stamina.

Bind Attack and Follow to keys you can press without thinking, so you can send your demon in or pull it back before it runs into another group. The new Move command lets you place your demon anywhere.

## Health and mana

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_burningspirit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Life Tap</a> now scales with Spirit. Rank 3 converts 140 health plus your Spirit into the same amount of mana, plus 10% per point in Improved Life Tap. With Demonic Energies, every Life Tap also restores your demon's mana. Rank 2 of Searing Pain costs 68 mana, so one Life Tap of 200 mana pays for about 3 casts. Two rules keep you from drinking:

- **A lot more health than mana?** Cast Life Tap.
- **Low on health?** Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Drain Life</a> until you have enough health to tap again.

## Stats and gear

**Spell Power, Shadow damage and Fire damage** first, then **Stamina and Spirit**, then **Intellect**. Spell Power also makes your demon stronger. Shadow damage helps Corruption and Bane of Agony, Fire damage helps Searing Pain. Stamina gives more health to tap, Spirit more mana per Life Tap. Hit and crit are rare on gear at level 30, and this build has no Suppression, so you miss enemies of your level 4% of the time.

- **Tailoring** has new soulbound Shadow pieces such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=253967"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_cloth_48.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Gown</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=253919"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gauntlets_27.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Gloves</a>, and Flame pieces that boost Searing Pain. Only the tailor can wear them.
- **Weapons.** Your weapon is a stat stick, its Spell Power matters more than its damage. The Warlock quest for the Orb of Soran'ruk lets you choose between <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15109"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_09.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Staff of Soran'ruk</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6898"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_orb_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Orb of Soran'ruk</a>. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2549"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_25.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Staff of the Shade</a> from Razorfen Kraul has more Shadow damage, but does nothing for Searing Pain.
- **Wands.** <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7001"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gravestone Scepter</a> from Blackfathom Deeps is the strongest wand before level 30. At 30 Searing Pain is your filler; use the wand to finish enemies below about 20%.
