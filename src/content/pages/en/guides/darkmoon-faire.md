The Darkmoon Faire is a travelling carnival that comes to Azeroth one week a month. In WoW Forever it works much like it did in Classic: a strong buff from Sayge, tickets for turn-ins, a 14-slot bag, three frog pets and four decks that turn into epic trinkets.

|---|---|
| **When** | once a month, for one week |
| **Where** | Elwynn Forest or Mulgore, in turn |
| **Buff** | Sayge, 2 hours, every 4 hours |

## When and where

The Faire opens on the Monday after the first Saturday of the month and stays a week. The in-game calendar shows the start and end at 04.00 realm time, which is 04.00 Belgian time on European realms. The workers set up the tents a few days before it opens.

| Month | Where | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| November 2026 | Elwynn Forest | 9 to 15 November |
| December 2026 | Mulgore | 7 to 13 December |
| January 2027 | Elwynn Forest | 4 to 10 January |
| February 2027 | Mulgore | 8 to 14 February |
| March 2027 | Elwynn Forest | 8 to 14 March |
| April 2027 | Mulgore | 5 to 11 April |

The DMF button in the bar at the top of the site counts down to the next Faire.

- **Elwynn Forest:** just south of Goldshire, at `/way 42 69`. Alliance players follow the road from Stormwind. The Horde takes the zeppelin to Stranglethorn Vale and rides north through Duskwood.
- **Mulgore:** at the foot of the lift to Thunder Bluff, at `/way 37 38`. The Horde takes the lift down. The Alliance takes the boat from Booty Bay to Ratchet and runs west through The Barrens.

There are no guards at the Faire, but the zone belongs to one faction. On the PvP ruleset, entering it flags you for PvP, so expect fights at the gates.

World buffs do not work in raids in WoW Forever, only in the open world and in dungeons. The text of the buffs now says so as well. The English texts of every answer are in [our post about the first Faire](/news/a-14-slot-bag-waits-at-the-darkmoon-faire/).

With the buff you also get a Darkmoon Faire Fortune. Open it for a numbered fortune. A few of them start the quest Your Fortune Awaits You..., which leads to a chest with a Mysterious Lockbox: food, materials or potions for your level, plus some experience and reputation.

## Tickets and turn-ins

Look for the blue question marks around the fairgrounds. Those are repeatable quests that turn items into Darkmoon Prize Tickets. Each step gives 250 reputation with the Darkmoon Faire and stops at a reputation cap, so you work up from the first step to the last. The last step stays open until the end of Exalted.

The three prize bundles hold random items. The Minor prize holds gear of level 21 to 25 or a bag with 10 slots. The Lesser prize holds gear of level 31 to 35, gems or a bag with 12 slots. The Greater prize holds gear of level 46 to 50, gems or recipes.

## Mini-games

Two games are just for fun and give no rewards.

- **Tonk Control Console:** stand at a console on the ground and steer a Steam Tonk for 5 minutes. Fight other tonks with its cannon, plus one extra ability per console, such as a flamethrower, a mine or a shield.
- **Blastenheimer 5000 Ultra Cannon:** shoots you across the zone with Magic Wings. Cancel the buff to drop, and try to land in the floating ring in the pond. The cannon stands next to the fairgrounds in Elwynn Forest, or on the edge of the Lower Rise in Thunder Bluff.
