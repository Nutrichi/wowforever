The Hall of Thanes is the dungeon under Old Ironforge, for levels 13 to 18, and five quests belong to it. Each one gives between 3,900 and 4,900 experience, according to Wowhead, so a run with all five is one of the biggest early boosts in WoW Forever. Warcraft Tavern mapped where every quest starts.

![A Dwarf with a glowing weapon in front of a closed vault door in the Hall of Thanes, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-vault-door.jpg)

## The five quests at a glance

| Quest | Where it starts |
|---|---|
| [Important Heirlooms](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96403) | Thom Filch, just outside the dungeon |
| [The Restless Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96394) | Afadra Dunwall, up the stairs from the entrance |
| [Old Ironforge Incursion](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96393) | Earthseer Farsen in Dun Morogh, after a short chain |
| [An Ancient Grudge](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96395) | A Ghostly Attendant inside the dungeon |
| [The Treaty of Understanding](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98423) | A tablet in a vault in the room of the final boss |

Start with the chain in Dun Morogh. It is the only quest that does not begin at the dungeon, and it asks for the final boss, so it pays to have it before the first pull.

## Before you go: the chain in Dun Morogh

Earthseer Farsen stands at /way 64.8 58.5 in Dun Morogh. He gives [Nip 'Em in the Bud](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96390): slay 10 Dark Iron Spies, found around /way 76.7 60.7. Wowhead adds that the road to Farsen starts with A Visitor to Dun Morogh, from Beldin Steelgrill at Steelgrill's Depot.

![Earthseer Farsen in the snow of Dun Morogh, with the quest window of Old Ironforge Incursion open](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-earthseer-farsen.jpg)

One of the spies drops a [Dark Iron Map](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274268). Use it from your bags to start a short quest that sends you back to Farsen. He then hands out Old Ironforge Incursion: enter the Hall of Thanes and bring back the head of Durgen Dirgehammer.

![The loot window of a Dark Iron Spy with the Dark Iron Map and its tooltip, This Item Begins a Quest](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-dark-iron-map.jpg)

## Important Heirlooms

Thom Filch waits by the bridge that leads into the Hall of Thanes. He asks for 8 [Dwarven Heirlooms](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274289).

![Thom Filch with the quest window of Important Heirlooms open, just outside the Hall of Thanes](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-thom-filch.jpg)

The heirlooms lie all over the dungeon, most of them on raised, candle-lit platforms in the corridors. There is no need to fight the group for them. The vaults in the last room, the Reliquary of Kings, hold enough for everyone, writes Warcraft Tavern.

![A raised stone platform with candles and Dwarven Heirlooms in a dark corridor of the Hall of Thanes](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-dwarven-heirlooms.jpg)

## The Restless Dead

Afadra Dunwall, a Lorekeeper of Ironforge, stands up a flight of stairs on the way to the entrance. She wants 15 Enraged Apparitions and 10 Tormented Souls dead. Those are the ghosts in the first chambers of the dungeon. The quest text also asks to put the spirit of Anvilmar to rest.

![Afadra Dunwall in a library full of books, up the stairs from the entrance to the Hall of Thanes](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-afadra-dunwall.jpg)

## An Ancient Grudge

A Ghostly Attendant waits in the second room of the dungeon, Anvilmar's Rest. She asks you to put the spirit of Faldrim Anvilmar, the first boss, to rest.

There is no hurry to hand it in. Whoever leaves without a Hearthstone passes her again on the way out, writes Warcraft Tavern.

![The Ghostly Attendant with the quest window of An Ancient Grudge open, between the ghosts of the first room](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-ghostly-attendant.jpg)

## The final boss and the vault

Durgen Dirgehammer waits in the Reliquary of Kings, the last room. Loot [Durgen Dirgehammer's Head](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274286) from him for Old Ironforge Incursion. How the fight works is in [the walkthrough of the four bosses](/news/the-hall-of-thanes-has-four-bosses-and-five-quests/).

Behind a vault door in the same room lies a tablet that starts The Treaty of Understanding. It is a quest for experience only: take the treaty to King Magni Bronzebeard in Ironforge. The head goes to him as well. After the boss a switch in the back vault opens the locked doors, so the way out is short, writes Wowhead.

![The tablet of The Treaty of Understanding in a vault of the Reliquary of Kings, with the quest window open](../../../../assets/posts/2026-09-26-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-treaty-of-understanding.jpg)

## Rewards

| Reward | Type | Quest |

| Ironforge Greathammer | Two-handed mace | Old Ironforge Incursion |
| Deepblaze | Wand | Old Ironforge Incursion |
| Dwarven Tome | Off-hand | Important Heirlooms |
| Catacomb Cloak | Cloak | An Ancient Grudge |

Wowhead lists no item for The Restless Dead and The Treaty of Understanding.

## Getting there

The door to Old Ironforge is on the left just inside the High Seat in Ironforge, writes Wowhead. A path leads down to the portal. The dungeon is the Alliance counterpart of Ragefire Chasm. The Horde can go in, but faces a long trip through the Wetlands and Loch Modan and past the guards of Ironforge.

Who may take the quests is not clear. The news article of Wowhead calls all five Alliance only. The dungeon guide on the same site makes an exception for Important Heirlooms and An Ancient Grudge.
