The Paladin levels slowly but safely. You heal yourself, you have a shield against almost every mistake, and in Forever you press more than auto attack: Judgement and Holy Strike go out on cooldown. You choose between Retribution, Protection and a Holy build that deals damage with Holy Shock. This guide covers the builds, the rotation, the key levels, the class quests and the seals, auras and blessings.

## Which spec to level with

You can level with all three. **Retribution** is the classic choice: a large two-handed weapon, Seal of Command and big hits. **Protection** is just as fast if you like to gather four monsters and burn them down with Consecration while you barely lose health. **Holy** does little damage until it has Holy Shock, but then plays much like Protection, with more spell power and more healing.

For the first 20 to 30 levels, spend your points in Retribution, even if you plan to tank dungeons with a shield later. Most of your damage comes from the same three buttons in every spec: a seal, Judgement and Holy Strike.
## Levels 1 to 10

You start with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20154"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_thunderbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Righteousness</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=635"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_holybolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Light</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=465"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_devotionaura.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Devotion Aura</a>. Judgement and Blessing of Might follow at 4, Divine Protection and Holy Strike at 6, Hammer of Justice at 8. Until level 6 you mostly hit with your weapon.

1. Keep a blessing and an aura on yourself.
2. Cast Seal of Righteousness before you pull.
3. Cast Judgement as you run to the monster.
4. Cast Holy Strike.
5. Cast Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown and renew your seal when it runs out.

Use Holy Light to heal yourself when needed. If you need a heal in combat, cast Divine Protection on yourself first: then nothing can interrupt the cast.

Stats at these levels: Strength and attack power first, then Stamina, then spell power, then armor.

## Gear

**Weapons** are the most important upgrade, whether you hit with them or carry them for their stats.

- **Retribution** wants slow, heavy weapons. They make Holy Strike and Seal of Command stronger.
- **Protection** wants faster weapons, for more hits from Seal of Fury. A weapon with a lot of spell power often has less damage per second, and that trade is usually worth it.
- **Holy** wants a one-handed weapon with as much spell power as possible. Seal of Righteousness deals more damage per swing with slower weapons, so you do not need the fastest one.

**Librams** go in the relic slot and give class-specific bonuses. The first one is <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249397"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_book_13.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tenets of the Silver Hand</a>, made by enchanters and soulbound. It is not worth the effort while leveling, unless you level Enchanting yourself.

## Professions

Level your professions alongside your Paladin, so you gather and craft at the right level. Camps give buffs to every profession.

- **Cooking** is a must: buff food gives extra experience, and you need it for a campfire.
- **Blacksmithing** makes your own gear, including soulbound pieces with stats you rarely find elsewhere. If you want to build around spell power, Blacksmithing gives you much more of it.
- **Mining** goes with Blacksmithing, unless you would rather buy all your ore.

## Consumables

- **Food and water.** Stay Well Fed for the extra experience; any buff food is better than none. Carry water of your level, because you will stop for mana. New water comes about every 10 levels.
- **Potions.** Healing potions for emergencies, now also from First Aid. Mana potions help when you pull one group after another.
- **Elixirs** with the right stats are expensive to keep up, but worth it for a difficult quest or monster.
- **Reagents.** You need none while leveling. Greater Blessings from level 52 cost <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=21177"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_symbolofkings_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Symbol of Kings</a>, and Divine Intervention costs <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=17033"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_stone_weightstone_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Symbol of Divinity</a>.

## Seals

A seal is a buff on yourself for 30 seconds. Judgement unleashes it on the monster for an extra effect, without using up the seal. Only one seal can be active at a time.

- **Seal of Righteousness** adds Holy damage to your swings, more per swing with a slower weapon. Its Judgement deals a burst of Holy damage.
- **Seal of the Crusader** gives attack power and makes you attack 40% faster, but each hit deals less. Its Judgement makes the monster take more Holy damage, which helps a lot, because most of your damage is Holy.
- **Seal of Fury** is the tank seal. It adds Holy damage to your swings and, while you carry a shield, gives you an absorb shield with every hit. Its Judgement is your taunt.
- **Seal of Command** is a Retribution talent. Your swings sometimes hit again for Holy damage. Its Judgement deals almost double damage to a stunned or incapacitated target, so combine it with Hammer of Justice or Repentance.
- **Seal of Justice** gives your swings a chance to stun. Its Judgement keeps a monster from fleeing at low health.
- **Seal of Light** heals you a little on your swings. Its Judgement lets everyone who hits the monster in melee heal a little.
- **Seal of Wisdom** restores some mana on your swings. Its Judgement lets attacks and spells against the monster restore mana.

Since 11 November Judgement counts as a ranged attack. It no longer triggers procs that work on melee hits, and it uses your ranged hit chance, [more in our post](/news/judgement-now-counts-as-a-ranged-attack/).

## Auras

An aura affects everyone in your group around you. You can have one active.

- **Devotion Aura** gives extra armor, good against monsters that hit in melee.
- **Retribution Aura** deals Holy damage to every monster that hits you. Your aura for leveling.
- **Concentration Aura** makes it less likely that damage pushes back your casts. Mostly for PvP and some bosses.
- **Resistance auras** for Shadow, Frost and Fire, for monsters or classes that deal that kind of damage.

## Blessings

A blessing lasts one hour. Each Paladin can put one blessing on each player.

- **Blessing of Might** gives attack power.
- **Blessing of Wisdom** gives mana regeneration.
- **Blessing of Kings** raises all stats by a percentage.
- **Blessing of Light** raises the healing you get from Paladin heals.

Then there are short blessings for a specific moment. They count as your one blessing on that player, so give them their normal blessing back afterwards.

- **Blessing of Protection** makes the target immune to physical attacks, but they cannot attack with a weapon either.
- **Blessing of Freedom** makes the target immune to slows and roots.
- **Blessing of Sacrifice** moves part of the damage of each hit to you.

From level 52 you learn Greater Blessings, which buff every player of one class in your group at once. While leveling you rarely need them.

**Salvation** is no longer a blessing in Forever. It lowers the threat of a group member by 30% for one hour, which helps everyone in a dungeon except the tank. Cast it on your tank by mistake? Remove it with Revoke.

## Healing and defense

Even if you are not a healer, you heal yourself often.

- **Holy Light** is slow and heals a lot.
- **Flash of Light** is fast and heals less.
- **Lay on Hands** heals as much as your maximum health, but costs all your mana. It has a 20 minute cooldown.
- **Holy Shock**, a Holy talent, heals an ally or damages a monster, instantly.

Hammer of Justice stuns for 3 seconds, one of the strongest stuns in Forever. Turn Undead is a fear for undead only. Divine Shield, Divine Protection and Blessing of Protection are your immunities: catch your breath, heal and fight on. They share Forbearance, so you cannot chain them. Divine Shield also halves your damage while it lasts. Purify removes poison and disease, and Cleanse from level 42 also removes magic.
