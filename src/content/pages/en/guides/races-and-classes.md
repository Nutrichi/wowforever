World of Warcraft: Forever has ten races, five per faction, and the nine classes of Classic. Blizzard opened six combinations that Classic never allowed and added the Skyborne, a new race that picks its faction at character creation. For the first time, every class can be played on both sides.

| | |
|---|---|
| Races | 10, five for the Horde and five for the Alliance |
| Classes | 9, all of them on both factions |
| Combinations | 56, 28 per faction |
| New compared to Classic | 16: six for the old races, ten for the Skyborne |
| Skyborne | Skyborne Heroic Pack or a higher edition |

Warrior is the only class every race can play. Paladin has the fewest options, with three races in total.

## What is new

Six combinations of the old races are new: Orc Mage, Troll Warlock and Undead Paladin for the Horde, Human Hunter, Gnome Priest and Dwarf Shaman for the Alliance. The [announcement](/news/orc-mages-and-undead-paladins-arrive/) of 23 September covers them.

In Classic the Paladin belonged to the Alliance and the Shaman to the Horde. The Undead Paladin and the Dwarf Shaman end that split, so a player no longer has to change faction for a class.

## The Skyborne

The Skyborne are a new race of elves. At character creation, a Skyborne chooses a faction, and that choice decides one class. Every Skyborne can be a Warrior, Hunter, Rogue or Druid. The High Order Skyborne of the Alliance add Mage, the Windshaper Skyborne of the Horde add Shaman.

The race requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack or a higher edition; the other eight races are part of the subscription. More on the editions and the starting zone, Zephras Isle, is on the [WoW Forever page](/forever/).

## Racials

Blizzard changed several racials to fit the wider class list. The racials of every race, with what is new or changed, are on [Classes](/classes/).

Whether the table changes again before the launch on 4 November 2026, Blizzard has not said.
