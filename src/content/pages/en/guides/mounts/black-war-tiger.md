## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=12783">Lieutenant Karter</a> sells it in Alterac Valley, Arathi Basin and Warsong Gulch for 100 gold. The vendor is friendly to the Alliance.

It needs the PvP rank Commander / Lieutenant General (Rank 11).

Riding it needs Tiger Riding.

## What changed since Classic

The item now teaches the mount: it becomes a spell, no longer takes a spot in your bags and counts for every character on the account, according to the tooltip on the beta.
