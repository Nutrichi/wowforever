Warhorse is a class mount for Paladins in World of Warcraft: Forever.

## In short

| | |
|---|---|
| Source | Class mount |
| Class | Paladin |
| New in Forever | no, from Classic |

## How to get it

At level 40, with a free class quest. Alliance Paladins get The Tome of Nobility from Duthorian Rall in Stormwind. Horde Paladins start Report to Danitha in the Undercity and learn the horse with A Paladin's Steed at Bandarion Keep. You do not need to buy Apprentice Riding for 100 gold.

Every mount of WoW Forever, with where it comes from, is in the [mount guide](/guides/mounts/).
