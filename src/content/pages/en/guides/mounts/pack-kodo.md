## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256400">Okamache</a> sells it in The Barrens for 6000 Merchant's Favor.

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256736">Huey Sunnydale</a> sells it in Redridge Mountains for 6000 Merchant's Favor.

Buying or using it needs Exalted with Durotar Supply and Logistics.

Buying or using it needs Exalted with Azeroth Commerce Authority.

Riding it needs Journeyman Riding.
