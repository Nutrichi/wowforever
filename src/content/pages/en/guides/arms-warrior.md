The Arms Warrior in WoW Forever swings one big, slow weapon. Rend keeps bleeding, Overpower lights up again and again, and every hit takes a visible chunk off the enemy. Many of its talents lean towards PvP, which makes Arms the natural pick for battlegrounds. This guide covers the level 30 build, rotation, Rend and Overpower, Rage, stats, weapons and races.

## What changes for Arms

- **Bloodthrill is new.** Main hand attacks on a target with your Rend have a 20% chance to make Overpower usable, at 5/5.
- **Improved Rend** gives Rend 35% more bleed damage at 3/3.
- **Improved Slam does much more.** Shorter cast and global cooldown, no interrupted swing and a 3 seconds shorter cooldown.
- **Spearing Strike is new.** 40% weapon damage, and 80% extra against Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets, which it pulls off their mount.
- **Impale** now gives your abilities a 20% bigger critical strike damage bonus at 2/2.
- **Weaponmaster is new.** A bonus per weapon type: critical strike chance with axes and polearms, armor penetration with maces and staves, extra attacks with swords.

## Rotation

1. Spend extra Rage on Heroic Strike, or Cleave against several enemies, so it is never wasted.
2. Keep Battle Shout up.
3. Stack Sunder Armor.
4. Overpower whenever it lights up.
5. Put Rend on the target and keep it there.
6. Mortal Strike, once you have it.
7. Spearing Strike.
8. Slam.
9. Hamstring when nothing else is ready and you have Rage to spare.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Retaliation** and **Shield Wall** when you are the target.
- **Berserker Rage** to ignore Fear and Incapacitate effects.
- **Recklessness** is the strongest damage cooldown in the game.
- **Bloodrage** freely, for extra Rage.

## Movement

Stay on the boss. Out of combat, always open with Charge for Rage and an instant start. In combat you have Intercept, but only in Berserker Stance, so switching costs the Rage you had.

## Rend, Overpower and Rage

**Rend** must always be ticking on your target, mainly for the Bloodthrill procs. Rend on several targets does no harm, but does not give you more procs.

**Overpower** only works when it lights up, even when it is off cooldown. Watch for it and press it every time.

**Rage.** Plan ahead and keep enough for your important attacks. If Heroic Strike or Slam leaves Mortal Strike or Overpower waiting for a second or two, that was a mistake. Keep your Rage moving: never full, never empty.

## Stats and gear

The exact order at level 60 follows after the launch. These stats help an Arms Warrior:

- **Strength and attack power.** Your main stat: 1 Strength gives 2 attack power, so Strength is worth more per point.
- **Hit chance.** A miss deals nothing and gives no Rage.
- **Critical strike chance.** More damage and more Rage. Agility adds some critical strike chance too.

**Armor.** Plate is ideal, but Mail or Leather with the right stats can be better. Forever has no penalty for a lighter armor type, you just have less Armor. Blacksmiths make good leveling gear, and dungeons have plenty more.

**Weapons.** Look for slow, hard-hitting two-handed weapons. Most of your damage comes from instant attacks such as Mortal Strike, Overpower and Slam, which scale with weapon damage. A ranged weapon only counts for its stats.
