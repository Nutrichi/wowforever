Dungeon Set 2, also called Tier 0.5, is a long quest chain that upgrades your Dungeon Set 1 piece by piece. In WoW Forever every spec gets its own set, and most pieces are pre-raid best in slot. This guide walks through the chain in four parts.

| | |
|---|---|
| Level | 58 |
| Start | <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16013">Deliana</a> in Ironforge or <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16012">Mokvar</a> in Orgrimmar |
| Reputation | Honored with the Argent Dawn, from Part 3 |
| Gold | 220 in fees, around 600 to 700 with materials |
| Time | 12 to 20 hours |
| Reward | 8 pieces, set bonus at 3 and 6, a Legacy Point |

## Before you start

**You do not need the full Dungeon Set 1.** Each part asks only for the pieces it upgrades, so you can start with just the wrists. The quests take those pieces, so put any duplicates in your bank before every turn-in.

**What is new in Forever.** The summon in Silithus is gone. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=270451">Executor Asharell</a> in the Burning Steppes replaces The Duke of Cynders, so you no longer need Twilight Cultist gear or Cenarion Circle reputation. Each class has its own version of the quests with Deliana and Mokvar. The name is the same for every class.

**Buy the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=21939"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fel Elemental Rod</a> early.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16015">Vi'el</a> sells it for 40 gold, but players report the price goes up to 50 once you have the quest.

## Part 1: Wrists

| | |
|---|---|
| Reward | Tier 0.5 wrists |
| You need | Dungeon Set 1 wrists, 20 gold |
| Group | Solo |

1. **Get the quest.** Talk to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16013">Deliana</a> in Ironforge, in the throne room (/way 44.0 51.0), or <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16012">Mokvar</a> in Orgrimmar, in Thrall's room (/way 38.0 36.0). The quest is An Earnest Proposition.
2. **Collect 15 samples.** Alliance: 15 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=21928"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Winterspring Blood Sample</a> from frostsabers and bears in Winterspring. Horde: 15 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=22381"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_slime_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Silithus Venom Sample</a> from spiders and scorpions in Silithus.
3. **Hand in.** Bring the samples, your Dungeon Set 1 wrists and 20 gold back for your Tier 0.5 wrists.

## Part 3: Boots, legs and shoulders

| | |
|---|---|
| Reward | Tier 0.5 boots, legs and shoulders, 6-piece bonus |
| You need | Honored with the Argent Dawn, the Dungeon Set 1 boots, legs and shoulders, the materials of Part 3 |
| Group | Stratholme, Dire Maul and Blackrock Depths |

1. **Find Anthion.** Use the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=22115"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_20.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Extra-Dimensional Ghost Revealer</a> at the main gate of Stratholme in the Eastern Plaguelands (/way 31.0 12.8) to see <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16016">Anthion Harmon</a>. He wanders on the bridge.
2. **Save Ysida.** For <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=8945">Dead Man's Plea</a> beat <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=10440">Baron Rivendare</a> in [Stratholme: Undead](/guides/dungeons/stratholme-undead/). Once you open the second service gate, you have 45 minutes to start the fight. Dying removes the timer debuff, but the run still counts.
3. **Bring the locket.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16031">Ysida Harmon</a> gives you <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=8946">Proof of Life</a>: return it to Anthion.
4. **Pay for the banner.** For <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=8947">Anthion's Strange Request</a> bring Anthion 3 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=11371"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ingot_mithril.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dark Iron Bar</a>, 20 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=12810"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_rune_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Enchanted Leather</a>, 3 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=14342"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_fabric_moonrag_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mooncloth</a> and 4 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15407"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_pelt_bear_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cured Rugged Hide</a>.
5. **Find Falrin.** <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=8948">Anthion's Old Friend</a> sends you to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16032">Falrin Treeshaper</a> in the library of [Dire Maul North](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-north/). Someone in the group needs the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18249"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Crescent Key</a> from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14354">Pusillin</a> in Dire Maul East.
6. **Collect 25 warbeads.** For <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=8949">Falrin's Vendetta</a> take 25 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=21982"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_necklace_19.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ogre Warbeads</a> from ogres in Dire Maul or Blackrock Spire.
7. **Bring the essence.** For <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=8950">The Instigator's Enchantment</a> bring Falrin 1 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=22224"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_poison_mindnumbing.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Jeering Spectre's Essence</a> from the ghosts in [Dire Maul West](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-west/), plus 8 <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=14344"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_enchant_shardbrilliantlarge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Large Brilliant Shard</a> and 4 <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=20520"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_sealofkings.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dark Rune</a>.
8. **Beat Theldren.** For <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=9015">The Challenge</a> go to the Ring of the Law in [Blackrock Depths](/guides/dungeons/blackrock-depths/). Drop the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=21986"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_brd_banner.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Banner of Provocation</a> before <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=10096">High Justice Grimstone</a> appears, then step onto the grate: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=16059">Theldren</a> and his team come in. Loot the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=22047"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_necklace_28.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Top Piece of Lord Valthalak's Amulet</a> and take it to Anthion.
9. **Hand in.** At Deliana or Mokvar, turn in your Dungeon Set 1 boots, legs and shoulders.

If Grimstone starts the normal arena event first, Theldren does not come. The group then has to reset the dungeon.
