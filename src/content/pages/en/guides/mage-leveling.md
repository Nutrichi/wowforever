The Mage in WoW Forever levels fast and alone, with endless food and water and a lot of control. Frost is the quickest spec by far, thanks to the new <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> and Fingers of Frost. This guide covers the specs, the rotation, the key levels, class quests and the new scrolls.

| | |
|---|---|
| Best spec for leveling | Frost |
| Key talents | Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost, Frostbite, Shatter, Ice Shards |
| Strength | Damage, crowd control, free food and water |
| Weakness | Mana, threat in dungeons, low health |
| Talent order per level | [Mage level build](/leveling/mage/) |
| Builds to level 60 | After the launch on 4 November |

## Which spec to level with

**Frost** has always been the safe choice: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> slows every target, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11185"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_icestorm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Blizzard</a> freeze or slow whole packs. In Forever it is also the fastest. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> deals 200% more damage to Frozen targets, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> treats your next two spells as if the target were Frozen. Together with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11170"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shatter</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11207"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_iceshard.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Shards</a> that gives enormous crits. The beta build of 15 October lowered that bonus from 300% to 200%.

**Fire** spends the least mana, but only shines with a lot of critical strike chance. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400624"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_firebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heating Up</a> makes <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pyroblast</a> cast faster after crits, so it relies on luck at low levels.

**Arcane** can be good, but depends on <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12536"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_manaburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Clearcasting</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400588"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_missilebarrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Missile Barrage</a> procs, careful mana use and gear. Fire and Arcane do better in dungeons than in the open world.

## The Frost build

The [Mage level build](/leveling/mage/) gives the talent for every level from 10 to 35. In short: first <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11070"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Frostbolt</a> and Elemental Precision, then <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> at 15 to 17, Ice Shards, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> at 20, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11170"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostshock.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shatter</a> at 25 to 27, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11958"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frost.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Block</a> at 28 and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> at 30 and 31.

| Talent | What it does in Forever |
|---|---|
| **Ice Lance** | Instant, 45 mana, 200% more damage to Frozen targets |
| **Fingers of Frost** | Chill effects have a 15% chance to treat your next 2 spells as if the target were Frozen |
| **Frostbite** | Chill effects have a 15% chance to freeze the target for 5 seconds |
| **Shatter** | 50% more critical strike chance against Frozen targets |
| **Ice Shards** | Doubles the critical strike bonus of your Frost spells |
| **Frost Channeling** | 15% cheaper Frost spells and 30% less threat |

With points in <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225474"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_marksmanship.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Talented</a> from the Legacy system, you reach each talent up to five levels sooner.

## Rotation

**Levels 1 to 10.** You start with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=133"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flamebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fireball</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=168"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Armor</a>. Use Fireball until level 4, then <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> as your main spell, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> on cooldown and your wand to finish targets. Conjure food and water between pulls.

**Frost**
- Keep <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_magicalsentry.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Intellect</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=168"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Armor</a> up.
- Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> as your main spell.
- Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a> when <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> procs, or when <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11071"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbite</a> freezes the target.
- When a target gets close: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a> and Ice Lance until the freeze breaks.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_glacier.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cone of Cold</a> if Frost Nova is on cooldown, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> while you reposition.
- Against a pack: Frost Nova, then <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_selfdestruct.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Flamestrike</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_wispsplode.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Explosion</a>. In a group, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_icestorm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blizzard</a> from a safe distance.

**Fire**
- Pre-cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pyroblast</a> on a boss, open with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=133"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_flamebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fireball</a> on trash.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2948"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_soulburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scorch</a> until <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11095"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_windsofwoe.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Scorch</a> has 5 stacks.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> on cooldown.
- Pyroblast at 3 stacks of <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400624"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_firebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heating Up</a>, otherwise Fireball.

**Arcane**
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400574"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Blast</a> to build stacks and trigger <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400588"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_missilebarrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Missile Barrage</a>.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5143"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_starfall.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Missiles</a> with 1 or 2 stacks of Arcane Blast.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2136"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fire Blast</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_wispsplode.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Explosion</a> to spend the stacks if Missile Barrage does not proc.

In every spec: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blink.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blink</a> to get away, Frost Blast from level 24 to slow, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=118"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_polymorph.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Polymorph</a> on a dangerous extra target.

The beta build of 15 October reworks Arcane Blast, which now adds spell damage per stack for 15 seconds, and moves Missile Barrage to the first row. The Arcane rotation above follows the earlier build. All changes are in [this post](/news/battle-shout-and-totems-now-buff-the-whole-raid/).

## Consumables and gear

- **Food.** Conjured food keeps you going, but buff food from Cooking now also gives extra experience. Stay Well Fed.
- **Elixirs and oils.** <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3383"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_potion_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Elixir of Wisdom</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=250342"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_potion_107.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lesser Arcane Elixir</a> for Intellect and spell power, <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=287979"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_potion_103.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lesser Wizard Oil</a> on your weapon, <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=20745"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_potion_98.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Minor Mana Oil</a> for Arcane.

## Mana and threat

Arcane is the hardest on your mana, but gets the most tools: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12536"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_manaburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Clearcasting</a> procs and a stronger <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18462"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_siphonmana.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Meditation</a>, which lets half of your regeneration continue while casting. Fire gets mana back from crits with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=29074"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_masterofelements.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Master of Elements</a>. Frost gets expensive without <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11160"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_stun.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Channeling</a>. Always keep enough mana for a <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a> and a <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blink.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blink</a>, and use <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12051"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_purge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Evocation</a>, mana gems and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6117"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_magearmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mage Armor</a> from level 34.

In dungeons your damage pulls threat fast. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11160"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_stun.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Channeling</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11210"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_dispelmagic.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Subtlety</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11083"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Burning Soul</a> lower the threat of their school. Hold back your area spells when the tank does not have the pack yet, and use <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blink.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blink</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_glacier.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cone of Cold</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostnova.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Nova</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11958"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frost.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Block</a> when a boss turns to you. Remove curses with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=475"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_removecurse.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Remove Lesser Curse</a>, and hand out food and water: a Mage always finds a group.
