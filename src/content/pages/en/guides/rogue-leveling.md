The Rogue was never the easiest class to level, but WoW Forever helps. Poisons can crit, single target damage is higher and each spec has its own identity. At level 30 you choose between Assassination, Combat and two kinds of Subtlety. This guide covers the four builds, the rotation, the key levels, the poison quests, weapons and professions.

## Which spec to level with

**Combat** is the most forgiving start. Deflection and Riposte keep you alive, and you walk from monster to monster without pauses. Combat can also use every weapon type, thanks to Hack and Slash. **Subtlety with Ambush** opens from Stealth with a huge critical strike and kills fast, but is weaker in dungeons. **Subtlety with bleeds** builds on Garrote, Hemorrhage and Rupture. **Assassination** only gets strong once Mutilate arrives at level 40, so it is not a starter build.

The builds below were made on the beta, which stopped at level 30. Every build has a version with five extra points for Talented from the Legacy system. The talent calculator shows those versions as level 35, because it counts every point as a level. Builds for level 60 follow once we can test them in the live game.

## Levels 1 to 10

You have no talents yet, only <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1752"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_ritualofsacrifice.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sinister Strike</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2098"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_eviscerate.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Eviscerate</a>. Stealth, Backstab and Evasion follow quickly.

1. Build Combo Points with Sinister Strike.
2. Spend them with Eviscerate.
3. With a dagger: open from Stealth with Backstab for a fast kill.
4. Use Evasion against a dangerous monster.
5. Use Gouge to interrupt a cast or to get away.

An opener from Stealth on every monster slows you down at these levels. Save it for strong monsters and simply walk up to the rest.

Stats at these levels: Agility, then Attack Power, Strength, Stamina and armor.

## Professions

A few professions make a big difference in how fast you kill and how well you survive. Level them alongside your Rogue, so you do not miss bonuses. Camps give buffs to every profession.

- **Skinning** pays well, because you kill many beasts.
- **Leatherworking** is the natural pair with Skinning and one of the best professions for a Rogue, with many new and reworked recipes and strong gear later on.
- **Herbalism** delivers everything for Alchemy, but single herbs sell for little.
- **Alchemy** gives potions and elixirs, and becomes more profitable later on.
- **Cooking** is a must: buff food gives extra experience, and you need it for a campfire.
- **Fishing** goes well with Cooking and catches crates and chests.
- **First Aid** saves you in a tight spot and now also makes healing potions.

## Energy and Combo Points

Energy is your resource for almost every ability, Stealth included. Abilities come in three kinds: utility and crowd control, abilities that build Combo Points, and finishers that spend them. Combo Points stay on one target. If you build them on another monster, the old ones are gone. So spend them before you switch targets. Kick and Blind do not touch them.

## Stealth and openers

Stealth makes you invisible at the cost of movement speed. Your action bar switches to the openers. Cheap Shot, Garrote and Ambush give Combo Points; Pick Pocket, Distract and Sap do not. Sap no longer breaks Stealth in Forever, so you can take an extra monster out of a pull. More about that is in the [class deep dive](/news/rogues-get-mutilate-and-axes-warlocks-get-banes/).

## Crowd control and cooldowns

Crowd control makes up for the little utility. Cheap Shot and Kidney Shot stun, Blind disorients, Sap and Gouge incapacitate. Use a stun while you keep attacking. Blind, Sap and Gouge last longer but break on damage, so use them to take a monster out of the fight.

Each spec has its own cooldowns. Assassination has Cold Blood for a certain critical strike. Combat has Blade Flurry for speed and a second target, and Adrenaline Rush for Energy. Subtlety has Preparation, which finishes the cooldown of your other abilities, and Premeditation for Combo Points before the fight. Every Rogue has Evasion, Sprint and Vanish to survive or to get away. Distract and Pick Lock are your utility; with Pick Lock you open shortcuts in some dungeons.
