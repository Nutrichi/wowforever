The Destruction Warlock in WoW Forever sets things on fire and blows them up. You spread Immolate, detonate it with Conflagrate and fill the gaps with Shadow Bolt. Conflagrate now arrives at level 25 and Hellfire can crit. This guide covers the build, rotation, Hellfire, mana and stats.

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | Ranged burst and area damage, strongest in dungeons |
| Build at level 30 | 21 points in Destruction and 5 in Affliction, with the 5 extra points from Talented |
| Key talents | Bane, Aftermath, Shadowburn, Conflagrate, Agonizing Flames, Suppression |
| Strength | Burst with Conflagrate and Shadowburn, Hellfire against packs |
| Weakness | Mana, threat, standing still |

## What changes for Destruction

- **Conflagrate at level 25.** It sits higher in the tree, so you reach it 15 levels sooner than in Classic.
- **Hellfire can crit.** It arrives at level 30 and makes Destruction one of the strongest area specs of the bracket.
- **Agonizing Flames does more.** 10% more damage for all your Destruction spells at 3/3, and 10% more crit chance for Searing Pain.
- **Bane of Havoc is new.** It copies 15% of the damage you deal to other enemies onto its target, every Hellfire tick included.
- **Aftermath does more.** 50% more initial damage for Immolate at 5/5, and Conflagrate slows the target by 50% for 5 seconds.
- **Banes are not Curses.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofsargeras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bane of Agony</a> is now a Bane, so you can add <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=440892"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/warlock_curse_shadow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of the Elements</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6205"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofmannoroth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Weakness</a> on the same target.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Conflagrate and Shadowburn burst down priority targets | Shadow Bolt costs 160 mana, Immolate 155 and a full Hellfire 645 |
| Immolate on a pack and Hellfire for strong area damage | A lot of instant threat from Immolate, Conflagrate and Hellfire |
| Soulstone, Healthstones and summons for the group | You stand still for most of each fight |

## The build at level 30

This build uses 26 points: the 21 points of level 30 plus 5 from <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225474"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_marksmanship.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Talented</a> in the Legacy system. Take Bane, Aftermath, Shadowburn, Conflagrate and Agonizing Flames, then 3 points in Cataclysm and 2 in <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17917"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_corpseexplode.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Destructive Reach</a>, for 20% more range, to reach 20 points in Destruction. Five points in <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18174"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unsummonbuilding.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Suppression</a> give hit and less threat, and the last point goes to Bane of Havoc for dungeons. Mostly solo? Take 3 points in Suppression, 2 in Improved Life Tap and 1 in Improved Corruption, and skip Bane of Havoc. [Open the solo version](/talents/warlock/?t=231--20503501031).

After level 30, Fire and Brimstone, Shadow and Flame and Incinerate follow lower in the tree.

## What the key talents do

| Talent | What it does in Forever |
|---|---|
| **Bane** | Half a second off the cast of Shadow Bolt and Immolate at 5/5 |
| **Aftermath** | 50% more initial damage for Immolate, and Conflagrate slows the target |
| **Shadowburn** | An instant finisher that costs a Soul Shard and gives it back if the target dies within 8 seconds. The most valuable single point in the build |
| **Conflagrate** | Consumes your Immolate for instant Fire damage, on a 10 second cooldown |
| **Agonizing Flames** | 10% more damage for all Destruction spells at 3/3 |
| **Suppression** | 1% hit per point and less threat |
| **Cataclysm** | 10% lower mana cost for Destruction spells at 3/3 |
| **Bane of Havoc** | 15% of your damage on other enemies goes to its target. Worth it on packs of 4 or more with Hellfire |

Ruin and Improved Shadow Bolt need more crit than you have at level 30. Fire and Brimstone adds only about 2 damage per second at this level, less than 3 points of Suppression.

## Rotation

**One enemy**
1. Send your demon in.
2. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=172"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_abominationexplosion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Corruption</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=348"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_immolation.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Immolate</a>. Add <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofsargeras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bane of Agony</a> only on enemies that live longer than 20 seconds, because it deals most of its damage at the end.
3. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=686"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_shadowbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Bolt</a> as your filler while you have the mana.
4. Low on mana? Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_burningspirit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Life Tap</a> if you have health to spare. Without a healer, or low on both, cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Drain Life</a> instead. Shoot your wand if you cannot afford that either.
5. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17962"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Conflagrate</a> just before Immolate runs out, then recast Immolate if the enemy lives long enough.
6. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17877"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_scourgebuild.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadowburn</a> as the killing blow.

Shadow Bolt is not very mana efficient. Solo, the drinking time after a pull can make Drain Life the better filler. If an enemy runs at you from range, open with Immolate and cast Bane of Agony once it reaches you, so your casts are not pushed back.

**Two enemies.** Put Immolate, Bane of Agony and Corruption on the second enemy and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5782"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_possession.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fear</a> it. With Bane of Havoc, use that instead of Bane of Agony. Send your demon to the first, kill it with Shadow Bolt, Conflagrate and Shadowburn, and Fear the second again when it comes back.

**Dungeons.** Give the tank about 5 seconds before your area damage. On trash, Immolate 2 enemies or more and skip Bane of Agony, Corruption and curses. On 3 or more grouped enemies, run in and channel <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1949"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_incinerate.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hellfire</a>. With Bane of Havoc and 4 or more enemies, put it first on the enemy your group kills last. On bosses, cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=440892"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/warlock_curse_shadow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of the Elements</a> in a group with many casters or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=704"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unholystrength.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Recklessness</a> for mostly melee, then your DoTs and Shadow Bolt.

## Immolate, Conflagrate and Shadowburn

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=348"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_immolation.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Immolate</a> is your most important spell. Rank 4 at level 30 costs 155 mana and deals about 60 Fire damage up front, 50% more with Aftermath, and about 95 more over 15 seconds.

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17962"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_fireball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Conflagrate</a> consumes Immolate, so do not cast it as soon as it is ready. Let Immolate tick and cast Conflagrate before the last tick, unless the enemy is about to die anyway. Only from level 40, with Shadow and Flame at 5/5, does Conflagrate leave Immolate on the target.

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17877"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_scourgebuild.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadowburn</a> deals about 150 damage with level 30 gear and costs 130 mana and a Soul Shard, which you get back if the target dies within 8 seconds. Cast it as the killing blow, or while you move. From level 28, put a Firestone from <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gem_bloodstone_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Create Firestone</a> on your weapon for extra Fire damage and crit on Immolate, Conflagrate and Hellfire.

## Hellfire and threat

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1949"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_incinerate.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hellfire</a> is a 15 second channel that deals about 83 Fire damage per second to every enemy around you, and the same to you. A full channel costs 645 mana and about 1,250 of your own health. It is your most mana efficient spell against packs, but your healer pays with you, so tell them before the pull.

Destruction makes a lot of instant threat. Put Immolate only on enemies the tank has already hit, expect Hellfire to be risky, and keep a Healthstone ready before every pull.

## Health and mana

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_burningspirit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Life Tap</a> now scales with Spirit. Rank 3 converts 140 health plus your Spirit into mana, plus 10% per point in Improved Life Tap. A full Hellfire costs more than 3 Life Taps, and Cataclysm cuts the cost of your Destruction spells by 10%. Three rules keep you casting:

- **A lot more health than mana?** Cast Life Tap.
- **Low on both?** Shoot your wand, or cast Drain Life and tap the drained health back into mana.
- **After a pull**, tap freely if your healer heals you. If not, tap down to half health, then eat and drink.

## Stats and gear

**Spell Power, Fire damage and Shadow damage** first, then **Intellect and Spirit**, then **Stamina**. Fire damage helps Immolate, Conflagrate and Hellfire, Shadow damage helps Shadow Bolt and Shadowburn. Intellect matters more than for the other specs, because Destruction spends the most mana. With Suppression at 5/5 you no longer miss enemies of your level. Crit helps Destruction more than the other specs, but it is rare on gear at level 30.

- **Tailoring** has new soulbound Flame pieces that boost Immolate, Conflagrate and Hellfire, and Shadow pieces such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=253967"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_cloth_48.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Gown</a>. Only the tailor can wear them.
- **Weapons.** Your weapon is a stat stick, its Spell Power matters more than its damage. The Warlock quest for the Orb of Soran'ruk lets you choose between <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15109"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_09.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Staff of Soran'ruk</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6898"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_orb_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Orb of Soran'ruk</a>.
- **Wands.** <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7001"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gravestone Scepter</a> from Blackfathom Deeps is the strongest wand before level 30. Use it when you are low on both mana and health.
