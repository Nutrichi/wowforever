The Fury Warrior in WoW Forever is the berserker: two weapons, fast swings and a Rage bar that fills with every critical strike. The rotation is quick and reactive, and Cleave and Whirlwind make Fury one of the best specs against several enemies. This guide covers the level 60 build, rotation, Rage, stance dancing, stats, weapons and races.

## What changes for Fury

- **Cruelty** gives 5% more critical strike chance with melee attacks at 5/5.
- **Unbridled Wrath** now has a 60% chance to give 1 extra Rage on every melee hit at 5/5.
- **Furious Precision is new.** 10% more chance to hit with your off-hand at 3/3.
- **Dual Wield Specialization does more.** 25% more off-hand damage and 50% more Rage from off-hand attacks.
- **Enrage** gives a 30% chance for 10% more physical damage for 12 seconds after you take a hit.
- **Death Wish** gives 20% more physical damage and Fear immunity for 30 seconds, but you take 5% more damage.
- **Bloodthirst is no longer based on your weapon.** It deals 45% of your attack power plus 30, which makes it strong with one-handed weapons, and gives 10% movement speed.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Every critical strike gives a burst of Rage, so the rotation stays lively | Berserker Stance and Death Wish make you easier to kill |
| Cleave and Whirlwind make Fury unmatched against packs | Nothing in the spec helps you against players |
| Recklessness and Death Wish for big burst | Whirlwind hits everything around you, so your tank must keep up |

## Rotation

1. Spend extra Rage on Heroic Strike, or Cleave against several enemies, so it is never wasted.
2. Keep Battle Shout up.
3. Stack Sunder Armor.
4. Execute when the target is low enough.
5. Bloodthirst, once you have it.
6. Whirlwind, once you have it.
7. Hamstring when nothing else is ready and you have Rage to spare.

**Stance dancing.** Rend scales better in Forever and Overpower costs little Rage, so short trips to Battle Stance for Rend and Overpower can pay off. You lose most of your Rage when you switch, so do not build Rage in Battle Stance and keep the trip short.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Retaliation** and **Shield Wall** when you are the target.
- **Berserker Rage** to ignore Fear and Incapacitate effects.
- **Recklessness** is the strongest damage cooldown in the game.
- **Death Wish** only for Fury, strongest together with Recklessness.
- **Bloodrage** freely, for extra Rage.

## Movement

Stay on the boss. Out of combat, Charge gives Rage and puts you in the fight at once, but switching stances to use it drains most of your Rage, so it is often only worth it when the tank is ahead of you. In combat you have Intercept, which works in Berserker Stance but costs Rage.

## Rage

Rage is everything. With endless Rage you would never stop pressing buttons, so plan ahead and keep enough for your important attacks. If a Heroic Strike leaves Bloodthirst or Whirlwind waiting for a second or two, that was a mistake. Keep your Rage moving: never full, never empty.

## Stats and gear

The exact order at level 60 follows once we can test level 60 in the live game. These stats help a Fury Warrior:

- **Hit chance.** A miss deals nothing and gives no Rage.
- **Critical strike chance.** More damage and more Rage, one of the best stats you can get. Agility adds some critical strike chance too.
- **Strength and attack power.** Your main stat: 1 Strength gives 2 attack power, so Strength is worth more per point.

**Armor.** Plate is ideal, but Mail or Leather with the right stats can be better. Forever has no penalty for wearing a lighter armor type, you just have less Armor. Blacksmiths make good leveling gear for every Warrior spec, and dungeons have plenty more.

**Weapons.** The most important part of your gear. You dual wield, ideally with a slow, hard-hitting main hand. A ranged weapon only counts for its stats.
