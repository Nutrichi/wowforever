In WoW Forever you ride from level 40. Every race has its own riding trainer and mount vendor near its capital, and your first mount is free. This guide covers what riding costs, how the free mount works and where every trainer stands.

## How riding works

Riding has two ranks. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=33388"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_swiftness.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Apprentice Riding</a> at level 40 lets you ride the normal mounts with 60% speed. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=33391"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_swiftness.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Journeyman Riding</a> at level 60 lets you ride the swift mounts with 100% speed. You need Apprentice before you can learn Journeyman.

When you learn Apprentice Riding, you also learn the riding skill of your race, such as <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6743"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_swiftness.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Horse Riding</a> for Humans or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6746"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_swiftness.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wolf Riding</a> for Orcs. That skill is what the mount tooltips ask for. To ride a mount of another race, you need Exalted with that race and pay 100 gold for its riding skill, for example <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6745"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_swiftness.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tiger Riding</a> for a Human who wants a Darnassus saber.

The mount item teaches the mount: it becomes a spell and counts for every character on the account, according to the tooltips. You find your mounts in the collections window (Shift+P). Since [the hotfix of 10 November](/news/racial-vendor-mounts-become-bind-on-pickup/) racial vendor mounts are Bind-on-Pickup, so you can no longer buy one for a friend of another race.

## Your first mount for free

At level 40 the riding trainer of your race gives Learning to Ride. Learn Apprentice Riding and hand the quest in at the mount vendor next to the trainer. The vendor then gives a quest to inspect the mounts on display: click the one you want, go back and it is yours.

## Paladins and Warlocks

Paladins and Warlocks get their mounts from class quests at level 40 and 60, and those quests also teach the riding skill. Since [the hotfix of 25 November](/news/epic-riding-fixed-for-paladins-and-warlocks/) the level 60 quests teach Journeyman Riding too, so they can ride every swift mount. The class mounts are the [Warhorse](/guides/mounts/warhorse/), the [Charger](/guides/mounts/charger/), the [Felsteed](/guides/mounts/felsteed/) and the [Dreadsteed](/guides/mounts/dreadsteed/).
