The Survival Hunter in WoW Forever fights at range and in melee. You weave Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite and the new Strider Kick into your shots, or you stay in melee the whole fight. New talents make melee attacks and traps much stronger, and Lone Wolf adds damage without a pet. This guide covers the level 60 build, the rotation, melee weaving, mana, stats and races.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Very high damage.** With good melee weaving, Survival is a strong damage spec for dungeons and raids.

**Two ways to play.** Weave melee attacks into your shots, or stay in melee the whole fight. Both use the same build.

**Strong at range and in melee.** The other Hunter specs mostly shoot. Survival deals real damage in both places.

**A demanding rotation.** Melee weaving asks for precise timing and positioning. A mistake costs more damage than in the other Hunter specs.

**Positioning.** Fights with a lot of movement, or mechanics that keep you out of melee, break your rotation.

**The dead zone.** Between melee range and 8 yards you can use neither melee nor ranged attacks. Survival has that problem too.

Three choices can move:

- **Lone Wolf** to 1 point in **Efficiency** or **Improved Stings** if you want to keep your pet, for example while questing.

## Rotation

Keep Hunter's Mark on the target: Expose Prey only opens Mongoose Bite on a marked target.

### Single target

1. Put an Immolation Trap where the tank will stand, if you can.
2. Cast Aimed Shot as the pull starts.
3. Cast Serpent Sting.
4. Cast Arcane Shot.
5. Run into melee range.
6. Cast Raptor Strike.
7. Cast Mongoose Bite, if it is available.
8. Drop Immolation Trap, if it is ready.
9. Cast Strider Kick and run back out of melee.
10. Shoot again from range and repeat.
11. Cast Rapid Fire on cooldown.

### Several targets

1. Put an Explosive Trap where the tank will stand, if you can.
2. Cast Multi-Shot.
3. Cast Serpent Sting.
4. Cast Arcane Shot.
5. Run into melee range.
6. Cast Raptor Strike.
7. Cast Mongoose Bite, if it is available.
8. Drop Explosive Trap, if it is ready.
9. Cast Strider Kick and run back out of melee.
10. Shoot again from range and repeat.
11. Cast Rapid Fire on cooldown.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Rapid Fire** is your damage cooldown: 40% more attack speed.
- **Explosive Trap** and **Immolation Trap** add damage every time you run into melee. Survivalist's Discipline makes them come back 40% sooner.
- **Frost Trap**, **Freezing Trap**, **Wing Clip** and **Concussive Shot** slow, root or remove monsters from the fight.
- **Feign Death** drops you out of combat. Without a pet it is your main way out of trouble.
- **Scorpid Sting** now lowers the target's chance to hit by 2% for 20 seconds. It does not stack with Insect Swarm.
- **Tranquilizing Shot** removes Enrage and Frenzy effects.
- **Tracking** shows monsters and players nearby. With Improved Tracking you also deal 5% more damage to the tracked type.
- **Aspects:** Aspect of the Hawk gives ranged attack power, Aspect of the Beast melee attack power. Cheetah and Pack are faster, but you get dazed when hit.

## Melee weaving and movement

Melee weaving is moving between range and melee without losing damage. Serpent Sting, Arcane Shot and your melee abilities are instant, so you keep attacking while you move. Moving does interrupt Auto Shot, so time your steps between shots.

Strider Kick is the key: it hits and gives you 30% movement speed for 3 seconds to get back to range. In a fight, stay in Aspect of the Hawk or Aspect of the Beast and walk. Cheetah and Pack get you dazed when a monster hits you.

## Mana

Survival uses a lot of mana, because the rotation has many abilities. Resourcefulness makes traps and melee abilities 60% cheaper. After a critical strike, 50% of your mana regeneration keeps going while you cast for 30 seconds. Lethal Attacks and Savage Strikes give you the critical strikes to keep that effect up. Efficiency is the optional talent for cheaper shots.

## With or without a pet

The build takes Lone Wolf and plays without a pet. In dungeons and raids that is the most damage. In the open world, a pet that holds monsters off you is often worth more: swap Lone Wolf for a point in Efficiency or Improved Stings and keep your pet. Use **Stay** and **Move To** to keep it out of area damage. More about pets is in the [Beast Mastery Hunter guide](/guides/beast-mastery-hunter/).

## Stats and gear

Stat priorities for level 60 follow after the launch. Until then:

- **Agility**: 2 ranged and 1 melee attack power per point, and critical strike chance, about 1% per 53 Agility at level 60. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19168"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_invisibilty.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lightning Reflexes</a> adds 10%.
- **Hit**: against a level 63 boss you need 9% with 300 weapon skill, or 6% with 305. That goes for ranged attacks and two-handed melee weapons. Surefooted already gives 3%.
- **Critical strike chance**, which keeps Resourcefulness going.
- **Attack power**, which Agility usually beats point for point.
- **Haste**, which changes the timing of melee weaving. Its real value shows at level 60.
- **Intellect**, for mana and through Careful Aim also for attack power.
- **Spirit**, for mana regeneration together with Resourcefulness.

**Armor.** Leather and mail both work. Leatherworking has good patterns while you level.

**Quiver or ammo pouch.** Both raise your ranged attack speed. Keep yours up to date.

**Weapons.** Survival needs a good ranged weapon and a good melee weapon. Look at damage per second and at speed, because both change the timing of your rotation.

## Races and professions

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Skyborne | A haste bonus, rare on gear, the strongest choice |
| Alliance | Human | 2% more critical strike chance, good for Resourcefulness |
| Alliance | Dwarf | 1% hit from Artisan's Precision |
| Alliance | Night Elf | A critical strike cooldown |
| Horde | Skyborne | A haste bonus and a movement speed cooldown for weaving |
| Horde | Orc | An attack power cooldown and 5% more pet damage from Command |
| Horde | Troll | More damage against Beasts and an attack speed cooldown |
| Horde | Tauren | An area stun, 5% more Health, and Plainsrunning |

The differences are small, so play the race you like. For a pure ranged build, see the [Marksmanship Hunter guide](/guides/marksmanship-hunter/).

**Leatherworking** makes good leather gear, **Engineering** brings gadgets and scopes, **Alchemy** with Herbalism keeps you in potions, and **Enchanting** keeps your gear enchanted.
