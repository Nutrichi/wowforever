The Feral Druid in WoW Forever is two specs in one: a cat that deals melee damage with Energy and Combo Points, and a bear that tanks with Rage. You can build for one of them or keep both. The old powershifting is gone, but Shifting Power turns mana into Energy, and you can use consumables and talk to most NPCs without leaving form. This guide covers four builds for level 60, the key talents, both rotations, resources, stats and races.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Two roles.** A cat deals damage like a Rogue, a bear tanks like a Warrior, and you can switch in the middle of a fight. Out of form you still have Rebirth, Innervate, Remove Curse and a few heals.

**Raid buffs.** Mark of the Wild, Thorns and Faerie Fire like every Druid, plus Leader of the Pack: 3% more melee and ranged critical strike chance for the whole party or raid within 45 yards. Demoralizing Roar lowers the damage of the enemy.

**Mobility.** Feral Swiftness makes you 30% faster in Cat Form, indoors too. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1850"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_dash.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dash</a>, Feral Charge and Pounce close gaps in combat, and Prowl gets you past danger.

**Hybrid damage.** Blizzard keeps hybrids a little below the pure damage classes on average. You can still top the meters, just less often than a Rogue.

**Three resources.** A cat watches Energy, Combo Points and, for Shifting Power, mana. A bear has Rage. Add bleeds and debuffs, and Feral is one of the harder specs to master.

## Cat rotation

1. Keep Thorns on yourself when you are alone.
2. Keep Faerie Fire on the target.
3. Rip at 5 Combo Points.
4. Keep Rake up.
5. Shifting Power on cooldown when you are below 50 Energy.
6. Shred to build Combo Points, or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1082"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_rake.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Claw</a> when you cannot get behind the target.

Save enough Energy to put a bleed back as soon as it ends, but do not refresh it before then. On monsters that die quickly, skip the bleeds and just Shred or Claw. Ferocious Bite is not worth its Energy for now.

## Bear rotation

1. Keep Thorns on yourself.
2. Keep Faerie Fire on the target; it also pulls from range.
3. Keep Demoralizing Roar up.
4. Primal Bite on the main target on cooldown.
5. Lacerate up to 5 stacks and keep them up.
6. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=779"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_monsterclaw_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Swipe</a> against several targets, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6807"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_maul.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Maul</a> against one.

On weak monsters, Primal Bite and Maul for one target, or only Swipe for a group.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Growl** taunts one enemy, **Challenging Roar** all enemies around you, on a 10 minute cooldown.
- **Cower** lowers your threat as a cat.
- **Barkskin** lowers the damage you take, in any form.
- **Frenzied Regeneration** turns Rage into health in Bear Form. Only when you are in danger, because that Rage is your threat.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5229"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_enrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Enrage</a>** gives Rage but lowers your armor. Good for threat, risky against heavy physical hits.
- **Innervate** and **Rebirth** cost you your form. Rebirth is the only battle resurrection in the game, on a long cooldown.

## Resources and forms

**Mana keeps regenerating in form.** It is hidden by default. Turn on the Personal Resource Display in Options, Combat, so you see whether you have mana for Shifting Power, a utility spell or a shift back.

**Energy** caps at 100 and ticks back like mana. **Combo Points** go up to 5 per target and do not move to another target.

**Rage** starts at 0, or at 10 with Furor, and caps at 100. You get it from taking damage, from critical strikes with Blood Frenzy, from dodges with Natural Reaction and from Enrage.

**Stay in one form.** Powershifting and bear weaving no longer pay off. In return, you can now use consumables and talk to most NPCs and objects without leaving form.

**Bleeds** ignore armor and switch on Rend and Tear. Keep them up and refresh them just after they run out.

## Positioning

As a cat, stay behind the target: Shred needs it, the enemy cannot parry you and you avoid frontal attacks. Feral Charge puts you behind the target, but only from Bear Form. As a bear, you set the pace: pull casters around a corner to group them, then turn the pack away from your group.

## Stats and gear

**Cat:** hit up to the cap (Nature's Reach gives 4%, against raid bosses you need 9%), then expertise up to its cap, then critical strike chance, Agility (attack power and critical strike), Strength (twice the attack power of Agility) and attack power. Haste is rare.

**Bear:** the cat stats for threat, plus defense up to 440 against raid bosses so they cannot land critical hits, dodge (you cannot block or parry), armor and Stamina. A Druid tank takes heavy hits with a large health pool rather than with mitigation.

**Gear.** Leather with the stats above. Feral attacks use weapon damage in Forever, so look for a hard-hitting two-handed weapon, but not one with useless stats. An **idol** in the relic slot gives a small bonus; carrying two to swap is fine. Level 60 gear is not known yet.

## Races and professions

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Night Elf | Critical strike bonus, good with a build full of critical strikes |
| Alliance | Skyborne | Haste and a mana regeneration cooldown |
| Horde | Tauren | Area stun and 5% more Health |
| Horde | Skyborne | Haste and a speed cooldown, less useful than the Alliance mana cooldown |

The differences are small, so pick the race you like. **Leatherworking** makes some of the best leather gear, part of it only for leatherworkers. **Engineering** gives useful gadgets. **Herbalism** suits a Druid, because you gather in Travel Form, and **Alchemy** turns those herbs into your own consumables.
