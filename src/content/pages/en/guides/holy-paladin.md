The Holy Paladin in WoW Forever is the most straightforward healer in the game. You cast heals with a cast time, heal for big numbers and live off critical strikes, which give you mana back through Illumination. You have no healing over time and no shields: you heal what is hurt. This guide covers the level 30 build, Holy Shock and Light's Vigil, the healing priority, mana, seals, stats and races.

|---|---|

| Build at level 30 | 21 points in Holy, Talented adds Healing Light, Purifying Power, Divine Precision and Holy Power |
| Key talents | Divine Intellect, Reverence, Illumination, Divine Favor, Holy Shock, Light's Vigil |
| Strength | Big heals, group utility, very hard to kill |

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Group utility.** Every group wants two Paladins. You bring blessings, an aura, Judgements and bubbles.

**Hard to kill.** Plate, Divine Shield and your own heals keep you alive in fights where others go down.

**Mostly reactive.** Without healing over time or shields, you respond to damage. You can still plan ahead: start a heal before the big hit lands and cancel it if it turns out not to be needed.

**One area heal.** Light's Vigil is your only way to heal the whole party, and it is expensive.

## What the key talents do

| Talent | What it does in Forever |
|---|---|
| **Divine Intellect** | 10% more Intellect |
| **Healing Light** | Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock heal 12% more |
| **Spiritual Focus** | 70% chance not to lose cast time when you take damage, for Flash of Light, Holy Light and Light's Vigil |
| **Reverence** | 30% of your mana regeneration continues while you cast |
| **Purifying Power** | Cleanse and Purify cost 20% less, Exorcism and Holy Wrath come back 33% sooner |
| **Infusion of Light** | A critical Holy Shock or Flash of Light makes your next Holy Light 1 second faster |
| **Illumination** | A critical heal gives back mana equal to 50% of the base cost of the spell |
| **Divine Favor** | Your next Flash of Light, Holy Light or Holy Shock is a critical effect |
| **Divine Precision** | 18% more chance to hit with Holy spells, so Judgement and Holy Shock land |
| **Holy Shock** | An instant blast of Holy damage on an enemy, or an instant heal on an ally |
| **Holy Power** | 15% more critical strike chance for Holy Shock and Holy Strike, 5% for all other spells |
| **Light's Vigil** | Your next Holy Shock on the target has no cooldown and heals their whole party |

Light's Vigil is in the seventh row, out of reach at level 30. Spiritual Focus works best together with Concentration Aura. Divine Favor and Illumination work together: a guaranteed critical heal also gives mana back.

## Healing priority

Keep your seal up, most likely Seal of Wisdom, and keep its Judgement on the target.

1. Holy Shock when someone may die before a heal with a cast time lands, or when you must heal while moving.
2. Holy Light when a target is about to take a lot of damage. Start it ahead of time.
3. Holy Light right away after an Infusion of Light proc.
4. Flash of Light for light damage.
5. Light's Vigil only when the group really needs area healing.

## Seals and Judgements

Each Paladin can keep only one Judgement on a target.

- **Seal of Wisdom** and its Judgement give mana back to your group. Keep it on the boss.
- **Seal of Light** and its Judgement give a little healing back, useful against a lot of small area damage.
- **Seal of the Crusader** raises the Holy damage a target takes. That rarely helps a healer, and a Protection or Retribution Paladin in the group will usually bring it.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Divine Shield**, **Divine Protection** and **Blessing of Protection** are strong defensive cooldowns, and all three cause Forbearance.
- **Lay on Hands** heals a target to full instantly. It costs all your mana and gives a little back, so use it with care.
- **Blessing of Sacrifice** moves part of the damage on an ally to you, best against many small hits. Give the ally their normal blessing back afterwards.
- **Divine Intervention** protects one ally from everything for 3 minutes, but kills you. Use it as wipe protection on another healer far from the boss.

Before a fight, give everyone the right blessing, and everyone except the tank also Blessing of Salvation. During the fight: Blessing of Protection on someone who pulls threat, Blessing of Freedom on someone who is rooted.

## Stats and gear

Stat priorities for level 60 follow after the launch. Until then:

- **Healing power and spell power**, for stronger heals and cheaper ones: with more healing power, a lower rank does the job.
- **Intellect**, for more mana and more critical strike chance.
- **Critical strike chance**, for bigger heals and more Illumination procs.
- **Spirit and mana per 5 seconds.** Thanks to Reverence, Spirit also regenerates mana in combat, which counts in long fights.

**Armor.** Look at the stats, not the armor type. Cloth and leather with healing power are often better than plate. At low levels, Blacksmithing helps, because plate and mail with spell power are rare.

**Weapon.** Paladins cannot use staves, so you will mostly carry a one-handed weapon with a shield or an off-hand. Your weapon is often your most important item even though you rarely swing it.

**Librams.** A libram in the ranged slot gives a small passive bonus. Pick one that fits your heals.

## Races and professions

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Human | Extra critical strike chance, the best choice |
| Alliance | Dwarf | More critical strike damage and healing, and Stoneform as a defensive cooldown |
| Horde | Undead | A proc that deals damage and heals you a little; the only Horde Paladin |

**Blacksmithing** makes some of the best plate and mail, much of it only available to blacksmiths. **Engineering** gives useful gadgets to every spec. More about healing in groups in our [dungeon guides](/guides/dungeons/).
