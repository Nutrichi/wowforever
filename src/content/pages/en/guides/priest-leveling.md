The Priest in WoW Forever levels calmly: heal yourself, shield yourself and let damage over time and a wand do the work. You choose between Discipline, Holy and Shadow. This guide covers the three builds, the rotation, the key levels, racial spells, wands and professions.

| | |
|---|---|
| Easiest spec for leveling | Discipline with a wand |
| Key talents | Wand Specialization, Twin Disciplines, Spirit Tap, Penance, Mind Flay |
| Strength | Healing, shields, steady damage with few pauses |
| Weakness | Slow kills early, mana, little crowd control |

## Which spec to level with

The first 20 to 30 levels barely differ. You start with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=585"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_holysmite.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Smite</a>, get a wand as soon as you can, and then keep your health up with heals and shields while the wand kills. Around level 30 the road splits.

**Discipline** keeps that calm style and adds Penance at 30, for damage or a quick heal. **Shadow** kills faster with Mind Flay and Mind Blast, but only shines from about level 28, unless you have decent spell power. **Holy** is for players who spend a lot of their leveling time healing dungeons. Leveling alone as Holy is slow.
## Levels 1 to 10

You have no talents yet. Smite and Lesser Heal are your spells until you have a wand.

1. Attack with Shoot from your wand.
2. Cast Lesser Heal when your health gets low.

Use <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_powerwordshield.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Power Word: Shield</a> when you need to cast without interruption, and keep <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=589"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_shadowwordpain.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Word: Pain</a> up when your mana allows.

Stats for every build at these levels: first Intellect and spell damage, then Stamina and Spirit, then armor.

Mental Strength now sits in row 2, where it raises Intellect, and Improved Power Word: Shield in row 3. Shadow gets Dark Inspiration, loses Improved Fade and Shadow Infinity, and Improved Mind Blast moves to row 2. The builds below and the talent calculator follow the current trees. All changes are in [this post](/news/battle-shout-and-totems-now-buff-the-whole-raid/).

## Professions

Leveling a profession alongside your Priest gives gear, consumables and gold, and saves catching up later. In Forever it matters more: spell damage coefficients changed, which makes crafted gear more attractive, and camps give buffs to every profession.

- **Cooking** helps every class: food gives extra experience, and you need it for a campfire.
- **Tailoring** has strong soulbound leveling gear you cannot get elsewhere.
- **Enchanting** gives enchants, wands without the Auction House and oils for spell power. Disenchant your spare Tailoring gear for materials.
- **Fishing** levels on the side and catches crates and pearls for Tailoring and Enchanting.
- **Alchemy** gives potions and a buff for mana regeneration.

## Consumables

- **Food and water.** Cooked food gives extra experience, so stay Well Fed. Carry water of your level, because even with a wand you will need to drink.
- **Potions.** Health potions for emergencies, now also made with First Aid, and mana potions.
- **Elixirs.** Expensive to keep up, but useful against a hard monster or quest.
- **Oils.** Enchanting makes oils for spell power or mana.

More about food, camps and rested XP is in the [experience guide](/guides/experience/).

## Damage and healing over time

Spells that work over time are the core of the Priest. Renew heals every few seconds, Shadow Word: Pain deals damage every few seconds, and Devouring Plague does the same with a longer cooldown and heals you for the damage it deals.

On the beta these spells do not snapshot. Their value updates while they run, instead of keeping the value from the moment you cast them. A buff to your spell power during the spell counts right away. Blizzard has not announced this.

## Mana

Mana matters for every Priest, even if you mostly shoot your wand. Carry plenty of water and a few mana potions. Spirit helps a lot, because it gives mana regeneration between pulls, and Spirit Tap and Meditation make it even stronger.

## Healing

Even as Shadow you heal yourself and others.

- **Heal** is your basic heal, with Lesser Heal before it and Greater Heal from level 40.
- **Penance** heals in bursts as Discipline, or deals damage on an enemy.
- **Power Word: Shield** is not a heal, but a shield that works as a second health bar.

Holy adds Prayer of Mending, which jumps to the next target each time someone takes damage.

## Crowd control and utility

Crowd control is not the strength of the Priest. Shackle Undead takes one undead out of the fight, as long as nobody damages it. Psychic Scream makes nearby enemies flee, which can save a pull but also pull more monsters.

Power Word: Fortitude raises Stamina and belongs on every group member, yourself included. Divine Spirit does the same for Spirit. Power Infusion, the last Discipline talent, gives a friendly target a lot of extra spell power for a short time. Vampiric Embrace heals your party, not your raid, for part of your Shadow damage.
