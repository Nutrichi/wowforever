The Balance Druid in WoW Forever is a caster with two damage over time spells, two hard casts and a large bag of utility. In Classic it was known as the Druid who ran out of mana. In Forever new talents fix that and give the spec a real rotation. This guide covers the builds, rotation, stats, races and professions.

## What changes for Balance

- **Wrath is a real filler.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16814"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_abolishmagic.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Wrath</a> cuts the cast time of <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5176"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_abolishmagic.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wrath</a> by 0.5 seconds and its mana cost by half. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16845"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_sentinal.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Moonglow</a> makes all your damage spells 25% cheaper.
- **Eclipse is new.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=408248"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_eclipse.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Eclipse</a> makes your next two <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2912"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_starfire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Starfire</a> casts faster after every Wrath. You alternate both spells instead of spamming one.
- **More for your DoTs.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1223081"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_arcane03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Genesis</a> raises periodic damage and healing by 5%, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1223083"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_natureresistancetotem.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nature's Splendor</a> makes <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8921"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_starfall.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Moonfire</a> last 3 seconds and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5570"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_insectswarm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Insect Swarm</a> 2 seconds longer.
- **Built-in critical strike chance.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1223082"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nature's Majesty</a> adds 4% to your spells and attacks.
- **Omen of Clarity for every Druid.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16864"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_crystalball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Omen of Clarity</a> comes at level 20. Its Clearcasting is not consumed by Wrath.
- **Revive** at level 12 brings a dead player back to life out of combat.

## Rotation

**The DoT build**
1. Keep <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=467"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_thorns.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thorns</a> on yourself.
2. Pull with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5176"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_abolishmagic.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wrath</a>.
3. Put <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8921"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_starfall.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Moonfire</a> on the enemy.
4. Put <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5570"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_insectswarm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Insect Swarm</a> on it.
5. Root it with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=339"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_stranglevines.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Entangling Roots</a>.
6. Repeat for the next enemy.
7. Wear them down one by one with Wrath while your DoTs tick.

With 2 points in Overgrowth you root up to three enemies at once. A fourth works if you can handle one that is not rooted.

**The Eclipse build**
1. Keep Thorns on yourself.
2. Pull with Wrath.
3. Put Moonfire and Insect Swarm on the enemy.
4. Before level 30: cast Wrath until the enemy is almost dead or your mana runs low.
5. From level 30: alternate two Starfires with one Wrath.
6. Finish it with your weapon, or let your DoTs do it.

Root the next enemy with Entangling Roots while your DoTs finish the first.

**On the move** you cannot cast Wrath or Starfire. Use that time to refresh your DoTs or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=770"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_faeriefire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Faerie Fire</a>. Over a longer distance, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=768"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_catform.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cat Form</a> with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1850"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_dash.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dash</a> from level 26 gets you back in position faster.

## Mana and DoTs

With Improved Wrath and Moonglow, mana is much less of a problem than in Classic. Spend your Clearcasting procs on Starfire. Wrath cannot consume them, so you never waste one by accident. Casting lower ranks to save mana costs more damage than it saves.

Do not refresh a DoT before it runs out, because you lose the ticks it had left. Only do it when you have to move and have mana to spare, or when you will not be able to refresh it in time. Skip DoTs on enemies that are about to die.

## Stats and gear

The exact priority at level 60 follows once we can test level 60 in the live game. These stats help a Balance Druid:

- **Spell damage** first. Items with only Arcane or Nature damage help half of your spells.
- **Hit.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16819"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_naturetouchgrow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nature's Reach</a> gives 4%, enough while you level. For raids you will want to reach the hit cap.
- **Critical strike chance.** Nature's Majesty gives 4%, Moonkin Form 3%, and every crit from a non-periodic spell triggers <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16880"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_naturesblessing.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nature's Grace</a>, 10% faster casts for 3 seconds.
- **Intellect** for more mana and critical strike chance. It is on almost every piece you want anyway.
- **Haste** makes your casts faster, but not your DoTs. Crit is worth more.
- **Spirit** helps your regeneration out of combat and when you cast Innervate on yourself.

**Armor.** Wear leather or cloth, whichever has the right stats. Wearing a lighter armor type has no penalty in Forever, you only get a little less armor. Leatherworking makes good leveling gear for every Druid spec.

**Idols.** Your relic slot takes an Idol. Carry a few and swap them for the moment. The [Druid leveling guide](/guides/druid-leveling/) lists the Idols you find while leveling.

**Weapons.** For a caster a weapon is a bag of stats. A one-handed mace or dagger with an off-hand item usually gives the most, but a good staff works too.
