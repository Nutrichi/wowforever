The Fire Mage in WoW Forever lives on critical strikes. Heating Up turns crits into a fast Pyroblast, Ignite burns the target after every crit, and the reworked Fire Power rewards every Fire effect on your target. The spec scales very well with gear, but it is slow to start while leveling. This guide covers the builds, rotation, mana, survival and gear.

## What changes for Fire

- **Heating Up is new.** A talent at level 25. Every non-periodic critical strike with Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast or Scorch makes your next Pyroblast within 20 seconds 25% faster, up to 3 stacks. Fire Blast gives a stack every time it hits.
- **Critical Mass is stronger.** 2% critical strike chance for Fire spells per point, 10% at 5/5.
- **Fire Power is reworked.** It now counts your Fire effects on the target, up to four: 3% more Fire damage per effect at 3/3, so 12% at most.
- **Ignite is weaker.** It burns for 40% of the critical strike over 4 seconds. Rolling Ignite from one crit to the next is no longer worth it.
- **Flamestrike no longer stacks.** Only one Flamestrike burn stays on a target.

## Rotation

**One enemy**
1. Pre-cast Pyroblast before a boss fight. Pull a normal monster with Fireball.
2. Cast Scorch until Improved Scorch has 5 stacks.
3. Cast Fire Blast on cooldown: it always gives a Heating Up stack.
4. Cast Fireball, and Scorch again when the stacks are about to run out.
5. Cast Pyroblast as soon as Heating Up has 3 stacks.
6. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1326053"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Blast</a> to slow a target from a distance, Frost Nova rank 1 to lock down a dangerous monster, Cone of Cold when it reaches you.
7. Counterspell on a dangerous cast, Polymorph on a dangerous extra enemy.

**A pack of enemies**
1. Cast Flamestrike, and again when its burn runs out. It no longer stacks.
2. Cast Blast Wave on cooldown after Flamestrike, if you took it.
3. Spam Arcane Explosion.

At higher levels the rotation looks like this: Improved Scorch at 5 stacks, Fire Blast, Fireball, Pyroblast, Fireball, Fire Blast, Pyroblast, and so on.

## Heating Up and Fire Power

**Heating Up** is the core of the spec. Each non-periodic critical strike gives a stack, and each stack makes your next Pyroblast 25% faster, up to 75% at 3 stacks. At low levels it hardly procs, because your critical strike chance is low. Incineration, Critical Mass, gear and levels make it more reliable. Fire Blast gives a stack on every hit and two on a critical strike, so you cast it on cooldown.

**Fire Power** counts the Fire effects on your target, up to four: the burn of Fireball, Improved Scorch, Ignite and the burn of Pyroblast. Effects that mix schools, such as <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=401502"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_frostfirebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostfire Bolt</a>, do not count. The bonus does not show as a buff or a debuff on the target. It moves between 2 and 4 effects: Improved Scorch and Fireball are almost always there, Ignite and Pyroblast depend on your critical strikes.

## Mana

Fire spells cost a lot, but Fire needs less mana control than Arcane.

- **Master of Elements** refunds 30% of the base mana cost of every Fire or Frost critical strike at 3/3. Out of reach at level 30, but later it procs almost all the time.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6117"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_magearmor.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mage Armor</a>** from level 34 keeps 50% of your mana regeneration while casting. A dip into Arcane Meditation makes it 100%.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12051"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_purge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Evocation</a>** restores your mana over a few seconds, with a long cooldown. Save it for long boss fights. A High Order Skyborne can combine it with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259705"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_incantersabsorbtion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Read Leyline</a>.
- **Mana gems** such as <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=759"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gem_emerald_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Conjure Mana Agate</a> restore mana with one charge per gem. Make a new one before every long fight.
- **Mana potions** restore a lot, depending on their level.

## Survival

Fire has less survival than Frost and no Ice Block. Kill a target before it kills you, and pick your fights. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blink.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blink</a> and Frost Nova get you out of most trouble. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1463"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_detectlesserinvisibility.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mana Shield</a> costs too much mana to use all the time, but Mana Shield with Evocation can save you as a last resort.

Use Frost Blast or rank 1 Frostbolt to slow a target from a distance, Cone of Cold as an instant slow (the first four targets are slowed the most), and Polymorph to take a target out of the fight. Blast Wave also dazes everything around you. Fences, hills and buildings break line of sight, which buys you casts.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Frost Nova** is for attack and defense. Use it as often as you can.
- **Fire Blast** goes on cooldown for Heating Up.
- **Blast Wave** deals heavy damage around you and dazes, good for burst or as a slow when Cone of Cold is on cooldown.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=475"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_removecurse.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Remove Lesser Curse</a>** removes Curses and Banes. Use it fast on dangerous ones.
- **Amplify Magic** makes a group member take more damage from spells, but receive more healing. Good against physical damage.
- **Dampen Magic** lowers spell damage taken, but also the healing received. Use it against casters, not against enemies that burst you down.
- **Polymorph** a dangerous target in every group pull.

## Stats and gear

The exact priority at level 60 follows after the launch. These stats help a Fire Mage:

- **Spell damage and Fire spell damage** first. Fire spell damage only helps Fire spells, and it works with Fire Power.
- **Critical strike chance** is the heart of the spec: a critical strike deals 50% more damage, and Heating Up, Incineration, Critical Mass, Ignite and Combustion all build on it.
- **Intellect** for a bigger mana pool and more critical strike chance.
- **Haste** means faster casts. At low levels only Troll (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20554"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/racial_troll_berserk.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Berserking</a>) and High Order Skyborne (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259710"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_volatileair.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wind Blessed</a>) get it.
- **Spirit and mana per 5 seconds** help once Mage Armor keeps part of your regeneration going while casting.
- **Stamina** comes on most gear. You do not need to look for more.

**Armor.** Cloth with spell damage, Intellect and Stamina. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273044"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_cloth_17.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Violet Sorcerer's Robes</a> at level 27 is a new robe with Intellect and spell damage. Later you find critical strike chance and Haste on gear.

**Weapons.** Weapons got a lot more spell damage in Forever. Look for Intellect, Stamina and spell damage, such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274158"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_17.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Death Prophet Spine</a>, but a weapon with only spell damage, such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2169"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_shortblade_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Buzzer Blade</a>, can be the better pick.

**Wands.** The new Enchanting wands carry you: <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=11287"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lesser Magic Wand</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=11288"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Greater Magic Wand</a> early, <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249232"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lesser Eternal Wand</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249237"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_14.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Greater Eternal Wand</a> later. <a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279246"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_torch_lit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Torch of Light</a> waits at level 60. Look for spell damage and wand damage per second.

Orc is the strongest pick in PvE, Troll the second. For the Alliance, Human comes first for the critical strike chance and High Order Skyborne second. Gnome took nerfs on the beta and mainly stays interesting with Engineering. None of it is strong enough to skip a race you like more.

**Professions.** Tailoring makes cloth sets with spell damage and Intellect, and pairs with Enchanting for your own wands and enchants. Engineering is the best partner for Tailoring if you want the most damage. Herbalism feeds Alchemy, which makes elixirs, potions and flasks.
