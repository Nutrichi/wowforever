## What Journey does

A panel shows the next step and an arrow points the way. Journey detects your faction and class and loads the right route; an existing character gets a catch-up step at its level. The addon also says which flight path to take, what to buy at a vendor and which quest to abandon.

Journey works without TomTom and runs alongside Questie and ElvUI. Hard quests can be skipped, and the route adjusts when a quest is no longer available. Quest rewards for your class are named in the steps.

## What is in the free part

Levels 1 to 30 already have Forever's new quests and the Skyborne start on Zephras Isle for both factions. The new race and class combinations, such as the Undead Paladin, the Orc Mage and the Dwarf Shaman, have their class quests in the route. Steps for Cooking and the new Camping system use their experience bonus.

## How to install Journey

1. Download the free addon from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/journey), with the CurseForge app or by hand into the AddOns folder of WoW Forever.
2. Log in. Journey detects your faction and class and opens the route for your level.
3. For levels 30 to 60, buy the route on the [website of Journey](https://getjourney.gg/?code=nutri). The link fills in the code NUTRI for 10% off.

## Levels 30 to 60

On 4 November, levels 30 to 60 start on the classic route. About a week after launch, Forever's new quests and zones are added to it, around five levels every four to five days. Dungeon quests come later as an option. Duo and group routes are planned, and so is Hardcore with a Self-Found version, which is not part of this purchase.

## Price and the code NUTRI

There is no subscription. Until 4 December, both factions cost 30 dollars and one faction 25 dollars, plus tax. After that the price becomes 45 and 30 dollars. A purchase covers every character on one Battle.net account, and a refund is possible within 14 days.

The creator code NUTRI takes 10% off: 27 dollars for both factions, 22.50 for one. <a href="https://getjourney.gg/?code=nutri" rel="noopener sponsored">Our partner link</a> applies the code at checkout; typing it in by hand works too. The addon itself and levels 1 to 30 stay free, as Blizzard's addon policy requires.

## Who makes the routes

The Forever routes are by Alenya, a Classic speedrunner who has written 1 to 60 guides for eight years, with a team of record-holding speedrunners and class experts. They build on Joana's original 1 to 60 routes. Journey licenses that addon from TomCat's Gaming, and Joana's Guides continues as its own product.
