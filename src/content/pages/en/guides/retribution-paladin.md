The Retribution Paladin in WoW Forever is the holy warrior with a big two-handed weapon. You hit slowly but hard, cast a few spells in between, and almost all of it now scales with spell power. You do not press a button every global cooldown, but Judgement, Holy Strike and Consecration keep you busy. This guide covers the level 60 build, the rotation, seals, blessings, stats and races.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Group utility.** Every group wants two Paladins. You bring blessings, an aura, Judgements and bubbles. None of that is unique to Retribution, but you bring all of it.

**Hard to kill.** Plate, Divine Shield, Lay on Hands and your own heals keep you standing when your healer cannot. That makes Retribution strong in rough fights.

**Slow to get anywhere.** You have no charge, no blink and no sprint. Pursuit of Justice gives you 15% movement speed, and that is it.

**Mana.** You do not build a resource before a fight, but mana can run out. Sanctified Judgement help, and Judgement of Wisdom helps the whole group.

## Rotation

1. Cast Seal of the Crusader before the pull.
2. Open with Judgement, for Judgement of the Crusader on the target.
3. Cast Seal of Command and keep it up.
4. Cast Judgement on cooldown.
5. Cast Holy Strike whenever it is ready.
6. Keep Consecration up.
7. Against Undead, cast Exorcism whenever it is ready.

## Seals and Judgements

Each Paladin can keep only one Judgement on a target, so you choose per group and per fight.

- **Seal of the Crusader** is weak on its own. You use it for its Judgement, which raises the Holy damage the target takes. That is a big boost for you, but not for the rest of the group.
- **Seal of Wisdom** and its Judgement give mana back. Your group will thank you for keeping it on the boss.
- **Seal of Light** and its Judgement give a little healing back, useful in fights with a lot of small area damage.
- **Seal of Command** is your damage seal. Its Judgement also deals a large hit of Holy damage. Since 13 November that Judgement counts as a melee attack and triggers on-hit effects, unlike the other Judgements.
- **Seal of Righteousness** adds Holy damage to every swing and also hits hard when judged.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **Divine Shield**, **Divine Protection** and **Blessing of Protection** are strong defensive cooldowns, and all three cause Forbearance. You cannot attack under Divine Protection or Blessing of Protection, so Divine Shield comes first.
- **Lay on Hands** heals you to full instantly. It costs all your mana and gives a little back, so use it with care.
- **Blessing of Sacrifice** moves part of the damage on an ally to you. The amount is small, so it works best against many small hits. Give the ally their normal blessing back afterwards.
- **Divine Intervention** protects one ally from everything for 3 minutes, but kills you. Use it as wipe protection on a healer far from the boss, so they can resurrect the group.

## Blessings in a group

Before a fight, give everyone the right blessing, and everyone except the tank also Blessing of Salvation. During the fight, watch your group: Blessing of Protection on someone who pulls threat, Blessing of Freedom on someone who is rooted in a bad spot, and Blessing of Sacrifice on a caster buried under a pack of small monsters.

## Stats and gear

Stat priorities for level 60 follow once we can test level 60 in the live game. Until then, these are the stats that count:

- **Hit**, so your attacks and spells land. Judgement uses your ranged hit chance since 11 November.
- **Critical strike chance**, which works with Conviction and Vengeance.
- **Strength and attack power** for your melee damage. Strength gives 2 attack power per point.
- **Spell power and Intellect**, because most of your kit scales with spell power. Champion of the Light adds spell damage from Intellect.
- **Agility**, for a little critical strike chance.

**Armor.** Look at the stats, not the armor type. Leather or even cloth can be better than plate if the stats fit. At low levels, Blacksmithing is almost a must, because plate and mail with spell power are rare. Dungeons have good pieces too.

**Weapon.** A slow two-handed weapon with high weapon damage, for Seal of Command and Holy Strike.

**Librams.** Paladins carry a libram in the ranged slot, with a small passive bonus. Pick one that fits your spells.

## Races and professions

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Human | Extra critical strike chance, the best choice |
| Alliance | Dwarf | 1% hit from Artisan's Precision, and Stoneform as a strong defensive cooldown |
| Horde | Undead | A proc that deals damage and heals you a little; the only Horde Paladin |

**Blacksmithing** makes some of the best plate and mail, much of it only available to blacksmiths. **Engineering** gives useful gadgets to every spec. More about the races in [Races and classes](/guides/races-and-classes/).
