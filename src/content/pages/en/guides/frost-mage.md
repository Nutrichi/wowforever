This guide covers the build, rotation, kiting, mana and gear.

## What changes for Frost

- **Ice Lance is new.** A talent at level 20: instant, 45 mana, and 200% more damage to Frozen targets. It used to be 300%.
- **Fingers of Frost is new.** Your Chill effects have a 15% chance to treat your next 2 spells as if the target were Frozen. That works on targets you cannot freeze, such as most bosses.
- **Missile Barrage is in reach.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400588"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_missilebarrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Missile Barrage</a> sits in the first row of the Arcane tree. At 2/2 every <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=116"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostbolt02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostbolt</a> has a 20% chance to make your next <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5143"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_starfall.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Missiles</a> free and twice as fast.
- **Frost Blast** at level 24 is a weak, quick blast that slows the target by 40% for 9 seconds.
- **Lower ranks proc less.** A lower rank of a spell has a much smaller chance to trigger effects. Rank 1 Frostbolt still slows, but no longer fishes for Frostbite or Fingers of Frost.

**One enemy**
1. Keep <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1459"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_magicalsentry.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Intellect</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=168"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostarmor02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frost Armor</a> up.

3. Cast Ice Lance when Fingers of Frost procs, or when Frostbite freezes the target.

## Kiting

Frost keeps enemies away. Besides your slows, the way you move matters. Fences, hills, buildings and trees break line of sight, and an enemy has to walk around them while it is slowed. That works well against elites. Strafing can also disturb the path of a melee enemy, but not of every enemy, and not of casters. Against a caster, break line of sight when Counterspell is on cooldown.

Frost Blast or rank 1 Frostbolt slow an enemy from a distance when you do not want it near you.

## Frozen and Shatter

Freezing a target and shattering it is the heart of the spec. Frost Nova is the only way to freeze on demand. Frostbite and Fingers of Frost do it by chance, and only Fingers of Frost works on bosses. Use as many Ice Lances as you can while a target is Frozen.

Your health stays low. Until <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11426"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_ice_lament.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Barrier</a> at level 40, you rely on freezes, Mana Shield and Blink. Elites and several enemies at once are where that matters.

## Stats and gear

The exact priority at level 60 follows after the launch. These stats help a Frost Mage:

- **Spell damage and Frost spell damage** first. Frost spell damage only helps Frost spells.
- **Haste** makes every cast faster, which means more damage and more procs. It is rare at low levels.
- **Critical strike chance**, although Shatter already gives a lot against Frozen targets.
- **Intellect** for mana and critical strike chance, without giving up spell damage.
- **Spirit and mana per 5 seconds** in moderate amounts, more so once you have Mage Armor.
- **Stamina** comes on most gear. You do not need to look for more.

Orc is the strongest pick in PvE, Troll the second. Gnome is the weakest for Frost, unless you take Engineering. None of it is strong enough to skip a race you like more.

**Professions.** Tailoring makes cloth sets with spell damage and Intellect, and pairs with Enchanting for your own wands and enchants. Engineering is the best partner for Tailoring if you want the most damage. Herbalism feeds Alchemy, which makes elixirs, potions and flasks.
