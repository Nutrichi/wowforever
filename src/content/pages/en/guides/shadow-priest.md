The Shadow Priest in WoW Forever deals Shadow damage with two damage over time spells, Mind Blast and Mind Flay, and heals the party at the same time through Vampiric Embrace. Mana is rarely a problem. Movement is: the Priest has almost no tools for it. This guide covers the build, rotation, Shadowform, mana, stats and gear.

## What changes for Shadow

- **Improved Mind Flay is new.** Every tick of Mind Flay deals 20% more than the tick before at 2/2, so the last tick is the strongest.
- **Vampiric Embrace lasts 30 seconds.** It heals all party members for 20% of the Shadow damage you deal and can stay up for the whole fight.
- **Shadowform does more.** 10% more Shadow damage, 50% lower mana cost for Shadow spells, 100% more critical strike damage bonus for Shadow spells and 15% less physical damage taken.
- **Silence also interrupts.** 5 seconds of silence and a 3 second interrupt in one button.
- **Devouring Contagion is new.** Devouring Plague costs half the mana, and when a target dies with it, the plague jumps to 2 nearby enemies.
- **Dark Inspiration is new.** Mind Blast and a fully channeled Mind Flay give you and your party 15% more Spirit for 10 seconds.
- **Early Demise is new.** 30% more critical strike chance for Shadow Word: Death on targets below 20% health at 2/2.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Very good mana thanks to Shadowform, Meditation and Mental Strength | Almost no way to move fast or get away |
| Vampiric Embrace heals the party all fight long | Damage needs time to ramp up with DoTs and Shadow Weaving |
| DoTs and burst spells fit together without a complicated rotation | Weak direct damage against more than four targets |
| Silence, Psychic Scream and Dispel Magic for utility | No healing spells in Shadowform |

**Free points.** A few points can move without much loss:

- Inner Focus does little for Shadow. Its point can go elsewhere.
- Devouring Contagion only helps when targets die with Devouring Plague on them. In a raid you rarely need the mana, so one or two points can go.
- More points in Early Demise or Mental Strength are small gains.
- Spirit Tap helps a lot in dungeons, where enemies die all the time, and little in raids.
- If your raid needs you to dispel enemies, one point in Holy Precision makes Dispel Magic miss less.

## Rotation

**One enemy**
1. Cast Fade before the pull to lower your threat.
2. Pre-cast Mind Blast so it lands as the fight starts.
3. Devouring Plague, then Shadow Word: Pain.
4. Shadow Word: Death and Mind Blast.
5. Vampiric Embrace.
6. Mind Flay, and let it finish.

After the opener you follow a priority: keep Devouring Plague and Shadow Word: Pain up, cast Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Death when they are ready, keep Vampiric Embrace up as close to 100% as you can, and fill every gap with Mind Flay.

**Several enemies**
1. The same opener on the main target, with Devouring Plague only there.
2. Shadow Word: Pain on every target. That is the biggest part of your damage against a pack.
3. Below four targets, keep using Shadow Word: Death and Mind Blast.
4. Vampiric Embrace on the target that takes most of your Shadow damage.
5. Fill with Mind Flay.

Shadow has no damage cooldowns of its own. Vampiric Embrace is the only buff you manage, and it should simply never drop.

## Shadowform, DoTs and Mind Flay

**Shadowform** is what makes the spec, but it blocks every heal. To heal someone directly, you leave the form, cast the heal and go back in, which costs a global cooldown and mana. Leave healing to the healers and to Vampiric Embrace.

**DoTs.** Your damage stands or falls with the uptime of Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague. Refresh them when they run out, not long before.

**Mind Flay.** With Improved Mind Flay the third tick deals most of the damage. Moving or casting something else before the end of the channel throws that tick away.

**Devouring Contagion.** When a target is about to die, a Devouring Plague on it spreads to two other enemies. Do not hold Devouring Plague back for it, but keep it in mind in dungeon packs.

## Mana

Mana is one of the strengths of Shadow. Shadowform halves the mana cost of Shadow spells, Meditation keeps 50% of your mana regeneration going while you cast, and Mental Strength adds 10% Intellect at 5/5. Devouring Contagion halves the cost of Devouring Plague. In dungeons Spirit Tap adds a lot of regeneration after every kill.

## Movement

Casters live by one rule: always be casting. The Priest has almost no tools to move quickly, so pick your spot before a fight and move as little as you can. Cast your instant spells, such as Shadow Word: Pain and Shadow Word: Death, while you have to walk.

## Stats and gear

The order for a Shadow Priest:

1. **Hit chance** until the cap. A spell that misses deals nothing and lets the Shadow Weaving stacks run out.
2. **Spell damage and Shadow spell damage.** The biggest gain once you are hit capped. Shadow spell damage counts the same for you.
3. **Critical strike chance.** Shadowform doubles the critical strike damage bonus of your Shadow spells, so crits matter more than their place in this list suggests.
4. **Intellect** for a bigger mana pool and more critical strike chance with spells.
5. **Spirit** comes with your gear. Do not look for it.

**Armor.** Cloth with spell damage or Shadow spell damage is good for you. Cloth with Strength or Agility is not.

## Races and professions

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Dwarf | Big Game Hunter: 5% more damage to Beasts. The best pick against Beast bosses, barely behind Human against the rest |
| Alliance | Human | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20597"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_eviscerate.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ruthless</a>: 1% more critical strike chance. A real option in Forever |
| Horde | Troll | Beast Slaying: 5% more damage to Beasts. The best pick against Beast bosses |
| Horde | Undead | Touch of the Grave got stronger. Against non-Beast bosses as good as Troll, or better unless Shadowguard procs often |

The differences between races are very small. Pick the race you enjoy more than the one that wins a fraction of a percent.

**Professions.** Tailoring suits every Priest: cloth gear with spell damage, and Forever adds new patterns, from gear for one school of magic to new belts at level 10 with spell damage. Enchanting gives you your own wands and enchants, and makes you the Enchanter of your guild. Engineering gets new schematics and new trinkets in Forever and is always a strong main profession.
