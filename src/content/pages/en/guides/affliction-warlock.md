The Affliction Warlock in WoW Forever puts damage over time on the enemy and drains its health back. Four changes shape the spec: new drain talents, Banes split off from Curses, DoTs that can crit and a <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_burningspirit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Life Tap</a> that scales with Spirit. This guide covers the build, rotation, demons and stats.

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | Damage, the most self-sufficient Warlock spec solo |
| Build at level 30 | Drain tank, 21 points in Affliction |
| Key talents | Improved Corruption, Suppression, Improved Drains, Soul Siphon |
| Strength | Self-sustain, damage on the move, group utility |
| Weakness | Slow start, stationary filler, health as a resource |
| Talent order per level | [Warlock level build](/leveling/warlock/) |

## What changes for Affliction

- **New drain talents.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=403511"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_haunting.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Drains</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17804"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Soul Siphon</a> make <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Drain Life</a> strong enough to tank with.
- **Banes are not Curses.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofsargeras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bane of Agony</a> is now a Bane, so you can add <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6205"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofmannoroth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Weakness</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1714"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseoftounges.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Tongues</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=704"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unholystrength.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Recklessness</a> on the same target.
- **DoTs can crit.** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=427712"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unstableaffliction_2.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pandemic</a> raises the critical strike bonus of your DoTs and drains.
- **Life Tap scales with Spirit.** Rank 3, from level 26, converts 140 health plus your Spirit into mana.
- **Ritual of Summoning matters more.** Meeting Stones no longer summon, so <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=698"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_twilight.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ritual of Summoning</a> is the only way to summon players.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Health and mana work as one resource | DoTs need time to pay off |
| Instant DoTs keep ticking while you move | Drain Life is a channel, you cannot move |
| DoTs on several targets at once | Every Life Tap costs health |
| Soulstone, Healthstones and summons | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1949"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_incinerate.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hellfire</a> also burns your own health |

## The drain tank build at level 30

This build uses 21 points for drain tanking. With 5 points in <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225474"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_marksmanship.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Talented</a> from the Legacy system you have 26 points at level 30. Then take <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17804"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Soul Siphon</a> to 3/3 first, and two more points in Grim Reach, which replaced Malediction on 15 October, reach 25 points in Affliction, which opens <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18271"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_shadetruesight.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Mastery</a> for the last point.

| Talent | What it does in Forever |
|---|---|
| **Improved Corruption** | At 5/5 <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=172"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_abominationexplosion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Corruption</a> becomes instant and deals 10% more damage |
| **Suppression** | 1% hit and 4% less threat per point. 4 points remove misses against enemies of your level |
| **Improved Drains** | Drain Life and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_haunting.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Drain Soul</a> deal 20% more at 3/3 |
| **Fel Concentration** | Up to 70% chance that damage does not interrupt your drains |
| **Improved Life Tap** | 20% more mana from Life Tap at 2/2 |
| **Amplify Curse** | A stronger Bane of Agony on every cooldown, needed for <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18223"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_grimward.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Exhaustion</a> |
| **Soul Siphon** | Drains deal 4% more per Affliction effect on the target per point, up to 36% at 3/3 |
| **Malediction** | 1% more periodic damage per point, Drain Life included |

**Pandemic** only raises critical strike damage. With little crit at level 30 it adds little, so it can wait. **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18265"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_requiem.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Siphon Life</a>** deals little damage, but it pays for itself on long fights and counts as an Affliction effect for Soul Siphon.

The [Warlock level build](/leveling/warlock/) on this site takes another route to level 35, with Pandemic and Malevolence for more crit on your DoTs. Both work. The drain tank gives up some damage for fewer breaks.

## Rotation

**One enemy**
1. Send your demon in.
2. Pull with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofsargeras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bane of Agony</a>. Its first ticks are weak, which gives your demon time to take threat.
3. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=172"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_abominationexplosion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Corruption</a>.
4. Cast a curse, such as <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6205"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofmannoroth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Weakness</a> on enemies that hit hard. It is your third Affliction effect for Soul Siphon.
5. Channel <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Drain Life</a> until the enemy dies. Use your wand when it is almost dead.
6. Cast <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_burningspirit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Life Tap</a> whenever you have more health than mana.

**Two enemies.** Put Bane of Agony and Corruption on the first, plus Siphon Life if you have it, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5782"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_possession.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fear</a> it. Send your demon at the second, put your DoTs on it and drain it. Fear the first again when it comes back. With a focus target and a modifier key you can cast on the feared enemy without switching targets.

**Dungeons.** Curse, then Bane of Agony and Corruption, and Drain Life on the tank's target. Skip DoTs on enemies that die within about 12 seconds. From level 50, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311680"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/warlock_curse_shadow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of the Elements</a> raises the magic damage your group deals. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=704"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unholystrength.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Curse of Recklessness</a> suits a group with mostly melee.

## Health and mana

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_burningspirit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Life Tap</a> turns health into mana, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Drain Life</a> takes that health back. With the drain talents, a full channel heals you for more than the Life Taps you need to pay for it. Two rules keep you from drinking:

- **A lot more health than mana?** Cast Life Tap. Do not stay at full health while Drain Life heals you.
- **A lot more mana than health?** Cast Drain Life. Do not shoot your wand while your mana is full.

Do not refresh DoTs early, because you lose the ticks they had left. Bane of Agony deals most of its damage at the end of its 24 seconds, so let it run out. Corruption ticks every 3 seconds.

## Demons

- **Succubus or Incubus** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=712"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonsuccubus.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Succubus</a>): the most damage. It does not hold threat without Demonology talents, but that does not matter while you drain tank. Your default solo demon.
- **Voidwalker** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=697"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonvoidwalker.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Voidwalker</a>): your tank for elites and dangerous pulls.
- **Felhunter** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=691"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonfelhunter.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Felhunter</a>): <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19244"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_mindrot.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Spell Lock</a> from level 36 and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19505"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_purge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Devour Magic</a>. Strong against casters and in PvP.
- **Imp** (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=688"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonimp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Imp</a>): little damage, but <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6307"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_bloodboil.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blood Pact</a> gives the group Stamina.

The new Move command lets you place your demon anywhere. Bind Attack and Follow to keys you can press without thinking.

## Stats and gear

**Spell damage and Shadow damage** first, then **Stamina and Spirit**, then **Intellect**. Stamina gives more health to tap, Spirit gives more mana per Life Tap. Hit and crit are rare on gear at level 30, and Haste does nothing for DoTs or channels.

- **Tailoring** has new soulbound Shadow pieces such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=253967"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_cloth_48.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Gown</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=253919"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gauntlets_27.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shadow Gloves</a>, which only the tailor can wear.
- **Weapons.** The Warlock quest for the Orb of Soran'ruk lets you choose between <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15109"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_09.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Staff of Soran'ruk</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6898"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_orb_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Orb of Soran'ruk</a>. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2549"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_25.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Staff of the Shade</a> from Razorfen Kraul is excellent if it drops.
- **Wands.** <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7001"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gravestone Scepter</a> from Blackfathom Deeps is the strongest wand before level 30. After that Drain Life deals more in this build.
