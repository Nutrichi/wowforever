The Shaman calls on the elements with totems, shocks and lightning. It can deal damage from range, fight in melee or heal, and every group wants its totems. In WoW Forever totems work for the whole raid, Fire Nova is its own spell, and Dwarves bring the Shaman to the Alliance.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Totems with stats, Windfury and resistances for the whole raid | Mana runs out fast, Elemental most of all |
| Strong area damage with Magma Totem and Fire Nova | No hard crowd control |
| Purge, an interrupt and cures for poison and disease | Totems need the right position |
| Improved Ghost Wolf for speed in combat | Few defensive cooldowns |

### Talents

### Rotation

- **Elemental:** Searing Totem down, pull with Lightning Bolt, keep Flame Shock up, fill with Lightning Bolt; on packs Magma Totem and Fire Nova. [Full rotation](/classes/shaman/elemental/).
- **Enhancement:** Lightning Shield and Windfury Weapon up, Flame Shock on the target, Stormstrike on cooldown, instant Lightning Bolt with Maelstrom Weapon. [Full rotation](/classes/shaman/enhancement/).
- **Restoration:** Chain Heal for the group, Healing Wave for the tank, the right totems down before the pull. [Full guide](/classes/shaman/restoration/).

| Horde | Troll | Berserking and survival abilities |
| Horde | Tauren | Defensive racials |

The differences are small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Leatherworking and Blacksmithing make mail and leather for every spec. Engineering gives gadgets, and Alchemy keeps a mana-hungry Shaman in potions.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Drop your totems where they reach everyone:** Windfury Totem near the melee, stat totems near the group.
- **Purge** removes a buff from an enemy, including shields and heals over time.
- **Ghost Wolf** gets you between quests fast, indoors too with the talent.
- **Watch your mana** as Elemental: drink between pulls rather than in a fight.

### Macros and UI

Many Shamans put their totems on a few buttons with macros per situation. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
