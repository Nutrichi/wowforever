Druids learn Cure Poison and Aquatic Form through two class quest chains. They visit the same zones, so do them together: pick up both at 16, or start Cure Poison at 14 and add Aquatic Form when you reach 16. Both start at your Druid trainer and run through Moonglade, which you reach with Teleport: Moonglade.

## At a glance

| Level | Learns | Alliance start | Horde start |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | Cure Poison | <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6121">Lessons Anew</a>, Mathrengyl Bearwalker, Cenarion Enclave, Darnassus (35.3 8.3) | <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6126">Lessons Anew</a>, Turak Runetotem, Elder Rise, Thunder Bluff (76.5 27.6) |

## Moonglade: the first trials

1. **A Lesson to Learn and Lessons Anew.** Teleport to Moonglade and go upstairs in the building in front of you. Hand both in to Dendrite Starblaze in Nighthaven (56.2 30.5).
2. **Trial of the Lake.** Dive into Lake Elune'ara and find a Shrine Bauble on the bottom; it has several spots. Breathe in the bubbles of the Bubbling Fissures. The bauble lasts five minutes: swim straight to the Shrine of Remulos in the northwest, use it there and hand in to Tajarri (36.6 40.2).
3. **Trial of the Sea Lion.** You need two halves of a pendant. The flight masters in Nighthaven give hints, but you can skip them; the locations are below for your faction.

## Alliance: Westfall and Darkshore

1. **Half Pendant of Aquatic Endurance:** swim far out from the west coast of Westfall to a Strange Lockbox (18.0 33.1).
2. **Half Pendant of Aquatic Agility:** swim out from the north coast of Darkshore to the second Strange Lockbox (48.9 11.3). Stay in Darkshore for the three Cure Poison quests.
3. **The Principal Source:** climb to the naga cave at the Cliffspring Falls in northeast Darkshore (54.9 33.2). Stand in the water at the mouth and use the Empty Cliffspring Falls Sampler. Hand in to Alanndarian Nightsong in Auberdine (37.7 40.7).
4. **Gathering the Cure:** bring 5 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_herb_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Earthroot</a> (Herbalism, or the Auction House in Darnassus) and 12 Lunar Fungus from the Lunar Fungal Bloom mushrooms around the moonkin camp east of Auberdine (43.1 50.5). Hand in to Alanndarian Nightsong.
5. **Curing the Sick:** use the Curative Animal Salve on 10 Sickly Deer in Darkshore; they have a bright green debuff.
6. **Back to Moonglade:** at the Shrine of Remulos, use the Half Pendant of Aquatic Agility to make the Pendant of the Sea Lion. Hand in both quests to Dendrite Starblaze, then return to Mathrengyl Bearwalker in Darnassus for Aquatic Form and Cure Poison.

## Horde: Silverpine Forest and The Barrens

1. **Half Pendant of Aquatic Endurance:** swim far out from the northwest coast of Silverpine Forest to a Strange Lockbox (29.6 29.5).
2. **Half Pendant of Aquatic Agility:** dive to the bottom of the lake in the Sludge Fen, The Barrens, for the second Strange Lockbox (56.7 8.3). Stay in The Barrens for the three Cure Poison quests.
3. **The Principal Source:** climb Dreadmist Peak in the north of The Barrens to a small green pool at the top (48.4 19.0). Stand in it and use the Empty Dreadmist Peak Sampler. Hand in to Tonga Runetotem at the Crossroads (52.2 31.9).
4. **Gathering the Cure:** bring 5 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_herb_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Earthroot</a> (Herbalism, or the Auction House) and 5 Kodo Horn from Lost Barrens Kodos around the Crossroads, mostly to the west. Hand in to Tonga Runetotem.
5. **Curing the Sick:** use the Curative Animal Salve on 10 Sickly Gazelles in the same area.
6. **Back to Moonglade:** at the Shrine of Remulos, use the Half Pendant of Aquatic Agility to make the Pendant of the Sea Lion. Hand in both quests to Dendrite Starblaze, then return to Turak Runetotem in Thunder Bluff for Aquatic Form and Cure Poison.

The Aquatic Form quest also rewards <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=263411"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_relics_idolofrejuvenation.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Idol of Shifting Tides</a>, an Idol that lowers the mana cost of every shapeshift. The other class quests and the full build are in the [Druid leveling guide](/classes/druid/leveling/), and the [Druid page](/classes/druid/) has every spec.
