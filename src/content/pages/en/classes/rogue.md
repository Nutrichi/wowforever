The Rogue is the melee damage dealer of the shadows: Stealth, poisons, combo points and more crowd control than any other class. In WoW Forever the Rogue got a large rework, with Shank, Venom, Death Mark and poisons that land critical strikes.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Very high damage on one target | Little area damage outside Blade Flurry |
| Kick, Gouge, Sap, Cheap Shot, Kidney Shot and Blind | A busy rotation with several finishers to keep up |
| Stealth to pick fights and skip trash | Depends heavily on weapons and gear |
| Poisons for every situation | Leather armor and few defensive cooldowns |

## Talents and rotation

### Talents

The [talent calculator](/talents/rogue/) has the Rogue trees of WoW Forever. Each spec guide has its builds for level 60, and the [leveling guide](/classes/rogue/leveling/) has a build for every level.

### Rotation

- **Assassination:** open with Ambush, Sinister Strike and Shank for combo points (Mutilate from 40), keep Slice and Dice up, Eviscerate at 5 combo points. [Full rotation](/classes/rogue/assassination/).
- **Combat:** open from Stealth, Sinister Strike as generator, Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush on cooldown, Slice and Dice at 5 combo points. [Full rotation](/classes/rogue/combat/).
- **Subtlety:** open with Ambush or Cheap Shot, burst while the target is controlled, finish before it recovers. [Full guide](/classes/rogue/subtlety/).

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Human | 1% more critical strike chance, also for poisons |
| Alliance | Night Elf | Elune's Light, Quickness, Shadowmeld and better Stealth |
| Alliance | Gnome | Eureka for burst and Engineering perks |
| Alliance | High Order Skyborne | 2% Haste |
| Alliance | Dwarf | Stoneform as a defensive cooldown |

On the Horde side, see the spec guides. The differences are small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Leatherworking makes good leather gear while leveling. Engineering gives gadgets, bombs and grenades that a Rogue uses well, and Skinning pairs with Leatherworking.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Sap one monster** before a pull, and fight the rest.
- **Keep both poisons up**, one on each weapon, and buy or make them before you leave town.
- **Vanish resets a bad fight**, and Evasion holds a strong melee monster for a few seconds.
- **Open locked boxes** for gold and Lockpicking skill on the way.

### Macros and UI

Most Rogues use a macro that casts Sap on a focus target and a stance-free opener macro. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
