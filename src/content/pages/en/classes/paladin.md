The Paladin is the holy warrior of WoW Forever: plate armor, a shield or a two-handed weapon, and Holy magic for healing and damage. Every group wants two of them for the blessings, the aura and the bubbles. In WoW Forever the Horde can play a Paladin too, as a Forsaken.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Holy** | Healer | Holy Shock and Light's Vigil, hard to kill, mostly reactive healing | [Guide](/classes/paladin/holy/) · [BiS](/bis/forever/preraid/paladin-holy/) |

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Blessings, an aura, Judgements and bubbles for the group | No charge, blink or sprint |
| Plate, Divine Shield and Lay on Hands: hard to kill | Mana can run out in long fights |
| Can heal itself between fights | Holy has only one area heal |
| Levels well in all three specs | Retribution depends heavily on its weapon |

## Talents and rotation

### Talents

The [talent calculator](/talents/paladin/) has the Paladin trees of WoW Forever. The spec guides have their build for level 60, and the [leveling guide](/classes/paladin/leveling/) has a build for every level.

### Rotation

- **Holy:** react to damage with the right heal, start a heal before a big hit lands and cancel it if it is not needed, keep Light's Vigil for the whole party. [Full guide](/classes/paladin/holy/).
- **Retribution:** Seal of the Crusader before the pull, open with Judgement, then Seal of Command, Judgement on cooldown, Holy Strike when ready and Consecration up. [Full rotation](/classes/paladin/retribution/).
- **Protection:** Righteous Fury on, Consecration for threat on packs, Judgement and Holy Strike on the main target.

## Leveling and class quests

### Leveling

All three specs level. Retribution is the classic choice with a large two-handed weapon and Seal of Command, and Protection is just as fast if you like to pull a lot at once. For the first 20 to 30 levels, spend your points in Retribution, even if you plan to tank later. The [Paladin leveling guide](/classes/paladin/leveling/) has the build, the key levels and the class quests.

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Human | Extra critical strike chance, the best choice |
| Alliance | Dwarf | 1% hit from Artisan's Precision, Stoneform as a defensive cooldown |
| Horde | Undead | A proc that deals damage and heals you a little |

The differences are small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Blacksmithing makes some of the best plate and mail, much of it only for blacksmiths. Mining goes with it. Engineering gives useful gadgets to every spec.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Keep the right blessing on each player:** Might for melee, Wisdom for casters and healers, Kings when you have the talent.
- **Divine Shield** gets you out of almost any bad pull, and Lay on Hands saves the tank or yourself once per fight.
- **Seal of Wisdom or Judgement of Wisdom** keeps your mana up while leveling.
- **Heal between pulls** instead of eating, and save time on every fight.

### Macros and UI

A simple macro that casts Judgement and then your seal again saves a button on every pull. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
