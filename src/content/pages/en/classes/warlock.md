The Warlock deals Shadow and Fire damage from range and always has a demon at its side. Health and mana work as one resource with Life Tap and Drain Life, which makes the Warlock one of the strongest classes to level alone. Every group wants one for Soulstones, Healthstones and summons.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Health and mana work as one resource | DoTs need time to pay off |
| A demon that tanks, deals damage or controls | If your demon dies, you lose a large part of your power |
| Soulstone, Healthstones and summons for the group | Drain Life is a channel, you cannot move |
| Fear and DoTs on several targets | Every Life Tap costs health |

## Talents and rotation

### Talents

The [talent calculator](/talents/warlock/) has the Warlock trees of WoW Forever. Each spec guide has its build for level 60, and the [leveling guide](/classes/warlock/leveling/) has a build for every level, including the level 40 build.

### Rotation

- **Affliction:** send your demon in, pull with a DoT, put all DoTs up and channel Drain Life until the enemy dies; Life Tap when mana is low. [Full rotation](/classes/warlock/affliction/).
- **Demonology:** send your demon in, brand the target, cast your DoTs and Searing Pain until it dies; every cast refreshes the Brand. [Full rotation](/classes/warlock/demonology/).
- **Destruction:** Immolate and Corruption, then Shadow Bolt as filler and Conflagrate when ready. [Full rotation](/classes/warlock/destruction/).

## Leveling and class quests

### Leveling

Level as Affliction: DoTs, a Voidwalker and Drain Life let you fight without stopping. The [Warlock leveling guide](/classes/warlock/leveling/) has the build for every level, the key levels, demons and wands.

### Class quests

A Warlock learns most demons from class quests: the Voidwalker at level 10, the Succubus or Incubus at 20 and the Felhunter at 30. At level 40 Summon Felsteed is free, see the [Felsteed guide](/guides/mounts/felsteed/). The steps per faction are in the [leveling guide](/classes/warlock/leveling/).

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Horde | Undead | Cannibalize, mana and Will of the Forsaken against Fear |
| Horde | Orc | Blood Fury for spell power and more pet damage |
| Horde | Troll | A strong heal over time |
| Alliance | Human | 10% more Spirit and 1% more critical strike chance |
| Alliance | Gnome | More mana and a short window of stronger, cheaper spells |

The differences are small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Tailoring has new soulbound Shadow pieces in WoW Forever that only the tailor can wear. Enchanting makes your own wands and enchants.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Life Tap between pulls** and drink to fill both bars at once.
- **Keep a Soulstone on the healer** in dungeons.
- **Fear only when it is safe:** a feared monster can pull more.
- **Use your Voidwalker's Sacrifice** as an emergency shield.

### Macros and UI

A pet attack macro on your first DoT saves a button on every pull. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
