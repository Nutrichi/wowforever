The Priest is the healer of WoW Forever, with shields, heals over time and some of the strongest buffs in the game. In Shadow it turns to damage over time and heals the group while it fights. Every Priest also learns racial spells that only their race gets.

## Specs and roles

| Spec | Role | In short | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discipline** | Healer | Shields, Penance, Divine Aegis and Power Infusion, a strong tank healer | [Guide](/classes/priest/discipline/) · [BiS](/bis/forever/preraid/priest-discipline/) |
| **Holy** | Healer | Prayer of Mending and Litany of Light, a solid raid healer | [Guide](/classes/priest/holy/) · [BiS](/bis/forever/preraid/priest-holy/) |
| **Shadow** | Ranged damage | Two DoTs, Mind Flay and Vampiric Embrace for the group | [Guide](/classes/priest/shadow/) · [BiS](/bis/forever/preraid/priest-shadow/) |

All builds open in the [talent calculator](/talents/priest/).

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Strong healers in both healing specs | Almost no way to move fast |
| Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit and Power Infusion | Low health and cloth armor |
| Fear Ward, Dispel Magic and Abolish Disease | Shadow needs time to ramp up its damage |
| Shadow heals the party with Vampiric Embrace | Discipline is less effective when the whole group takes damage |

### Talents

### Rotation

- **Discipline:** shield the tank, Penance for burst healing, Flash Heal and Greater Heal for the rest, Power Infusion on your strongest damage dealer. [Full guide](/classes/priest/discipline/).
- **Holy:** Renew and Prayer of Mending on the tank, Prayer of Healing and Holy Nova for the group, Greater Heal for big hits. [Full guide](/classes/priest/holy/).
- **Shadow:** keep Devouring Plague and Shadow Word: Pain up, Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Death when ready, Vampiric Embrace up, Mind Flay as filler. [Full rotation](/classes/priest/shadow/).

## Leveling and class quests

### Leveling

The first 20 to 30 levels barely differ: get a wand as soon as you can, keep your health up with shields and heals, and let the wand kill. After that, Discipline keeps the calm style and adds Penance at 30, and Shadow kills faster with Mind Flay and Mind Blast. The [Priest leveling guide](/classes/priest/leveling/) has the build, the key levels and the wands.

### Class quests

Priests learn their racial spells from class quests: the first at level 10 and the second at level 20. Which spells you get depends on your race. The steps per race are in the [leveling guide](/classes/priest/leveling/).

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Gnome | Eureka for mana, a bigger mana pool and Contingency Plan as an emergency heal |
| Alliance | Human | Critical strike chance, and Divine Grace removes Weakened Soul to shield again |
| Horde | Undead | Dark Sacrifice is a very good mana tool |

The differences are very small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Tailoring gives you cloth gear, and WoW Forever adds new patterns, such as belts with healing power at level 10. Enchanting makes your own wands and enchants, which helps a lot while leveling.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Get a wand early.** Enchanting makes the Lesser Magic Wand at level 5 and the Greater Magic Wand at 13.
- **Shield before the pull** so the first hits do not interrupt your casts.
- **Fear Ward the tank** before a boss that fears.
- **Drink between pulls** instead of fighting at low mana: a Priest without mana has nothing left.

### Macros and UI

Healers do best with mouseover macros on their unit frames, so you heal without changing target. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
