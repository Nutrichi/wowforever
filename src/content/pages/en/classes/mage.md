The Mage deals damage from range with Arcane, Fire and Frost spells, and controls the fight with slows, freezes and Polymorph. Every group wants one for the food, water and portals. In WoW Forever each tree plays differently: Arcane builds stacks of Arcane Blast, Fire turns critical strikes into Pyroblasts, and Frost shatters frozen targets with Ice Lance.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| High damage from range, on one target or a whole pack | Low health and cloth armor |
| Slows, freezes and Polymorph keep enemies away | Area spells drain mana fast |
| Food, water, teleports and portals for the group | Damage swings with procs in all three trees |
| Frost is safe and fast to level | Fire and Arcane need gear before they shine |

## Talents and rotation

### Talents

The [talent calculator](/talents/mage/) has the Mage trees of WoW Forever. Each spec guide has builds for level 60. The [leveling guide](/classes/mage/leveling/) has a build for every level.

### Rotation

- **Arcane:** build Arcane Blast stacks, spend Clearcasting on Arcane Blast and Missile Barrage on Arcane Missiles, cast Arcane Wrath at 3 or 4 stacks. [Full rotation](/classes/mage/arcane/).
- **Fire:** Scorch up to 5 stacks of Improved Scorch, Fire Blast on cooldown, Fireball as filler and Pyroblast at 3 stacks of Heating Up. [Full rotation](/classes/mage/fire/).
- **Frost:** Frostbolt as main spell, Ice Lance on Fingers of Frost or a frozen target, Frost Nova and Ice Lance when the enemy gets close. [Full rotation](/classes/mage/frost/).

## Leveling and class quests

### Leveling

Level as Frost. It slows every target, freezes whole packs and in WoW Forever it is also the fastest: Ice Lance deals far more damage to frozen targets. Fire spends the least mana but needs critical strike chance, and Arcane depends on procs and gear. The [Mage leveling guide](/classes/mage/leveling/) has the build, the key levels, AoE pulls and mana tips.

### Class quests

The Mage has no form quests like the Druid. What a Mage learns besides the trainer: teleports from level 20 and portals from level 40 at a Portal Trainer, see [portals and teleports](/classes/mage/portals/), and scrolls with Comprehend Scroll from level 6, see [Mage scrolls](/classes/mage/scrolls/).

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Horde | Troll | Berserking: faster casts, plus a strong heal over time |
| Horde | Orc | Blood Fury: more spell power, and less magic damage taken |
| Horde | Undead | Strongest in PvP |
| Alliance | Human | 1% more critical strike chance and 10% more Spirit |
| Alliance | High Order Skyborne | 2% Haste and extra mana regeneration, also with Evocation |

The differences are small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Tailoring makes some of the best cloth gear for a Mage, including the Belt of the Archmage and Robe of the Archmage. Enchanting goes with Tailoring and makes the wands. Engineering and Alchemy give gadgets and potions for every spec.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Conjure food and water** before you leave town, and hand some out to your group.
- **Polymorph one monster** in a dangerous pull, and never break it with an area spell.
- **Blink away** when a melee monster reaches you, then Frost Nova and step back.
- **Evocation** gives back a large part of your mana: use it before you drink in a long fight.

### Macros and UI

Many Mages use a focus or mouseover macro for Polymorph, and a macro that casts Frost Nova and then Blink. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
