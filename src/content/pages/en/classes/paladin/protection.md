The Protection Paladin in WoW Forever is the tank with a shield, a mana bar and Holy damage. For years the spec missed one thing to be welcome in raids: a taunt. Forever fixes that with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20423"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_retributionaura.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Fury</a>, and the talent trees add damage reduction and help with mana. This guide covers the changes, six builds for level 60, the rotation, cooldowns, seals, stats and races.

|---|---|

| Standard build | 0/35/16 |

| Strength | Magic damage, up to four targets, group buffs |
| Weakness | Snap threat, mana in long fights, crushing blows |

## What changes for the tank

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20423"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_retributionaura.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Fury</a>** is new. Your melee attacks deal extra Holy damage, and with a shield every hit gives you an absorb shield worth half that damage. Judgement with this seal active deals Holy damage and **taunts** the target for 4 seconds.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311015"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_shieldofthetemplar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Templar's Bulwark</a>** is your only defensive cooldown: an absorb shield equal to 100% of your maximum health for 8 seconds. It applies Forbearance, so no Divine Shield right after.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310994"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_judgementred.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Swift Judgement</a>** is a second Judgement on a 1-minute cooldown that does not share a cooldown with Judgement. Use it for extra threat in the opener, to keep threat in a bubble, or as a backup taunt.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17143"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_thunderbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Strike</a>** is an instant strike that is now part of every rotation. With <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1328544"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_greaterblessingofsanctuary.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shieldsworn</a> its cooldown is 4 seconds shorter, it strikes with your shield, triggers Holy Shield and Shield Specialization without using their charges, and lets you block spells for 6 seconds. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311034"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_improvedresistanceauras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Iron Creed</a> adds 30% threat and 6% less damage taken for 6 seconds while Righteous Fury is active, so you can keep it up all the time.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20923"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_innerfire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Consecration</a>** is weaker on big packs: little damage to everything, extra damage to the first 4 enemies that enter it. Your AoE now works like a Warrior's or Druid's.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20468"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_sealoffury.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Righteous Fury</a>** now lowers all damage you take by 9% while Righteous Fury is active, instead of raising threat. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20096"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_magic_lesserinvisibilty.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Anticipation</a> gives 20 Defense instead of 10, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20148"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shield_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shield Specialization</a> gives mana back on blocks, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20177"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_blessingofstrength.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Reckoning</a> can also trigger when you block.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| The best tank against magic damage | Weak snap threat, unless you go into the Holy tree |
| Tanks up to four targets with Consecration and Retribution Aura | Mana can run low, also when you are not being hit |
| Blessings, Cleanse and Blessing of Protection for the group | Few tools to push crits and crushing blows off the table |
| Divine Shield plus a taunt: tank a boss while immune | No percentage damage reduction cooldown |

## Builds for level 60

Six builds, each with the 51 talent points of level 60. Threat and survival are a scale: most builds sit in the middle, a few lean to one side. Every build opens in our talent calculator.

### Standard build, 0/35/16

Your default build for raids. It takes every strong defensive talent in Protection, Holy Shield, Reckoning, Iron Creed and Shieldsworn, plus <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311087"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sacred Arbiter</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26022"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_persuitofjustice.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pursuit of Justice</a> in Retribution. Pursuit of Justice frees your boot slot for any enchant and still gives more speed than Minor Speed. [Open the standard build in the talent calculator](/talents/paladin/?t=-05302102313501531-50233000201).

It skips two points of <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20143"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_devotion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Toughness</a>: before the Defense cap, Toughness and Anticipation lower damage by about the same amount, and Anticipation makes it smoother. Sacred Arbiter keeps your Judgement debuffs up; you only need it when your raid has a Holy Paladin and no Retribution Paladin.

### Dungeon build, 0/32/19

Almost the same, but without Anticipation: Defense matters less against dungeon monsters. The points go to <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20174"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_sealofprotection.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Guardian's Favor</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=9452"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_vindication.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Vindication</a>. The gain over the standard build is small, so do not pay for a respec if you already have it. [Open the dungeon build](/talents/paladin/?t=-05322102313001531-50233300201).

### Shockprot build, 23/28/0

More threat on one target. You go into Holy for <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311606"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_searinglight.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Shock</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310905"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_innerfire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Consecrated Ground</a> (10% more Holy damage), <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310904"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_mindsooth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Divine Precision</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1237268"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_devineaegis.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Conduit</a>, the most mana-efficient talent in the game. You give up some defense, so keep it for fights that need the threat. [Open the Shockprot build](/talents/paladin/?t=05025211220021-0530210221310152).

### Retprot build, 0/21/30

Slightly more defensive than Shockprot, with 5 points in <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20060"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_parry.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Deflection</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=9452"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_vindication.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Vindication</a>, and strong on several targets thanks to <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311084"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_enlightenedjudgements.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Champion of the Light</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20049"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_racial_avatar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Vengeance</a>. It scales well with Intellect. [Open the Retprot build](/talents/paladin/?t=-05302102213101-5223330120100035).

### Offensive off-tank build, 23/0/28

No Protection talents at all: Holy for Holy Shock, Retribution for Vengeance and Champion of the Light. Only Deflection is defensive. Use it as a second tank who mainly deals damage, or for fights where the damage cannot be blocked anyway. You can even switch to a two-handed weapon when the fight no longer needs a second tank. [Open the off-tank build](/talents/paladin/?t=05025211220021--5523300120100033).

### PvP build, 23/28/0

A Shockadin who also heals. Stun with Hammer of Justice, then Consecration, Divine Favor, Holy Shock plus Judgement, and Holy Strike plus Swift Judgement plus Judgement. Templar's Bulwark, Sacred Duty and Guardian's Favor let you put a Forbearance spell on yourself every minute. [Open the PvP build](/talents/paladin/?t=50325210020021-503221300130215).

## Rotation

Protection is a slow, steady tank. About 30% of your damage on one target comes from melee with Seal of Fury; the rest comes from Consecration, Holy Shield and Holy Strike, each on cooldown when your mana allows. Before the pull, put up Righteous Fury and Retribution Aura. Open on your assigned seal debuff, such as Judgement of Wisdom, unless the target dies within 20 to 30 seconds.

**One target**
1. Keep <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20163"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_retributionaura.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Fury</a> up while you tank.
2. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20271"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_righteousfury.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Judgement</a> on cooldown: it is off the global cooldown.
3. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26573"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_innerfire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Consecration</a>, if your mana allows it.
4. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20930"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_searinglight.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Shock</a>, if you have it.
5. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20928"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_blessingofprotection.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Shield</a>.
6. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10333"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/classicon_paladin.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Strike</a>. Try to fit two Holy Strikes into one Holy Shield window.
7. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=879"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_excorcism_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Exorcism</a> on Undead or Demons.
8. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=24239"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_thunderclap.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hammer of Wrath</a> when you tank one target or none.

**Several targets.** The same, but on Undead or Demons <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10318"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_excorcism.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Wrath</a> comes right after Consecration, and Holy Shield goes before Holy Shock.

**Opener.** Assigned seal and Holy Shield before the pull, Judgement, Seal of Fury, Judgement plus Swift Judgement, Holy Strike, Exorcism on Undead or Demons, then Consecration. With the Shockprot build, add Divine Favor before the pull and Consecration plus Holy Shock before Holy Strike.

## Cooldowns and utility

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311015"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_shieldofthetemplar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Templar's Bulwark</a>**: your answer to Shield Wall. An absorb shield for your full health for 8 seconds, a 5-minute cooldown, 4 minutes with Sacred Duty.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10310"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_layonhands.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lay on Hands</a>**: heals you or a friend for your full health and uses all your mana. - **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1020"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_divineintervention.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Divine Shield</a>**: 12 seconds immune, with 50% less damage dealt. Perfect for a Mage or Warlock who pulled aggro with AoE.

## Stats and gear

Threat and survival are a scale, so start balanced. The balanced priority:

1. Stamina
2. Spell power, your main threat stat: Righteous Fury only boosts Holy damage
3. Melee hit up to 8%
4. Defense up to 440, the point where a boss can no longer crit you
5. Armor
6. Expertise up to 6.5%, the soft cap
7. Agility and block chance
8. Defense above 440, parry and dodge
9. Critical strike chance, spell hit up to 17%, expertise up to 14%
10. Haste, Strength and Intellect

For a more defensive tank, put Defense and Armor before spell power and hit. For more threat, put spell power, hit, expertise and crit before Stamina. As a rough guide, 1% hit before the melee cap is worth about 21 spell power, 1% expertise before the soft cap about 15 and 1% crit about 12.

**Precision** gives 3% hit to melee and spells. In the Shockprot build, take only 2 of 3 points in Divine Precision, or you overshoot.

**Block chance** matters more than it looks: Holy Shield and Shield Specialization trigger on blocks, and a blocked hit cannot crit or crush. Pick gear on the whole item, not on what a tank item is supposed to look like: the [pre-raid BiS list](/bis/forever/preraid/paladin-protection/) has the pieces.

**Enchants and consumables.** Stamina on chest, shield and boots, spell power from armor kits (Mystic Rugged Armor Kit at level 50) and Enchant Necklace - Spell Power. Wizard Oil, Arcane Elixirs and spell power food give threat; the food also gives 5% more experience from kills while you level. Shield spikes from Blacksmithing add damage on every block.
