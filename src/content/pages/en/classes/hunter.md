The Hunter fights from range with a bow, gun or crossbow, and from level 10 with a pet at its side. It is one of the easiest classes to level and one of the strongest on its own. In WoW Forever each spec plays differently: Beast Mastery leans on its pets, Marksmanship sends its pet away for more damage, and Survival weaves melee attacks into its shots.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Excellent solo, with a pet that holds threat | A dead pet costs a lot of damage for Beast Mastery |
| A short rotation, easy to learn | The dead zone between melee and 8 yards |
| Very high damage for Marksmanship and Survival | Marksmanship and Survival have no pet to save a bad pull |
| Traps, Feign Death and Freezing Trap for control | Survival's melee weaving asks for precise timing |

## Talents and rotation

### Talents

The [talent calculator](/talents/hunter/) has the Hunter trees of WoW Forever. Each spec guide has its build for level 60 and explains the key talents. The [leveling guide](/classes/hunter/leveling/) has a build for every level.

### Rotation

- **Beast Mastery:** Serpent Sting, Aimed Shot when ready, keep two hawks out with Summon Hawk, Arcane Shot in between, Rapid Fire and Bestial Wrath on cooldown. [Full rotation](/classes/hunter/beast-mastery/).
- **Marksmanship:** open with Sniper Shot, keep Serpent Sting up, Aimed Shot and Arcane Shot when ready, Rapid Fire on cooldown. [Full rotation](/classes/hunter/marksmanship/).
- **Survival:** shoot between swings and weave melee attacks into your Auto Shot timer, or stay in melee the whole fight. [Full rotation](/classes/hunter/survival/).

## Leveling and class quests

### Leveling

All three trees level well. Beast Mastery is the smoothest: the pet deals more damage, survives more and holds the monster while you shoot. Marksmanship deals more damage from your own shots. The [Hunter leveling guide](/classes/hunter/leveling/) has the build, the key levels, pets and ammunition.

### Class quests

At level 10 the Hunter class quests teach you to tame a beast and keep a pet. After that every beast you meet can become your pet, each with its own family and abilities. The steps per faction are in the [leveling guide](/classes/hunter/leveling/).

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | High Order Skyborne | A haste bonus, rare on gear, the strongest choice |
| Alliance | Human | 2% more critical strike chance, which the pet gets too |
| Alliance | Dwarf | 1% hit from Artisan's Precision |
| Alliance | Night Elf | A critical strike cooldown |
| Horde | Windshaper Skyborne | A haste bonus and a movement speed cooldown |

The differences are small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Leatherworking makes mail and leather pieces for leveling and some of the best gear before raids. Engineering gives scopes, ammunition and gadgets, and Skinning goes well with a class that kills beasts all day.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Step back from melee.** The dead zone is the most common reason a Hunter stops shooting.
- **Feed your pet** and keep it happy: an unhappy pet deals less damage.
- **Feign Death drops threat** and resets a bad pull in a dungeon.
- **Trap before the pull.** Freezing Trap takes one monster out of the fight while the group kills the rest.
- **Carry enough ammunition** and a stack of pet food when you leave a city.

### Macros and UI

Most Hunters bind a pet attack macro to their opening shot, and a macro that casts Feign Death and then places a Freezing Trap. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
