The Druid is the hybrid of WoW Forever. In Bear Form it tanks, in Cat Form it deals melee damage, as a Moonkin it casts, and out of form it heals. No other class can fill every role, and you can switch between them in the middle of a fight. The price is that each spec is a little behind the classes that do only one thing.

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Fills every role: tank, melee, ranged and healer | Each spec deals a little less than a pure class |
| Mark of the Wild, Thorns, Innervate and a battle resurrection | Shapeshifting costs mana, and forms block spells |

## Talents and rotation

### Talents

The [talent calculator](/talents/druid/) has the Druid trees of WoW Forever. Each spec guide has three builds for level 60, one for raids and dungeons, one for leveling and one for PvP. To level, almost every Druid takes the Feral build from level 10; see the [leveling guide](/classes/druid/leveling/).

### Rotation

- **Balance:** keep Moonfire and Insect Swarm up, then cast Wrath to build Eclipse and spend it on two fast Starfires. [Full rotation](/classes/druid/balance/).
- **Feral, Cat:** open from Prowl, build combo points with Shred or Claw, keep Rake and Rip up and finish with Ferocious Bite. [Full rotation](/classes/druid/feral/).
- **Feral, Bear:** Maul and Swipe for threat, Demoralizing Roar on every pull, Frenzied Regeneration when health drops. [Full rotation](/classes/druid/feral/).
- **Restoration:** Rejuvenation and Regrowth on the tank, Wild Growth when the group takes damage, Swiftmend and Nature's Swiftness for emergencies. [Full guide](/classes/druid/restoration/).

## Leveling and class quests

### Leveling

Level as Feral. A cat hardly needs to stop for mana or healing, and Prowl, Dash and Feral Swiftness carry you from one quest to the next. The start is slower: you get Bear Form at 10 and Cat Form at 20. The [Druid leveling guide](/classes/druid/leveling/) has the build for every level, the key levels, gear, weapons and Idols. Skyborne Druids start with a step-by-step route through [Thendal Village](/guides/leveling/skyborne-druid-1-6/).

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Night Elf | Elune's Light: more critical strike chance for 15 seconds |
| Alliance | High Order Skyborne | 2% Haste and a strong mana regeneration cooldown |
| Horde | Tauren | 5% more Health and War Stomp, an area stun |
| Horde | Windshaper Skyborne | 2% Haste and a run speed cooldown |

The differences are small, so pick the race you like. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Leatherworking makes some of the best leather gear for every Druid spec, part of it only for leatherworkers. Herbalism suits a Druid, because you gather in Travel Form without dismounting. Alchemy turns those herbs into your own potions and elixirs, and Engineering gives useful gadgets in every role.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Swap forms to escape.** Shifting removes slows and roots, so a quick shift out and back breaks most snares.
- **Gather in form.** Herbs can be picked in Travel Form, which saves a mount on every node.
- **Keep an Idol for each role** in your bags and swap it with your spec.
- **Innervate yourself** when you need the mana more than your healer does.
- **Cast Rebirth** on a fallen healer or tank in a hard fight: it brings them back in combat.

### Macros and UI

A few macros save a lot of clicks: one button that shifts into Cat Form and casts Prowl, one that cancels a form and casts a heal, and a mouseover macro for Rejuvenation as a healer. The [Restoration guide](/classes/druid/restoration/#macros) has ready-made healer macros, and our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
