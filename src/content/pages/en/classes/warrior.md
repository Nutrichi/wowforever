The Warrior fights in plate with Rage, a resource that grows when it deals or takes damage. It is the main tank of WoW Forever and a strong melee damage dealer, with three stances for three jobs. Every race can play one.

## Specs and roles

| Spec | Role | In short | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arms** | Melee damage | Slow two-handed hits, Sweeping Strikes and Mortal Strike, best for battlegrounds | [Guide](/classes/warrior/arms/) · [BiS](/bis/forever/preraid/warrior-arms/) |
| **Fury** | Melee damage | Dual wield, Bloodthirst, Whirlwind and Death Wish, unmatched against packs | [Guide](/classes/warrior/fury/) · [BiS](/bis/forever/preraid/warrior-fury/) |
| **Protection** | Tank | Shield Slam, Revenge and Sunder Armor, the main tank | [Guide](/classes/warrior/protection/) · [BiS](/bis/forever/preraid/warrior-protection/) |

## Strengths and weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| The main tank of the game | Rage comes slowly with bad gear |
| Strong cleave with Sweeping Strikes, Cleave and Whirlwind | Depends heavily on weapons and gear |
| Every race can play one | Needs healing or food after almost every fight while leveling |
| Big burst with Recklessness and Death Wish | Fury and Berserker Stance make you easier to kill |

## Talents and rotation

### Talents

The [talent calculator](/talents/warrior/) has the Warrior trees of WoW Forever. Each spec guide has its build for level 60, and the [leveling guide](/classes/warrior/leveling/) has a build for every level.

### Rotation

- **Arms:** Battle Shout up, Sunder Armor, Overpower whenever it lights up, Rend, Mortal Strike, extra Rage into Heroic Strike or Cleave. [Full rotation](/classes/warrior/arms/).
- **Fury:** Battle Shout up, Bloodthirst and Whirlwind on cooldown, Execute when the target is low, extra Rage into Heroic Strike or Cleave. [Full rotation](/classes/warrior/fury/).
- **Protection:** Shield Slam and Revenge on cooldown, Sunder Armor for threat, Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout on packs. [Full guide](/classes/warrior/protection/).

## Stats and gear

### Stat priority

| Spec | Stats, most important first |
|---|---|
| **Arms** | Strength and attack power, hit, critical strike chance |
| **Fury** | Hit, critical strike chance, Strength and attack power |
| **Protection** | Stamina, defense, armor, avoidance, then threat stats |

1 Strength gives 2 attack power, so Strength is worth more per point than attack power.

## Races and professions

### Races and racials

| Faction | Race | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Human | 1% more critical strike chance |
| Alliance | Dwarf | 1% hit from Artisan's Precision |
| Alliance | Gnome | A burst cooldown that can be very strong |
| Alliance | Night Elf | Elune's Light for Rage and damage on demand |

Every race can play a Warrior; see the spec guides for the Horde races. All racials are on the [races and classes page](/guides/races-and-classes/).

### Professions

Blacksmithing and Mining make plate armor and weapons. Engineering gives gadgets and grenades that a Warrior uses well, and First Aid saves food and downtime.

## Tips, macros and UI

### Tips and tricks

- **Charge in, then swap stance** when you need Overpower or Defensive Stance.
- **Never waste Rage:** Heroic Strike or Cleave takes the extra.
- **Eat and bandage** between pulls: a Warrior heals only with food and First Aid.
- **Pull with a ranged weapon** to bring one monster to you.

### Macros and UI

A stance macro that swaps stance and casts the ability in one press saves a lot of clicks. Our [UI tools guide](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/) shows the Cooldown Manager and the other built-in tools of WoW Forever.
