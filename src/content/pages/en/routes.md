A leveling route is the order of zones and quests that gets a character to the level cap with the least wasted time. Every expansion of WoW Classic has its own route, and each route has its own page with the zones per faction, the dungeons worth a run and the habits that save the most time.

Each class also has its own guide per expansion on [Classes](/classes/), with talents per level and a rotation.

## What works in every expansion

- **Leave a zone on time.** Once most quests turn green, they give far less experience, and the next zone gives more.
- **Group quests close together.** Pick up everything in a hub, finish what lies on the same path, and hand it all in at once.
- **Collect dungeon quests first.** One run of a dungeon with its quests gives much more experience than the same run without them.
- **Use rested experience.** Logging out in an inn or a capital city doubles the experience from kills for a while.
- **Avoid deaths.** A corpse run costs more time than a careful pull.
- **Save gold for travel.** The mount at level 40 in Classic Era and flying in later expansions change how fast you move.

## Addons that help

- **Questie** is free and shows quests and their objectives on the map and the minimap. It does not choose the order.
- **Guidelime** is free and shows routes step by step; how good a route is depends on who wrote it.
- **RestedXP** and **Zygor** are paid addons that show a full route step by step, with an arrow to the next objective.
