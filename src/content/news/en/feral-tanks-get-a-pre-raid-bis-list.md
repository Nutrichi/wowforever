Bear tanks have their own list on our [BiS page](/bis/) for World of Warcraft: Forever now. The [Feral tank list](/bis/forever/preraid/druid-feral-tank/) shows the best pre-raid gear at level 60, separate from the [Cat Form list](/bis/forever/preraid/druid-feral/). That makes 27 of the 28 specs.

## The Feral tank list

Most slots go to Feralheart, the Tier 0.5 set for Druids: head, shoulders, chest, wrists, legs and feet. The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9449"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_mace_14.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Manual Crowd Pummeler</a> is the top weapon, with the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18531"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_22.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Unyielding Maul</a> as the second option. Only Assassination Rogue is still "Coming soon".

## Five updated lists

These lists follow the latest changes, with new picks in several slots:

- [Restoration Druid](/bis/forever/preraid/druid-restoration/): new rings and Tier 0.5 in four slots.
- [Marksmanship](/bis/forever/preraid/hunter-marksmanship/): a new top pick for the ranged slot.
- [Survival](/bis/forever/preraid/hunter-survival/): six slots changed, including trinkets and the ranged weapon.
- [Retribution](/bis/forever/preraid/paladin-retribution/): Soulforge, the Tier 0.5 set for Paladins, is now first in five slots.
- [Shadow](/bis/forever/preraid/priest-shadow/): a new main hand, off hand and staff, and a wand slot.

Every list shows the date of its last update, and up to three options per slot with where the item comes from.
