Players with a recurring 12-month World of Warcraft subscription are receiving new rewards in the Gifts section of the Battle.net app. For WoW Forever, that is a tabard, a backpack and a murloc pet. Blizzard confirmed the offer for Europe and America on 15 October.

## What Forever players get

The Forever rewards are the <a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=285352"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/8119728.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tabard of Eternal Exploration</a>, the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=285353"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_cape_special_camping_d_01_green.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Eternal Explorer's Backpack</a>, which is a cloak transmog, and the companion pet <a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=285354"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pet_babymurlocs_blue.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Azurky</a>, a blue murloc with a banner. All three carry the same blue and gold emblem. WoW Forever itself launches on 4 November.

The Forever part of the bundle also shows a blacked-out pet in the shape of a goat. What it is and when it arrives, Blizzard does not say. 2027 is the Year of the Goat in the Chinese calendar. Blizzard calls it the six-month bonus for Lunar New Year, to be revealed and delivered by 31 January 2027.

## Blizzard confirms the offer

On 15 October Blizzard [announced the offer](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24310658/unlock-a-year-of-world-of-warcraft-and-get-bonus-treasures) for Europe and America as well. It counts for a 12-month subscription or 365 days of game time, and for the special 6-month and 12-month subscription offers. The bonuses arrive by 31 October 2026 and must be redeemed by 30 April 2027. The tabard and the backpack are transmog, and the three rewards only work in Forever.

Players who already pay for 12 months keep auto-renewal on with a future renewal date. They then get both the 12-month and the 6-month bonus at no extra cost, through the Battle.net app.

## The other game versions

Retail players get two mounts, the Ashen Dreadglider and the Giant Grrloc, plus a blacked-out goat mount for later. In MoP Classic, the reward is the Cerulean Drake, with the same blacked-out goat pet as in Forever.

The Sunflare Driftmoth mount and the Sunflutter Driftmoth battle pet are not part of this batch. They were the six-month reward in July and have already been handed out.
