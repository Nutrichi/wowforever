A leaked Blizzard timeline mentions a project called Camelot in the third quarter of 2026. The same document correctly listed The War Within, MoP Classic, the TBC Anniversary and Midnight. Blizzard has not confirmed it. Many players expect a Classic+ version.

The timeline itself does not say what Camelot is. The expectation of a Classic+ version comes from the community, which has been asking for new content in the original world since 2019.

Datamined references show a version jump from 1.15 to 1.60, according to dataminers. Store files also contain separate licenses for a Heroic and an Epic edition. Blizzard has confirmed neither.

Content creators visited the Blizzard campus under a non-disclosure agreement shortly before BlizzCon 2026, according to reports from players. What they saw there is not known.

Whether Camelot comes with fresh realms, and when Blizzard would announce it, is unknown.
