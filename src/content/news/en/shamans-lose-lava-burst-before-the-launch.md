Elemental Shamans in World of Warcraft: Forever lose Lava Burst, a day before the launch. Blizzard removes the talent in the development notes of 2 November and puts Call of Thunder in its place. Lightning becomes the heart of the spec: Lightning Bolt hits much harder and Lightning Overload triggers three times as often.

Lava Burst was one of the big new talents in the [class deep dive of October](/news/mages-get-frostfire-bolt-shamans-get-lava-burst/). Blizzard does not explain the removal itself. In general, it writes that some changes undo solutions it took too quickly from Burning Crusade and Wrath of the Lich King, instead of new ones that fit Forever.

## Elemental

| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lava Burst** | Removed. **Call of Thunder** takes its place: 5% more critical strike chance for Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning |
| **Lightning Overload** | 10, 20 or 30% chance at a copy of the spell (was 3, 7 or 10%) |
| **Lightning Mastery** | The new name of Elemental Mastery, with a new icon |
| **Lightning Bolt** | More base damage on every rank, 80% more at the top rank. About 78% of the Classic Era damage, with a shorter cast time |
| **Chain Lightning** | 2.5 second cast. The first target takes about 145% of a Lightning Bolt of the same rank, for about 225% of the mana |
| **Shocks** | Back to the Classic Era damage, higher than in the beta |

## Enhancement

| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Stormstrike** | 150% normalized weapon damage (was 100%), 6 second cooldown (was 8) |
| **Maelstrom Weapon** | Triggers twice as often |
| **Mental Dexterity** | 50, 100 or 150% of your Intellect as attack power (was 33, 67 or 100%) |
| **Flurry** | Up to 30% attack speed (was 25%) |
| **Improved Ghost Wolf** | Moves from row 2 to row 4 |

Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem now show the extra swing as an animation.

## Restoration and totems

**Riptide** costs about 40% less mana but has a 12 second cooldown (was 6) and no longer heals right away: only the heal over time remains. **Totemic Projection** is now called **Totemic Call**. It brings your totems to where you stand and has a 30 second cooldown. Elemental Reach no longer shortens it.
