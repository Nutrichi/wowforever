Blizzard has published the numbers of the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever. Testers created 8,862,354 characters and scored 9,959,014 PvP kills. The Hall of Thanes was the deadliest dungeon, and the Kobold Miner the deadliest creature.

The numbers appeared on X on 30 October, a week after the beta closed.

## All nine numbers

| Statistic | Result |
|---|---|
| **Characters created** | 8,862,354 |
| **PvP kills** | 9,959,014 |
| **Skyborne** | 1,889,084 |
| **Duels fought** | 1,248,125 |
| **Deaths from falling** | 1,157,204 |
| **Forsaken Paladins** | 612,096 |
| **Deadliest dungeon** | The Hall of Thanes |
| **Deadliest creature** | Kobold Miner |
| **Zone with the most PvP** | Hillsbrad Foothills |

If the Skyborne and Forsaken Paladin figures count characters, about one in five characters was a Skyborne and about 7% a Forsaken Paladin. The card does not say so explicitly. Falling killed almost as many characters as there were duels.

The [Hall of Thanes](/guides/dungeons/hall-of-thanes/) is a dungeon for levels 13 to 18, one of the first that players run. Blizzard does not give a death count for it or for the Kobold Miner.

How many players made those characters is not known. Testers can still look up their own numbers on the [personal statistics page](/news/a-blizzard-page-shows-your-own-beta-statistics/), as far as Blizzard keeps it online. WoW Forever launches on 5 November at 00:00 Belgian time.
