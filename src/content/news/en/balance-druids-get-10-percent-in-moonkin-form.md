Balance Druids get a surprise in World of Warcraft: Forever. Blizzard reworked part of the Balance tree, and the changes are already live. The biggest one: Moonkin Form now raises your Arcane and Nature damage by 10%.

## What changes

| Talent | Change |
|---|---|
| Improved Wrath | Now called **Starlight Wrath**. Also makes Starfire cast 0.1 seconds faster per point, 0.5 seconds at 5/5 |
| Improved Moonfire | Now called **Moon and Stars**. Also gives Starfire a 50/100% chance not to lose cast time when you take damage |
| Improved Starfire | 2 points instead of 5, with an 8/15% chance |
| Eclipse | Also raises the critical strike chance of Wrath by 7/13/20%, at all times |
| Moonfury | Removed. **Dreamstate** takes its place: 20/40/60/80/100% of the effect of Innervate when you cast it on someone else |
| Moonkin Form | Also raises your Arcane and Nature damage by 10% |

Did you have 3 or more points in Improved Starfire? Then all your talent points were reset, so visit the talent window before your next fight.

## Why Blizzard does this

Starfire needed too many points to be worth casting, and many Balance builds leaned on Nature's Focus from the Restoration tree. With the faster Starfire in Starlight Wrath and the shorter Improved Starfire, you need fewer points for the same rotation. Blizzard says Balance Druids should have more points left for utility talents.

The extra crit on Wrath also makes the rotation of two Starfires and one Wrath flow better, and Moonkin Form finally feels like a damage form, not only a buff for the raid.
