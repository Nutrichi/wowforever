An Alliance quest in the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever gave experience without end. Applejack Still, just outside Stormwind, only asked for 4 Shiny Red Apple from a vendor. Aggrend, a designer at Blizzard, says the farm is fixed for the launch.

A video on Reddit shows a level 21 character getting 170 experience per turn-in. Because a vendor sells the apples, players could keep repeating it, and many used it to reach level 30 quickly during the beta.
