How does PvP work in World of Warcraft: Forever? Our new [PvP guide](/guides/pvp/) puts it on one page: Rank Points (RP), Honor, Marks of Honor, all 14 ranks with their rewards, the battlegrounds and Darkspear Islands.

## Rank Points against Honor

Rank Points decide your rank and are never spent. A weekly cap rises to 24,750 for Rank 14, Grand Marshal or High Warlord. Honor is the currency for gear. The ranking is expected to reset every few months, at the start of a new season.

## No rank for blue gear

The rare PvP set asks for Honor only. A rank only matters for the Premier Emboldened Seals that make a piece epic: wrists and belt from Rank 8, up to helm and chest at Rank 13. Rank 11 gives the black war mounts, Rank 14 the epic weapons. Forever has no arena and no rated battlegrounds.

## Marks of Honor and brackets

A win gives three Marks of Honor and bonus Honor, a loss one Mark. Warsong Gulch opens at level 10, Arathi Basin at 20, Darkspear Islands at 30 and Alterac Valley at 51.

## What Darkspear Islands sells

The reputation vendors in The Barrens sell trinkets at Friendly, a cloak, two rings and a necklace at Honored, and an off-hand and three weapons at Revered. Exalted adds epic gloves and shoulders for every armor type and a battle tabard. Each piece comes in versions for level 28, 38, 48 and 58. The guide has every item, the vendors and their coordinates.
