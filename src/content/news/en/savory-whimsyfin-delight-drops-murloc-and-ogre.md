<a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=251525"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_fish_69.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Savory Whimsyfin Delight</a> no longer turns you into a Murloc or an Ogre. Those forms were placeholders, Blizzard says in the notes of the beta build of 1 October. The dish now gives an orc version of the ninja and pirate costumes.

The Cooking recipe drops in Redridge Mountains and is the Alliance counterpart of <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6657"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_monsterhead_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Savory Deviate Delight</a> from The Barrens. The Classic dish turns you into a ninja or a pirate. On 29 September players found that the new one [turned them into a Murloc or an Ogre](/news/savory-whimsyfin-delight-turns-you-into-a-murloc-or-an-ogre/).

The note in the build says:

> Savory Whimsyfin Delight now uses an orc equivalent of the Ninja and Pirate outfits from the Savory Deviate Delight.
>
> *Developers' notes: The prior transformations were placeholder, but we may reintroduce similar ones in the future!*

![Two ninjas and two pirates next to a Molten Giant, the costumes of Savory Deviate Delight in the original World of Warcraft](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-classic-ninja-and-pirate-costumes.jpg)

Blizzard keeps the door open for the Murloc and the Ogre. When and in what form they come back, the note does not say. What the orc costumes look like, Blizzard has not shown yet.
