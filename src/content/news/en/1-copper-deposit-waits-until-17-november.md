Selling cheap items on the Auction House in World of Warcraft: Forever stays expensive for a few more days. The minimum deposit of 1 copper is not live yet. It comes with the weekly maintenance on 17 November, says Blizzard.

## What happened

The hotfix notes of 10 November listed the new minimum, and we wrote in [our post about those hotfixes](/news/racial-vendor-mounts-become-bind-on-pickup/) that it was live. On 12 November at 01.48 Belgian time, Blizzard took it back out:

> We've temporarily removed Auction House changes from yesterday's update, as they will go live with scheduled weekly maintenance on November 17.

The change returns in the hotfix notes after that maintenance.

## Why it matters

Until then every auction costs at least 1 silver in deposit, see [how the deposit works now](/news/auction-deposits-go-back-to-1-copper/). For cheap items that eats a large part of the profit, and if the auction does not sell, the silver is gone. After 17 November the deposit follows the vendor price again: 5, 20 or 60% of it for 2, 8 or 24 hours. Low-level crafters can wait a few days with their cloth and leather.
