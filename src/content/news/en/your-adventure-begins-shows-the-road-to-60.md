The video appeared on 9 October at 18.00 Belgian time on the official World of Warcraft channel. Blizzard sums it up as the new quests, characters and rewards that wait on your levelling journey through Azeroth.

## The first steps

Every character starts with a weapon and a few coins, and explores the starting zone from there. Every race gets new quests in its starting zone, and those zones hold more to discover than before.

The Skyborne begin with quests that introduce their culture. New stories explain how Paladins emerge among the Forsaken. More about both is in the posts about [the origin of the Skyborne](/news/wow-forever-skyborne-origin-and-new-quests/) and about [Orc Mages and Undead Paladins](/news/orc-mages-and-undead-paladins-arrive/).

## On the road

Cooks can light a fire and set up a camp, and other players can add to it. How camping works is in the [camping guide](/guides/camping/).

The Wetlands get new quests. Along the way there are new pets and weapon quests for each class, such as the [Shaman chain at level 30](/news/shamans-earn-a-weapon-with-a-level-30-quest-chain/). Every pet found so far is in the [pet guides](/guides/pets/).

## Travel and dungeons

World Travel changes in Forever as well. The route from the Wetlands to Darkshore gets a new stop, where players can get off in Hillsbrad. The new ports are in the post about [the four new zones and three ports](/news/four-new-zones-and-three-ports-in-wow-forever/).

Nine new dungeons wait on the way to 60. The ones that are known, with their level ranges, are in the [dungeon guide](/guides/dungeons/).
