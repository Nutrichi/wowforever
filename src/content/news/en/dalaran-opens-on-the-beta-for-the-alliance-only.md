Dalaran is open on the beta of WoW Forever. The Alliance walks in freely; the Horde is not welcome, even though the city is marked as a Sanctuary. Inside wait trainers, battlemasters and the way into a new dungeon, writes Wowhead.

## Getting in

The city stands in the Alterac Mountains, northwest of Southshore. There are three ways in:

- **The stairs** just north of Hillsbrad Fields.
- **A flying ship** between Dalaran and Valanaar on Zephras Isle, the hub of the Skyborne. It lands outside the Violet Citadel, south of Fenris Isle.
- **A portal** in the Violet Citadel to the Wizard's Sanctum in Stormwind City, with a portal back.

No flight path lands in Dalaran yet.

## Battlegrounds in The Underbelly

Down in The Underbelly, four battlemasters queue for every battleground: "Techs" Rickard Rustbolt for Warsong Gulch, Minzi the Minx for Arathi Basin, Sai for Alterac Valley and Bimble Sparkfingers for the new Darkspear Islands. "Dapper" Danik Blackshaft, Nixi Fireclaw and Blazik Fireclaw sell PvP gear.

## The way into the City of Dalaran

The [City of Dalaran](/guides/dungeons/) is a new dungeon for levels 28 to 33. The Alliance reaches it through a pipe in Cantrips & Crows, in the Dalaran Sewers: open the gate and follow the pipe to the portal.

![A wide sewer pipe behind wooden crates in the Dalaran Sewers, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-dalaran-sewer-pipe.jpg)

The Horde enters from outside the city, at /way 8.8 59.6, with the <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=277507"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dalaran Sewer Key</a>.

## A jeweler without a tradeskill

In Cartier & Co. Fine Jewelry, at /way 18.1 61.9, stands Timothy Jones, an Apprentice Jeweler. That title usually belongs to a trainer, notes Wowhead. Jewelcrafting is not part of Forever, and he teaches nothing yet.

![Timothy Jones, Apprentice Jeweler, in a jewelry shop in Dalaran, with an empty dialog window, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-dalaran-apprentice-jeweler.jpg)

## What is not known

Where the Horde gets the Dalaran Sewer Key, Blizzard does not say. Nor whether a flight path to Dalaran follows, or whether the jeweler ever becomes a trainer.
