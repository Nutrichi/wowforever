<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=412">Stitches</a> no longer attacks players in Duskwood on the World of Warcraft: Forever beta. The level 35 elite has been neutral since the latest beta build. That is a bug and will be fixed, said Principal Game Designer Johnny Cash on X.

Stitches is one of the most feared monsters of the Eastern Kingdoms. On his way to Darkshire, he usually kills every low player in his path. Now he walks past them. Player lepan posted a screenshot of a peaceful Stitches in Darkshire: "no more dying in darkshire running out of the inn".
