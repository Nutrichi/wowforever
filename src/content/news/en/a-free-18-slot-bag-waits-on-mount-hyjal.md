From level 56, a short quest chain in World of Warcraft: Forever gives you an 18-slot bag for free. Three mail quests between Mount Hyjal and Everlook also reward a trinket that makes you run 5% faster.

## The three quests

| Quest | What you do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93197">Mending Communications</a> | Bring the mail parcels to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11118">Innkeeper Vizzie</a> | From <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=243815">Sebelia</a> on the summit of Mount Hyjal, /way 70.8 51.4, to Everlook |
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93406">Unfrozen Deliveries</a> | Find 4 pieces of Missing Mail | The hills north of Everlook |
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93269">Top of the Post</a> | Take the mail back up to Sebelia | From Everlook to Mount Hyjal |

All three need level 56. Expect to fight the yetis in the hills while you look for the mail.

## The rewards

The last quest gives both items, you do not have to choose:

- <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=260307"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_18.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hyjal Postal Satchel</a>: an 18-slot bag.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=260308"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_herb_gromsbloodleaf.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hyjal Postal Clasp</a>: a trinket with +10 Nature Resistance, +10 Frost Resistance and 5% more running and swimming speed.

Onyxia is not in the game yet, so a free 18-slot bag is worth a lot. Smaller bags come earlier: the Darkmoon Faire has [a 14-slot bag](/news/a-14-slot-bag-waits-at-the-darkmoon-faire/).
