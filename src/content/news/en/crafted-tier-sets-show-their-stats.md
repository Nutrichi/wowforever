The crafted versions of the first tier sets in World of Warcraft: Forever show their stats since a hotfix. There are 18 sets, one for each spec, each with crafted legs, gloves, shoulders and boots. You can wear only one crafted tier piece at a time.

## Stats on purpose, bonuses not final

Blizzard confirmed that the stats are visible on purpose. Items from vendors and recipes are not hidden, unlike the loot of the raids. The set bonuses are placeholders and will still change. The raid versions stay hidden until the raids open on 9 December.

Every piece is an epic of item level 60, Binds when equipped, with Unique-Equipped: Artisan's Tier (1). A set has six pieces; the crafted versions cover four slots. Every piece has Nature Resistance, 9 on the legs and 7 on the rest. How a crafted piece is meant to fit next to the raid pieces, Blizzard does not say. The Tier 1 pieces from the raids [are spread over all three raids](/news/your-tier-1-set-is-spread-over-all-three-raids/).

## Who crafts what

Every recipe needs skill 300. The recipe for the legs also needs Friendly with Guardians of Hyjal and the quest Friendly Advice. <a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274030"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_cyclone.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Malleable Essence of Nature</a> comes from disenchanting items from Hyjal Summit or Barrow Deeps.

| Profession | Classes | Legs | Other pieces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tailoring | Mage, Priest, Warlock | 5 Malleable Essence of Nature, 3 Mooncloth, 22 Bolt of Runecloth, 8 Rune Thread | 3 Malleable Essence of Nature, 2 Mooncloth, 16 Bolt of Runecloth, 8 Rune Thread |
| Leatherworking | Druid, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman | 5 Malleable Essence of Nature, 4 Cured Rugged Hide, 24 Fel Leather, 8 Rune Thread | 3 Malleable Essence of Nature, 2 Cured Rugged Hide, 18 Fel Leather, 8 Rune Thread |
| Blacksmithing | Paladin, Warrior | 5 Malleable Essence of Nature, 4 Arcanite Bar, 18 Heavy Thorium Bar, Elemental Flux | 3 Malleable Essence of Nature, 2 Arcanite Bar, 12 Heavy Thorium Bar, Elemental Flux |

On the beta, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=264797">Aisarra Nightmeadow</a> sold the recipes for Malleable Essence of Nature. In the newest build she no longer does. How the recipes come back, is not known.

The other bonuses follow one pattern. With 2 pieces you get 1% hit (casters and Rogues), 1% attack and casting speed (melee and Hunters), 10 Spirit (healers) or 7 Defense (tanks). With 3 pieces a utility spell gets better, such as a shorter cooldown on Counterspell, Kidney Shot or Fear Ward. With 4 pieces you get 21 spell damage or 36 attack power against one creature type, 26 healing for healers, or 1.2% less chance to be dodged or parried for tanks.

The bonus of The Spiritcaller's Storm still names Lava Burst, a talent that Elemental Shamans [lost before the launch](/news/shamans-lose-lava-burst-before-the-launch/).
