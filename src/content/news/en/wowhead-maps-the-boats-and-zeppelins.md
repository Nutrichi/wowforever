The boat and zeppelin routes of WoW Forever are now drawn on an online map of Azeroth. The map shows which dock leads where, the new routes included. That matters in Forever, because travel across the world runs on that network and several lines are new.

Forever has six new routes on top of the ones Classic has. Stormwind Harbor gets a boat to Auberdine, and a boat from Menethil Harbor calls at Southshore on its way to Auberdine. A boat links Powderfuse Port in The Riverglades with Steamwheedle Port. Two new zeppelins fly to Valanaar on Zephras Isle, the home of the Skyborne: one from Dalaran for both factions, one from Skywatcher Plateau for the Horde. All fourteen routes, old and new, are in the table under [Boats and zeppelins](/guides/flight-paths/#boats-and-zeppelins) in our flight paths guide.

## Why a map helps here

Classic players know the zeppelin towers by heart, but Forever changes the picture. A new zone means new docks, and a route that existed for twenty years may now end somewhere else.

A map is useful in two directions. Heading for a dungeon, you can check which zeppelin from Orgrimmar drops you closest. Heading for The Riverglades, you can see which boat gets you there. For the way through the air, the same [flight paths guide](/guides/flight-paths/) lists every flight master in Forever.

The routes come from the beta, so they can still change before the launch on 4 November.
