Wowhead has added the boat and zeppelin routes of WoW Forever to its Azeroth map tool. The map now draws which dock leads where, the new routes included. That matters in Forever, because travel across the world runs on that network and several lines are new.

Forever adds [three ship routes](/news/wow-forever-three-new-ship-routes/) on top of the ones Classic has. Stormwind Harbor gains a connection to Auberdine, a second runs from Menethil to Southshore and on to Auberdine, and a third links the new zone The Riverglades to Steamwheedle Port, which in turn connects to Gadgetzan in Tanaris.

## Why a map helps here

Classic players know the zeppelin towers by heart, but Forever changes the picture. A new zone means new docks, and a route that existed for twenty years may now end somewhere else.

The tool is useful in two directions. Heading for a dungeon, you can check which zeppelin from Orgrimmar drops you closest. Heading for The Riverglades, you can see that the boat from Gadgetzan gets you there.

Wowhead says it welcomes suggestions for what the tool should do next. The routes themselves come from the beta, so they can still change before launch.
