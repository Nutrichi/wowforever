Paladins in World of Warcraft: Forever find no mail armour with spell power in quests or dungeons up to level 30. In the beta, only green items with a suffix such as "of Magic" add spell power. Crafting is the way out.

## Two quest rewards

Among the quest rewards of the beta, only two mail items carry spell power, and both lean towards healing.

Dungeons drop only Uncommon items with a random suffix that adds spell power, such as "of Magic" or "of the Knight". That is what testers have found so far; it is no proof that nothing else exists.

## Only the Paladin

From level 1 to 30 this hits one class. The Paladin is the only class with no quest or dungeon gear made for it in those levels. Every other class finds pieces for its role.

## Crafting instead

A Paladin who does not want to buy upgrades at the Auction House has to craft them. The plans drop in dungeons or are sold for Merchant's Favor by the [Azeroth Commerce Authority](/reputations/azeroth-commerce-authority/) and [Durotar Supply and Logistics](/reputations/durotar-supply-and-logistics/). Every recipe is in [Blacksmithing for Forever](/tradeskills/forever/blacksmithing/).

![A vendor window with Blacksmithing plans for chain belts, silvered chain helms and shirts at 30 each, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-22-wow-forever-blacksmithing-plans.jpg)

The crafted gear for low levels is new in Forever. Whether Blizzard adds mail with spell power before launch is not known. The beta closes on Friday 23 October. A talent build from 1 to 60 is in the [Paladin leveling guide](/guides/paladin-leveling/).
