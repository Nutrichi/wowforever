The Soulforge set for Paladins in World of Warcraft: Forever turns into a set for healers. A hotfix takes Strength off every piece and adds Intellect and Spell Power instead. Five other items change too, among them three Darkmoon cards.

## The Soulforge pieces

Soulforge Armor is the dungeon set for Paladins. With Strength, Stamina and Defense it used to suit a tank more than a healer. Now every piece has more Intellect and Spell Power, which makes it a set for the [Holy Paladin](/guides/holy-paladin/).

For the Handguards, the published list gives +1 Intellect, down from +10. That is most likely a typo. The tooltips can still show the old values for a while. Blizzard has not named these changes in its hotfix notes yet.

- <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=284097"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_eye_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Everstaring Abomination Eye</a> now gives +6 Spirit, down from +14.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=287026"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_musket_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Escape Plan</a> now deals 54 to 102 damage, where it dealt 70 before.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=19288"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_ticket_tarot_bluedragon_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Darkmoon Card: Blue Dragon</a> now has a 3% chance to trigger, up from 2%.
