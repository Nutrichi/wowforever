World of Warcraft: Forever has a two-handed quest weapon for each faction around level 16. The Alliance gets the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279895"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_21.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ironforge Greathammer</a> in the Hall of Thanes, the Horde the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15424"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Axe of Orgrimmar</a> in Ragefire Chasm. Both have item level 18 and bind when picked up.

## Ironforge Greathammer for the Alliance

1. **Find the map.** Kill Dark Iron Spies at Ironband's Compound in Dun Morogh, around `/way 77.5 60.4`, until one drops the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274268"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_map06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dark Iron Map</a>. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=264936">Earthseer Farsen</a> also gives <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96390">Nip 'Em in the Bud</a> for ten of these spies.
2. **Bring it to Farsen.** Use the map from your bags. It starts <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96391">Underground Map</a>, which goes to Earthseer Farsen at `/way 64.8 58.4`.
3. **Clear the Hall of Thanes.** Farsen then gives Old Ironforge Incursion. Kill <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=261319">Durgen Dirgehammer</a>, the final boss of the dungeon beneath Old Ironforge, and loot his head.
4. **Turn it in.** Bring the head to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=2784">King Magni Bronzebeard</a> in Ironforge. The Greathammer is one of three rewards, next to the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279894"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_rifle_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Calibrated Blunderbuss</a> and the wand <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279896"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Deepblaze</a>.

## Axe of Orgrimmar for the Horde

1. **Start with Thrall.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=4949">Thrall</a> in Orgrimmar, at `/way 32.0 37.8`, gives <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5726">Hidden Enemies</a>. Kill Burning Blade Apprentices and Burning Blade Fanatics at Skull Rock in Durotar, around `/way 53.6 9.4`, until one drops the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=14544"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_rune_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lieutenant's Insignia</a>.
2. **Show it to Neeru.** Bring the insignia to Thrall. He sends you with it to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3216">Neeru Fireblade</a> in the Cleft of Shadow, to see how he reacts, and then back to Thrall.
3. **Clear Ragefire Chasm.** The last part asks for <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11519">Bazzalan</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11518">Jergosh the Invoker</a>, two bosses of the dungeon beneath Orgrimmar.
4. **Turn it in.** Return to Thrall and pick the Axe of Orgrimmar as your reward. The quest also gives 1,150 experience and 100 reputation with Orgrimmar.

Every quest of both dungeons, with the coordinates and the other rewards, is in the guides to the [Hall of Thanes](/guides/dungeons/hall-of-thanes/) and [Ragefire Chasm](/guides/dungeons/ragefire-chasm/).
