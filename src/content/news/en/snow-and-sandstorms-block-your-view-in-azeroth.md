Snow in World of Warcraft: Forever can now almost take away your view. Blizzard showed light snow and a heavy, fog-like storm in Dun Morogh in a video on X. Testers also filmed a sandstorm in Tanaris during the beta.

Dun Morogh always had some snow, near the gates of Ironforge and across the zone. The video adds a light, speckled snowfall and a storm that hides much of the landscape. "More varied snowy weather is coming to WoW: Forever", Blizzard writes.

## A sandstorm in Tanaris

Snow is not the only new weather. A video from the beta on Reddit shows Tanaris under a thick cloud of sand.

Earlier in the beta, [heavy rain, lightning and snowstorms in the Alterac Mountains](/news/lightning-and-snowstorms-arrive-on-the-beta/) appeared. How often these storms occur, and whether they affect anything other than your view, Blizzard does not say.
