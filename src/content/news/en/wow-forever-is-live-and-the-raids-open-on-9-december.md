World of Warcraft: Forever is live. The realms opened on 4 November at 15:00 Pacific time, 00:00 Belgian time on 5 November, after [five hours of maintenance](/news/five-hours-of-maintenance-before-the-launch/).

## Open now

- **Levels 1 to 60** on PvE, PvP, RP and RP-PvP realms, with more than 1,000 new quests.
- **The Skyborne** on Zephras Isle, with the Skyborne Heroic Pack or a higher edition.
- **Every dungeon** up to level 60, and the battlegrounds.
- **Invite-A-Friend codes** work until 11 November.

## Next: the raids on 9 December

On 9 December Barrow Deeps opens for 10 players, Hyjal Summit for 20 and Onyxia's Lair for 40, see [the three raids of 9 December](/news/blizzard-details-the-three-raids-of-9-december/). Blizzard has not named the hour yet. Onyxia needs the [attunement quest chain](/news/onyxias-lair-keeps-its-attunement-quest-chain/), and the [tier pieces need a quest chain](/news/tier-pieces-need-a-quest-chain-with-group-steps/) with group steps. Hardcore follows later this winter.
