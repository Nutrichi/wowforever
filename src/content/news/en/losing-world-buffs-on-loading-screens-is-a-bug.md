> **Update 24 November:** The fix is live. World buffs survive loading screens again, and Fengus' Ferocity now gives half as much. More in [our post](/news/world-buffs-survive-loading-screens-again/).

World buffs in World of Warcraft: Forever should not disappear on a loading screen. Senior Game Designer Aidan "Zirene" Moon says it is a bug, and that a fix comes the next day.

## What went wrong

Since this weekend, [every loading screen removes your world buffs](/news/world-buffs-vanish-on-every-loading-screen/): entering a dungeon, taking a boat to the other continent, even logging in. According to Moon, a fix for another bug caused this side effect.

## What comes back

Once the fix is live, world buffs work as before:

- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16609"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_teleportorgrimmar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Warchief's Blessing</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=460940"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_teleportstormwind.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Might of Stormwind</a> follow you into dungeons again;
- you can take them across to the other continent;
- the Tribute Run buffs from [Dire Maul North](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-north/) stay when you leave the dungeon.

World buffs still do not work in raids. That was a choice for Forever from the start and does not change.

Moon replied on 22 November, US time, so the fix should arrive on 23 November, which is the night to 24 November in Belgium. We update this post when it is live.
