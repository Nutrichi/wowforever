The class hotfixes that Blizzard announced for Tuesday 10 November are now live for Warlocks and Hunters. Affliction gets the biggest change: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1316697"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_deathknight_hemorrhagicfever.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wrack</a> deals less damage on its own, but makes your damage over time spells tick twice as fast while you channel.

## Warlock

Blizzard [promised last week](/news/hunter-and-warlock-pets-get-tougher-on-tuesday/) that Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony would always be worth casting next to Wrack, without changing the total damage of Affliction. Until now, channeling Wrack on its own was the best you could do.

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1316697"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_deathknight_hemorrhagicfever.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wrack</a>:** 60 Shadow damage per second at rank 1, down from 86, and the 10% extra damage from your other Shadow DoTs is gone. In return, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=172"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_abominationexplosion.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Corruption</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18265"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_requiem.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Siphon Life</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_curseofsargeras.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bane of Agony</a> on the target deal their damage 100% faster while you channel.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17804"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Soul Siphon</a>:** now names Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony. Before, every other Affliction effect counted, so a curse no longer adds to the bonus for Drain Life, Drain Soul and Wrack.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=3110"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_firebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Firebolt</a>:** the Imp deals more damage. The increase is largest at low levels and smaller at higher ones.

Put your three DoTs up first, then channel Wrack: it now pays off on enemies that live long enough. Our [Affliction Warlock guide](/guides/affliction-warlock/) has the new rotation.

## Hunter

Two shots now cost a flat 11% of your base mana, at every rank:

- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=3044"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_impalingbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Shot</a>
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293241"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_animalhandler.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Hawk</a>, the new Beast Mastery talent

Not every change from that announcement is in yet, so more may follow. On the same day, the [Enhancement Shaman's quest mace](/news/rage-of-the-storm-swaps-intellect-for-strength/) got Strength instead of Intellect.
