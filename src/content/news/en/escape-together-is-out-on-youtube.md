Blizzard has put Escape Together on YouTube, the show in which a Horde team and an Alliance team race through challenges and an escape room. The final room is inspired by the Scarlet Monastery. Liquid Eiya hosts, and the video lasts 54 minutes.

The teams first face off in head-to-head challenges, with puzzles and riddles, before the escape room itself. Which faction wins is in the video. The show was [announced on 23 October](/news/horde-and-alliance-race-through-an-escape-room/) without names; the video appeared on 25 October.
