The Chain of the Scarlet Crusade now drops on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever. Three of the six pieces went from green to blue. The bonuses at 4 and 6 pieces are new: a Holy proc and a shield.

On 3 October the new bonuses were only known from the files of the beta, as described in the [post on the Scarlet Monastery loot](/news/scarlet-monastery-loot-gets-new-effects/). Now the pieces drop in the dungeon, and their tooltips show the bonuses and the stats.

## The set bonus

| Pieces | Classic Era | Forever |
|---|---|---|
| **2** | +10 Armor | +10 Shadow Resistance |
| **3** | +1 Defense | +30 Attack Power against Undead |
| **4** | +5 Shadow Resistance | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293674"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unholyfrenzy.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Enraging Light</a> |
| **5** | +15 Attack Power against Undead | +1% chance to hit |
| **6** | +1% chance to hit | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293670"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_tabard_scarletcrusade_b_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scarlet Guardian</a> |

The Armor and the Defense are gone. Shadow Resistance and Attack Power against Undead are doubled, and the chance to hit moves from 6 to 5 pieces.

Enraging Light triggers when the wearer takes damage, with a chance of 15%. For 10 seconds, melee attacks then deal 20 extra Holy damage, three times as much against Undead. The tooltip lists a cooldown of 15 seconds between procs.

Scarlet Guardian triggers on damage at or below 20% health. It gives a shield that absorbs 480 damage for 10 seconds, once every 4 minutes.

## Less experience in dungeons

Mail wearers such as Warriors, Paladins and Enhancement Shamans have long sought this set. In Classic, farming it also paid off in experience. On the beta that is less true: monsters in dungeons give less experience, and [dungeon quests give 50% less bonus experience](/news/dungeon-quests-give-50-percent-less-bonus-experience/).

![The quest The Defias Brotherhood in the quest log, with its rewards and 6,200 experience, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-07-wow-forever-the-defias-brotherhood-rewards.jpg)

Blizzard wants players to level in the open world, and [green monsters give less experience too](/news/green-mobs-give-less-experience-than-in-classic/). Several pieces of the set drop from trash in the Scarlet Monastery, so groups that skip monsters see fewer of them. Whether the experience values change before launch, is not known.
