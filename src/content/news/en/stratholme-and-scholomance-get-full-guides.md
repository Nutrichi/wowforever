Stratholme and Scholomance now have their own guides on the site, with loot, quests and the full route. Stratholme is split into two guides, one for each side. Together they cover 24 quests and 31 bosses for levels 58 to 60.

Neither dungeon was open on the beta, which stopped at level 30. The routes therefore follow Classic, with the boss levels from the Forever data.

## Stratholme Living

The [Living side](/guides/dungeons/stratholme-living/) belongs to the Scarlet Crusade. The guide shows the bosses on Blizzard's map, the six postboxes that summon Postmaster Malown, and The Scarlet Key from Scarlet Monastery that opens the Scarlet Bastion. The fight ends with Grand Crusader Dathrohan, who turns into Balnazzar.

In the Forever data, the quest for the tobacco shop still carries the name The Great Fras Siabi, while the shopkeeper is now Ezra Grimm.

## Stratholme Undead

The [Undead side](/guides/dungeons/stratholme-undead/) runs through three ziggurats and Slaughter Square to Baron Rivendare. Deathcharger's Reins is in the Forever data; whether the Baron drops it, is not known. The Flesh Does Not Lie now asks for 20 Plagued Flesh Samples instead of 10.

## Scholomance

The [Scholomance guide](/guides/dungeons/scholomance/) covers the Skeleton Key, the long chain of Eva Sarkhoff, and the six rooms of the Barov Family Vault before Darkmaster Gandling. Blizzard's map shows the later version of the dungeon, so the guide has none.

## The loot

Most of the old loot is not in the Forever data yet: 33 of 46 items on the Living side, 35 of 53 on the Undead side and 52 of 71 in Scholomance. The guides list the missing items per boss. The rewards of the quests are not known yet either.

All dungeon guides are on the [dungeon overview](/guides/dungeons/).
