Skyborne players now get 10% off riding training in World of Warcraft: Forever. A hotfix on 19 November gives them the discount at Honored, like every other race. Journeyman Riding now costs 900 gold instead of 1,000. Honored itself still takes a lot of cloth.

## What the hotfix changes

Blizzard fixed the discount on Apprentice and Journeyman Riding at <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=265756">Genn Fairweather</a> on Zephras Isle. Until now the Skyborne paid full price at every standing, see [our earlier post](/news/skyborne-pay-full-price-for-riding-training/).

The hotfix names the riding skills. Whether the Galestrider mounts also get cheaper, Blizzard does not say.

## Honored takes cloth

After Zephras Isle, no quests give reputation with High Order, the Alliance faction of the Skyborne, or with the Windshapers on the Horde side. Past Friendly, only cloth turn-ins remain:

1. **Three first turn-ins** with Wool Cloth, Silk Cloth and Mageweave Cloth: 60 cloth for 150 reputation each.
2. **<a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=99199">A Donation of Runecloth</a>** from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=276171">Oura Stormspinner</a>: 60 Runecloth for 150 reputation.
3. **<a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=99200">Additional Runecloth</a>**, repeatable: 20 Runecloth for 50 reputation.

How many turn-ins you need depends on how far you are into Friendly. One player paid about 70 gold for the Runecloth to reach Honored, against 100 gold saved on Journeyman Riding.
