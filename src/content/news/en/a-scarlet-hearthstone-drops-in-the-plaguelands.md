World of Warcraft: Forever has its first cosmetic hearthstone. The Scarlet Hearthstone gives your character its own animation during the 10-second cast. According to a player on the Blizzard forums, it drops from High General Abbendis in the Eastern Plaguelands.

## A toy that makes a hearthstone

The drop is the toy <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=284164"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_mage_flameorb.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scarlet Hearthstone</a>. It goes into your Toy Box, but you do not cast it from there. The toy creates a separate <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=284163"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_mage_flameorb.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scarlet Hearthstone</a>, and that one does take a bag slot. It works like the normal hearthstone: you burn in scarlet flames and return to your inn. The toy has a cooldown of 1 hour.

Cosmetic hearthstones are new in Forever. In the original game they did not exist.

## Where it drops

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=10828">High General Abbendis</a> leads the Scarlet Crusade in the Eastern Plaguelands. How often he drops the toy, is not known, and Blizzard has not confirmed where it comes from. Whether more cosmetic hearthstones are hidden in the game, is not known either.
