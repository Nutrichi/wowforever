Siege of Orgrimmar is open on all Mists of Pandaria Classic realms. It is the final raid tier of MoP Classic. Players fight through fourteen bosses to Garrosh Hellscream. The raid is available in Flex, Normal and Heroic.

Siege of Orgrimmar was also the last raid of the original Mists of Pandaria in 2013. It takes place in and under the Horde capital, which Garrosh Hellscream rules at that point in the story.

Gear from the Timeless Isle helps characters catch up before their first raid night. The island offers item level upgrades outside of raids, so an alt can reach the raid without clearing the earlier tiers.
