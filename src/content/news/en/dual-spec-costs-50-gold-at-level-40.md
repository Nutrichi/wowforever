Dual Spec is in World of Warcraft: Forever from day one, but it is not cheap. At level 40 your class trainer sells a second talent tree for 50 gold, right when you also want your first mount.

## Two builds, one switch

With a second spec you keep two separate talent builds and swap between them whenever you want. A tank and a damage build, or one for raids and one for PvP. A respec at the trainer costs more every time, so for anyone who changes often, the 50 gold pays for itself.

## Mount or second spec first?

Apprentice Riding and Dual Spec together cost 150 gold at level 40. That is a lot this early, so many players will have to pick one first. It gets easier as you level: items sell for more, and [quests give more gold from level 48](/news/blizzard-adds-gold-to-quests-from-level-48/).

Healers and tanks who level with a damage spec gain the most. The [Shaman leveling guide](/guides/shaman-leveling/), for example, adds Restoration as a second spec at level 40.
