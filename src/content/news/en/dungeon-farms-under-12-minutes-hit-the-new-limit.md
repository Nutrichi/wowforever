Since the hotfixes of 20 November, you can enter at most 5 dungeons per hour in World of Warcraft: Forever, down from 10. We covered it briefly in [our post about Looking for Group](/news/looking-for-group-shows-more-groups-again/). Who notices it, and why?

## Who notices it

Forever launched with a limit of 10 dungeons per hour. The new limit of 5 matches Classic Era again. Whether 10 was a choice or a side effect of the modern client, is not known.

A full clear for experience or gear never hits the limit. Most dungeons in Forever take at least 30 minutes. It only matters for short runs: with 5 per hour, every run under 12 minutes brings you to the limit.

## Why it matters for gold

Soloing a dungeon gives decent gold, at any level, from the items you sell to a vendor. Players often use spots where they can jump up and down in a second, while monsters need a detour of 10 seconds or more. That way one player clears what was made for a group.

A farm in [Krol'dok Stronghold](/guides/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold/) takes about 5 minutes, and the [farm in Blackmaw Hold](/news/a-gold-farm-in-blackmaw-hold-is-hotfixed/) was quick too. Farms like that now run into the limit after 5 runs. Blizzard has not said why it lowered the number, but it slows down the gold that solo farms bring into the economy.
