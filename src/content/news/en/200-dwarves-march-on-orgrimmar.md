On a US [RP-PvP realm](/guides/rulesets/) of World of Warcraft: Forever, a guild of Dwarves has decided to conquer Azeroth. Clan Battlehammer brought together about 200 Dwarves, all in the same race and in neat rows.

It started at home. The army lined up outside The Commons in Ironforge, shoulder to shoulder, to guard the city against anyone who would threaten it.

Then it was the Horde's turn. Clan Battlehammer struck at Thunder Bluff and took the Tauren capital. That was not enough, so the 200 Dwarves set off for the gates of Orgrimmar. How that ended, the screenshots do not say.

It is the kind of thing an RP-PvP realm is made for, and it will not be the last raid on a capital.
