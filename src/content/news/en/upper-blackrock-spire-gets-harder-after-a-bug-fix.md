Upper Blackrock Spire was easier than Blizzard intended. A bug made the health and damage of its monsters far too low. The hotfixes of 17 November fix that, so the dungeon again needs a full and careful group.

## Upper Blackrock Spire

Blizzard's note: the fix should bring UBRS back to the difficulty it was built for. Did you clear it with a loose group last week? Expect tougher pulls now. Our [Upper Blackrock Spire guide](/guides/dungeons/upper-blackrock-spire/) has the bosses and quests.

## Shen'dralas

The zone gets a list of quality-of-life fixes:

- Quest items like the Depleted Crystal Heart for Heart of Crystal now drop for everyone in a party, and repairing pylons for Powering the Pylons counts for the whole group.
- Agamaggan's Blood, the last quest of a chain, is no longer a dungeon quest. You can finish it in the elite area outside Razorfen Downs.
- Miners can track Spectral Iron Ore, and it spawns more often.
- Bristleback Skullcrushers and Decrepit Pylon Protectors cast their abilities less often, and Summon Undead Ravager can be interrupted.

## Other fixes

- The Cozy Sleeping Bag no longer works while mounted, can only be placed in certain spots, like hunter traps, and shows a Resting aura when you are inside its area. More in our [sleeping bag guide](/guides/cozy-sleeping-bag/).
- Might of Stormwind and Warchief's Blessing now always end when you die and no longer land on non-players.
- Shamans: Flurry no longer loses stacks when Windfury Totem attacks hit.
- Shadow Priests can hold a Night Watchman's Torch, and Whiteout Staff and Crackling Staff now have the same stats.
- The Battle for Alterac Valley quests need level 58, and you can no longer mount in the starting rooms of Warsong Gulch.
- Ignore lists now survive logging out.

The same notes confirm the [Soulforge change](/news/soulforge-armor-swaps-strength-for-spell-power/) for Paladin tanks.
