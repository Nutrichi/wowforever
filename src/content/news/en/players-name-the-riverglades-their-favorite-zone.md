Many players name the Riverglades their favorite leveling zone in World of Warcraft: Forever. The new zone on the east coast of the Eastern Kingdoms is meant for levels 36 to 44, and its quests together are worth 4 to 5 levels.

## Why players like it

The quests are more linear than in most Classic zones. Finish a round and the next one appears, usually in the same town. Players also notice little competition for monsters. Many advise going there around level 40, so the last quests are not too hard.

## How to get there

On foot over the roads from Redridge Mountains or the Badlands, or by boat from Steamwheedle Port in Tanaris, east of Gadgetzan. The boat docks at Powderfuse Port, the neutral goblin town. The Alliance quests at Farholde Keep, the Horde at Rog'mar.

## Tips before you go

- **Horde:** kill <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=257079">Cavalry Scout Alturin</a> on the main road out of Farholde Keep. He drops <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93971">Farholde Scouting Report</a>, a hidden quest worth 4,850 experience. Attacking him flags you for PvP.
- **Bring items:** the Horde needs 10 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=769"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_food_14.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Chunk of Boar Meat</a> and 10 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3241"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_stone_weightstone_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heavy Weightstone</a>. Both factions need 12 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3575"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ingot_iron.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Iron Bar</a> and 4 <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4304"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_leatherscrap_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thick Leather</a> for the key of Krol'dok Stronghold.
- **The dungeon:** Krol'dok Stronghold, for levels 40 to 45, has six bosses. Most of its quests open after the story of the zone.
- **The Bloodmaster's Ring:** each fight gives about as much experience as a dungeon quest, and the chain ends with a ring for level 45.

Coordinates, flight points, the ring and gathering are in our new [Riverglades guide](/guides/riverglades/). The [Krol'dok quests](/guides/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold/quests/) for both factions are in the dungeon guide.
