Camping in WoW Forever gets a second and third tier from blueprints. Several drop in dungeons, from The Deadmines to Shadowfang Keep. The first object of every tradeskill comes free with a quest at skill 20, writes Warcraft Tavern.

## Three tiers per tradeskill

Every tradeskill crafts three objects for a campsite. Tier 1 is learned at skill 20 from the trainer, or for free with the Camping 101 quest of your tradeskill in the starting zones. Tier 2 follows at 140 and tier 3 at 300, both from a blueprint that you loot or buy from another player.

A higher tier gives the same buff as tier 1 and adds a workstation or a service: an <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1263041"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_blacksmith_anvil.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Anvil</a> for Blacksmithing, a <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1263034"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_robotpet.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Repair Bot</a> that also repairs, a <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1262980"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_herb_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Greenhouse</a> that grows herbs from seeds.

## The rules of a camp

A campfire comes from Cooking and burns for about 10 minutes, with a 5-minute cooldown. Each character places one object, and all camp objects share a cooldown of 1 hour. Anyone who sits within range for a minute gets the buffs, weaker than the class buffs they replace and not stacking with them.

How a campsite works, what every object costs and which buff it gives, is in the [camping guide](/guides/camping/).
