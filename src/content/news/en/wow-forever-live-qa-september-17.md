Blizzard holds a live Q&A with the World of Warcraft development team on Thursday 17 September. The stream starts at 10.00 a.m. PDT, 19.00 Belgian time, on the official Warcraft channels on Twitch and YouTube. The questions come from the forums.

Blizzard asked players for questions on the North American and European forums earlier and has now gathered them for the team. The stream covers both Midnight and Forever, writes Wowhead.

The developers will look back at the reveals of BlizzCon 2026 and forward to what is coming for modern and Classic WoW, says Blizzard in its own announcement. Which team members take part, Blizzard does not say.

The stream stays available afterwards as a VOD on the same channels.
