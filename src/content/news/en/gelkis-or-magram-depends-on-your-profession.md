In Desolace you can side with the Gelkis or the Magram centaurs, never both. In World of Warcraft: Forever both clans now go to Exalted and sell twelve recipes. Your profession decides which side gives you yours cheaper.

## Pick by profession

Each clan sells all twelve recipes, for <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=254432"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_vrykul_runestick.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Carved Centaur Totem</a>. The six of its own professions cost 15 totems at Honored, and one recipe per profession even comes from a quest at Friendly. The six of the rival clan cost 60 totems and need Exalted.

| Your profession | Pick |
|---|---|
| Enchanting, Leatherworking, Tailoring | [Gelkis](/reputations/gelkis-clan-centaur/) |
| Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering | [Magram](/reputations/magram-clan-centaur/) |

## Totems and quartermasters

The totems drop from centaurs of every clan in Desolace, so you collect them along the way. Molkar sells the recipes in the Gelkis Village, Gorhak in the Magram Village.

- **Carved Centaur Totems:** a quest for 5 totems, at Nijel's Point for the Alliance and Ghost Walker Post for the Horde.
- **Buying Friendship:** bring a <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=257685"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_enchantedrunecloth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Totem Tote</a> to the quartermaster of your clan for a big jump in reputation, from Neutral on.

## The last quest

Both quest chains end in a choice of rare weapons and off-hands. The Gelkis rewards suit melee players; their off-hand has no spell power. Casters usually do better with the Magram, whose rewards include a two-handed polearm that suits other classes too.

Everything per clan, with the standings and the quest chains, is in our guides to the [Gelkis](/reputations/gelkis-clan-centaur/) and the [Magram](/reputations/magram-clan-centaur/).
