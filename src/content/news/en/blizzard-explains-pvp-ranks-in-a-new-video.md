## Rank Points set your rank

Honor no longer decides your rank. Rank Points do, and they come from daily and weekly sources, weekly battlegrounds, the Season Journey quests and some world PvP events, like the one at Gurubashi Arena. That makes the climb more varied and more predictable.

The Season Journey is the biggest source: every week a new quest gives Rank Points. Missed a week? There is catch-up for those quests. Blizzard expects most players to reach the highest rank around halfway through a season.

## Honor buys the rewards

Rank Points decide which rewards you can buy, Honor is what you pay with. Low ranks unlock rare gear. From rank 8 (Knight-Captain or Legionnaire) you can upgrade that gear to epic quality, and rank 14 unlocks epic weapons as strong as raid gear.

All 14 ranks and their rewards are in our [PvP guide](/guides/pvp/). Earlier this week Blizzard also made clear that [only battleground wins raise your rank](/news/only-battleground-wins-raise-your-pvp-rank/).
