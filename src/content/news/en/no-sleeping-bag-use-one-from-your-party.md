The Cozy Sleeping Bag takes a quest chain of one to two hours across several zones. You can skip that: lie in the bag of another player in your party and you get the same experience buff, for two hours.

## How it works

Lie in the bag for a minute and you get Well-Rested, 1% more experience. After three minutes that is 3%, the maximum. The buff lasts two hours and stays when you die. It only works if you are in the same party as the owner of the bag. See someone resting in a bag in the open world? Ask for an invite and lie down next to them.

## Your own bag

Want your own? The chain starts at level 14, in Westfall for the Alliance and The Barrens for the Horde. Every step is in our [Cozy Sleeping Bag guide](/guides/cozy-sleeping-bag/). How the buff stacks with rested XP, food and camps is in the [experience guide](/guides/experience/).
