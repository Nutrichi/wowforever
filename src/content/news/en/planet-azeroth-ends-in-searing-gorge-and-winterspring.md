Planet Azeroth is over. In the third and last episode, Bill Bailey goes to the extremes of the world: the heat of Searing Gorge, the cold of Winterspring and the Plaguelands, with the murlocs never far away. The episode lasts just over five minutes.

Bailey also asks the viewer a question: what are you waiting for? The wait is almost over, because World of Warcraft: Forever launches on 5 November at 00:00 Belgian time.

## Episode 2: the coast

The second episode came out on 1 November on the official World of Warcraft channel. Bailey travels along the shores of Azeroth: clear water, beautiful beaches, and of course something dangerous close by.
