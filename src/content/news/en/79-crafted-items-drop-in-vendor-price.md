In the beta build of 16 October, 79 crafted items sell to a vendor for less. They come from six professions, and together they bring in 44% less than before. Crafting items only to sell them pays less again.

Buying materials, crafting an item for less than a vendor pays for it and then selling that item is called a vendor shuffle. The wands from Enchanting went first. On 1 October they [dropped to 1 copper](/news/crafted-wands-now-sell-for-1-copper/), and a week later they [sold for silver again](/news/crafted-wands-sell-for-silver-again/), but for less than before.

![Two treasure chests, one of wood and one of dark iron, half buried in a sea of gold coins with a few swords sticking out, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-16-wow-forever-gold-and-chests.jpg)

The new build came with the [maintenance of 15 October](/news/beta-goes-down-for-a-new-build-with-many-fixes/). The old prices in this post come from the beta build of 13 October. Whether the cuts are aimed at bots, Blizzard does not say. They come in the same weeks as the measures against gold sellers, such as the [economy hold](/news/an-economy-hold-freezes-trade-for-suspected-rmt/) that freezes trade.

## Blacksmithing loses the most

Of the 79 items, 54 come from Blacksmithing. <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=250603"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_spear_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bronze Dory</a> drops the most, by 85%: from 32 silver 53 copper to 4 silver 88 copper. <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=250487"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_chain_09.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sterling Silver Breastplate</a> falls from 28 silver 23 copper to 4 silver 85 copper. Half of all 79 items lose 40% or more.

All 8 items from Enchanting, such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249442"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_relics_libramofhope.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Libram of Invocation</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249443"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_relics_totemofrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Totem of Ancestral Protection</a>, lose more than half. Two bars from Mining drop by 40%: <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249726"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ingot_livingsteel.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Azerothium Bar</a> from 50 to 30 silver and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=251291"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/item_elementiumbar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heavy Thorium Bar</a> from 25 to 15 silver.
