Camping is one of the new systems in WoW Forever, and every tradeskill feeds it. Each one crafts items that go at a campsite, and anyone who sits there for a minute picks up the buff. You do not need the tradeskill yourself, and you do not need to be in a group.

Wowhead listed the full set from the beta. Blizzard has not published the numbers.

## How you get at it

A quest early in the game, The Great Outdoors, unlocks the perks. After that, a buff called Campfire Nearby appears on your bar whenever you come near a camp someone has placed. Walk over, type /sit and stay put for a minute.

That is the whole social trick of the system: a camp left standing by a stranger works for you too.

## What each tradeskill gives

| Tradeskill | The buff | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alchemy | Mana regeneration | Mana Well | Fermenter | Alchemy Laboratory |
| Blacksmithing | Strength | Sharpening Wheel | Anvil | Master Forge |
| Enchanting | Armor, all stats and resistances | Enchanted Lute | Arcane Salvager | Arcane Forge |
| Engineering | No stat buff | Reagent Bot | Repair Bot | Anarchist's Workbench |
| Herbalism | Intellect | Incense Candle | Greenhouse | Seed Hybridizer |
| Leatherworking | Rested experience, up to 5 percent of a level | Camp Tent | Tanning Rack | Sewing Machine |
| Mining | Melee Attack Power | Lodestone | Rock Garden | Molten Foundry |
| Skinning | 2 percent Crit | Camp Chair | Field Guide | Trapper's Workbench |
| Tailoring | Spirit | Faction Banner | Spinning Wheel | Loom |
| Cooking | More room in the camp | Basic Campfire Kit | Journeyman Campfire Kit | Expert Campfire Kit |
| First Aid | Stamina | First Aid Kit | Toxin Study | Plague Doctor's Laboratory |
| Fishing | 8 percent to stats | Fish Bowl | Fishing Rack | Fishing Hut |

The stat buffs run through all three tiers of a tradeskill. What the higher tiers add is the workbench: an Anvil you can use, a Forge, an Arcane Forge, a Repair Bot that also repairs. Herbalism grows herbs from planted seeds at tier 2 and combines seeds into rarer ones at tier 3. Mining spawns a common node over time. Skinning hands out Track Beasts.

Cooking is the odd one out. Its kits do not buff a stat; they decide how much else fits in the camp, from 3 extra features at tier 1 to 10 at tier 3.

How much each buff is worth in numbers, and whether the values survive the beta, Blizzard has not said.
