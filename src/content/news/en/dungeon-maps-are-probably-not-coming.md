Dungeons in World of Warcraft: Forever will probably not get a map. A map makes players look for the shortest route, says Senior Game Designer Jay Hartman. Blizzard wants a dungeon to feel like an adventure, with corners worth exploring.

Hartman said it in the third episode of The WoW: Forever Podcast, on 8 October. Some players ask for dungeon maps, to find their way more easily and finish a run faster. Others enjoy finding the way themselves. Classic had no maps inside dungeons either.

> We want you to feel like it's an adventure and it's threatening and we want you to explore those nooks and crannies. If you have a dungeon map, people find the shortest route possible and try to avoid stuff. We want to try to incentivize you to go explore [...] This is a good reason of why we probably won't have dungeon maps because of all of these things.

Hartman does not rule maps out completely. In the same episode, Blizzard announced [20% more experience from kills in dungeons](/news/dungeon-kills-give-20-percent-more-experience/). Until a map arrives, the [dungeon guides](/guides/dungeons/) describe the route through each dungeon.
