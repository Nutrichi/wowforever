Student Fodder no longer gives rested experience in World of Warcraft: Forever. In Season of Discovery the trail mix granted four bars of it, writes Wowhead. In Forever it heals, restores some of your resource and speeds you up.

Student Fodder is a reward from the quest chain for the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=211527"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_bigbagofenchantments.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cozy Sleeping Bag</a>, which returns from Season of Discovery. The chain pays out five of them. The [guide to the Cozy Sleeping Bag](/guides/cozy-sleeping-bag/) walks through it step by step.


Eating Student Fodder still heals at once and restores part of the resource bar of your class. On top of that it now applies <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1302113"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_sprint.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Trail Snack</a>, which gives 60% more movement speed for a moment, writes Wowhead. Trail Snack also heals 175 health per tick over time.

![The combat log with six lines in which Trail Snack heals for 175 per tick, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-04-wow-forever-combat-log-trail-snack.jpg)

Why the rested experience is gone, Blizzard does not say. Wowhead suspects that Forever already has enough ways to level faster. A [campfire](/guides/camping/) gives one bar of rested experience every hour, almost every new food gives 5% bonus experience, and the Cozy Sleeping Bag up to 3% for an hour. Legacy talents also make levelling alts easier.
