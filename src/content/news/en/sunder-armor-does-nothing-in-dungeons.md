The armor debuffs in World of Warcraft: Forever seem to do nothing in dungeons. Players who went through their combat logs found that Sunder Armor and the rest lower the armor of a boss on paper, while your hits are reduced as if the debuff was not there.

## What players found

According to a [bug report on GitHub](https://github.com/ClassicWoWCommunity/forever-bugs/issues/555), the debuffs show up in the combat log, but the share of each player or pet hit that armor removes does not move. A Warrior gets nothing from their own Sunder Armor either.

The testers saw it in every level 60 dungeon they checked: [Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/lower-blackrock-spire/), [Stratholme](/guides/dungeons/stratholme-living/), [Scholomance](/guides/dungeons/scholomance/), [Dire Maul](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-east/), [Blackrock Depths](/guides/dungeons/blackrock-depths/), [The Shaper's Terrace](/guides/dungeons/shapers-terrace/) and [Blackmaw Hold](/guides/dungeons/blackmaw-hold/). On <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=10430">The Beast</a>, a level 60 should lose about 14% of their physical damage with Faerie Fire and Sunder Armor up. Right now it is 40%.

Two details stand out:

- **NPCs do benefit.** An NPC that fights next to you hits harder with every stack of Sunder Armor.
- **The open world works.** Outside a dungeon, the debuffs lower armor for players as they should.

## What it means for you

Physical damage dealers such as Warriors, Rogues and Hunters lose the most. Until Blizzard fixes it, a Sunder Armor in a dungeon is only worth it for threat. Blizzard has not responded yet, and some players say the bug has been there since the beta.
