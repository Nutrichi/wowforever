The weekly gold quest for level 60 in World of Warcraft: Forever has a new task. After two weeks of Dire Maul, Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay now sends you into Blackrock Mountain. The reward stays the same: a satchel with an item worth up to 100 gold.

## The quest

<a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96075">In Demand - Blackrock Legal Records</a> asks for three items from three bosses, two in Blackrock Spire and one in Blackrock Depths. Bring them to Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay.

|---|---|---|
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=272017"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nefarion's Remit</a> | General Drakkisath | [Upper Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/upper-blackrock-spire/) |
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=272018"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Overseer's License</a> | Overlord Wyrmthalak | [Lower Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/lower-blackrock-spire/) |
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=272019"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bronzebeard Wedding Contract</a> | Emperor Dagran Thaurissan | [Blackrock Depths](/guides/dungeons/blackrock-depths/) |

Drakkisath waits at the end of Upper Blackrock Spire, which needs a full group. Our guides for [Upper](/guides/dungeons/upper-blackrock-spire/) and [Lower Blackrock Spire](/guides/dungeons/lower-blackrock-spire/) and [Blackrock Depths](/guides/dungeons/blackrock-depths/) have the routes and bosses. Note that Upper Blackrock Spire just [got harder after a bug fix](/news/upper-blackrock-spire-gets-harder-after-a-bug-fix/).
