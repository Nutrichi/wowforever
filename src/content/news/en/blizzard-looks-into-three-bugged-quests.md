> **Update 21 November:** A hotfix on 20 November fixes Ti'ik's Head: it now drops for every player in the group with the quest. More in [our post](/news/looking-for-group-shows-more-groups-again/).

Three quests in World of Warcraft: Forever do not work as intended, and Blizzard is looking into them. Blizzard support confirmed the bugs in comments on <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=92468">Call of Earth</a>, <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=80455">Biding Our Time</a> and <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98203">Witch Doctor Ti'ik</a>. When a fix comes, is not known.

## Call of Earth

Skyborne Shamans who finish the quest at level 3 do not get Stoneskin Totem. Other races learn their first Earth totem from the same quest in their starting zone, see the [Shaman leveling guide](/classes/shaman/leveling/).

> We're aware that completing the quest as a level 3 Skyborne Shaman does not grant Stoneskin Totem spell. We're already investigating this issue.

## Ti'ik's Head

Ti'ik's Head drops for only one player when a group does the quest for Witch Doctor Ti'ik.

> We're aware that Ti'ik's Head only drops for one party member when completing the quest in a group. We're already investigating this issue.

## Biding Our Time

The quest can give a reward with a red question mark that cannot be used. Some say they have received a working cloak since.

> We're aware that the quest may award an item with a red question mark that cannot be used. We're already looking into this issue.
