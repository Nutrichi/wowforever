Three more specs on our [BiS page](/bis/) for World of Warcraft: Forever traded "Coming soon" for a full pre-raid list at level 60. That makes 26 of the 28 specs.

## Three new lists

- [Feral Druid](/bis/forever/preraid/druid-feral/)
- [Retribution Paladin](/bis/forever/preraid/paladin-retribution/)
- [Discipline Priest](/bis/forever/preraid/priest-discipline/)

The Feral list is for Cat Form. A list for bear tanks follows, just like one for Assassination Rogues.

## Ten updated lists

These lists follow the latest changes, with new picks in several slots: Restoration Druid, Beast Mastery, Marksmanship, Combat, Elemental, Restoration Shaman, Affliction, Demonology, Destruction, Arms.

Every list shows the date of its last update, and up to three options per slot with where the item comes from.
