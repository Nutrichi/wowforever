Wowhead spotted a new branch for the retail version of World of Warcraft: Forever on the CDN servers of Blizzard. The site reported the find on 16 September. Blizzard has not confirmed it. The game launches on 4 November.

What the branch holds is not known. Wowhead did not describe the contents, and Blizzard has said nothing about the entry.

The beta of Forever starts on 17 September, a day after the find. More will become visible then, writes Wowhead.

Whether the branch belongs to that beta or to the version that goes live on 4 November, is not known.
