World buffs no longer disappear on a loading screen in World of Warcraft: Forever. The hotfixes of 23 November fix the bug that [removed them on every zone change](/news/world-buffs-vanish-on-every-loading-screen/), just as Aidan "Zirene" Moon [promised a day earlier](/news/losing-world-buffs-on-loading-screens-is-a-bug/).

You can take <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16609"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_teleportorgrimmar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Warchief's Blessing</a> into a dungeon again, cross to the other continent with it, and walk out of [Dire Maul North](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-north/) with the Tribute Run buffs.

## Two buffs were too strong

The same hotfixes fix two buffs that gave twice as much as intended:

| Buff | Change |
|---|---|
| <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=22817"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_undyingstrength.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fengus' Ferocity</a> | Spell power halved, from 230 to 115 |
| Darkmoon Faire buffs | Half as strong as before |

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=22820"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_lesserheal02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Slip'kik's Savvy</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=22818"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_massteleport.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mol'dar's Moxie</a>, the other two Tribute Run buffs, stay the same.

## Still nothing in raids

World buffs still fall off when you enter a raid. That was a choice for Forever from the start. Blizzard has now also flagged 16 buffs so they cannot be used inside a raid, among them <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16609"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_teleportorgrimmar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Warchief's Blessing</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15366"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_mindvision.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Songflower Serenade</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=22888"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_head_dragon_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=24425"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_creature_poison_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Spirit of Zandalar</a>, the Darkmoon Faire buffs and all three Tribute Run buffs.

For dungeons that changes nothing: grab a buff before your next run and it lasts the whole way.
