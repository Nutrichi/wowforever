Paladins and Warlocks who finish their epic mount quest in World of Warcraft: Forever do not get the epic riding skill with it. That means they can ride their new class mount, but no other epic mount, such as the <a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=280544"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_catskybornemount_c60_blue.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cerulean Prideclaw</a>.

Blizzard confirmed the bug on the European forums:

> We've found the bug here, and we think it's affecting both Paladins and Warlocks. We're working on a fix, but the fix is going to take another day or two.

So hold off on buying another epic mount for now. At level 40 the class quest [does teach the normal riding skill](/news/paladins-and-warlocks-ride-for-free-at-level-40/), so this only touches the epic version. The [Dreadsteed](/guides/mounts/dreadsteed/) and the [Charger](/guides/mounts/charger/) themselves work.
