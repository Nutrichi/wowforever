Every week a board stands behind the developers in the WoW Forever podcast, with a new message on it. In [episode 8](/news/podcast-episode-8-blizzard-wants-to-get-rid-of-bis/) it hangs behind Aidan "Zirene" Moon and reads:

> STUDY THE WELL 196.5.86.259

Players in the secret finding community already narrowed it down to one well on Mount Hyjal. What the numbers mean, and what the well hides, is not known yet.

Earlier boards led to beta codes, coordinates of a beautiful spot and secret codes, and one carried a line in Darnassian that [hints at a Hidden Blade](/news/a-podcast-board-hints-at-a-hidden-blade/). We will add the answer here once someone solves it.
