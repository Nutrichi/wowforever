Two drops from The Deadmines now help professions in World of Warcraft: Forever. <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5199"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_pants_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Smelting Pants</a> make smelting faster and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5198"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_staff_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cookie's Stirring Rod</a> speeds up Cooking, writes Wowhead. A crafted hammer also replaces the Blacksmith Hammer in your bags.

Both drops are blue, and the [guide to The Deadmines](/guides/dungeons/the-deadmines/) lists them with their bosses. The pants cut the crafting time of Smelt Copper, Smelt Tin, Smelt Bronze and Smelt Silver by 25%, handy for early Miners. The wand raises Cooking speed by 25% and got that effect in the latest build.

The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=285281"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_20.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcanite Blacksmith Hammer</a> is a thrown weapon for Blacksmiths, with Blacksmithing 270 required. It has a chance on hit to stun the target for 1 second. According to its tooltip it also serves as a <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5956"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_20.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blacksmith Hammer</a>, so the old hammer no longer takes up a bag slot. How the recipe is learned, Wowhead does not say.

Such items are upgrades that do not always add power, writes Wowhead. That is the horizontal progression Forever aims for.
