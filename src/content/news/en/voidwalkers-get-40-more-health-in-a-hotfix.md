Blizzard has added a second, much longer round of hotfixes to its list for World of Warcraft: Forever. The Voidwalker already gets about 40 more health at every level, Druid healing with Bloom works again, three dungeons are fixed and the Tanning Rack needs less Leatherworking skill. The first round of 5 November is in [our earlier post](/news/erland-respawns-faster-in-the-first-hotfixes/).

## Classes

- **Warlock**: the Voidwalker has about 40 more base health at every level. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17804"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Soul Siphon</a> no longer makes <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1316697"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_deathknight_hemorrhagicfever.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wrack</a> raise its own damage. The bigger changes to pets and Affliction follow [on Tuesday](/news/hunter-and-warlock-pets-get-tougher-on-tuesday/).
- **Druid**: Bloom heals its target again. The tooltips of Bear Form, Ravage and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18562"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_relics_idolofrejuvenation.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Swiftmend</a> showed the wrong numbers; only the text changed.
- **Mage**: Mages no longer learn Research and then lose it on relog. That spell should not exist, you only get Study after its quest. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400574"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_blast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Arcane Blast</a> now lasts the full 18 seconds.
- **Rogue**: Shank shows Rank 1 and Rank 2 in its tooltip, and with Boot Knife it shows the right number of combo points.
- **Warrior**: the tooltip of Reckless Swings now says that it activates <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1323967"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/racial_troll_berserk.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gore Drinker</a>.
- The legacy talent The Quick and the Dead sometimes did not work. Fixed.
- The Cooldown Manager always shows the right duration of crowd control, also with diminishing returns or the Orc stun reduction.

## Races

- **Night Elf**: Druids in Shadowmeld could use Ravage and Pounce. Fixed.
- **Orc**: some Warlock pet abilities gained too much from the Orc racial Command.

## Dungeons

- [Maraudon](/guides/dungeons/maraudon/): <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=12238">Zaetar's Spirit</a> did not always appear after <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=12201">Princess Theradras</a>. Fixed.
- [Blackmaw Hold](/guides/dungeons/blackmaw-hold/) and [The Drowned City](/guides/dungeons/the-drowned-city/): some monsters dropped combat for no reason. The portal of The Drowned City now takes you inside.

## Leatherworking

The Tanning Rack (<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1263031"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_10_skinning_leather_rarehide_color1.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tanning Rack</a>) now needs skill 125, down from 140, so it no longer blocks patterns at 125. It gives skill points 15 earlier to match. More on the profession on [our Leatherworking page](/tradeskills/forever/leatherworking/).

## Quests

- <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6641">Vorsha the Lasher</a>: Vorsha no longer lashes everyone in the zone.
- <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5321">The Sleeper Has Awakened</a>: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11218">Kerlonian Evershade</a> got a fresh pot of coffee and falls asleep less often. You also start with the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=13536"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_horn_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Horn of Awakening</a> to wake him.
- <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=91751">Rough Wolf Pelts</a> is cancelled when you unlearn Skinning.
- <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=90902">Rediscovering the Light</a>: healing the NPCs works again.
- <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5541">Ammo for Rumbleshot</a>: you see the loot even right after another player.
- <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95212">Never Saddle on Quality</a>: more spawns and more quest items.
- In Riverglades, party members now share quest items.
- The <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=280438"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ui_darkshore_warfront_horde_abomination.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Abominable Head</a> from The Baron in the Ruins of Lordaeron offers Unending Torment to the Horde from level 15, was 16.
- The <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=16790"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_note_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Damp Note</a> drops more often. Blizzard adds: "Eww."

## Monsters and the world

- **Dun Morogh**: the troggs in Grol'bolar Quarry respawned too fast.
- **Durotar**: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=266849">Ridgeshade Lurkers</a> have loot again.
- **Riverglades**: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=263615">Krek the Noxious</a> drops the right loot.
- **Swamp of Sorrows**: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=254770">Pelluk</a> sometimes did not spawn.
- **Teldrassil**: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=275703">Lasher Sproutlings</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=275708">Blooming Lashers</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=276099">Wrathvine</a> were immune to Nature damage.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273003"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_shortblade_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Searing Dagger</a> only triggers on spells that deal damage.
- You see other players and their targets better from far away.
- The barbershop in Thunder Bluff was too dark. In Blizzard's words, they "moooved the lights around".
