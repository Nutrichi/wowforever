The Alliance gets its own version of Warchief's Blessing in World of Warcraft: Forever. The world buff <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=460940"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_teleportstormwind.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Might of Stormwind</a> goes to Alliance players when the head of Rend Blackhand is turned in. Blizzard first added it in Season of Discovery.

## What the buff does

Might of Stormwind gives 300 extra health, 15% haste on melee attacks and 10 mana every 5 seconds. It is the counterpart of <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16609"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_arcane_teleportorgrimmar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Warchief's Blessing</a>, the buff the Horde gets for the same head. Before Season of Discovery, only the Horde had this buff.

The two tooltips differ in one place. Warchief's Blessing gives 15% haste to all spells and attacks, Might of Stormwind to melee attacks only. Whether that difference also holds in the game, is not known.

## How to get it

<a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=10429">Warchief Rend Blackhand</a> in Upper Blackrock Spire drops the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=12630"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_head_orc_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Head of Rend Blackhand</a>. Alliance players take it to Master Mathias Shaw in Stormwind for the quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84377">End of the Dark Horde</a>. The fight is in the [Upper Blackrock Spire guide](/guides/dungeons/upper-blackrock-spire/guide/).

World buffs work in the open world and in dungeons, but not in raids in WoW Forever. How long Might of Stormwind lasts in Forever, is not stated.
