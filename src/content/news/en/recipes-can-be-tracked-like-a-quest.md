Professions in WoW Forever get three conveniences. A recipe can be tracked like a quest, a whole profession book can be linked in chat, and new filters sort the recipes. Wowhead showed all three from the beta.

## Tracking a recipe

The profession window has a new checkbox, Track Recipe, at the bottom near Create All.

![The Alchemy window with Elixir of Minor Force selected and the Track Recipe checkbox at the bottom](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-25-wow-forever-alchemy-track-recipe.png)

Ticking it puts the reagents on the right of the screen, under the quests, in the same list as the objectives. That helps when buying materials at the Auction House or farming them in the world, writes Wowhead.

![The objective tracker with a quest and, under Profession, Elixir of Minor Defense with its three reagents](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-25-wow-forever-tracked-reagents.jpg)

## Linking the profession book

A small chat bubble sits to the right of the skill bar. Clicking it opens a menu that posts the profession book in a chat channel of choice, such as Guild, Party, General or Trade. Other players click the link and see the skill and every recipe.

![The Cooking window with the Post in chat menu open next to the skill bar](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-25-wow-forever-profession-post-in-chat.png)

## New filters

Forever adds [over 600 new recipes](/news/professions-camping-and-legacy-points-in-wow-forever/), and new filters help to find the right one:

- **Has skill up** shows only recipes that still raise the skill.
- **Have materials** shows only what can be crafted right now.
- **Slots** narrows the list to one gear slot, such as trinkets.
- **The search bar** finds a recipe by name.
