[Best-in-slot lists](/bis/mop/) for Mists of Pandaria Classic now cover all 34 specs, Monks and all four Druid specs included. There are 136 lists, from pre-raid gear to Siege of Orgrimmar. Every item shows where it drops.

## The phases

The pre-raid lists are built around the Celestial Dungeons, which drop Siege of Orgrimmar loot at the item level of Normal raids. Phase 1 covers Tier 14 Heroic in Mogu'shan Vaults, Heart of Fear and Terrace of Endless Spring. Phase 2 is Throne of Thunder with Tier 15, and the last phase is Siege of Orgrimmar.

Tier tokens follow a fixed pattern per boss. In Tier 14, helms come from Sha of Fear, shoulders from Lei Shi, chests from Grand Empress Shek'zeer, hands from Wind Lord Mel'jarak and legs from Amber-Shaper Un'sok.

Some specs keep trinkets from Throne of Thunder in later phases. Feral and Windwalker keep Rune of Re-Origination, Demonology keeps Unerring Vision of Lei Shen. Those trinkets do not drop in the Celestial Dungeons, and the lists mark them.

## Sources

The lists are based on the gear guides of Icy Veins. A few wrong item origins on the source pages were corrected against the encounter lists, such as the raid of Jin'rokh the Breaker, the first boss of Throne of Thunder.
