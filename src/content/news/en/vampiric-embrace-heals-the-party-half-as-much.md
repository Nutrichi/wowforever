Shadow Priests heal their party for half as much in World of Warcraft: Forever. Since the weekly maintenance of 17 November, Vampiric Embrace returns 10% of their Shadow damage instead of 20%. The Priest gets twice that healing, so solo nothing changes.

## Vampiric Embrace

The change came with the weekly maintenance, the rest of the hotfixes of 16 November went live right away. The Priest who casts Vampiric Embrace gets 100% more healing from it, so 20% as before. Blizzard adds:

> This change results in no difference for Priest solo play.

Dark Inspiration also no longer stacks when a party has more than one Shadow Priest. The [Shadow Priest guide](/classes/priest/shadow/) has the new numbers.

## Windfury Totem and Warriors

Attacks from Windfury Totem ignored armor and had no internal cooldown. Both are fixed. The extra attacks now do generate Rage. Mutilate and Execute lost an extra chance to trigger Windfury, and Shield Slam, Shield Bash and empowered Holy Strikes from Shieldworn can no longer trigger it at all.

For Enhancement Shamans, Maelstrom Weapon no longer works on two casts, and Thundering Strikes gives its critical strike chance without a weapon too. Fury Warriors get Unbridled Wrath and Reckless Swings from Cleave and Heroic Strike again, and those two no longer use up Flurry. The Revered Champion now offers Legacy of Valor to Warriors who qualify.

## Other classes

- **Druid:** the ranks of Wild Growth now go on cooldown. Genesis now raises the bleed damage of Pounce.
- **Rogue:** poisons can no longer go above their highest rank. Shank no longer removes combo points from other targets when Boot Knife is not talented.

## Dungeons, items and the rest

- **The Drowned City:** Captain Dreadrise can be stunned, and his Abyssal Bubble can no longer block, dodge or parry. The rare "Pyromancer" Braxton now joins the fight. See the [Drowned City guide](/guides/dungeons/the-drowned-city/).
- **Sunken Temple:** the serpent statues of Atal'alarion no longer stop working when you click them in the wrong order.
- **Camping:** the Fishing Rack now gives lures to everyone, and Bloated Fish from it drop more often, with new loot. More in the [camping guide](/guides/camping/).
- **Items:** Formula: Enchant Boots - Speed from reputation is now bind on pickup. Outrider's Gloves work, and the Violet Tragan no longer breaks crowd control.
- **PvP:** dying in the Gold Mine of Arathi Basin no longer sends you to a graveyard in the Riverglades.
- **Quests:** Blood Red Bandanas drop more often, Witch Doctor Zum'rah spawns for The Talisman of Kazdor, and the scout prisoners of No Soldier Left Behind count for the whole party. The repeatable Water Pouch Bounty no longer gives experience.
