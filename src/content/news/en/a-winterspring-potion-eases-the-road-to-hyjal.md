Mount Hyjal is one of the new zones of World of Warcraft: Forever, with quests from level 57. The road there is the hard part: you have to run through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring, full of elite demons at level 60. A new quest in Everlook makes that run a lot easier.

The quest is repeatable, so you can always make more potions.

## What the potion does

The potion lowers the aggro range of creatures in Winterspring for 10 minutes. That is enough time to walk through Darkwhisper Gorge to Hyjal. A few things to know:

- It is not invisibility. Walk into melee range of a monster and it still attacks you.
- Your pet does not get the effect. Hunters and Warlocks best dismiss their pet first.
- It only works in Winterspring. Leave the zone and the effect is gone.

Warlocks and Priests also need Darkwhisper Gorge for some class quests at level 60, so the potion helps them too. Once you are in Hyjal, the [free 18-slot bag](/news/a-free-18-slot-bag-waits-on-mount-hyjal/) from the mail quests is a good first stop.
