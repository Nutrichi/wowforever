Three escort NPCs in World of Warcraft: Forever can disappear near a zone border, and the quest fails. Blizzard is investigating, its customer support writes. The same goes for an Undead Paladin quest where healing does not always count.

## Three escorts

An escort quest fails at once when the NPC dies, or in this case simply disappears. Blizzard's customer support left the same message under three quests:

| Quest | NPC | Zone |
|---|---|---|
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6482">Freedom to Ruul</a> | <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=12818">Ruul Snowhoof</a> | Ashenvale |
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=898">Free From the Hold</a> | <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3465">Gilthares Firebough</a> | The Barrens |
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=155">The Defias Brotherhood</a> | Defias Traitor | Westfall |

> We're aware that Ruul/Gilthares Firebough/Defias Traitor may disappear when moving near or across zone and area boundaries, causing the quest to fail. We're already investigating the issue.

## Rediscovering the Light

One of the first quests of an Undead Paladin is <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=90902">Rediscovering the Light</a>. You heal <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=259377">Injured Deathguard</a> with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=635"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_holybolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Light</a>, but that healing sometimes gives no progress.

> We're aware that healing Injured Deathguards may not grant progress and we're already investigating the issue.

When a fix follows, Blizzard does not say. Another escort did get help in the first hotfixes: [Deathstalker Erland respawns much faster](/news/erland-respawns-faster-in-the-first-hotfixes/).
