Hunters on the live servers of World of Warcraft: Forever lose about half of <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=13165"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_ravenform.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Aspect of the Hawk</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1299346"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_trueshot.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Trueshot Aura</a>. The hotfixes appeared during the name reservation. <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3383"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_potion_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Elixir of Wisdom</a> was weakened as well. Blizzard has not confirmed the changes in development notes.

The new values come from hotfixes on the live servers since [Early Name Reservation opened on 27 October](/news/early-name-reservation-opens-on-27-october/). Whether they are final, or still change before the launch on 4 November, is not known.

## Aspect of the Hawk

Aspect of the Hawk raises ranged attack power. The higher ranks lose the most.

| Rank | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 22 | 15 |
| **2** | 35 | 22 |
| **3** | 50 | 30 |
| **4** | 78 | 38 |
| **5** | 90 | 47 |

## Trueshot Aura

Trueshot Aura is the Marksmanship talent that gives ranged attack power to every party or raid member within 45 yards. Since [the beta build of 15 October](/news/battle-shout-and-totems-now-buff-the-whole-raid/), it reaches the whole raid.

| Rank | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 30 | 15 |
| **2** | 40 | 22 |
| **3** | 50 | 30 |
| **4** | 75 | 40 |

The [Marksmanship Hunter guide](/guides/marksmanship-hunter/) still lists the old value of 30 at rank 1.

## Elixir of Wisdom

The low-level elixir from [Alchemy](/tradeskills/forever/alchemy/) now gives 3 Intellect instead of 6, for 30 minutes instead of 1 hour. The recipe comes from a vendor and needs Alchemy 90.
