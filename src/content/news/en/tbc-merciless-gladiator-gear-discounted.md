Arena Season 2 of The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary has ended. The Arena vendors now sell the Merciless Gladiator set at a discount. The rating requirements are gone. Every piece, weapons included, is open to every player with enough Arena Points.

The discount covers all Season 2 set pieces, weapons, off-hands and relics. The full set can be bought, set bonuses included.

The weapon page of the vendor lists Merciless Gladiator's War Staff at 1,245 Arena Points. The Spellblade, the Gavel and the Salvation cost 1,030 points each, the Slicer 870.

Season 3 starts in the week of 1 September. At that moment, Arena Points that are left are converted into Honor Points. At what rate, Blizzard has not said.
