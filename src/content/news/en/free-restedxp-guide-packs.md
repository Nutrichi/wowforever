Three free route packs for the RestedXP guide addon are available on CurseForge. They cover Classic from level 1 to 60, The Burning Crusade from 60 to 70 and Wrath of the Lich King from 70 to 80. The packs cost nothing.

The packs install like any other addon, through the CurseForge app. They load into the guide window of RestedXP in the game, which shows the next step of the route while playing.

The Classic pack follows the leveling zones of the original game. The TBC pack covers Outland, and the WotLK pack the routes through Northrend.

The complete RestedXP guides are sold separately and contain more routes. The free packs work without that paid version.
