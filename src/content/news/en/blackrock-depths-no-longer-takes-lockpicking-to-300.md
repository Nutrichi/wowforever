Rogues who want 300 Lockpicking in World of Warcraft: Forever need a new plan for the last stretch. The old trick in Blackrock Depths no longer gets you there.

## What changed

In Classic, Rogues went to [Blackrock Depths](/guides/dungeons/blackrock-depths/) from 250, opened the Shadowforge lock and the three doors that normally need the Shadowforge Key, reset the dungeon and did it again until 300. In Forever, the Shadowforge lock turns grey around 265. After that, the loop gives no more points.

## Where to go instead

Tyr's Hand in Eastern Plaguelands is the best spot now. It is full of Scarlet Footlockers, and from 280 the pockets of the mobs hold strongboxes too. Bring care or a friend: almost everything there is elite.

## What 300 gets you

With 300 you open the door of Dire Maul North yourself, so the group does not need the Crescent Key from Pusillin in Dire Maul East. And players in capital cities always want their lockboxes opened, often for a tip.

The full route from 1 to 300 is in our [Lockpicking guide](/classes/rogue/lockpicking/), now without the Blackrock Depths loop.
