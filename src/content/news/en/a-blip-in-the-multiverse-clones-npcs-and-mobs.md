For a short time this evening, NPCs and mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever stood in the world several times over. Quest givers had clones, and some areas suddenly had far more monsters than normal. Blizzard announced in a server message that it is fixing every zone:

> [SERVER] Greetings! There was a slight blip in the multiverse. We are in the process of de-blipping all zones now.

The copies seem to come from the layers: one copy per layer, all visible in the same world at once. In Silverpine Forest, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=1938">Dalar Dawnweaver</a> stood there four times, with the names stacked on top of each other.

## What to do

Nothing, Blizzard fixes it zone by zone. Until then, take care in areas with more monsters than usual, because a pull can be much bigger than you expect. Earlier today Blizzard also promised to [make empty layers busier](/news/eu-players-pick-french-to-find-busier-layers/).
