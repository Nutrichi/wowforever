Blizzard showed the Forsaken Charger during the World of Warcraft: Forever panel Found Photographs. It is the mount of the Forsaken Paladin, the new combination of race and class in Forever. Forsaken Paladins get an epic mount quest of their own at level 60.

Every Paladin race has its own Charger, and the Forsaken is no exception, writes Wowhead.

The path of the Forsaken Paladin follows the new Warcraft III campaign Forsaken Kingdom. It starts at a moment when these former paladins are still mistrusted by the other Forsaken and are learning what it means to wield the Light in undeath, says Blizzard.

Bandarion Keep in the Whispering Wood in Tirisfal Glades is the place where that story plays out.

Where Alliance Paladins claim their epic charger in the name of redemption, the Forsaken ride in the name of retribution.

The Forsaken Paladin is also coming to modern WoW in patch 12.1. Whether the mount there looks the same is not confirmed, and the chargers of the Night Elves and Trolls have not been shown.
