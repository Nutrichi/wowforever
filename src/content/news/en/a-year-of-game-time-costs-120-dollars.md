Planning to play World of Warcraft: Forever for a while? Then this is a good moment to buy game time. To celebrate the launch, Blizzard sells 365 days of game time for $120 until 7 December.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | WoW Celebration Pass |
| What | 365 days of WoW game time |
| Price | $120, about 23% less than a year at $12.99 a month |
| Until | 7 December 2026 |
| Games | WoW: Forever, WoW Classic and modern WoW, up to the expansion before Midnight |

Already playing? Blizzard adds the 365 days to the game time or subscription you already have, so you lose nothing by buying now.

## Good to know

The pass is game time, not a yearly subscription. The [12-month subscription with a tabard, backpack and pet](/news/yearly-subscribers-get-a-tabard-backpack-and-pet/) is a separate plan. Prices in euros and other currencies can differ: check the Battle.net shop for your region.
