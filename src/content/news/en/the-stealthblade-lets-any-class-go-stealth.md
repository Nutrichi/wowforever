You do not have to be a Rogue to sneak around in World of Warcraft: Forever. The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=10625"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_41.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Stealthblade</a> is an old dagger, but in Forever it gives every class that can use daggers a Stealth of its own.

## What it does

- Level 44 main-hand dagger, Bind on Equip
- **Rogues:** your stealth level goes up by 2, so you are harder to spot.
- **Every other class:** Stealth out of combat, at half your movement speed, with a 30-minute cooldown.

That makes it a fun pick for Hunters, Mages, Priests, Warlocks, Druids and Shamans, for example to slip past a group of mobs to a quest item or a herb.

## Where it drops

The Stealthblade is a rare drop, about 0.2%, from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=5263">Mummified Atal'ai</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=5243">Cursed Atal'ai</a>. You find them around the [Sunken Temple](/guides/dungeons/sunken-temple/) in the Swamp of Sorrows. Because it is Bind on Equip, you can also keep an eye on the Auction House.
