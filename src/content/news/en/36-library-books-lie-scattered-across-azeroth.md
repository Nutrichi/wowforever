Players on the WoW Forever beta have found 36 library books so far, from Elwynn Forest to Tanaris. Each one counts toward three quests with rewards at the library of your faction. A new guide lists every book with its /way coordinates.

The Alliance hands the books in to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=211033">Garion Wendell</a> in Stormwind, the Horde to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=211022">Owen Thadd</a> in Undercity. At 10 books comes a necklace, at 20 books a ring from level 20, and at 25 books a bow, a shield or an off-hand from level 30.

Fifteen books lie in zones up to level 20: nine in the Eastern Kingdoms and six in Kalimdor. Seven more wait in zones from level 30, and fourteen in zones from level 40, up to Blackrock Mountain, Everlook and Lost Rigger Cove.

Each book also gives experience and some silver. One warning: a book you delete cannot be looted again, as things stand on the beta.

All 36 locations, the tricky spots and the rewards are in the guide [Library books](/guides/library-books/).
