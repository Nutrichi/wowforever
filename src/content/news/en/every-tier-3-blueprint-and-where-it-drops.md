Cooking has two: the Iron Oven, and the Expert Campfire with room for 10 camp objects.

## How the workstations work

- You craft the workstation with your profession, and it is used up when you place it.
- You can put it down anywhere you can light a campfire.

Besides the workstation, most Tier 3 objects give a buff to your camp, such as Strength from the Master Forge, Spirit from the Loom or 2% critical strike from the Trapper's Workbench. The full list per profession is in our [camping guide](/guides/camping/).

## Buy or farm?

The blueprints can be traded, but on the Auction House they are expensive right now. The bosses that drop them also drop the reagents to build the workstation, so farming your own is often the better deal.
