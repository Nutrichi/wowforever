World of Warcraft: Forever opens everywhere at the same moment: on 4 November at 15:00 Pacific time. In Belgium and the Netherlands that is 00:00 on the night from 4 to 5 November.

| Region | Launch |
|---|---|
| **Los Angeles** (PST) | 4 November, 15:00 |
| **New York** (EST) | 4 November, 18:00 |
| **São Paulo** (BRT) | 4 November, 20:00 |
| **London** (GMT) | 4 November, 23:00 |
| **Belgium, Netherlands, France, Germany** (CET) | 5 November, 00:00 |
| **Seoul, Tokyo** | 5 November, 08:00 |
| **Sydney, Melbourne** (AEDT) | 5 November, 10:00 |

Before that, the game is down for about [five hours of maintenance](/news/five-hours-of-maintenance-before-the-launch/), from around 19:00 Belgian time.

## Download now

You can install the game in advance. Open the Battle.net launcher, choose World of Warcraft: Forever, select Forever in the version menu and click Install. When the download is done, a green message appears under the Play button.

## In the game at the launch

- **Realms**: PvE, PvP, RP and RP-PvP, up to level 60.
- **Skyborne**: the new race for the Skyborne Heroic Pack and higher. Alliance Skyborne can become Mage, Horde Skyborne Shaman.
- **New class combinations**: Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Gnome Priest, Orc Mage, Undead Paladin and Troll Warlock.
- **Systems**: camping, Legacy, transmog and the choice between [Classic and Enhanced](/news/the-experience-preset-classic-or-enhanced/).
- **The world**: all old zones plus Zephras Isle, the Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Mount Hyjal, with more than 1,000 new quests.
- **Dungeons and PvP**: every dungeon up to level 60, the honor system, and the battlegrounds Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Darkspear Islands.

The raids follow on 9 December. Everything after that is in our [timeline of WoW Forever](/forever/).
