Jokerd was one of the [five players who hit level 60 first](/news/five-players-hit-level-60-in-a-record-launch/) in World of Warcraft: Forever. This week Jokerd joins Pull 10, a weekly podcast about Forever, to talk about the race and everything that came before it.

## What the episode covers

The hosts Joardee, rencognito and JurdiJD ask how the preparation for the race to 60 went, and how Forever feels next to the earlier Classic versions and other MMORPGs. A new episode comes out every week on the YouTube channel of Pull 10.
