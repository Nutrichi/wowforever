## Grouping and Recent Allies

Nobody is forced into a group, but working together is rewarded, says Blizzard. Players on the same quest can invite each other, finish twice as fast and share the tags on monsters. That helps most in starting zones and busy quest hubs.

The [Recent Allies](/news/recent-allies-remembers-every-player-from-a-group/) panel lists everyone you played with in a party: class, level, where you grouped and what you did together. Friends get a blue name tag in the world.

## Campfires

A camp needs the first level of Cooking, <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4470"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_tradeskillitem_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Simple Wood</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=4471"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_stone_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Flint and Tinder</a>. Cooking trainers and general goods vendors sell both. Other players can add their own items, also without being in your group.

A basic campfire holds 3 items, a Journeyman campfire 5 and an Expert campfire 10. The buffs only come after at least 60 seconds of sitting by the fire. Each of the 12 primary and secondary professions brings its own camp item, see the [camping guide](/guides/camping/).

## Professions

Every character can learn all three secondary professions: Cooking, First Aid and Fishing. Of the primary professions, you pick two. Mining, Herbalism and Skinning gather. Blacksmithing, Leatherworking, Engineering, Alchemy, Enchanting and Tailoring craft, and Tailors also make bags. Trainers and recipes are under [Tradeskills](/tradeskills/).

## Capital cities

Entering a city such as Stormwind or Orgrimmar moves you to the Trade channel. Cities have the auction house, the bank, weapon trainers and vendors, and a guard points the way. Blizzard advises restocking food, drink, potions and ammunition on every visit.

Cities are also where players find a Mage portal or a Warlock summon, a fast way to the next dungeon. The destinations are in the [Mage portals guide](/classes/mage/portals/).

## Guilds

Joining a guild takes one accepted invite. A new guild starts with a charter from Aldwin Laughlin in the Trade District of Stormwind or Urtrun Clanbringer in the Valley of Strength in Orgrimmar. Nine other players sign it, then the same guild master registers the guild. Leaving takes /gquit.

## At level 60

Raids take groups of 10, 20 or 40 players, and they [open on 9 December](/news/wow-forever-is-live-and-the-raids-open-on-9-december/). The dungeons at max level all need a group too. Solo players have collections, profession mastery and the [Legacy system](/news/you-can-spend-16-legacy-points-and-earn-more-for-a-bear/).
