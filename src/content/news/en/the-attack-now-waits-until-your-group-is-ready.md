Alliance players no longer wait in line in the garden of Stormwind Keep in World of Warcraft: Forever. The quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=434">The Attack!</a> now waits until your group is ready, and you can pick up and share it while the event is running.

## What changes

In Classic, the event started the moment one player accepted the quest from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7766">Tyrion</a>. Only group members standing nearby could accept it along with them. Everyone else had to wait until <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=1754">Lord Gregor Lescovar</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=1755">Marzon the Silent Blade</a> had finished their talk and were killed, which made for long lines at busy hours.

In Forever:

* after you accept the quest, Tyrion gets an extra line, "I'm ready when you are!", and the event only starts when you choose it;
* you can pick up the quest even while the event of another group is running;
* you can share it during the event, so a group with room can take in more players.

## Where it sits in the chain

The Attack! is part of the long Defias chain that starts with The Unsent Letter in [The Deadmines](/guides/dungeons/the-deadmines/quests/) and runs through [The Stockade](/guides/dungeons/the-stockade/quests/). Tyrion gives the quest from level 16, the quest itself is level 31, and it is worth 1,900 experience. After <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=394">The Head of the Beast</a> and <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=395">Brotherhood's End</a>, the chain ends with <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=396">An Audience with the King</a>. The reward is <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2933"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_ring_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Wrynn</a>, a ring with Strength, Agility, Stamina, Intellect and Spirit.
