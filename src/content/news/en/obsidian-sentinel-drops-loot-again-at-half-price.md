The <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7023">Obsidian Sentinel</a> in [Uldaman](/guides/dungeons/uldaman/) drops blue gear again in World of Warcraft: Forever. A few days ago the boss [lost its whole loot table](/news/obsidian-sentinel-in-uldaman-drops-no-more-loot/). The hotfixes of 23 November bring it back, with one catch: every item now sells to a vendor for half.

## The gold farm is mostly over

The boss stands right by the back entrance, so a level 60 could kill it and leave in about 90 seconds. With the new limit of [5 dungeons per hour](/news/dungeon-farms-under-12-minutes-hit-the-new-limit/) and the lower prices, a farm now gives about 4.42 gold for 7.5 minutes of work.

## Good news for leveling

The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9425"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_halberd_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pendulum of Doom</a> is back. You combine it from <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274484"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Axe of Inevitable Ruin</a>, which <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7228">Ironaya</a> drops, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274485"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Axe of Lingering Dread</a> from the Obsidian Sentinel. For a level 39 that is still one of the best two-handed axes around.
