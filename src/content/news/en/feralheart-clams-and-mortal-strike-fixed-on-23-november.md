Blizzard rolled out another round of hotfixes for World of Warcraft: Forever on 23 November. The big ones are covered in their own posts: [world buffs survive loading screens again](/news/world-buffs-survive-loading-screens-again/) and the [Obsidian Sentinel drops loot again](/news/obsidian-sentinel-drops-loot-again-at-half-price/). Here is the rest.

## Classes

- **Druid:** the 4-piece bonus of the Feralheart set now gives 4 Energy every second for 5 seconds, as intended.
- **Priest:** Mind Vision no longer procs effects. For Shadow, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1326159"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_warlock_demonsoul.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dark Inspiration</a> now gives the right Spirit bonus when Mind Flay triggers it, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1277331"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_chastise.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Chastise</a> works in Shadowform.
- **Warlock:** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18135"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_lavaspawn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Intensity</a> now works on Incinerate for Destruction.
- **Warrior:** some players could use Mortal Strike from their spellbook without the talent. It stays in the spellbook, but you can only use it with the talent point spent.
- **All classes:** a few spells and abilities caused more weapon procs than intended. That is fixed.

## Items and races

- <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=219343"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_cape_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Filcher's Cowl</a> no longer has a level requirement.
- Wisp Form could be put on players who were alive. Not anymore.

## Professions

- **Cooking:** Boiled Clams, Clam Chowder, Goblin Deviled Clams, Giant Clam Scorcho, Undermine Clam Chowder and Clam Linguine now give the right Well Fed bonus and sit under "Stamina Food" in the recipe list. <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=3663"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bowl_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Murloc Fin Soup</a> moves up a tier of food. More in our [Cooking guide](/tradeskills/forever/cooking/).
- **Engineering:** the Renewal Gifts of the Goblin and Gnome Engineers no longer show as broken question marks, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=13240"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_musket_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">The Mortar: Reloaded</a> is learned together with Goblin Mortar again.
- **Leatherworking:** <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274491"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_scroll_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pattern: Centaur Hoofwarmers</a> can be learned.
