Blizzard changed Alterac Valley in World of Warcraft: Forever on 25 November. Killing the enemy General now gives far less reputation and honor. Towers, graveyards, mines and Lieutenants give more instead. The NPCs in the battleground also got tougher.

Blizzard gives the reason in the hotfix notes:

> We've seen that players in Alterac Valley were frequently completing the battleground without capturing the objectives, so we've made some adjustments to incentivize a focus on the objectives rather than rushing to kill the enemy faction's General.

Honor per match should stay about the same, and reputation per match should go up, even when matches take longer.

## Reputation and honor

- Killing the enemy General gives a lot less reputation and honor.
- Towers, graveyards and mines give more reputation, and a little more honor.
- Killing Lieutenants gives a lot more reputation.
- Killing enemy NPCs gives more reputation.
- The bonus for a long match now starts after 20 minutes instead of 60. Holding graveyards and mines at the end of such a match gives a lot of reputation.

Later that day Blizzard raised the reputation from Lieutenants and from the bonus for long matches a second time:

> The overall intent for all of these changes was for games to feel more meaningful while trying to maintain a similar amount of rep gained over a similar play time within the battleground, even with longer match lengths.

How much you earn now depends more on how many towers you destroy and what you hold when the match ends. Exact new numbers have not been published.

## Tougher NPCs

- Every enemy creature in Alterac Valley has more health and deals more damage.
- The Warmasters and Marshals return when they leave their room, and they notice you from further away.
- While the objectives stand, the Generals, Warmasters and Marshals get a buff with 4 stacks that lasts 20 minutes. Every objective you take removes a stack.
- The Generals deal more damage, see stealth from further away, and some of them now defend as hard as intended.

Storm Crystals and other quest items you leave on the ground are no longer mailed to you.

The numbers in our [Frostwolf Clan](/reputations/frostwolf-clan/) and [Stormpike Guard](/reputations/stormpike-guard/) guides come from Classic Era and are no longer exact for the General and the end of a match.
