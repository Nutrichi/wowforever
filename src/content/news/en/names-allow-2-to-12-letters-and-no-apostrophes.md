Name reservation for World of Warcraft: Forever opens tomorrow, 27 October, at 18:00 Belgian time. Each character needs a first and a last name of 2 to 12 letters, without spaces, hyphens, numbers or apostrophes. Character creation works in full during the reservation.

## The rules for a name

Every character carries [a first and a second name](/news/every-character-gets-a-first-and-a-second-name/). Neither has to be unique on its own; the combination has to be free across all rulesets of the region.

- Each name counts 2 to 12 characters.
- Accented letters such as ñ and ö are allowed.
- No spaces, hyphens, numbers, symbols or punctuation. That includes the apostrophe.
- The same letter cannot appear more than twice in a row: no "xxx" or "eee".
- The first letter is a capital, the rest lowercase.

## What is not allowed

Names keep to the in-game Code of Conduct. A name that passes the filter can still be flagged later. The result is a forced rename, and the account can be punished too, as [happened on the beta](/news/forced-renames-follow-offensive-names-on-the-beta/).

- Hate speech, discriminatory language, obscenities and slurs
- Explicit sexual references, anatomical terms included
- Real-world political figures
- Names that pose as a Blizzard employee
- Major lore figures such as Thrall, Arthas and Sylvanas

## Stricter on the Roleplay ruleset

The [Roleplay ruleset](/news/rulesets-replace-the-realm-list/) adds three rules. A name can be flagged there when it does not sound medieval or fantasy, when it refers to well-known people, characters, brands, places or icons, or when it consists of several words.

## Reservation from tomorrow

Owners of an upgrade edition can reserve up to three characters before the launch on 4 November. Names go to whoever is first.
