Eight items that drop in the starting zones of World of Warcraft: Forever are green now instead of white. Each one gains one or two points of a stat, some also get a little more armor, and all eight now bind when equipped. The change came with the [beta build of 16 October](/news/beta-goes-down-for-a-new-build-with-many-fixes/).

Most of the items drop in Elwynn Forest and Dun Morogh, a few in Tirisfal Glades. More drops may follow, but Blizzard has not said anything about it.

## What it changes

One point of Agility or Stamina is small, but in the first ten levels you have little else. The items also help Enchanting: a white item cannot be disenchanted, a green one can, so early enchanters get a few more sources of materials. And because they bind when equipped, you can sell them on the auction house.

Not every stat fits the armor. <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6202"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gauntlets_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingerless Gloves</a> are cloth with Agility, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6147"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_belt_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ratty Old Belt</a> is leather with Strength.
