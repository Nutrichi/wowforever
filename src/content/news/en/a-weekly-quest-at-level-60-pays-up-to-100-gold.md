Reached level 60 in World of Warcraft: Forever? Then a new weekly quest waits for you in Booty Bay. Pix Xizzix sends you through all three wings of Dire Maul, and the reward is a satchel with an item worth up to 100 gold.

## The quest

<a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96076">In Demand - Ancient Elven Curiosities</a> is offered to level 60 characters once a week by Pix Xizzix in Booty Bay. In the first two weeks it asked for the same three items, one from each wing of Dire Maul:

| Item | Boss | Wing |
|---|---|---|
| Felvine Seed | Alzzin the Wildshaper | [Dire Maul East](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-east/) |
| Ancient Memento | Prince Tortheldrin | [Dire Maul West](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-west/) |
| Earthstone Diamond Pinky Ring | King Gordok | [Dire Maul North](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-north/) |

There have only been two weekly resets so far, so the task may still change.

## The reward

You get an <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271994"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_31.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Undermine Satchel</a>. It holds some materials and one item that you sell to a vendor for gold:

| Item | Sells for |
|---|---|
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=272016"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ore_gold_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gold Nugget</a> | 10 gold |
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=272015"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_gem_diamond_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Huge Quartz Gem</a> | 20 gold |
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=272014"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_necklace_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Silver Necklace</a> | 40 gold |
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=272001"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_ring_38.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gold Ring</a> | 100 gold |

How often each item drops is not known.

## Why it is there

Quests in Forever [pay no gold at level 60](/news/quests-give-no-gold-at-level-60-in-wow-forever/), and Blizzard [added gold to quests from level 48](/news/blizzard-adds-gold-to-quests-from-level-48/) instead. Back then a developer already hinted at a weekly quest with random gold. Pix Xizzix also hands out weekly dungeon quests, which since 13 November [leave your log at the weekly reset](/news/dual-spec-no-longer-wipes-your-action-bars/).
