Mages in World of Warcraft: Forever have a sword that no other class can make. The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=276631"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Coldflame Saber</a> comes from a dungeon drop and one of the rare scrolls you decipher with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1296017"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/8188276.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Comprehend Scroll</a>.

## What the sword does

- Level 21, main hand, 17 to 32 damage (12.25 damage per second), +7 Intellect
- Up to 32 extra damage and healing from spells
- Your melee hits deal 98 extra Fire damage to frozen targets

Players report that the Fire damage works with Fingers of Frost and grows with your spell power. For a Frost Mage who likes to get close, that is a fun toy for leveling.

## How to get it

1. **Get the blade.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3887">Baron Silverlaine</a> in [Shadowfang Keep](/guides/dungeons/shadowfang-keep/) can drop the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273637"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blade of Silverlaine</a>. It is not a sure drop, so you may need a few runs.
2. **Find the scroll.** Decipher scrolls of Comprehension 15 or higher until you get the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=277490"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_10_inscription2_repcontracts_scroll_02_uprez_color5.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scroll of the Saber</a>. The scrolls drop all over the world and sell on the Auction House. Some players had it fast, others needed close to 120 scrolls.
3. **Combine them.** Use the Scroll of the Saber on the blade. After a cast of about 10 seconds you have your Coldflame Saber.

The Scroll of the Saber is Bind on Pickup, so you cannot buy it. How Comprehension works and what the other scrolls do is in our [guide to Mage scrolls](/classes/mage/scrolls/).
