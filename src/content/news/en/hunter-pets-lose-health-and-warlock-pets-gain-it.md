The hotfix of 10 November pulls Hunter pets back from 150% to 115% of their normal health. That 150% was a bug, says Blizzard. Warlock demons get about 15% more health, and the Beast Mastery Hawk hits much harder.

## Hunter

- **Pets:** 115% of their normal health. A bug gave them 150%. For Hunters who let their pet tank, that is a clear nerf.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1293241"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_animalhandler.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Hawk</a>:** the Hawk deals about 80% more melee damage. The talent already costs a flat 11% of base mana, the same as Arcane Shot, [since yesterday](/news/wrack-makes-affliction-dots-tick-twice-as-fast/).

Last week Blizzard said that pets [did not hold up well enough as tanks](/news/hunter-and-warlock-pets-get-tougher-on-tuesday/). The goal was about the level of Classic Era. With 115%, Hunter pets stay above their normal health, and in Forever they still take part of their stats from their master.

## Warlock

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=688"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonimp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Imp</a>:** about 15% more base health. The first four ranks of Firebolt also deal more base damage.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=697"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonvoidwalker.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Voidwalker</a>:** about 15% more base health.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=712"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_summonsuccubus.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Succubus</a>** and **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=713"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_warlock_incubus.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Summon Incubus</a>:** about 15% more base health each.

The Felhunter is not in the list. The whip sound of the Succubus also plays much less often now: a bug made it play far too often.
