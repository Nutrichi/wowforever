Your friends list is full of level 60s and you are still questing in the Wetlands? You are not behind. Three weeks after the launch of World of Warcraft: Forever, half of all characters are level 22 or lower, and only 2.6% have reached level 60.

## The figures

The count covers 7,258,194 characters on all regions and rulesets, from level 10. The median is level 22. Every level from 11 to 22 has more characters than level 60, and so does level 25. Above level 30 the numbers drop fast, until the peak at 60.

Alts pull the figures down, because many players have more than one character. Characters below level 10 are not counted at all, which pulls them up again. Either way, players at the level cap are a small group.

## No rush

The first raids open in two weeks, and the players who want to be there on day one are busy with [dungeons](/guides/dungeons/), reputations, professions and the Tier 0.5 quests. That is easy to feel bad about. But Forever is about the road to 60, and there is plenty to do on the way, from transmog and Legacy points to an evening of fishing.
