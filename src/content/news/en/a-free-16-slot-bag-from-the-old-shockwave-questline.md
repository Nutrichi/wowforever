Many questlines from Season of Discovery came along to World of Warcraft: Forever. One of them is the chain that gave Warriors the Shockwave rune. In Forever every class can do it, and halfway through it gives the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=226697"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_22.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Red Bag</a>, a free bag with 16 slots.

The bag has one catch: it lowers your effective stealth level by 2. Only Rogues and Druids notice that; everyone else can use it without a downside. More free bags are in [our post about quest bags](/news/quest-bags-save-you-gold-while-you-level/).

## The questline step by step

1. Find the <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/object=456682">Half-Buried Mech</a> off the south coast of Tanaris (/way 59.2 91.5). Click it twice: that accepts and completes <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84135">Beach Bot</a> and gives you the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=226856"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_potion_109.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Guided Buoyancy Accelerant</a>.
2. Accept <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84137">Mechanical Romance</a>, drink the potion and swim towards the lighthouse in the distance. Past the edge of the fatigue zone you see a small island: swim straight to it.
3. Hand in to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=228956">Sebastian Jurgens</a> on the island and accept <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84138">Frosty Favors</a>.
4. Bring it to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=228216">Nami</a> in Everlook, Winterspring (/way 61.2 37.0).
5. Accept <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84146">Red Bag Blues</a> and travel to the Burning Steppes. Climb the mountain at /way 59 29 to the remote camp at /way 53.0 24.8. This is where you get the Red Bag.
6. Accept <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84211">Poacher's Den</a> and kill the level 59 <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=228610">Blackrock Bootlegger</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=228609">Sulfuron Smuggler</a> for the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=226698"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gizmo_felironcasing.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Vertically Composited Patch Hampler</a> and the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=226699"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_28.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Brass-fitted Flam-Tamp Flange</a>. Some parts also lie on the ground.
7. Back in Everlook you get the book <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=227285"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_book_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fantastic Inventions</a>. <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84212">Bookin' it Back</a> sends you back to the island off Tanaris.
8. In <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84213">Rift Away</a> you follow Sebastian to a teleporter. It takes Alliance players to Westfall and Horde players to Tirisfal Glades.
9. Talk to Sebastian again to summon <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=212252">Harvest Golem V000-A</a>, an elite with more than 200,000 health. Bring a healer and one or two damage dealers.

## The boss

The fight has three phases:

- **Phase 1:** interrupt the casts of the golem.
- **Phase 2:** kite it, so its armor stacks fall off.
- **Phase 3:** use your defensive cooldowns to survive the hits, and finish it.

Loot the <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/object=459259">Mech Arm</a> on the ground afterwards. The end of <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84213">Rift Away</a> gives <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=208424"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_helmet_47.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sun Shades</a>, a unique transmog item, and the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=243177"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_27.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ingenious Toy</a>. In Season of Discovery that toy was a reagent for another item; what it does in Forever is not known yet.
