A level 30 Paladin has beaten a level 60 Abomination in World of Warcraft: Forever. Rextroy stacked 242 Healing Power, so rank 1 Flash of Light heals 250 for only 35 mana. With Blessing of Wisdom, his mana never runs out.

## The build

Rextroy bought green items "of Healing" at the Auction House and added enchants and consumables. That brought his Healing Power to 242 at level 30. With the Holy talent Oath of Light, rank 1 Flash of Light then heals 250 for 35 mana. Blessing of Wisdom gives back mana faster than that spell costs.

## What it does

He heals himself through anything that does not interrupt him. That is how he beat an Abomination 30 levels above him. Against classes without an interrupt, he can keep himself alive endlessly. The video shows the fights and his explanation.
