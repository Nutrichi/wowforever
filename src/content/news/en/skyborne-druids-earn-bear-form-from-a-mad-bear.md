Skyborne Druids in World of Warcraft: Forever learn Bear Form their own way. At level 10 a bear spirit on Zephras Isle asks them to kill his mate, who has gone mad. Cat Form follows at level 20, in Moonglade.

## Bear Form at level 10

Both factions of the Skyborne start on Zephras Isle, so the High Order and the Windshapers get the same quest. The Druid trainer at Valanaar gives <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94006">The Great Ursera Spirit</a>, which leads to Urs'endris, the Great Windborne Bear Spirit, on a small island in the east.

Urs'endris then gives <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94638">Strength and Mercy</a>. His mate Ur'endra has gone mad and waits in a cave on a hill in the Shen'dar Highlands, in the south of the island. Kill her and return to Urs'endris for Bear Form, Growl and Maul.

Other Druids still get Bear Form from Heeding the Call in Darnassus or Thunder Bluff. The Skyborne learn Teleport: Moonglade from a separate quest, Moonglade, which starts in Stormwind for the High Order and in Thunder Bluff for the Windshapers.

## Cat Form at level 20

Cat Form brings the Skyborne back to the capitals: Darnassus for the High Order, Thunder Bluff for the Windshapers. The trainer sends you to Dendrite Starblaze in Moonglade. From there the chain is <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98341">The Great Windborne Cat Spirit</a>, with the Avatar of Saeyleenan instead of the Great Cat Spirit of other Druids.

In the Stormrage Barrow Den, a cat statue at the entrance gives a buff that lets you sneak past everything. You pick up three relics: the Claw, the Fang and the Silent Shadow. After <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98738">Blessings of the Great Windborne Cat Spirit</a> your trainer teaches Cat Form, Claw, Prowl and Rip.
