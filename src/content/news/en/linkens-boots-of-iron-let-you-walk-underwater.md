Linken's Boots of Iron let you walk on the bottom of the sea in World of Warcraft: Forever. The level 38 mail boots drop in the Drowned City. On land they come at a price: you can only walk, not run.

## A hidden effect

At first sight <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271718"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_boots_chain_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Linken's Boots of Iron</a> are ordinary boots with +8 Agility and +12 Stamina, from level 33. The only hint is the text on the tooltip: "Incredibly heavy, useless boots. Who made these?" A player named HaroldTheHammer found out what they do. With the boots on, you sink and walk on the sea floor.

Mounts do not work with them. Plainsrunning of the Tauren does, so similar effects such as Ghost Wolf probably work as well. That is not confirmed. The name points to the Iron Boots of Link in The Legend of Zelda.

## Where they drop

The boots drop in the [Drowned City](/guides/dungeons/the-drowned-city/), from one of the bosses and from three rare elites, among them <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=264177">Gill</a>. Be careful under water without a way to breathe: walking back to the surface takes time, and in combat you cannot take the boots off.
