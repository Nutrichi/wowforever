Paladins and Warlocks in World of Warcraft: Forever now get epic riding with their level 60 class mount. Blizzard fixed the bug in the hotfixes of 25 November. Players who already finished the quest can learn Journeyman Riding for free from one NPC.

## Free Journeyman Riding

The quests below now teach Journeyman Riding when you finish them. Already done? Talk to the NPC in the last column and pick the new dialogue option.

| Who | Quest | Free riding from |
|---|---|---|
| **Forsaken Paladins** | <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=97185">Justice and Retribution</a> | <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=246378">Danitha Morr</a> in Bandarion Keep |
| **Dwarf and Human Paladins** | <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=7647">Judgment and Redemption</a> | <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=928">Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker</a> in the cathedral of Stormwind |
| **Horde Warlocks** | <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=7631">Dreadsteed of Xoroth</a> | <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3326">Zevrost</a> in the Cleft of Shadow in Orgrimmar |
| **Alliance Warlocks** | <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=7631">Dreadsteed of Xoroth</a> | <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=5495">Ursula Deline</a> in the Slaughtered Lamb in Stormwind |

With Journeyman Riding you can ride every epic mount, not only the [Charger](/guides/mounts/charger/) or the [Dreadsteed](/guides/mounts/dreadsteed/). That was the bug [Blizzard confirmed on 24 November](/news/paladins-and-warlocks-wait-for-an-epic-riding-fix/).

## The rest of 25 November

The same hotfixes brought the [Balance changes](/news/balance-druids-get-10-percent-in-moonkin-form/), the [Revelation nerf](/news/revelation-now-crits-one-tick-of-a-channel/), the [Night Watchman's Torch on mounts](/news/the-night-watchmans-torch-works-on-mounts-again/) and a [new Alterac Valley](/news/alterac-valley-rewards-objectives-over-the-general/). Those have their own posts. Here is the rest.

### Classes

- **Druid:** Hurricane lands its final hit again. Genesis now raises the damage of Hurricane and the healing of Tranquility.
- **Hunter:** pets had 0% crit chance on their basic melee attacks. That is fixed. For Beast Mastery, the Summoned Hawks now attack the target they were summoned on, even with a passive pet, and your current target when they are idle. Dismiss Pet sends them away too, or right-click their icon under your portrait. They also get back the 5% crit chance they were missing.
- **Paladin:** Exorcism can crit. Judgements on enemies refresh from your attacks again when they drop under 20 seconds. For Holy, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1328548"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_greaterblessingoflight.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Oath of Light</a> lasts 60 minutes.
- **Rogue:** all poisons and <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5530"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_ammo_gunpowder_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blinding Powder</a> are bind on pickup for now, while Blizzard fixes a bug. Until then you cannot sell or trade them. Poised now goes away when you use Sap or Distract from stealth.
- **Shaman:** Chain Lightning uses up Clearcasting again when you also have 5 stacks of Maelstrom Weapon.
- **Warlock:** pets crit with basic melee attacks again. The Succubus and Incubus lose the 5% extra crit chance they should not have had.
- **Warrior:** the Arms talent Weaponmaster comes back after you die in a battleground.

### Legacy

- PvP Legacy achievements give credit again after you reach Exalted.
- Finishing Lord Valthalak Laid to Rest now gives Legacy credit.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225497"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_armorkit_17.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Reinforce</a> now lowers durability loss by 8 to 40%, as intended.
- The Quick and the Dead makes Shadowform, Moonkin Form, Travel Form and Trueshot Aura free, as intended.

### Dungeons and the world

- **Blackfathom Deeps:** the Altar of the Deeps can be used again after 3 seconds and gives the Blessing of Blackfathom.
- **The Temple of Atal'Hakkar:** the Avatar of Hakkar spawns again.
- **Desolace:** the centaurs in Magram Village respawn faster.
- **Duskwood:** the Lost Knight no longer spawns inside a tree.
- **Riverglades:** Clubtoe in Sunnyglade no longer flags you for PvP.
- Gnomes, Humans, Night Elves and Dwarves can no longer go to war with the Kirin Tor. Dwarves can no longer go to war with the Wildhammer Clan either.

- <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274517"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_20.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Unerring Purpose</a> sells to vendors for more than 2 copper.

The Hunter quest Saving the Best for Last can now be completed by the Horde. The repeatable turn-in of Encrypted Twilight Texts for Cenarion Circle reputation stays unavailable on purpose, says Blizzard. Still Believing does give a chance at recipes through a reward that arrives later by mail.
