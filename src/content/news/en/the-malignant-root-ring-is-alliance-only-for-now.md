World of Warcraft: Forever has a ring that you can wear from level 1: <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=282283"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_thorns_iron.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Malignant Root</a>. You get it in the Wetlands for an item that an elite drops. The NPC who gives the ring is hostile to the Horde, so for now only the Alliance can get it.

## The ring

| | |
|---|---|
| Item level | 32 |
| Level | None |
| Binds | When picked up |
| Stats | +7 Stamina, +8 Attack Power, up to 4 spell damage and healing |

## How to get it

1. **Find the Rotheap.** <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=270589">Nightveiled Rotheap</a> is a level 31 to 32 elite that roams all over the Wetlands. A macro with `/target Nightveiled Rotheap` helps you spot it while you ride around.
2. **Kill it.** It can be slowed, so you can kite it. Alone, only try it when you are well above level 25 to 30. A group is easier.
3. **Loot the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279537"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_eyeballclam_white.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Rotheap Innards</a>.** It drops for everyone in the group, with a chance of about 96%.
4. **Turn it in.** Bring it to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=1244">Rethiel the Greenwarden</a> at `/way 56.3 40.5` in the Wetlands. Pick the option "I found something" and he gives you the ring.

The Rotheap Innards do not bind, so you can also buy them on the Auction House and skip the elite. Horde players can buy them too, but there is no NPC for them to turn them in. Whether Blizzard adds one is not known.
