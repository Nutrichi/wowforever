The Drowned City hides a nod to The Legend of Zelda. Every boss in the new dungeon can drop <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271718"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_boots_chain_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Linken's Boots of Iron</a>, mail boots. Players report that they work like the Iron Boots: you walk along the bottom of the water, but slowly on land.

## The boots

- **Mail feet**, binds when picked up, item level 38
- **169 armor**, 8 Agility and 12 Stamina
- **Requires level 33**
- The flavor text calls them incredibly heavy, useless boots

The stats are modest for the [Drowned City](/guides/dungeons/the-drowned-city/), which is meant for levels 35 to 40. The fun is in the effect.

## Linken was already there

Linken has been in World of Warcraft since 2004. In Un'Goro Crater, the item It's a Secret to Everybody starts his quest chain, which ends with <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=11902"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_39.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Linken's Sword of Mastery</a>, the Master Sword of Azeroth. With the boots, Linken now gets a second piece of Zelda gear. Whether you need them for a secret later on, Blizzard does not say.
