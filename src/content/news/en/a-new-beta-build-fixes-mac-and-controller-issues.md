Blizzard has put a new client on the beta realms of WoW Forever: 1.60.1 Build 69977. It fixes display and stability problems on Macs and two things that went wrong with a gamepad. The next full weekly update follows on 24 September.

To get it, leave the beta, update the client in the Battle.net app and go back in.

## What is fixed

> Fixed some display issues and stability issues on Macs.
> Controller navigation should now work more properly in the character selection screen if you launch with gamepad active.
> You can now interact with items that give you quests on gamepad.

Blizzard says the build also carries other fixes for issues that cannot be tested in the game itself.

Which issues those are, Blizzard does not say.
