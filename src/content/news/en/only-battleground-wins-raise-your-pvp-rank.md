Kills do not raise your PvP rank in World of Warcraft: Forever. Rank Points only come from winning a Battleground. Players also report that Sap and Polymorph hit other players again and again without diminishing returns.

## Rank Points only from wins

Streamer Jokerd, part of the first group in the world to reach level 60, ran into it on stream. His rank only goes up when he wins a Battleground, "even if you kill a million players". Kills in a Battleground or in the open world give no Rank Points.

Whether kills should count, divides players. Fighting the other faction out in the world is PvP too. Blizzard has not said whether the system changes. How the ranks and the weekly cap work is in the [PvP guide](/guides/pvp/).

## Sap and Polymorph without diminishing returns

Players report that Sap and Polymorph on other players hardly get shorter when used again. Normally each new crowd control of the same kind lasts shorter, until the target is immune for a while. A post on Reddit shows a player kept in Sap over and over.
