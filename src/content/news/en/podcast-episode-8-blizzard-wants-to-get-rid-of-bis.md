## No more single BiS list

Zirene calls BiS a bad fit for a game about role-playing systems. That is why Forever has creature subtypes and biomes, and the raid will use them too: the left wing is snowy, the right wing haunted. "Your BiS should be different for every single boss", he says. He wants you to think about the fight in front of you, not about one list.

According to Zirene this works for both kinds of players: those who love to optimize, and those who simply want to use a niche item they collected earlier. Because more of those items are coming, the team promises bigger bags and better ways to sort and search them later on.

What does this mean for our [pre-raid BiS lists](/bis/)? Before the raids, the lists still show the best gear per slot for dungeons and the open world. Once the raids open, expect gear that wins per boss rather than per slot.

## An item for full bags

Asked for a teaser, Zirene hinted at an item that is "Master Loot BiS": it may give extra stats if your bags are completely full. It would work for anyone, but is a full bag worth those stats? It fits the odd items Forever has shown so far, such as a weapon for battle mages.

The episode also covers spell power on gear, burst damage in PvP and a part for every class. There is no episode next week because of Thanksgiving in the United States.
