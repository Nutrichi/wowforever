The last beta build of World of Warcraft: Forever changed a lot in Dalaran. Arcane Sentries are now level 55 elites, battlemasters stand in the Underbelly, and Alliance players get a new quest. The portal from Stormwind to Dalaran appears to be gone.

Dalaran [opened for the Alliance](/news/dalaran-opens-on-the-beta-for-the-alliance-only/) earlier in the beta. The changes came in the last build before the beta closed on 23 October.

## Guards and trainers

The Arcane Sentries are more prominent around the city. They are now level 55 elites.

![An Arcane Sentry of the Kirin Tor in Dalaran, a large purple construct with arcane crystals, with the tooltip Level 55 (Elite), a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-dalaran-arcane-sentry.jpg)

Kirin Tor Guards now point the way. They show the orphanage, the inn, the mailbox, the profession trainers, the barbershop, the stables and the transmogrifier.

![The dialog of a Kirin Tor Guard with the choices The Orphanage, The Inn, Mailbox, Professions, The barbershop, Stables and The transmogrifier, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-dalaran-kirin-tor-guard.jpg)

Besides profession trainers, Dalaran also has class trainers. Among them are a Druid trainer and a Mage trainer.

![Jessa Weaver, Mage Trainer, in a round hall in Dalaran with three Arcane Familiars, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-dalaran-mage-trainer.jpg)

## Battlemasters in the Underbelly

The Underbelly now has battlemasters for every battleground, also for [the new Darkspear Islands](/news/darkspear-islands-pits-15-players-against-15/). <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=29534">&quot;Baroness&quot; Llana</a>, the Arena Organizer in Wrath of the Lich King, is the Alterac Valley Battlemaster in Forever.

![“Baroness” Llana, Alterac Valley Battlemaster, on a pile of sandbags in the Underbelly, with a Dwarf Paladin in front of her, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-dalaran-baroness-llana.jpg)

## A quest for the Alliance

Alliance players find a new quest in Dalaran: <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96800">Past Due</a> from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=264265">Magus Olvek</a>. A mage named Doan took a book out of the old Dalaran library before the Third War and never returned it. Players retrieve The Lost Magic of the Runemasters from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=6487">Arcanist Doan</a>, the last boss of the Scarlet Monastery Library.

The reward is a choice between Salve and Salvation and Knucklebound Thimble. Magus Olvek is known from the World of Warcraft Trading Card Game.

![The quest Past Due from Magus Olvek in a library in Dalaran, with the rewards Salve and Salvation and Knucklebound Thimble, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-dalaran-magus-olvek.jpg)

## The portal to Stormwind

The portal from Dalaran to Stormwind still exists. The portal back from Stormwind to Dalaran appears to be gone.

![A High Order Mage in Stormwind beside a bookcase, where no portal to Dalaran stands, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-stormwind-portal-to-dalaran-gone.jpg)

In the Silver Enclave, where the Alliance portals stood in Wrath of the Lich King, Theresa Wolf casts a spell. What she is conjuring, is not known.

![Theresa Wolf casting a purple spell beside Sebastian Bower in the Silver Enclave, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-dalaran-theresa-wolf.jpg)

Whether Dalaran changes further before launch on 4 November is not known.
