Warlocks won 79 percent of their duels against other classes in the finals of the Forever Open, the dueling tournament on the beta. Mages did worst, with 25 percent. The figures cover every finals duel between two different classes.

![The logo of The Forever Open, a golden infinity sign over a stone circle, the logo of the dueling tournament on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-26-wow-forever-forever-open-logo.jpg)

## Win rate per class

A player counted every duel of the finals and shared the result on Reddit. Duels between two players of the same class are left out.

| Class | Win rate | Won-lost |
|---|---|---|
| **Warlock** | 79% | 26-7 |
| **Shaman** | 63% | 19-11 |
| **Paladin** | 55% | 16-13 |
| **Druid** | 46% | 12-14 |
| **Priest** | 43% | 9-12 |
| **Hunter** | 39% | 7-11 |
| **Rogue** | 31% | 5-11 |
| **Warrior** | 27% | 4-11 |
| **Mage** | 25% | 4-12 |

Only Warlocks, Shamans and Paladins won more than half of their duels. The tournament was played at level 30, so the balance at level 60 can look very different. The winner was a Warlock too: [Warlockguy beat Neepzen](/news/warlockguy-wins-the-50000-dollar-forever-open/), also a Warlock, in the grand final.
