> **Update 24 November:** The loot is back since the hotfixes of 23 November, but every item sells for half. The Pendulum of Doom can be made again. More in [our post](/news/obsidian-sentinel-drops-loot-again-at-half-price/).

The <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7023">Obsidian Sentinel</a> in [Uldaman](/guides/dungeons/uldaman/) no longer drops blue gear in World of Warcraft: Forever. Blizzard did not mention it in a hotfix post, but the boss lost its whole loot table in the last few days.

## Why it was farmed

The Obsidian Sentinel stands just inside the back entrance of Uldaman. You reach it without any trash, so a solo player could kill it and keep all the loot. A run took under five minutes and was worth about 1.77 gold on average from these vendor prices:

| <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274428"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_plate10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dark Golem Breastplate</a> | 1g 19s 86c |

Now the boss drops only some copper and <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=7912"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_stone_10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Solid Stone</a>. It fits the other nerfs to quick farms, such as the [new dungeon limit](/news/dungeon-farms-under-12-minutes-hit-the-new-limit/).

## The Pendulum of Doom is gone for now

There is a side effect for players who level. The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=9425"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_halberd_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pendulum of Doom</a> is a strong two-handed axe for level 39, combined from two Uldaman drops:

- <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274484"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Axe of Inevitable Ruin</a> from <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=7228">Ironaya</a>;
- <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274485"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Axe of Lingering Dread</a> from the Obsidian Sentinel.

Without the second axe, nobody can make the Pendulum of Doom. Whether Blizzard meant that, or will move the axe to another boss, is not known.
