> **Update 23 November:** Blizzard says this is a bug, caused by a fix for another bug, and a fix should follow the next day. More in [our post](/news/losing-world-buffs-on-loading-screens-is-a-bug/).

World buffs in World of Warcraft: Forever now disappear as soon as you see a loading screen. Blizzard did not announce the change, but it means the buffs no longer follow you into a dungeon, across the sea or past a log out.

## What changes

Every loading screen removes them, also the one when you log in. A Hearthstone from Orgrimmar to Thunder Bluff already takes Warchief's Blessing away. In practice:

* Warchief's Blessing no longer works in the Eastern Kingdoms, because Horde players turn in the head in Orgrimmar.
* Might of Stormwind no longer works in Kalimdor.
* The Dire Maul buffs are gone the moment you leave the dungeon, because that is a loading screen too.
* No world buff survives the loading screen into a dungeon.

So the buffs only help in the open world, on the continent where you got them, as long as you do not log out, take a portal or teleport. It looks unintended, but this is how it works for now. Blizzard has not said whether it stays.
