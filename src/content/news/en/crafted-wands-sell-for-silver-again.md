Wands made with Enchanting sell to a vendor for silver again in World of Warcraft: Forever. A week ago they [dropped to 1 copper](/news/crafted-wands-now-sell-for-1-copper/). The beta build of 8 October sets new prices, from 1 silver 10 copper to 25 silver.

The new prices are lower than before 1 October. Lesser Magic Wand brought in 5 silver 8 copper then, Greater Magic Wand 15 silver 35 copper. Crafting wands only to sell them earns less than it did in Classic, but part of the cost of levelling Enchanting comes back.

| Item | Before 1 October | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor Wizard Oil** | Not known | 1 silver |
| **Novice's Practice Wand** | Not known | 5 copper |
| **Lesser Magic Wand** | 5 silver 8 copper | 1 silver 10 copper |
| **Greater Magic Wand** | 15 silver 35 copper | 2 silver |
| **Lesser Mystic Wand** | 35 silver 81 copper | 4 silver 50 copper |
| **Greater Mystic Wand** | 52 silver 63 copper | 6 silver |
| **Twisted Nether Wand** | Not known | 10 silver |
| **Dreambough Wand** | Not known | 15 silver |
| **Lesser Eternal Wand** | Not known | 20 silver |
| **Greater Eternal Wand** | Not known | 25 silver |

The old prices come from the build before 1 October; for the other items they are not known. Whether the prices change again before launch, Blizzard does not say.

The recipe for Peace Tea now sells for 10 copper. More changes in the same build are in the [post about the build](/news/city-of-dalaran-opens-in-the-fourth-beta-week/).
