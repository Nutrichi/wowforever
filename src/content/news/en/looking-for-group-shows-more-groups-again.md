Looking for Group in World of Warcraft: Forever shows all the groups again. Some players saw fewer groups than intended, and a hotfix on 20 November fixes that. The same hotfixes lower the dungeon limit to 5 per hour, down from 10.

## Dungeons

You can now reset and enter each dungeon at most 5 times per hour, Blizzard says. That used to be 10 times. In Blackrock Spire, a stone pillar no longer traps players.

## Classes

* **Combat Rogue:** with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=13877"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_warrior_punishingblow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blade Flurry</a> active, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15248"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_backstab.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shank</a> now hits a second target, as intended.
* **Demonology Warlock:** players could lock the bonus of the Demonic Aegis talent into <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=706"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_ragingscream.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Demon Armor</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=687"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_ragingscream.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Demon Skin</a> and then unlearn the talent. That no longer works. Unlearning your talents now removes Demonic Aegis and Demon Skin.

## Items and professions

The 3-piece bonus of the Stormshroud set now has a 3% chance on a melee hit to restore 18 Energy. That was a 4% chance for 30 Energy. Toron Rockhoof has repaired his forge and anvil on the Summit of Eternity, so Blacksmithing and smelting work there again.

## Quests

* <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98203">Witch Doctor Ti'ik</a>: the witch doctor now drops a head for every player in the group who has the quest. Blizzard was [looking into this bug](/news/blizzard-looks-into-three-bugged-quests/).
* <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=7738">Perfect Yeti Hide</a>: the hide no longer drops again while you already have the quest.
* <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5863">The Dunemaul Compound</a>: Gor'marok in Tanaris respawns faster.
* Dunston Checks Out: Dunston Willis in the Riverglades no longer runs off halfway through the quest.

In Orgrimmar, Mayhoa Skyhoof no longer has glowing eyes.
