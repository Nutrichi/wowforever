The gathering tradeskills of WoW Forever have a new kind of material. Scarce materials have no nodes of their own but drop now and then from any herb, ore or skinned beast. Three are known on the beta, one per tradeskill.

![The tooltip of Pyrite in the bags, with the lines Scarce and Crafting Reagent, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-pyrite-tooltip.jpg)

## The three materials

| Tradeskill | Scarce material |
|---|---|
| Mining | <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249391"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ore_pyrite_nugget.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pyrite</a> |
| Herbalism | <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249399"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_food_109_honeylichen.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frilled Lichen</a> |
| Skinning | <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249427"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/item_pristinehide.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pristine Leather</a> |

The item itself says Scarce in its tooltip. It is a rarer extra on top of the normal loot, writes Icy Veins.

![A loot window with Peacebloom and Frilled Lichen after picking a herb, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-frilled-lichen-loot.jpg)

## What they are for

All three go into new recipes of other tradeskills. Frilled Lichen is used in <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1248757"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_enchant_formulagood_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Enchant Weapon - Insight</a>, which can double Spirit for 10 seconds when you cast a spell, and in <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249409"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_inscription_inkcerulean01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Cerulean Dye</a> from Alchemy. That dye goes into many Tailoring recipes.

Pyrite shows up in Tailoring too, for example in <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=253899"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_boots_cloth_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shining Boots</a>. Pristine Leather goes into Leatherworking, such as the <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=252504"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_helmet_31.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Brawler's Leather Hood</a>, and into Blacksmithing and Tailoring.

## What comes later

<a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249425"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ore_manticytenugget.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bauxite</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249426"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ore_elethium_nugget.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Pitchblende</a> are in the files as Scarce materials for Mining, writes Icy Veins. Blizzard has not said at what skill level they appear, or whether Herbalism and Skinning get more of them.
