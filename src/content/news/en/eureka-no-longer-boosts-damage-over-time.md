<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259813"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_gnometoy.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Eureka!</a>, the racial of Gnomes in World of Warcraft: Forever, no longer works on periodic effects. Damage over time and healing over time get nothing from it since the beta build of 1 October. It is the second nerf for the racial.

The note in the build is short:

> Gnome
>
> Eureka! no longer benefits periodic effects at all. Channeled spells do not count as periodics.

Eureka! is an active racial with a 2 minute cooldown. Your next 3 damaging abilities cost 10% less and deal 10% more damage. The first nerf already lowered the discount on resources, writes Wowhead.

## What it means per class

The racial has a version per resource. Wowhead lists four in the data of the build:

| Class | Resource | What drops out |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Rage | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=772"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_gouge.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Rend</a> |
| Rogue | Energy | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=703"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_garrote.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Garrote</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1943"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_rupture.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Rupture</a> |
| Warlock | Mana | damage over time |
| Priest | Mana | damage and healing over time |

Every tooltip now reads "non-periodic" abilities. Channeled spells still count, so a channeled spell such as Mind Flay keeps the bonus. The notes also list a known issue: Eureka! does not affect every damaging or healing spell for some classes. That is fixed in a later build, says Blizzard.

All racials of WoW Forever are on the [CLASSES page](/classes/).
