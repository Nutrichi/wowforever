Our guides for World of Warcraft: Forever have a new member: a leveling guide for the Rogue. It has four builds for level 30, each with a version for Talented from the Legacy system. The Priest guide now also lists the key levels up to 60.

## Four builds for the Rogue

**Combat** is the easiest start: Deflection and Riposte keep you alive, and you walk from monster to monster without pauses. **Subtlety with Ambush** kills fastest from Stealth. **Subtlety with bleeds** builds on Garrote, Hemorrhage and Rupture. **Assassination** only gets strong once Mutilate arrives at level 30.

Every build opens in our talent calculator, the Talented version too. The guide also covers the poison quests at level 20, the weapon trainers, gear from Leatherworking and the new elixirs of Forever. One-handed axes are new for the Rogue, and Combat gets a bonus for every weapon type through Hack and Slash.

Everything is in the new [Rogue guide](/guides/rogue-leveling/). Our [Rogue level build](/leveling/rogue/) gives a talent for every level up to 60.

## More key levels for the Priest

The [Priest guide](/guides/priest-leveling/) now also lists the trainer spells after level 30, such as Mana Burn at 24, Levitate at 34 and Prayer of Fortitude at 48, up to Prayer of Spirit at 60. The three builds did not change.
