The first Darkmoon Faire of World of Warcraft: Forever is open in Elwynn Forest, next to Goldshire. It stays until Monday 16 November. Sayge hands out a new buff every two hours, and 50 tickets buy a bag with 14 slots.

## When the Faire is open

The in-game calendar gives Monday 9 November at 04.00 as the start and Monday 16 November at 04.00 as the end, in realm time. On European realms that is 04.00 Belgian time. The Faire works the way it did in Classic and comes back once a month.

| Month | Where | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| November 2026 | Elwynn Forest | 9 to 15 November |
| December 2026 | Mulgore | 7 to 13 December |
| January 2027 | Elwynn Forest | 4 to 10 January |
| February 2027 | Mulgore | 8 to 14 February |
| March 2027 | Elwynn Forest | 8 to 14 March |
| April 2027 | Mulgore | 5 to 11 April |

The DMF button in the bar at the top of the site counts down to the next Faire, for European and American realms.
