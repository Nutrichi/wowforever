Mages in World of Warcraft: Forever can turn the Witchblade from Scholomance into a sword built for melee. Its melee hits can give Clearcasting, a free spell after a swing. It is the first real tool for a battle mage.

## The sword

<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=276632"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_11.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wit's Edge</a> is a main-hand sword for Mages only, for level 57 and up, bound when picked up:

| | Witchblade | Wit's Edge |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Dagger, speed 1.6 | Sword, speed 2.0 |
| **Damage** | 30 to 57 | 39 to 74 |
| **Intellect** | 8 | 4 |
| **Spell damage and healing** | up to 72 | up to 74 |
| **Spell penetration** | 4 | 5 |
| **Extra** | | <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1300141"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wit's End</a> |

Wit's End gives your melee attacks a 15% chance at <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12536"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_manaburn.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Clearcasting</a>: your next damage spell costs no mana. Swing between casts and you save mana.

## How to get it

1. **The Witchblade:** <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=13964"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_weapon_shortblade_25.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Witchblade</a> drops from Darkmaster Gandling, the last boss of [Scholomance](/guides/dungeons/scholomance/).
2. **The scroll:** <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=277504"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_10_inscription2_repcontracts_scroll_02_uprez_color5.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Scroll of Wit</a> is a tier 4 Mage scroll. You decipher it with the Study skill, from skill 175 and level 46. Where scrolls drop and how Study works is in our [Mage scrolls guide](/guides/mage-scrolls/).
3. **Combine:** use the scroll on the Witchblade and you get Wit's Edge.

## Worth it?

The sword trades 4 Intellect for 2 spell damage, a slower weapon and the proc. For a Mage who stands back and casts, the Witchblade stays the better choice. In melee range, while farming or in PvP, Wit's Edge saves mana.
