The hotfix of 10 November makes every racial vendor mount Bind-on-Pickup. Swiftstrike Shot and Swiftfeather Arrow go the other way and can be traded. Shatterspear Trolls no longer give experience, and Nature's Swiftness now works on Wild Growth.

The bigger parts of the same list have their own posts: [Enhancement Shamans](/news/enhancement-shamans-turn-back-to-windfury/), [Hunter and Warlock pets](/news/hunter-pets-lose-health-and-warlock-pets-gain-it/), [Wrack and the Hunter shots](/news/wrack-makes-affliction-dots-tick-twice-as-fast/), [vendor prices](/news/vendor-price-cut-ends-the-iron-grenade-shuffle/), [quest gold from level 48](/news/blizzard-adds-gold-to-quests-from-level-48/) and [Rage of the Storm](/news/rage-of-the-storm-swaps-intellect-for-strength/).

## Items

- **Racial vendor mounts** are now Bind-on-Pickup. Buying a mount from a vendor of another race to pass it on no longer works.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274388"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ammo_bullet_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Swiftstrike Shot</a>** and **<a class="wf-wh wf-q4" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274387"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_ammo_arrow_06.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Swiftfeather Arrow</a>** are no longer Bind-on-Pickup. They only become available when the raids open, but Blizzard mentions it now because it may influence which profession you pick.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=280031"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_wand_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sparklance Wand</a>** now deals Arcane damage instead of Nature damage.

## Classes

- **Druid:** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16864"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_crystalball.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Omen of Clarity</a> could not activate after a switch of specialization. Fixed. For Restoration, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16188"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_ravenform.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Nature's Swiftness</a> now also makes <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=408120"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_flourish.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wild Growth</a> instant.
- **Paladin:** Redoubt can be tracked in the Cooldown Manager.
- **Priest:** Vampiric Embrace healing no longer activates the Revelation enchant. Holy Nova is now correctly removed when you take the talent out.
- **Warlock:** Bane of Havoc now triggers the global cooldown.
- **Warrior:** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12292"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_slicedice.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sweeping Strikes</a> no longer loses charges when no second target is hit.

## Auction House and mail

The notes listed a minimum auction deposit of 1 copper, as [announced last week](/news/auction-deposits-go-back-to-1-copper/). On 12 November Blizzard took the Auction House changes back out: they come with the weekly maintenance on 17 November, more in [our post](/news/1-copper-deposit-waits-until-17-november/). The notes also listed two mail fixes: Some mail arrived after about 2 minutes instead of 60, and unread mail showed "Stormwind Auction House" as the sender for everyone. Both are fixed.

## World and quests

- **Shatterspear Trolls** no longer give experience. "They're dancers, not fighters", says Blizzard.
- **Innkeepers** give better answers to "Tell me about the dungeons I can explore".
- **Graveyards:** players were sometimes sent to the wrong graveyard after death.
- **Pyrewood Village:** the villagers did not always appear around dawn and dusk.
- **Quests:** more Glade Vipers in Riverglades for Poison Tips, more Nightbane worgen in Duskwood for Worgen in the Woods, and Murloc Tideskimmers drop their eyes again for A Taste of the Sea. Rescue OOX-22/FE! in Feralas can be started again, and Camping 101: Herbalism on Zephras Isle now completes in Shen'dar Village instead of Valanaar. Fixes also went into the Shaman quests for the Water Totem, Helping Hands, Chief Engineer Scooty and the quest item of Celastrus the Bitter.
