Blizzard holds a poster signing for World of Warcraft: Forever on 2 November. Fans can meet developers of the game at the Irvine Improv in California. Tickets are free but limited, and become available on 27 October.

Tickets go out via [TIXR](https://www.tixr.com/groups/bc/events/world-of-warcraft-forever-poster-signing-210889) from Tuesday 27 October at 12.00 PDT, 20.00 Belgian time. Each ticket comes with an arrival window. Entry is only possible with a valid QR code for that window, scanned at the check-in tent outside the venue.

Without a ticket there is no way in, says Blizzard, because time and space inside the venue are limited. Blizzard advises arriving early within the chosen window. The Irvine Improv is accessible for visitors with a disability.

Irvine is also the city where Blizzard has its headquarters. Which developers attend and at what time the signing starts, Blizzard does not say. Two days later, on 4 November, Forever launches.
