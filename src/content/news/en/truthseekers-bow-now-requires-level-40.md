Truthseeker's Bow now requires level 40 in World of Warcraft: Forever. It is one of three rewards for 25 library books, and until now the only one a player could wear at level 30. Blizzard changed it in the beta build of 8 October.

The other two rewards of Greater Friend of the Library already needed level 40, Blizzard says. The bow now matches them. It stays the same otherwise: a rare bow of item level 45 with 46 to 87 damage, 7 Agility and 3 Stamina.

| | |
|---|---|
| Quest | Greater Friend of the Library, 25 books |
| Item level | 45 |
| Damage | 46 to 87, speed 2.80 |
| Stats | 7 Agility, 3 Stamina |
| Level | 40 (was none) |

Since the level cap of the beta went to 30, players could hand in the books and use the bow right away. The [Forever Open](/news/spider-swarm-is-banned-from-the-forever-open/), the dueling tournament of the beta, had already banned it. The rewards of the library have changed before: the necklaces went from rare to uncommon, and the rings got a lower item level and a quest level. How the book quests work, is in the [post about the 25 books](/news/library-quest-for-25-books-opens-at-level-30/).
