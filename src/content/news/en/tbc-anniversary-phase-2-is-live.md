Phase 2 of The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary is live. Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep: The Eye are open on the Anniversary realms. Lady Vashj and Kael'thas Sunstrider drop Tier 5. Arena Season 2 starts at the same time.

The update is called Overlords of Outland. Phase 1 had Karazhan, Gruul's Lair and Magtheridon's Lair, with Tier 4.

The daily quest hubs of Ogri'la and the Sha'tari Skyguard are unlocked as well. They give reputation and gold between raid nights.

Characters that are still below level 70 can catch up in heroic dungeons, which drop gear for the first raids. When phase 3 with Mount Hyjal and Black Temple follows, Blizzard has not yet announced.
