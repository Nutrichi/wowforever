Blizzard has released the first hotfixes for World of Warcraft: Forever. Deathstalker Erland now respawns much faster for his escort quest, and Plainsrunning no longer stacks in battlegrounds. A Druid trainer on Zephras works again, and Mild Spices sell for less.

The hotfixes are dated 5 November and appeared that night on the forums of Blizzard, a day after the launch. Blizzard adds new fixes to the same list.

## Enemies and NPCs

- **Durotar**: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=3183">Yarrog Baneshadow</a> respawned more slowly than intended. Fixed.
- **Zephras**: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=252359">Lotheluum Starbreeze</a> trains Druids again.
- **Dustwallow Marsh**: some murlocs respawned too quickly. Fixed.
- **Swamp of Sorrows**: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=254770">Pelluk</a> sometimes did not spawn. Fixed.

## Items

- The trade supply vendors on Zephras Isle sell the apprentice tradeskill bags again.
- <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=2678"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_food_wheat_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mild Spices</a> sell to a vendor for 1 copper each, down from 2 copper.

## Player versus Player

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259918"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/achievement_zone_barrens_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Plainsrunning</a> could be stacked during the preparation phase of a battleground. That is fixed, and the buff no longer stacks at all. Carrying a flag cancels the stacks.

## Quests

- <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=1978">Deathstalker Erland</a> respawns much more quickly for <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=435">Escorting Erland</a>.
- The text of <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98391">The Sisterhood of Elune</a> is corrected.

Other escort quests still fail when the NPC crosses a zone border. Blizzard is investigating that, see [our post about the escorts](/news/escort-npcs-can-vanish-at-zone-borders/).
