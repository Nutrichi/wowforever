Rogues in World of Warcraft: Forever can no longer take a look inside a lockbox before they hand it back. A recent hotfix closes an exploit that let players reroll what was inside.

## How it worked

A Rogue who turned off auto loot could pick a lockbox and see the contents without taking anything. For boxes like the <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=5758"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_ornatebox.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mithril Lockbox</a>, which can hold expensive items such as recipes, some Rogues liked to peek before they mailed the box back to its owner.

Players then found that the contents could change. What the Rogue saw was not always what the owner found after the box came back in the mail. What triggers the reroll is not known, and neither is how long it went on or how many rare recipes it touched. It was clearly not intended.

## What changes

When you open a lockbox now, everything goes straight into your bags. Peeking without looting is no longer possible. Do you open boxes for other players? Then you can no longer hand them back unopened after a look inside.
