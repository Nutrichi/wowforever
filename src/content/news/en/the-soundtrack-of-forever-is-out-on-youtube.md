Blizzard released the soundtrack of World of Warcraft: Forever on 13 October. The album is on YouTube as one video of 42 minutes, and on Spotify and other streaming services. Forever itself launches on 4 November.

The music takes you to the new places of Forever, writes Blizzard: Zephras Isle, the Riverglades, Hyjal and more. The full album plays in one go in the video below.

## Where to listen

The album is on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/3Y0nJ9S6DFhavJDUYzeKgM). Blizzard collects the other streaming services on [one page](https://lnk.to/WoWForever).

The date was no surprise. Composer Adam Burgess shared it on X on 5 October. In the [post about the date](/news/a-soundtrack-of-19-tracks-streams-from-13-october/) you find all 19 tracks with their composers.
