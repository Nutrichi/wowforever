<a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6953"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Verigan's Fist</a>, the level 20 quest weapon of Alliance Paladins, has a proc since the beta build of 1 October. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1322218"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_excorcism.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Forgefire</a> deals 39 to 59 Holyfire damage. The Horde weapon <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=267369"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_25.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wolfsbane</a> already had such a proc, writes Wowhead.

Alliance Paladins get the two-handed mace from the questline that ends with <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1806">The Test of Righteousness</a>. Forever also has Forsaken Paladins, and they earn <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=267369"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_sword_25.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Wolfsbane</a> with <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95140">Old Fire-Eye</a>. That sword hits a little harder and had <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1282503"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_excorcism.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blazewind Blast</a> from the start, which deals triple damage to Wolves and Worgen.

Alliance players complained about the gap, among others [on the European forums](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/verigans-fist-rework/630071). The new proc closes it:

> **Holy Forgefire**: Ignite the target in the flames of the Great Forge, dealing 39 to 59 Holyfire damage. Deals 3 times as much damage to Fire Elementals and Earth Elementals.

## What else changes

The mace loses a little elsewhere, according to the files of the build. Stamina drops from 7 to 5 and Spirit from 12 to 10. Intellect stays at 6, and the damage of 65 to 99 at speed 3.20 stays too.
