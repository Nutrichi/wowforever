Bags cost gold, and gold is short while you level in World of Warcraft: Forever. Training every two levels, Dual Spec and riding at 40 all want their share. Fifteen quests and seven bosses give you a bag for free instead.

## Why your bags fill up

Your backpack has 20 slots. With four 6-slot bags from the starting zone you reach 42. That sounds like plenty, but quest items, food, drink and everything you pick up to sell fill it fast. Bags like the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=804"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_10_blue.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Large Blue Sack</a> drop at random from any monster, but the lists below are a surer way.

Three of these bags are new. The Scuffed Shipping Crate comes from Wrenix the Wretched in Ratchet, for parts from the upper pirate camp south of the town. Vyrin's Revenge at Farstrider Lodge in Loch Modan now gives the Hunting Satchel, and the Spidersilk Pouch asks for three strands of Crystalline Silk from Gloomrise Spinners.

The Old Toolbox is part of the chain for the Cozy Sleeping Bag, so the [Cozy Sleeping Bag guide](/guides/cozy-sleeping-bag/) takes you past it. The Hyjal Postal Satchel is the last quest of a [short mail chain on Mount Hyjal](/news/a-free-18-slot-bag-waits-on-mount-hyjal/). The Red Bag has a catch: it lowers your effective stealth level by 2, which Rogues and Druids will notice.

Not every quest is easy to solo the moment you can pick it up. Some of them ask for a group.
