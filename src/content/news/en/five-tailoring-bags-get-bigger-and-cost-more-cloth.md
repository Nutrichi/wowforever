Five Tailoring bags in WoW Forever were smaller than Blizzard intended. A hotfix on 11 November fixes that: every bag gets more slots, but also costs one more bolt of cloth. Good news for anyone who runs out of space while levelling.

The other materials stay the same. Blizzard does not say how many slots the bags had before the fix.

## For Tailors

One extra bolt per bag makes leveling Tailoring with bags a bit more expensive, but the bags are worth more on the auction house now. Every recipe, with its trainer or vendor, is in our [Tailoring recipes](/tradeskills/forever/tailoring/recipes/). Need a big bag for free? At level 56 [an 18-slot bag waits on Mount Hyjal](/news/a-free-18-slot-bag-waits-on-mount-hyjal/).

The rest of this hotfix, with Judgement as a ranged attack, is in [a separate post](/news/judgement-now-counts-as-a-ranged-attack/).
