A player who queues alone for a battleground in World of Warcraft: Forever will never face a premade. Premade groups are only matched against other premades. Blizzard said it in episode 4 of its podcast, which is all about PvP.

Friends who play together in a small group do not have to fear a full premade either. Senior Game Designer Josh Greenfield, known as Aggrend, says there will be a small-group option. Such a group will probably hold two or three players, but that number is not final.

Battleground matchmaking changed once before in WoW Classic. In Phase 2 of Season of Discovery, Blizzard made it less likely that a premade met a group of random players. After some time in the queue, those rules no longer applied. Whether Forever drops the rule after a long wait as well, is not known.

The rest of the PvP plans from the podcast, from less Honor in Alterac Valley to war games, are in [our post about episode 4](/news/podcast-episode-4-cuts-honor-in-alterac-valley/).
