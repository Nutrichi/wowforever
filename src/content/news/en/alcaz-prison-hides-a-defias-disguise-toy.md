Players have found a secret toy in World of Warcraft: Forever. The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279471"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/achievement_boss_edwinvancleef.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Alcaz Operative Insignia</a> disguises you as a Defias operative from Alcaz Island for 10 minutes. The chain starts in the new dungeon Alcaz Prison, and its first boss has to stay alive.

## How the chain works

Clear Alcaz Prison, but leave the first boss, The Questioner, alive. At the end of the dungeon you pick up the quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96335">The Man in the Iron Chains</a>. It asks you to recover the Alcaz Shackle Key from The Questioner, so you still need him.

![The Questioner, a Defias in a red hood with a curved blade, beside a second Defias and a torture rack in a dark prison cell, a screenshot from World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-11-13-wow-forever-the-questioner.jpg)

From there the trail leads to Moonbrook in Westfall. Alliance players then go on to Stormwind, Horde players to Booty Bay. There you receive a <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279450"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_armorkit_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fractured Insignia</a>, which needs level 40. Six of them combine into the Alcaz Operative Insignia.

## What the toy does

The Alcaz Operative Insignia is a toy that binds when picked up. It disguises you as a Defias operative from Alcaz Island for 10 minutes, with a cooldown of 30 minutes.

Players of the WoW Secret Finding Discord found the secret. How you collect six Fractured Insignia, and whether that takes six runs, is not known yet. The dungeon itself is in the [Alcaz Prison guide](/guides/dungeons/alcaz-prison/guide/).
