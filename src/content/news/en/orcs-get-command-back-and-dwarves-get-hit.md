Blizzard changes the racials of World of Warcraft: Forever once more before launch. Dwarves trade Earth Fury for 1% hit, and Orcs lose Savage but get Command back. Humans, Trolls, Undead, Tauren and the Skyborne get new values too.

The changes are in the development notes of the beta of 26 October. Blizzard heard two kinds of feedback: some new racials felt too generic, others were clearly stronger than the rest. In this final pass Blizzard puts racial identity first, and accepts that some races are slightly better at certain roles.

Where generic racials had replaced racials for a role or class, Blizzard rolls them back. The weapon specializations are the exception and [stay gone](/news/humans-dwarves-and-orcs-lose-their-weapon-racials/):

> One exception to this is the weapon specialization racials, like humans' Sword Specialization or orcs' Axe Specialization. While those have a strong fantasy component to them, it is not healthy in the long run for future raid and dungeon loot tables to be evaluated through the lens of "required" weapon types.

Blood Fury, Berserking and Touch of the Grave were added to the notes later on 27 October. Blizzard says they were meant to be in the list from the start.

## Skyborne

Wind Blessed gives 2% haste (was 3%). Read Leyline raises Health and Mana regeneration by 50% (was 100%) for 15 seconds. It cannot be used while Innervate is active; Innervate replaces it as the stronger effect. Used at a ley line, Read Leyline also gives Attuned for 1 hour, and then the bonus is 100%.

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259686"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_skyreach_lens_flare.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Skysight</a> gives 15% movement speed (was 10%) for 15 seconds. At an Elemental Convergence it also gives Charged for 1 hour, which raises the bonus to 30%. Attuned and Charged both last through death, and disappear when you enter a raid.

Blizzard also does a tuning pass on the remaining passive bonuses, so they do not push players towards one choice. The table with every racial per race on [Classes](/classes/) has been updated. Whether these values are final for the launch on 4 November, Blizzard does not say.
