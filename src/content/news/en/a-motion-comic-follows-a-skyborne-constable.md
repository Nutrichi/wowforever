## Undercover in a strange settlement

Aonda travels to a remote settlement that seems to be under strict control. Prisoners march under guard, strangers smile a little too much, and the silence hides a growing threat. She plays along to get closer to the truth, but the hidden plans and the blind devotion around her soon get the upper hand.

The Al'Aketh are the cult you fight as a Skyborne from your first quests. They want to tear Zephras Isle loose, and the High Order and the Windshapers have to work together to stop them. The whole story, quest by quest, is in our [Zephras Isle guide](/guides/zephras-isle/).

## Who made it

Sam Slade reads the part of Constable Aonda. Robert Brooks wrote the story, Stefano Porcu drew the art and the cover, Mauro Gulma did the colors and AndWorld Design the lettering. Blizzard also offers the comic as a PDF to download.
