The <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1263070"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_farm_herbseed.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seed Hybridizer</a> works again in World of Warcraft: Forever. Hotfixes on 19 November turn the Herbalism camp object back on, with new values and a chance to fail when you combine seeds. Scarce Herb Seeds no longer go in. Retribution Paladins and two dungeons also get fixes.

## Seed Hybridizer

The Seed Hybridizer needs Herbalism 300 and a campfire nearby. It multiplies seeds or combines them into rarer tiers. Blizzard does not say what the new values are or how big the chance to fail is. All camp objects are in the [camping guide](/guides/camping/).

![The tooltip of the Seed Hybridizer: requires Herbalism 300, multiplies seeds or combines them into rarer tiers, needs a campfire nearby, a screenshot from World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-11-20-wow-forever-seed-hybridizer-tooltip.jpg)

## Retribution

Two-Handed Weapon Specialization now raises all Holy damage you deal while you wield a two-handed weapon. Blizzard calls this a fix, so the talent missed part of that damage before. The talents are in the [Retribution guide](/classes/paladin/retribution/).

## Dungeons

- **City of Dalaran:** the Shade of the Archmage no longer despawns as soon as the fight starts. The boss is in the [City of Dalaran guide](/guides/dungeons/city-of-dalaran/guide/).
- **The Shaper's Terrace:** Poison Shock from Vitrix deals less damage.

## The rest of the hotfixes

- **Skyborne:** 10% off Apprentice and Journeyman Riding at Honored, see [our post](/news/skyborne-now-get-10-percent-off-riding-at-honored/).
- **Items:** Motes of Unstable Magic are soulbound, see [our post](/news/motes-of-unstable-magic-are-now-soulbound/). Squall-breaker's Potion can now be deleted.
- **Tauren:** Cultivation succeeds more often on herbs you have the Herbalism skill for, and works with Purple Lotus again.
- **Riverglades:** Mosh'gra can now drop its rare loot, which it never did before.
- **Shimmering Flats:** a Silithid Hive Drone stuck under the world near the Rustmaul Dig Site is fixed.
- **Booty Bay:** Rix Xizzix no longer gives access to a strange tome after the quest Rift Away.
- **Alterac Mountains:** during Stormpike's Deciphering you can get a new Ensorcelled Parchment if you destroyed the old one.
- **Hunting for Ectoplasm:** the quest now uses the right creature type for the ectoplasm.
