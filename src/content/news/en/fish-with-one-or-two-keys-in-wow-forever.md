Fishing pays better than ever in World of Warcraft: Forever, with Legacy talents and a [Fishing Hut in your camp](/guides/camping/) that boost your catches. With the right setup it also takes a lot less clicking.

## Two keys, no addon

Forever has an interact key that also works on your bobber.

1. Press Escape and open the options.
2. Turn on **Enable Interact Key** and bind a key to **Interact with Target**.
3. Put Fishing on your action bar and bind it to a key you like.

Now press your Fishing key, and when the bobber bobs, press your interact key. As long as the cog icon shows above your bobber, you pull it in. You do not have to point your mouse at it, or even look straight at it.

You still have to equip your rod and put on a lure yourself.

## One key, with an addon

For true one-key fishing you need an addon. [OneCast](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/onecast) is one of them. In its settings:

- **Cast and reel key** is your fishing key: it casts and reels in.
- Turn on **Auto-equip fishing pole** and **Apply lure with the cast key**.

Then that one key equips your rod, puts on a lure if you have none, casts and catches the fish. Want to know where to level your skill? See our [Fishing guide](/tradeskills/forever/fishing/).
