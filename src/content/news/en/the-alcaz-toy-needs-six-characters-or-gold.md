The Defias disguise from Alcaz Prison is no quick toy in World of Warcraft: Forever. The <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279471"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/achievement_boss_edwinvancleef.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Alcaz Operative Insignia</a> takes six <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=279450"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_armorkit_04.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fractured Insignia</a>, and every character earns only one. The quest chain cannot be repeated, so the other five come from alts or the auction house.

## One insignia per character

The chain runs through three quests:

| Quest | What you do |
|---|---|
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96335">The Man in the Iron Chains</a> | Leave the first boss, The Questioner, alive. At the end of the dungeon, Mason Connery asks you for the key The Questioner carries. Kill him, take the key and free Mason. |
| <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96337">More Valuable Than Gold</a> | Mason sends you to his hidden treasure: Connery's Trunk on the upper floor of the inn in Moonbrook, Westfall (/way 43.4 67.8). It gives a Document Box. |
| State Secrets | Hand in the Document Box for a Fractured Insignia. |

There are three places to hand in State Secrets:

| For | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Renzik "The Shiv" | SI:7 in Old Town, Stormwind (/way 75.8 60.2) |
| Horde | Nazgrel | Next to Thrall in the Valley of Wisdom, Orgrimmar (/way 32.4 36.0) |
| Both | Pix Xizzix | Booty Bay (/way 28.4 75.8), with 150 reputation with the Steamwheedle Cartel on top |

You can also sell the Document Box to a vendor for 25 gold instead.

## Six for the toy

Six Fractured Insignia combine into the toy, and the insignia needs level 40. With no repeat, you have three ways to get the rest: run the chain on five more characters, get them from friends, or buy them. On the auction house they cost 15 to 20 gold each right now, so the toy easily runs to 75 gold or more.

The toy itself is in [our first post](/news/alcaz-prison-hides-a-defias-disguise-toy/): 10 minutes as a Defias operative from Alcaz Island, with a cooldown of 30 minutes. The dungeon is in the [Alcaz Prison guide](/guides/dungeons/alcaz-prison/guide/).
