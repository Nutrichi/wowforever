Good news for everyone who rides through the night with a torch in World of Warcraft: Forever. The <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=280612"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_torch_lit.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Night Watchman's Torch</a> works on a mount and in shapeshift forms again.

## What happened

Players noticed this week that the torch stopped working while mounted. Threads on the forums and on Reddit asked why. A Blizzard developer answered that it was not intended: a bug fix had blocked the torch by accident. A hotfix now undoes that, so you can light your way on your mount and as a Druid in Cat Form or Travel Form again.

## How to get the torch

The torch comes from Duskwood. Look for a slain Night Watch guard: he gives a quest that you hand in right away, and the torch is your reward. Druids carry it in their mouth in animal form, a small detail that players asked for during the beta.
