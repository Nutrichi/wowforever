The Tribute chest in Dire Maul North no longer always holds three blue items in World of Warcraft: Forever. Every boss you reset takes one away, down to a minimum of one. Blizzard has not confirmed the change. Players found it by testing.

## How a Tribute run works

In a Tribute run you only kill <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=11501">King Gordok</a> and leave five bosses alive: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14326">Guard Mol'dar</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14321">Guard Fengus</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14323">Guard Slip'kik</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14325">Captain Kromcrush</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14324">Cho'Rush the Observer</a>. When the king dies you get <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=22799"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_crown_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">King of the Gordok</a>, every ogre turns friendly and <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/object=179564">Gordok Tribute</a> appears.

That chest holds pre-raid gear such as <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18531"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_22.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Unyielding Maul</a>, a strong weapon for Feral tanks, and a good amount of gold. Hunters can do the run alone. The full route is in our [Dire Maul North guide](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-north/).

## What costs a blue item

The chest starts with three blue items. Each penalty removes one, but never the last one.

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **A guard resets** | One blue item less |
| **Captain Kromcrush or King Gordok resets** | One blue item less |
| **Kromcrush is still at his post** | One blue item less |
| **No trap on Guard Slip'kik** | No effect |

A player who pulls a guard and dies to keep the run going now costs the group a piece of loot. That hurts most at Captain Kromcrush, who stands in the middle of the last corridor. Letting him reset and walking past no longer gives full loot.

Full loot still comes with <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18258"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_chest_chain_14.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Gordok Ogre Suit</a>, an invisibility potion or a stealth potion. The trap from <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1193">A Broken Trap</a> changes nothing for the chest, but it keeps a guard from being pulled by accident.

![Captain Kromcrush in the corridor of Dire Maul North, with his dialogue window open on the left](../../../assets/posts/2026-11-28-wow-forever-captain-kromcrush-gossip.jpg)

Kromcrush leaves his post when you talk to him. That also works after the king dies, as long as the chest has not appeared yet. Whether Blizzard intends that is not known.

## The test runs

| Run | Blue items |
|---|---|
| **No trap, Kromcrush reset, still at his post** | 1 |
| **No trap, past Kromcrush in stealth, still at his post** | 2 |
| **Trap, past Kromcrush in stealth, still at his post** | 2 |
| **Trap, Gordok Ogre Suit at Kromcrush** | 3 |
| **Trap, past Kromcrush in stealth, sent away after the king** | 3 |

Three blue items only came when Kromcrush left his post, before or after the king died.

The Tribute run already lost some strength on 23 November, when the hotfixes [halved Fengus' Ferocity](/news/world-buffs-survive-loading-screens-again/).
