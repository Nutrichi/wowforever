The Riverglades is one of the new zones of World of Warcraft: Forever. Players arrive around level 36, and both the Horde and the Alliance have quest hubs there. The Bolder'ok are ogres who broke away from the Twilight's Hammer after the Second War. The rest of the zone is in [the post about the video](/news/blizzard-shows-the-road-from-level-30-to-60/).

![An ogre fighting pit with a fence of wooden stakes, bloodstains on the ground and a campfire, with ogres standing beside it, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-20-wow-forever-bloodmasters-ring-arena.jpg)

## What the beta files show

In the video Blizzard gives the ring a single sentence. The beta files say more, although Blizzard has not confirmed any of it. The ring has a host: <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=257059">Chub'chob</a>, Bloodmaster of Bolder'ok, a level 45 elite. Whoever wins earns <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1280857"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/achievement_reputation_ogre.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Champion of the Bloodmaster's Ring</a>, with the line "Earned the respect of the Bolder'ok."

There is also a rare ring with the same name, <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=275043"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelry_ring_25.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bloodmaster's Ring</a>, item level 45, with Stamina, Intellect and spell power. How you get it is not known yet.

## The Ring of Blood in The Burning Crusade

The Ring of Blood was a quest chain in Nagrand in The Burning Crusade. The goblin Gurgthock and Wodin the Troll-Servant sent players into the pit against ever stronger opponents, until one of them was champion. The idea came back in almost every expansion after that, up to Dragonflight.

![The goblin Gurgthock and Wodin the Troll-Servant next to the Ring of Blood in Nagrand, a screenshot from The Burning Crusade](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-20-wow-forever-gurgthock-and-wodin.jpg)

Whether Gurgthock and Wodin return in Forever is not known. In the beta files the ring belongs to Chub'chob.
