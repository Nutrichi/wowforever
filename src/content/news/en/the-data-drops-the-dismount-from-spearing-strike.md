<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310222"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_spear_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Spearing Strike</a> no longer dismounts its target, according to the files of the new World of Warcraft: Forever beta build. Wowhead compared every spell with the previous build. Most changes match the class notes of 1 October, but a few are missing from them.

Blizzard has not confirmed the changes below that are not in its notes. Datamined tooltips can also change again before they reach the game.

## Changes that are not in the notes

- **Spearing Strike**, the Arms talent: the line "Mounted targets are dismounted" is gone. The extra 80% weapon damage against Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets stays. The notes do say that the talent no longer needs a two-handed weapon and now needs Battle Stance.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2687"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_racial_bloodrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bloodrage</a>**: the line that the Warrior counts as in combat for its duration is gone.
- **Feral**: <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1223242"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_druid_predatoryinstincts.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Predatory Instincts</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16966"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_vampiricaura.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shredding Attacks</a> move to another spot in the tree. That makes room for <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1322605"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_druid_displacement.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Shifting Power</a> in row 4 and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1322670"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_aspectmastery.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Improved Shifting Power</a> in row 5. Shredding Attacks also lowers the Rage cost of Lacerate by 1 to 3.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=24858"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_forceofnature.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Moonkin Form</a>**: the tooltip is rewritten. The values stay: 100% more chance for Omen of Clarity, 360% more armor from items and 3% critical strike chance for the party.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310315"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_whirlwind.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Raging Blows</a>**: in the data it lowers the Rage cost of Cleave by 2. Later that night Blizzard changed the talent again in a separate post about Warriors.

## What the data confirms

The numbers from the notes are in the files too. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20925"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_blessingofprotection.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Shield</a> raises block chance by 30%, up from 20%. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20127"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_defend.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Redoubt</a> goes to 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20%. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311084"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_enlightenedjudgements.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Champion of the Light</a> gives spell damage worth 20, 40 or 60% of Intellect and no healing.

For Hunters, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310687"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/hunter_pvp_snipershot.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sniper Shot</a> gets a range of 45 yards and adds 10 yards to the next three shots. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19295"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_parry.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Deflection</a> drops to 1 to 5% Parry. For Mages, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400624"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_firebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Heating Up</a> is the new name of Hot Streak, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11129"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_sealoffire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Combustion</a> ends after 3 critical strikes instead of 4.

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=23584"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_dualwield.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dual Wield Specialization</a> no longer adds Rage from the off-hand, and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=437032"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_elemental_primal_shadow.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Soul Harvest</a> is the new name of Soul Harvesting. The [talent calculator](/talents/) already follows this build.
