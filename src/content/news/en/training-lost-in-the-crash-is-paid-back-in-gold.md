Lost a skill you trained during the server crash of World of Warcraft: Forever on Monday? Blizzard pays the gold back. Everyone affected now has an in-game mail with the cost of the training they bought and lost.

## Who gets it

The refund covers training bought on Monday 9 November between 13.17 and 14.17 PST, or 22.17 to 23.17 Belgian time. In that hour the servers had problems, and some players lost skills they had just paid for.

> With in-game mail that has now gone out to all affected players, we have refunded the cost of skill purchases that were made on Monday, November 9 between 1:17 p.m. PST and 2:17 p.m. PST (8:17-9:17 a.m. on November 10 AEDT) when the WoW: Forever servers experienced issues that caused some players to lose training they purchased.

## What to do

Check your mailbox. The gold equals what you paid for the training you lost. The skills themselves do not come back with the mail, so visit your trainer again.

Shamans who lost a totem in the same crash [got it back on Tuesday](/news/shamans-get-back-totems-lost-in-the-crash/).
