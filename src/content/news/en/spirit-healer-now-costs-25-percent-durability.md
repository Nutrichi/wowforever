A resurrection at the Spirit Healer costs less durability in World of Warcraft: Forever. Gear loses 25% durability instead of 50%, according to the files of the fourth beta build. Blizzard does not mention the change in the notes of the build of 8 October.

The rest of the penalty stays. A player who lets the Spirit Healer resurrect them instead of running back to their corpse still gets Resurrection Sickness, which weakens the character for a while.

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Durability lost | 50% | 25% |
| Resurrection Sickness | Yes | Yes |

The change comes from datamining and is not confirmed by Blizzard. Whether it stays until launch, is not known. Other changes in the same build are in the [post about the build](/news/city-of-dalaran-opens-in-the-fourth-beta-week/).
