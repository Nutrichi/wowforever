> **Update 17 November:** Blizzard is removing all gold from the farm and suspends the worst abusers. More in [our post](/news/blizzard-suspends-the-blackmaw-hold-gold-farmers/).

A gold farm in Blackmaw Hold no longer works in World of Warcraft: Forever. Groups cleared a room of about 30 <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=277474">Blackmaw Devotees</a>, reset the dungeon and went again, for more than 100 gold an hour. A hotfix took away all loot from these monsters.

## How the farm worked

The room lies a little deeper in the dungeon. Groups rode through the first area and used a long drop to lose aggro. Mages even reached it alone, using spots the monsters cannot path to. The devotees are not elite and only use auto-attacks.

![About thirty Blackmaw Devotees, furbolgs of level 56 and 57 with red health bars, kneeling in a dark hall of Blackmaw Hold, a screenshot from World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-11-17-wow-forever-blackmaw-devotees.jpg)

## What they dropped

Each devotee gave about 25 to 50 silver, plus items for the vendor and green items. They also dropped <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274964"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_elemental_primal_mana.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mote of Unstable Magic</a> for the quest <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98477">Unstable Magic</a>, worth about 13 silver at the Auction House, and <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=273050"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_03.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blackmaw Pouch</a> for <a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=97327">Blackmaw Pouches</a>, about 1 gold each. Since the hotfix the devotees drop nothing, so the prices of both items may move.

Some players say they made thousands of gold with the farm. Whether Blizzard does more than close it, is not known. In an interview with But Why Tho?, Blizzard said it watches the economy closely and would even step in on a weekend against an unfair gold farm.

Blackmaw Hold is for levels 55 to 60, see the [Blackmaw Hold guide](/guides/dungeons/blackmaw-hold/). Gold at level 60 also comes from the [weekly quest in Booty Bay](/news/a-weekly-quest-at-level-60-pays-up-to-100-gold/).
