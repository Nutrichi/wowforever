Hunters in World of Warcraft: Forever get back most of what they lost last week, and more. Hotfixes on the live servers [cut Aspect of the Hawk and Trueshot Aura by about half](/news/hunters-lose-half-of-trueshot-aura-in-a-hotfix/). Now both go up again, close to their values before the cut, and several shots deal more damage. Blizzard confirms the changes in the development notes of 2 November.

Trueshot Aura works for the whole raid, so every melee and Hunter in it gains as well.

## Shots

| Ability | Change |
|---|---|
| **Aimed Shot** | Bonus damage about 110% higher on all ranks, at the top rank 350 (was 166) |
| **Sniper Shot** | Bonus damage doubled, at rank 3 590 (was 295) |
| **Arcane Shot** | About 30% more base damage, and attack power scaling from 0.10 to 0.15 |
| **Serpent Sting** | About 20% more base damage, and 5% attack power per tick (was 3%) |
| **Barrage** | Multi-Shot, Aimed Shot and Volley deal 5, 10 or 15% more damage (was 3, 7 or 10%) |
| **Lightning Reflexes** | Up to 15% Agility (was 10%) |

## Other changes

- All traps share one 30 second cooldown. Fire and Frost traps had separate cooldowns before.
- Hunter's Mark no longer stacks with itself through different ranks, and rank 4 no longer gives less than rank 3.
- Pathfinding moves from row 2 to row 3 in Beast Mastery.
- Lacerating Strikes from Survival always replaces your own older effect.
- Pets keep their innate attack speed when you untrain them.

The new values are in the game, and our [talent calculator](/talents/hunter/) now places Pathfinding in row 3. Builds are in the [Marksmanship](/guides/marksmanship-hunter/), [Beast Mastery](/guides/beast-mastery-hunter/) and [Survival](/guides/survival-hunter/) guides.
