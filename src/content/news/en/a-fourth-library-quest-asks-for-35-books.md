The library in World of Warcraft: Forever has one more reward than we knew. After 10, 20 and 25 books, a fourth quest asks for 35 books.

## The new quest

<a href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=84394">Greater Friend of the Library</a> is the fourth quest named Greater Friend of the Library. It needs level 50 and 35 books handed in. You choose one of three blue items, all item level 58:

| Item | Slot | Stats |
|---|---|---|
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=281637"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shield_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Antique Bulwark</a> | Shield | 1,994 armor, 36 block, +13 Stamina, +8 Defense |
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=277202"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_pendant-of-the-aqir.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Artifact Seeker's Pendant</a> | Neck | +9 Strength, +8 Agility, +14 Stamina |
| <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=281636"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_mithrilnecklace.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Museum Keeper's Chain</a> | Neck | +10 Stamina, +10 Intellect, up to 11 spell damage and healing |

Books from earlier quests count toward the total, so you do not have to start over.

## Three more books

To reach 35, you need books we had not listed yet. These three are now in our guide:

| Zone | Book | /way |
|---|---|---|
| Silverpine Forest | Ataeric: On Arcane Curiosities | 43.4 41.2, The Sepulcher, bottom floor next to the armorer |
| Swamp of Sorrows | A Luddite's Guide to Caring for Your Demonic Pet | 61.0 22.0, Fallow Sanctuary, in a cage: loot a Rusted Cage Key from the Lost Ones nearby |
| Blasted Lands | Conjurer's Codex | 55.3 32.2, in the rubble along the road |

That makes 39 books. Alliance players can skip the one at The Sepulcher, deep in Horde territory: there are enough books for every reward without it.

Every location with /way and the full reward list is in our [library books guide](/guides/library-books/).
