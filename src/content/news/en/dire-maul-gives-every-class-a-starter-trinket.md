Just hit level 60 and your trinkets are still from leveling? Dire Maul has one for every class. Bring the book for your class to the library, and you get a Royal Seal of Eldre'Thalas.

## A book for every class

Every class has its own book. It drops from the bosses in all three wings of Dire Maul, and now and then from A Dusty Tome, the books that lie around in the dungeon. The books are not soulbound, so the Auction House has them too.

The book starts a quest. Bring it to a Lorekeeper in the library between Dire Maul West and North, like <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14382">Lorekeeper Mykos</a>. The fastest way in is the <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18249"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_key_10.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Crescent Key</a>, which <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=14354">Pusillin</a> drops in [Dire Maul East](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-east/). It opens the door to [Dire Maul North](/guides/dungeons/dire-maul-north/), close to the library. A Rogue with enough [Lockpicking](/guides/lockpicking/) or a Blacksmith with an Arcanite Skeleton Key gets in without it.

## What each trinket gives

Every Royal Seal has +15 Nature Resistance. That is new: in Classic it was +10 Fire Resistance. The first raids of Forever, Barrow Deeps and Mount Hyjal, both have a nature theme, so the resistance may come in handy there.

## Weaker than in Classic

Almost every seal lost stats. The Druid seal gave +44 healing in Classic, the Hunter seal +48 ranged attack power and the Warrior seal +200 armor. In return, the healer seals now also give a little spell damage, so a Balance Druid or Shadow Priest gets something out of them too. Warriors come off worst.

Even so, they are fine trinkets for your first days at 60. Each book also gives reputation with the Shen'dralar, more on that in our [Shen'dralar guide](/reputations/shendralar/).
