Our [BiS page](/bis/) for World of Warcraft: Forever has grown. Five specs that showed "Coming soon" now have a full pre-raid list for level 60, and two lists got a big update. That makes 18 of the 28 specs.

## Five new lists

- [Beast Mastery Hunter](/bis/forever/preraid/hunter-beast-mastery/)
- [Marksmanship Hunter](/bis/forever/preraid/hunter-marksmanship/)
- [Fire Mage](/bis/forever/preraid/mage-fire/)
- [Frost Mage](/bis/forever/preraid/mage-frost/)
- [Destruction Warlock](/bis/forever/preraid/warlock-destruction/)

Each list gives up to three options per slot, best first, with where the item comes from: the dungeon boss, the quest, the profession or the Reals vendor in Booty Bay.

## Two reworked lists

- **[Protection Paladin](/bis/forever/preraid/paladin-protection/):** almost new. The Soulforge set now leads in most slots, since Blizzard [gave it Spell Power for tanks](/news/soulforge-armor-swaps-strength-for-spell-power/).
- **[Elemental Shaman](/bis/forever/preraid/shaman-elemental/):** our old list leaned on healing gear. It now has real caster pieces, with the Tier 0.5 set and the Reals vendor items.

## Still coming

Feral Druid (cat and bear), Retribution Paladin, Discipline Priest, the three Rogue specs, Demonology Warlock and Arms and Fury Warrior. We check the lists every day and add them as soon as they are ready. Every list shows the date of its last update, and the [class pages](/classes/) link to the list of each spec.
