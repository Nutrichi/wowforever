Senior Game Designer Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield hints that something happens on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever tonight. On the last full day of the beta, he advises players on X to log in once more "in a few hours".

He posted it on 22 October at 17:51 Belgian time: "Final full day of beta today. Might be good to log in once more and check it out here in a few hours." And then: "Just sayin'. No reason in particular."

## The end of a beta

Blizzard has closed earlier betas with an event in a capital, where developers summon a boss or a wave of monsters for testers to fight. Whether something like that happens on the Forever beta, and at what time exactly, is not known.

## Until Friday

It turned out to be a [24-hour farewell celebration](/news/epics-and-azuregos-as-the-beta-says-goodbye/) with epics, infernals, Azuregos and Kazzak.

The beta [closes for good](/news/the-beta-stays-open-until-23-october/) on Friday 23 October at 19:00 Belgian time. The launch follows on 5 November at 00:00 Belgian time.
