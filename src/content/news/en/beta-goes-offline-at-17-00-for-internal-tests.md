The World of Warcraft: Forever beta goes offline at 17.00 Belgian time on 14 October. Blizzard runs internal tests and expects the beta to be down for no more than a couple of hours. A new build is not mentioned.

Community manager Kaivax announced it on the American forums at 16.49 Belgian time, eleven minutes before the realms went offline:

> We’re taking the WoW Forever Beta offline in a few minutes for maintenance. We’re going to run some internal tests, and we don’t expect it to be down for more than a couple of hours.
>
> Beta realms should go offline at 8:00 a.m. PDT.
>
> We’ll see you on the other side!

![The maintenance banner of World of Warcraft: a white gear and wrench above the W logo on a blue background](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-14-wow-forever-beta-maintenance.jpg)

8.00 PDT is 17.00 in Belgium. An exact end time is not given; after a couple of hours the beta should be back in the evening. What the tests are for, Blizzard does not say. Whether they have to do with the [stress test](/news/the-launcher-shows-a-stress-test-on-19-october/) that the Battle.net launcher shows for 19 October, is not known either.
