Tarnished Undermine Reals are coming to World of Warcraft: Forever. Developer Josh Greenfield announced it during the live Q&A on 17 September, writes Wowhead. The currency comes from Season of Discovery. How players earn it, Blizzard did not say.

In Season of Discovery the currency dropped in various dungeons, writes Wowhead. Players spent it on gear, toys, patterns, reagents and more.

For players who skipped Season of Discovery, Wowhead compares it to the Badges of Justice from The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary.

How the currency is earned in Forever is unclear. Going by Season of Discovery it could come from end-game and pre-raid dungeons, writes Wowhead, but that is a guess and not an announcement.

What players buy with it in Forever, and at which vendor, Blizzard did not say either. Josh Greenfield, who goes by Aggrend, gave the news in the same Q&A after which [the beta opened](/news/wow-forever-beta-is-live/).
