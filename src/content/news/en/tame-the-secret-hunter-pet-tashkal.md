Hunters in World of Warcraft: Forever have found a secret pet. <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=268231">Ta'shkal</a> on Mount Hyjal wears the model of the <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=10657">Corrupted Cat</a>, the kitten from Winna's quest chain in Felwood that you could never tame in the original game.

## How to tame it

1. **Find the <a class="wf-wh wf-q0" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=276909"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_jewelcrafting_jadepanther.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Corrupted Cat Figurine</a>.** The first one lay in the Fel Pool on Mount Hyjal (/way 47.0 75.0). Later Hunters found it in other spots too, but always around that pool.
2. **Click the Lonely Lantern** at /way 26.0 65.0 with the figurine in your bags. That summons Ta'shkal <Escaped Experiment>.
3. **Tame it.** A level 59 Hunter found the trick, so bring a character close to 60.

Other Hunters hunt the same figurine, so you may have to wait for it to come back.

## Why it is worth it

Ta'shkal attacks with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1263110"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_eyeoftheowl.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Faster Attack VII</a>. Until now only <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=2850">Broken Tooth</a>, a rare cat in the Badlands, had that, and you had to camp it. Pet attacks are normalized: a faster pet hits more often but for less per hit. In dungeons and raids you will hardly notice the difference with a slightly slower cat. In PvP the fast hits push back casts more often.
