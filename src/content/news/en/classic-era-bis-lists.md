[Best-in-slot lists](/bis/classic/) for WoW Classic Era now cover all 27 specs. There are 189 lists. They cover pre-raid gear and six raid phases, from Molten Core and Onyxia's Lair to Naxxramas.

The phases follow the original release of 2019. Molten Core and Onyxia's Lair are phase 1, Blackwing Lair phase 3, Ahn'Qiraj phase 5 and Naxxramas phase 6. Every list names the source of each item.

## Sources

The lists are based on the gear guides of Icy Veins. For phase 2, Icy Veins has no separate list for Dire Maul. That phase uses its refined list for Molten Core and Onyxia's Lair, with PvP and world boss items added. The phase 4 list of Icy Veins covers Zul'Gurub and Blackwing Lair together. Both choices are noted on the lists themselves.

The first pre-raid lists, for Holy and Discipline Priests and for Protection Warriors, came from the community guides of Warcraft Tavern. They use gear from dungeons for level 51 to 60, such as Blackrock Depths, Scholomance and Stratholme, plus world drops and quests.

Classic Era has no gem sockets. Blizzard added those in The Burning Crusade. Each list stays marked as a draft until it has been checked by hand.
