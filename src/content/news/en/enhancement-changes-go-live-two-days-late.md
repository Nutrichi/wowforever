The Enhancement Shaman changes in World of Warcraft: Forever are live, two days later than the notes said. Due to an unexpected delay in deployment, they reached all regions on 12 November at 10.45 PST, 19.45 in Belgium.

## What is now live

The changes were in the hotfix notes of 10 November, so we wrote that they were live then. That was too early. Since this evening:

- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17364"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_shaman_stormstrike.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Stormstrike</a>:** 100% weapon damage instead of 150%, with an 8-second cooldown instead of 6.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=415140"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_mentalquickness.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mental Dexterity</a>:** attack power equal to 33, 66 or 100% of your Intellect, down from 50, 100 or 150%.
- **<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=408498"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shaman_maelstromweapon.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Maelstrom Weapon</a>:** 20% faster and cheaper per stack at every rank, with 6 to 10 procs per minute depending on your points, instead of 20.
- **Weapon imbues and totems:** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8232"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_cyclone.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Windfury Weapon</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8512"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_windfury.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Windfury Totem</a> can both proc on your attacks.

Why Blizzard does this and what it means for your gear is in [our post about the changes](/news/enhancement-shamans-turn-back-to-windfury/). Our [Enhancement Shaman guide](/guides/enhancement-shaman/) follows the new values and now gives the right date.
