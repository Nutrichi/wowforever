A hidden ledge in Blackrock Depths gives a new toy in World of Warcraft: Forever. In Season of Discovery the same spot held the quest for the Rainbow Generator. Now you find <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=221315"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_note_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Traveler's Symbols</a>, a sign that other players can read.

## What you get

Traveler's Symbols binds when picked up. It places a sign for 1 minute. Pick a message and the sign stays for 1 hour, visible to everyone who passes. The cooldown is 30 minutes.

You choose from a fixed list: Vigilantes Active, Here!, Watchful Eyes, Good Camping, Bad Farm, Missed you, Do not follow, Unsafe area, I went this way, Follow, Need special item, Secret nearby and Death Trap.

The same ledge also gives <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=221316"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_shirt_black_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Premo's Poise-Demanding Uniform</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=216619"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_food_pinenut.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Student Fodder</a>. The shirt turns you to face your target when you use it. In Season of Discovery it played a key role in the secret of Seventeen and the Secret Agents, so secret hunters expect it to matter in Forever too.

## How to reach the ledge

It takes a little parkour. Take your time on the jumps, because a miss means a long fall into the lava.

1. Go to the bridge just east of the Ring of Law. It leads north over the lava. Look down and to the left: a friendly Dark Iron dwarf stands on the base of a pillar.
2. Clear the Blazing Fireguards and the group of Shadowforge dwarves on the bridge.
3. Move to the left side of the bridge, near a small brazier. Get onto the railing, hug the rock wall and jump onto the platform by the wall.
4. Walk to the end of the platform, still against the wall, and push hard into the rock corner.
5. Turn your left shoulder to the wall and run carefully along it until you drop into the open corner. Fall onto the rock outcrop below. That is the ledge.

## A shortcut to Lord Incendius

The ledge also has a door. It ports you to a ledge right above the circle of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=9017">Lord Incendius</a>. There is no way back, so only take it when you want to go there. Follow the ledge to its end and jump carefully onto the bridge below.

The secret hunter SeventeenUncles found the spot. Earlier this week players also found a [Defias disguise toy in Alcaz Prison](/news/alcaz-prison-hides-a-defias-disguise-toy/). The dungeon itself is in the [Blackrock Depths guide](/guides/dungeons/blackrock-depths/guide/).
