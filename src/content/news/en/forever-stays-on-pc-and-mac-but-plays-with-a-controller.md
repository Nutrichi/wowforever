World of Warcraft: Forever is not coming to Xbox. Xbox UK put the game in a post about new Xbox games for November, with a picture of Forever. The post was removed and replaced by a version without Forever. Xbox UK then confirmed that Forever is not available on Xbox and only comes through Battle.net on PC and Mac.

The first post had started talk about a console version, because Forever supports a controller. That talk is over for now.

## A controller works everywhere

In the development notes of 2 November, Blizzard says gamepad support is in a complete, working state. All major gameplay and the core interface work with a controller: moving, spells, interacting and the menus. You set it up in Edit Mode and in the options.

The latest changes:

| Part | What changed |
|---|---|
| Targeting | Select your Focus Target with LB + RB + the lower face button, target friendly corpses, critters, and enemy players with or without a PvP flag |
| Action bars | Items with an area effect can be placed on the ground, confirmed with the same button |
| Bags | New bag screen with free space, ammo count, Keyring and search |
| Flight paths | End a flight early with Back: you land at the next flight point |
| Pets | The Pet Move command works with a controller |
| Ruleset and cinematics | Pick a ruleset and skip cinematics with a controller |
| Addons | Fewer conflicts between the gamepad interface and addons |

Pop-ups no longer take the focus of your controller on their own. Controller support was first announced together with the [training costs of mounts](/news/controller-support-and-mount-training-costs-in-wow-forever/). The other tools built into the game are in our [guide to the built-in UI tools](/guides/built-in-ui-tools/).
