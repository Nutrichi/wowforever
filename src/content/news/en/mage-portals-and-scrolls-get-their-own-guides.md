Mages in World of Warcraft: Forever now have two guides of their own on our site. One covers every teleport and portal with its trainer, the other the scrolls that only Mages can read, with Comprehension, Study and all 28 scrolls.

## Portals and teleports

Teleports come at level 20 and cost 20 silver per spell, portals at level 40 for 1 gold 50 silver. Each capital has its own Portal Trainer, so you visit all three cities of your faction. Every cast needs a rune from a reagent vendor, unless you take the Legacy talent <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1225503"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_candle_02.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Reagent Economy</a>. Everything is in the [Mage portals guide](/guides/mage-portals/).

## Scrolls and Study

At level 6 you learn <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1296017"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/8188276.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Comprehend Scroll</a> and with it the Comprehension skill, up to 300 at level 60. Scrolls drop from monsters for every class. With <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1302508"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_silverscrollcase.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Study</a>, which you get from your first library book, you make extra scrolls once an hour in a library. All four tiers are in the [Mage scrolls guide](/guides/mage-scrolls/).
