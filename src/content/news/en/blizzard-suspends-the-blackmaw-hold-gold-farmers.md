Blizzard takes back all the gold from the Blackmaw Hold farm in World of Warcraft: Forever. A couple hundred players farmed a bugged group of monsters on purpose. Their accounts were locked on Monday, and the worst abusers are now suspended.

## What Blizzard says

Over the weekend, players found a large group of <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=277474">Blackmaw Devotees</a> that are not elite, but dropped the full loot of an elite dungeon monster. A hotfix ended that early on Monday 16 November, as described in [our post about the farm](/news/a-gold-farm-in-blackmaw-hold-is-hotfixed/). Blizzard then looked at what it did to the economy.

> Players who simply ran Blackmaw Hold a few times and benefited organically from the extra loot did nothing wrong, and no one should worry that they're putting their account at risk if they accidentally benefit from a bug. But there were a couple hundred players worldwide who recognized that this was unintended and spent significant time exclusively farming that group of enemies.

The accounts of those players got an economy lock on Monday, so the gold could not spread. Blizzard is now removing all gold they earned this way, including gold from selling the bugged drops to vendors.

> Today, we are in the process of removing all such gold earned, as well as applying account suspensions to significant abusers.

How long the suspensions last and how many accounts get one, Blizzard does not say.

## Why players were angry

Before Blizzard's answer, the farm filled the front pages of the three big WoW subreddits. A petition to ban popular streamers who allegedly used the farm even reached first place on r/all. Most players are still leveling and cannot enter Blackmaw Hold before level 55, while a few made thousands of gold.

That much gold can buy up whole markets, such as every Arcane Crystal, and raise prices for everyone. Many players feared the old saying "exploit early, exploit often" would win again. Tim Jones already said in an interview with But Why Tho? that Blizzard watches the economy closer than ever:

> We're taking a much stronger stance on what we tolerate in terms of breaking the rules economy-wise.

Blackmaw Hold is for levels 55 to 60, see the [Blackmaw Hold guide](/guides/dungeons/blackmaw-hold/).
