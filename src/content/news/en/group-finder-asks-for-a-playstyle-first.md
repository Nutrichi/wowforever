The Looking For Group panel on the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever has a new required field. Before players can list themselves for a dungeon, they pick a playstyle. There are four options: Learning, Relaxed, Competitive and Carry Offered.

![The Looking For Group panel with the open dropdown Select Playstyle (required) and the options Learning, Relaxed, Competitive and Carry Offered, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-07-wow-forever-lfg-playstyle.jpg)

The choice sits in a dropdown at the top of the panel, above the list of dungeons. Without a playstyle, a player cannot queue. The text field for more details about the group stays optional.

The labels come from the modern game. In World of Warcraft: Midnight, groups in the premade group finder already show Learning, Relaxed or Competitive, mostly for Mythic+ keys.

![The premade group finder of World of Warcraft: Midnight, with Mythic+ groups labelled Competitive or Relaxed, a screenshot from World of Warcraft](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-07-wow-forever-midnight-premade-groups.jpg)

Forever has no Mythic+ and no timed dungeons. What Competitive means for a dungeon in Forever, Blizzard has not explained.

Carry Offered is the fourth option. A carry is usually a paid service, often for gold. Blizzard does not allow [GDKP raids in Forever](/news/gdkp-not-allowed-in-wow-forever/). Whether paid carries are allowed, is not known.

When the field came to the beta, and whether it also applies to raids, is not known either.
