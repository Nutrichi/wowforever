Engineers in World of Warcraft: Forever choose between Gnomish and Goblin Engineering at skill 200. A new guide on wowforever.be explains what each specialization makes, both quests, the membership card you renew every two weeks and how to switch.

## What the guide covers

Gnomish Engineering is the choice for gadgets, Goblin Engineering for explosives. Only three items need the specialization to use them: <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18986"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_12.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ultrasafe Transporter: Gadgetzan</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18660"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">World Enlarger</a> and <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=18984"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_enggizmos_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Dimensional Ripper - Everlook</a>. Many players choose for the teleport alone: Gnomes go to Gadgetzan, Goblins to Everlook.

Both quests need a character of level 30 and 200 Engineering. Gnomes hand in Mithril Tubes, an Accurate Scope and two Advanced Target Dummies, Goblins forty bombs and five Explosive Sheep. Every two weeks you pay 2 gold to renew your membership card, or you can no longer craft the schematics of your specialization.

Switching later means unlearning Engineering and leveling it again from 1. Everything is in the [guide to the Engineering specializations](/guides/engineering-specializations/).
