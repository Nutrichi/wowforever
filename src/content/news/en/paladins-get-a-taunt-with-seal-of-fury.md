Paladins in World of Warcraft: Forever get a taunt. Seal of Fury lets Judgement pull an enemy back, Blizzard shows in a new video. The second part of its Class Highlights series also covers Hunters and Mages.

The video lasts 7 minutes and appeared on 3 October at 19.00 Belgian time on the World of Warcraft channel. The [first part](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cb8r05IXiyI), about the Warrior, the Shaman and the Rogue, came out on 30 September. The third part covers the Priest, the Warlock and the Druid.

Part of the Hunter changes was already in the [first class deep dive](/news/hunters-get-foxes-and-druids-keep-potions-in-every-form/), on the Hunter and the Druid. The numbers below come from the tooltips on Wowhead.

## Hunter

Every Hunter spec gets a faster <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19434"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_spear_07.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Aimed Shot</a>. It now has a 2 second cast instead of 3 and no longer interrupts the <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=75"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_whirlwind.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Auto Shot</a> timer. That gives a steadier damage output, says Blizzard.

Marksmanship pairs it with <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310687"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/hunter_pvp_snipershot.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Sniper Shot</a>, a 4 second cast with a range of 45 yards that hits even harder. The new talent <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1223984"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_zenarchery.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Careful Aim</a> turns 100% of Intellect into Attack Power. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=409979"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mount_whitedirewolf.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Lone Wolf</a> raises damage by 20% while no pet is out. Both sit early in the tree, so hybrid builds can reach them too.

Beast Mastery gets the Fox as a new pet family. The gear of the Hunter now also raises the stats of every pet.

Survival unlocks <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1317257"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_pet_tallstrider.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Strider Kick</a> at 21 points, an instant kick for 100% weapon damage. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310532"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_swiftstrike.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Expose Prey</a> can activate <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1495"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_hunter_swiftstrike.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Mongoose Bite</a> without a dodge: a 10% chance per attack on a target with Hunter's Mark. Traps can now be placed in combat. Survival stays a ranged spec, but gets answers when enemies close the gap.

Frost gets <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1312002"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_frostblast.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Ice Lance</a>, an instant follow-up when <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=400647"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Fingers of Frost</a> triggers. Frozen targets take 300% more damage from it.

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=401502"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_mage_frostfirebolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Frostfire Bolt</a> now counts as both Fire and Frost damage, so talents from both trees strengthen the same spell. Fights become a wager of Mana on ever more expensive Arcane spells, with cheap Fire and Frost spells woven in between, says Blizzard.

Fire gets a reworked Hot Streak. Instead of waiting for one instant cast, every Fire spell now builds towards the finisher.

## Paladin

Protection Paladins could tank well, but Mana made it hard to hold threat in long fights. They also lacked a taunt. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311649"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_retributionaura.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Fury</a>, learned at level 10, adds Holy damage to every melee attack. With a shield equipped, each hit also grants an absorb shield of 50% of that Holy damage.

<a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20271"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_righteousfury.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Judgement</a> under Seal of Fury deals Holy damage and taunts the target for 4 seconds. That should let Paladins step into the role of main tank, says Blizzard.

Every Paladin now learns <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26573"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_innerfire.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Consecration</a> at level 20. The first 4 enemies who enter it take extra damage. Protection can take <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310994"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_judgementred.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Swift Judgement</a>, which ends the cooldown of Judgement at once and makes the next one free. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311015"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_shieldofthetemplar.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Templar's Bulwark</a> grants a shield equal to 100% of maximum health for 8 seconds, with a cooldown of 5 minutes.

Holy gets <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310909"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_paladin_judgementofthepure.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Light's Vigil</a>, which changes the next <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20473"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_searinglight.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Shock</a> on its target. On an ally, Holy Shock heals the whole party and its cooldown resets at once. Blessings, Greater Blessings included, now last an hour. In Classic a Blessing lasted 5 minutes and a Greater Blessing 15.

Retribution gets seal twisting as a talent. With <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310735"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_blessedresillience.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Twist of Light</a>, replacing <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20154"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_thunderbolt.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Righteousness</a>, Seal of Fury, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20375"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_warrior_innerrage.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Command</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20164"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_holy_sealofwrath.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Seal of Justice</a> with another Seal leaves an echo of the old one on the next melee swing. <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=678"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/classicon_paladin.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Holy Strike</a> strikes at once and adds Holy damage.

Every talent of the three classes is in the [talent calculator](/talents/), for the [Hunter](/talents/hunter/), the [Mage](/talents/mage/) and the [Paladin](/talents/paladin/). All of this applies to the beta. Whether the values stay the same for the launch on 4 November, Blizzard does not say.
