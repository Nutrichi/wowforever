Three weeks after the launch of World of Warcraft: Forever, Hunter is the most played class, at 15.1% of all characters. The figures cover 7,258,194 characters from level 10, across all regions and rulesets. Blizzard itself spoke of 15 million created characters, but that includes everything below level 10.

## Classes

| Class | Share | Characters |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter | 15.1% | 1,093,244 |
| Paladin | 13.5% | 980,146 |
| Druid | 12.1% | 875,357 |
| Warrior | 11.3% | 819,168 |
| Mage | 10.8% | 780,374 |
| Shaman | 9.8% | 712,824 |
| Warlock | 9.6% | 699,062 |
| Priest | 9.5% | 688,478 |
| Rogue | 8.4% | 609,541 |

Most Hunters pick [Beast Mastery](/guides/beast-mastery-hunter/) (74%). Paladins mostly play [Retribution](/guides/retribution-paladin/) (63.9%) ahead of Protection (24%), and 70.3% of all Druids are [Feral](/guides/feral-druid/), with Balance and Restoration both around 14%. Warriors go Protection (56%) before Arms (25%), and Mages Frost (57%) before Arcane (28%).

## Races

Undead are the most played race at 18.7%, just ahead of Humans at 18.6%. The gap is only 7,495 characters. The new Undead Paladin is the reason: with 479,674 characters it is the most played combination, ahead of the Human Paladin.

| Race | Share | Characters |
|---|---|---|
| Undead | 18.7% | 1,353,967 |
| Human | 18.6% | 1,346,472 |
| Night Elf | 11.1% | 807,006 |
| Orc | 9.4% | 684,406 |
| Tauren | 9.0% | 653,549 |
| Dwarf | 9.0% | 651,079 |
| Troll | 6.8% | 492,602 |
| Gnome | 6.6% | 476,731 |
| Windshaper Skyborne | 5.6% | 407,998 |
| High Order Skyborne | 5.3% | 384,384 |

The Skyborne are last on both sides. They are the only race you pay for; the rest come with the subscription.

## The factions are almost equal

The Alliance has 3,665,672 characters (50.5%), the Horde 3,592,522 (49.5%). That is across all rulesets, so including the ones where Blizzard does not keep the balance. More on that in our post about [the bigger faction on PvP rulesets](/news/creating-on-the-bigger-pvp-faction-gets-harder/).
