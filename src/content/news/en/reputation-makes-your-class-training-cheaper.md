Training costs less in World of Warcraft: Forever when you have a higher reputation with the faction of the trainer. Every new rank of a spell then costs a little less. Choosing the right capital before you train saves gold for your mount.

## How much you save

At Honored the discount is 10%, the same as at the riding trainers, see [our post about the Skyborne](/news/skyborne-pay-full-price-for-riding-training/). In Classic, Friendly gave 5%, Revered 15% and Exalted 20%. Whether Forever uses the same steps, is not confirmed.

Training from level 1 to 60 costs about 206 gold for every class. At Honored, that is some 20 gold you keep. Most players reach Honored first with the capital of their own race, so that is usually the cheapest place to train.
