World of Warcraft: Forever passed 15 million characters in its first two weeks. Blizzard shared the numbers on X, and they are big: almost 1.5 billion quests done and close to 10 billion enemies defeated.

## The numbers

| | First two weeks |
|---|---|
| Characters created | 15,160,147 |
| Quests completed | 1,480,343,357 |
| Enemies defeated | 9,786,925,806 |
| Player deaths | 196,851,761 |
| Parties formed | 71,309,889 |
| Camping items used | 28,696,386 |
| PvP kills | 28,938,569 |
| Guilds created | 140,764 |

The deadliest boss so far is Archmage Arugal in Shadowfang Keep, see our [Shadowfang Keep guide](/guides/dungeons/shadowfang-keep/).

## More players than in 15 years

Holly Longdale added on X that more people play Forever each week than WoW has seen in 15 years.
