The soundtrack of World of Warcraft: Forever comes out on 13 October. The album holds 19 new tracks for the new zones and dungeons. It appears on Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer and other streaming services, three weeks before the launch on 4 November.

Composer Adam Burgess shared the date on X on 5 October. Burgess worked at Blizzard before and wrote or co-wrote 10 of the 19 tracks. The album could already be pre-saved.

## The tracks

The preview of the tracklist names five composers: Adam Burgess, Brendon Williams, David Arkenstone, Neal Acree and Jason Hayes. Most titles carry the name of a new zone or place in Forever, such as Zephras Isle and Skyborne.
