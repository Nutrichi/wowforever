Warcraft Tavern has walked the Hall of Thanes, the new Alliance dungeon under Ironforge, room by room. Its route points out kegs that blow up Dark Iron packs, a disease worth curing and a totem that takes the fear out of the final boss.

## The way in

Face the High Seat from the Great Forge. Inside King Magni's hall, a cobwebbed passage opens on the left, at /way 43.5 52.0. It leads down over lava to the portal, and a fall is lethal.

![The passage on the left inside the High Seat, the way down to Old Ironforge, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-passage-to-old-ironforge.jpg)

## What the route teaches

- **Shoot the kegs.** Kegs of Seaforium Blasting Powder stand next to several Dark Iron packs. A ranged player shoots them and the pack takes the blast. Before the pack of four after the cave, shoot both kegs at the same time.
- **Cure the Runts.** Bloodhound Runts leave Infected Wounds for 5 minutes, which raises physical damage taken. A Simple Poultice from First Aid or the Dwarf racial <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20594"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_shadow_unholystrength.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Stoneform</a> removes it.
- **Drop a totem.** Durgen Dirgehammer fears the group into the rest of the room. A Shaman's <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8143"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_tremortotem.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Tremor Totem</a> stops that.
- **Faldrim comes alone.** The first boss does not pull the ghosts around him along.

![A group in a dark cavern next to kegs of Seaforium Blasting Powder, with Dark Iron Dwarves further on, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-29-wow-forever-hall-of-thanes-seaforium-kegs.jpg)

The full route, with every quest, boss and reward, is in the [Hall of Thanes guide](/guides/dungeons/hall-of-thanes/).

## What is not known

Whether the kegs come back after a wipe, the guide does not say. Nor is it known whether the Horde gets a shorter way to Ironforge before the launch on 4 November.
