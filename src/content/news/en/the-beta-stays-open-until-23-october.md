The beta of World of Warcraft: Forever gets two extra days. Blizzard now closes it on Friday 23 October at 10:00 Pacific time, which is 19:00 Belgian time. Until now, 21 October was the last full day. The launch stays on 4 November.

Community manager Kaivax announced the new date on the official forums, with a small pun: "We plan to close the WoW Forever Beta … forever at 10:00 a.m. PDT on Friday, October 23."

## First the stress test, then the beta again

Tonight, 21 October, Blizzard takes the beta offline for the [stress test](/news/the-stress-test-moves-to-21-october/) on the regional hardware that Forever will run on after launch. In Europe it runs from 19:00 to 03:00 Belgian time, and every player with a World of Warcraft account can join. When it ends, all stress test characters are deleted, names included, and the beta opens again.

## Two more days at level 30

Testers keep playing with the level cap of 30 until Friday evening. Blizzard has not said whether a new beta build comes in those two days.
