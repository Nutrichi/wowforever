Beasts in Elwynn Forest walk around with the world buff Might of Stormwind in World of Warcraft: Forever. The buff adds 300 health, more than double the 220 of a Young Forest Bear. Why it happens, is not confirmed.

## Probably runaway pets

All reports are about beasts. Some Hunters say they and their pet got the buff the normal way. They then did not feed the pet enough, and it left them and ran off into the forest, buff and all. A pet that is not fed loses loyalty and can leave its Hunter.

Besides the health, the buff gives 15% haste on melee attacks, so these monsters also hit faster. In Forever, world buffs no longer only drop in Stormwind and Orgrimmar, but also in towns such as Goldshire, Razor Hill, Lakeshire and Sentinel Hill.

## Friendly NPCs too

It also works the other way. A player on Reddit wrote that the escort quest The Defias Brotherhood in Westfall went much easier because The Defias Traitor had the buff. The buff itself and how to get it are in the [post about Might of Stormwind](/news/alliance-gets-the-might-of-stormwind-world-buff/).
