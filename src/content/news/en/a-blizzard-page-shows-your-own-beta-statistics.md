Blizzard has opened a page with personal statistics from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever. Testers see how often they jumped, how many quests they completed and which monster killed them most. The page is [timewalking.blizzard.com](https://timewalking.blizzard.com/).

Every number comes with a percentile, which shows how a tester compares with the others. The beta closed on 23 October at 19:00 Belgian time; the page appeared on the same day.

![A stats card from the beta with the WoW Forever logo: 9,658 jumps, 11 camping companions, 136 quests completed and 120,170 yards traveled, each with a percentile, an official image of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-beta-stats-card.jpg)

## Fourteen statistics

The page counts jumps, camping companions, quests completed and yards traveled. It also shows hours played, characters played, the most played class and zone, the nemesis, levels gained, social countries, gathering actions, items crafted and total chests looted.

## An image to share

At the bottom of the page, testers build their own image. They pick up to four statistics, choose whether their BattleTag is shown, and save the image or share it. The image carries the line "Adventure is Forever, launching 11/4".

![The Customize Your Stats tool with four chosen stats, buttons for the other stats, Show BattleTag, Save Image and Share, an official image of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-10-23-wow-forever-beta-stats-builder.jpg)

How long the page stays online, Blizzard does not say.
