Blizzard added three issues to the list of known problems on the WoW Forever beta on 8 October. The new textures of the Skyborne Druid's Cat Form follow later. Hunter pets and profession icons show display errors.

Community manager Kaivax posted the additions in the [known issues thread](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-known-issues-8-october/629369/7) on the Blizzard forums, alongside the build that [opened the City of Dalaran](/news/city-of-dalaran-opens-in-the-fourth-beta-week/).

## The Skyborne Druid forms

Bear Form and Travel Form of the Skyborne Druid already carry their updated textures in the new build. Cat Form follows in a later build, together with several creatures on Zephras Isle and some mounts, pets and other creatures. Which build that is, Blizzard does not say.

Blizzard showed the updated Druid forms on 8 October, in [a post about the Skyborne models](/news/the-skyborne-get-sd-models-in-early-2027/).

## Two display errors

Hunters who picked the Ferocity talent do not see the higher critical strike chance of their pet on the character sheet. Blizzard calls it a visual bug only: the bonus itself is not affected.

Some icons in the Professions window look broken.

The issues from earlier weeks remain on the same list. The previous update came with the build of 24 September, when [ten new known issues arrived](/news/ten-known-issues-arrive-with-the-new-beta-build/).
