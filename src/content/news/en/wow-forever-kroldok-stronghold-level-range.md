Krol'dok Stronghold is a dungeon for levels 40 to 45 in World of Warcraft: Forever. Blizzard corrected the level range after BlizzCon 2026, where several places listed it wrongly. With that correction came the brackets of all nine new dungeons.

| Dungeon | Levels |
|---|---|
| The Hall of Thanes | 13 to 18 |
| Ruins of Lordaeron | 15 to 20 |
| Excavation Site: Wetlands | 24 to 29 |
| City of Dalaran | 28 to 33 |
| The Drowned City | 35 to 40 |
| Krol'dok Stronghold | 40 to 45 |
| Alcaz Prison | 48 to 53 |
| Blackmaw Hold | 55 to 60 |
| Shaper's Terrace | 58 to 60 |

The list covers the dungeons that are there at launch, writes Wowhead. Whether more follow after launch, Blizzard does not say.
