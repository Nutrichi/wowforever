The lockbox change in World of Warcraft: Forever is not here to stay. Blizzard confirms that the hotfix which stopped Rogues from [peeking inside lockboxes](/news/rogues-can-no-longer-peek-inside-lockboxes/) was a temporary measure.

## What Blizzard says

The change went live on 22 November at 9.55 PST, which is 18.55 in Belgium. Blizzard is now working on a proper fix. It should bring back peeking and partial looting of lockboxes, and make them work as intended otherwise.

How long that takes is not known yet. Blizzard will post about it once it is done.

## Why it was needed

Blizzard does not give a reason, but the timing points to the exploit players found last weekend: a peek could reroll what was inside a box. The quick fix closed that, with a side effect. Junkboxes can no longer be partly looted and then handed in.

Until the real fix arrives, everything still goes straight into your bags when you open a box. More about the skill in the [Lockpicking guide](/classes/rogue/lockpicking/).
