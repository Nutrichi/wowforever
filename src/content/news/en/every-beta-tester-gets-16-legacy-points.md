Every tester in the World of Warcraft: Forever beta will get 16 Legacy points for free. Blizzard wants the system tested without players first levelling several classes. The grant comes in a later week of the beta, the exact week is not known.

Community manager Kaivax announced it on 1 October on the forums. Over the first two weeks of the beta, more and more testers chased Legacy points, he writes:

> Over the first two weeks of the WoW Forever Beta, we've seen an increasing number of testers pushing to earn points in the Legacy system.
>
> In a future week of the Beta, we're planning to grant all testers 16 Legacy points so that the system can be tested without first requiring a great deal of leveling different classes, clearing dungeons, etc.
>
> Once applied, we expect to keep that grant in place until the end of the Beta.

## What 16 points buy

[Legacy points](/news/legacy-points-one-per-challenge/) come from challenges: a first level 25 on a class, a skill milestone in a tradeskill, a first clear of a dungeon. Each challenge is worth one point. With 16 points a tester can try several nodes in the Legacy tree right away.

Which nodes fit within 16 points can be checked in the [Legacy calculator](/talents/legacy/). The grant only applies to the beta. Whether characters at launch start with any points, Blizzard does not say.
