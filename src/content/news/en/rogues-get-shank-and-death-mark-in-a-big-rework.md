The Rogue changes the most in the development notes Blizzard posted on 2 November. Every Rogue gets Shank, a cheap off-hand strike that gives Energy back. Subtlety ends in Death Mark, a finisher that stores your damage and releases it later. And a new passive, Poised, makes the first hit after a rest cheaper, for Rogues and for Druids.

## Two new abilities

| Ability | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Shank** | 24 | 5 Energy, 10 second cooldown. Strikes with your off-hand weapon for 50% weapon damage and gives 15 Energy back. It cannot miss. Rank 2 at level 44 gives 25 Energy |
| **Poised** <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1328825"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/ability_rogue_fleetfooted.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Poised</a> | 18 | Out of combat with full Energy you gain Poised: your next Energy ability costs 10 less |

Druids learn Poised at level 32, for their Cat Form. Deadly Poison and Instant Poison now scale twice as hard with attack power.

## Assassination

| Talent | Change |
|---|---|
| **Blood Splatter** | New, 1 point in row 3: Rupture lasts 6 seconds longer, Garrote 9 seconds |
| **Relentless Strikes** | Now 5 points in row 5: 4 to 20% chance per combo point to restore 25 Energy |
| **Venom** | Now a passive in row 5: Shank raises your poison damage by 30% and your chance to apply poisons by 20% for 6 seconds |
| **Seal Fate** | Now 3 points: 33, 66 or 100% chance at an extra combo point on a critical hit |
| **Lethality** | Now 2 points for 10 or 20% more critical strike damage |
| **Mutilate** | Moves up to row 7, deals 90% weapon damage (was 75%) |
| **Murder** | Removed |

## Combat

| Talent | Change |
|---|---|
| **Composure** | New, 2 points in row 3: Poised takes 5 or 10 more Energy off |
| **Flash of Steel** | New, 5 points in row 6: Shank gives up to 15 more Energy and its cooldown drops by up to 2 seconds |
| **Coup de Grace** | The new name of Improved Eviscerate: up to 20% more damage for Eviscerate and for Death Mark |
| **Efficient Strikes** | The new name of Improved Sinister Strike: also makes Hemorrhage cheaper |
| **Aggression** | Up to 15% more damage for Sinister Strike and Eviscerate, no longer Backstab |
| **Dual Wield Specialization** | Now 2 points for 25 or 50% more off-hand damage |
| **Flawless Execution** | Takes 15 Energy off (was 10) |

## Subtlety

| Talent | Change |
|---|---|
| **Death Mark** | New, 1 point in row 7. A finisher and curse, 25 Energy, 20 yards, 10 second cooldown. It stores part of your damage per combo point, Garrote and Rupture at twice the rate, and deals it after 6 seconds |
| **Boot Knife** | New, 5 points in row 6: up to 100% chance that Shank adds 2 combo points |
| **Quick Getaway** | New, 2 points in row 4: one extra Sap target, and up to 30% movement speed for 3 seconds after a Sap |
| **Ghostly Strike** | Your next Ambush or Garrote within 7 seconds needs no Stealth |
| **Opportunity** | Now 5 points: up to 20% more damage for Backstab, Garrote, Ambush and Mutilate |
| **Hemorrhage** | Now raises Rupture and Garrote damage by 15% |
| **Setup** | Lower chance: 15, 30 or 45% |
| **Camouflage** | Now 3 points: up to 15% speed in Stealth and 6 seconds off its cooldown |
| **Quietus, Cutthroat, Thousand Cuts, Improved Distract** | Removed |

Blizzard also fixed Hemorrhage and Ghostly Strike, which hit much too hard with a dagger.
