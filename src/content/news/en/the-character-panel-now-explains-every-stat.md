The character panel of World of Warcraft: Forever tells a player what every stat does. Hovering over a line gives a full description instead of a vague one, writes Wowhead after the beta. The numbers that matter for raids and for tanking stand in there too.

## What the old panel did not say

Every version of WoW before Cataclysm described its stats vaguely, writes Wowhead, and left the rest to the player. Forever puts the explanation in the panel itself, aimed at players who are new to it.

![The tooltip of Attack Power in the beta: 216, which raises melee weapon damage per second by 15.4](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-21-wow-forever-tooltip-attack-power.png)

The tooltip for Attack Power shows how plain that gets. It names the number, what it raises, that Strength is what feeds it, and the conversion: every 14 Attack Power adds 1 damage per second.

## One Crit line for attacks and spells

Hit and Crit apply to every school in Forever and are no longer split into melee, spell and ranged, writes Wowhead. The panel shows that as a single line.

![The tooltip of Critical Strike in the beta, with the melee and spell chances and what a critical strike does](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-21-wow-forever-tooltip-critical-strike.jpg)

One stat does not mean one number. In the tooltip, 6.1% Critical Strike works out as 6.08% for melee and 6.04% for spells, and the line underneath says Agility feeds the attack side while Intellect feeds the spell side.

The tooltip also answers the questions players keep asking, writes Wowhead. A melee critical strike deals 100% more damage, a spell or healing critical strike is 50% more effective, and most periodic effects can crit.

## How much Hit is enough

The Hit tooltip does the arithmetic a player would otherwise look up.

![The tooltip of Hit Chance in the beta, with the amounts needed to never miss a raid boss or a level 19 target](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-21-wow-forever-tooltip-hit-chance.jpg)

At 3.0% Hit Chance it lists what never missing takes: 8.00% melee and 17.00% spell against raid bosses, and 5.00% melee and 4.00% spell against a level 19 target or player. The level in that second line follows the character, so the figure moves along with them.

## The numbers a tank needs

Defense gets the same treatment, with the two thresholds that decide whether a tank can be hit for a spike.

![The tooltip of Defense in the beta, with crushing blows and the Defense needed against raid bosses](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-21-wow-forever-tooltip-defense.jpg)

Each point raises dodge, block and parry by 0.08% and lowers the chance to be hit or critically hit by the same amount. A creature crits for 200% damage, and one three or more levels above the player can land a crushing blow for 150%.

At 440 Defense a character can no longer be critically hit by raid bosses, worth 5.60% off their critical strike chance. Those two numbers are what players call the defense cap and the avoidance cap, writes Wowhead, which stresses that Defense keeps helping past that point.
