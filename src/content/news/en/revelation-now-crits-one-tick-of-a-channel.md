The Revelation enchant lost its best trick in World of Warcraft: Forever. Blizzard quietly nerfed it with a hotfix, and casters who farm dungeons with AoE spells feel it most.

## What Revelation does

<a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=249481"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_note_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Formula: Enchant Weapon - Revelation</a> is one of the new weapon enchants in Forever. You buy the formula with reputation tokens from the Azeroth Commerce Authority or Durotar Supply and Logistics. Once on your weapon, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1248805"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_enchant_formulagood_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Enchant Weapon - Revelation</a> can trigger on a spell that does not crit. Your next spell that is not a damage over time effect then crits for sure.

## The trick that is gone

Channeled spells used that proc in a special way. The guaranteed crit counted for the whole channel, so every tick of <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10187"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_frost_icestorm.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Blizzard</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11684"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_fire_incinerate.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hellfire</a> or <a class="wf-wh wf-spell" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17402"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/spell_nature_cyclone.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Hurricane</a> was a critical strike. Mages, Warlocks and Druids used it for huge AoE damage, for example in solo dungeon farms.

Since the hotfix, the proc is used up on the first tick of a channeled AoE spell. Only that tick crits, the rest hits as normal.

## What it means for you

For single target damage nothing changes. For AoE, Revelation is now a lot weaker, and solo farms take a bit longer. Many players expected this nerf: an AoE spell that always crits did not look intended.

Our class guides still list Revelation as an enchant option for casters. For AoE farming, compare it again with your other enchants.
