The Gnomeregan Exiles are the gnomes who fled their city Gnomeregan in Dun Morogh after the troggs overran it. Most of them now live in Tinker Town in Ironforge, with their leader High Tinker Mekkatorque. The reputation is for the Alliance only.

## Where you start

Where you start depends on your race. These are the values in the faction data of the Classic Era client, which the database wago.tools reads out of build 1.15.9.

| Your race | Standing at the start |
|---|---|
| Gnome | Friendly, 1,000 into the standing |
| Human, Dwarf, Night Elf | Friendly, 100 into the standing |
| Orc, Forsaken, Tauren, Troll | Hated, at the very bottom |

Friendly holds 6,000 reputation, so a character of the city's own race begins 900 ahead of the rest of its side.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Friendly |
|---|---|---|
| Friendly | 6,000 | 0 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 6,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 18,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 39,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

### Quests

Quests for the Gnomeregan Exiles give the most reputation. Wowhead points to Dun Morogh, Loch Modan, the Stonetalon Mountains, Thousand Needles, Tanaris and the Blasted Lands. Most quests for one Alliance city also give a quarter of that reputation to the other Alliance cities.

### Cloth donations

Bubulo Acerbus, the Alliance Cloth Quartermaster in Tinker Town in Ironforge, takes cloth. Every quest opens the next one, only for the Gnomeregan Exiles; Additional Runecloth opens after the four donations.

| Quest | Level | Asks | Reputation | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Donation of Wool | 12 | 60 Wool Cloth | 150 | 650 |
| A Donation of Silk | 26 | 60 Silk Cloth | 150 | 1,650 |
| A Donation of Mageweave | 40 | 60 Mageweave Cloth | 150 | 3,300 |
| A Donation of Runecloth | 50 | 60 Runecloth | 150 | 6,600 |
| Additional Runecloth, repeatable | 50 | 20 Runecloth | 50 | 0 |

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Do the quests in the zones above; the reputation also spills over to the other Alliance cities.
2. Turn in the four cloth donations as soon as your level allows: 600 reputation.
3. Then repeat Additional Runecloth. Revered to Exalted alone takes 420 turn-ins, or 8,400 Runecloth.

## Rewards

- **From Honored:** a 10 percent discount at Gnomeregan Exiles vendors, on repairs, training and flights.
- **At Exalted:** characters of the other Alliance races can buy mechanostriders from Milli Featherwhistle near Kharanos in Dun Morogh.
