The Silverwing Sentinels are night elf soldiers who want to drive the Warsong Outriders out of Ashenvale. Their battleground is Warsong Gulch. The reputation is for the Alliance only; the Horde earns the [Warsong Outriders](/reputations/warsong-outriders/) in the same battleground. Every character starts at Neutral.

## Where the Silverwing Sentinels are

At **Silverwing Grove** in the southwest of Ashenvale, near Fallen Sky Lake. The grove has an entrance to Warsong Gulch, the quest givers and the quartermaster Illiyana Moonblaze. There is no flight path; the closest one is Astranaar.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

| Source | Reputation |
|---|---|
| A flag capture | 35 |
| A flag capture on a Warsong Gulch weekend | 45 |
| 3 Warsong Gulch Marks of Honor turned in | 50 |
| Concerted Efforts, the first time | 150 |
| Concerted Efforts, every time after | 100 |

A win takes 3 flag captures, so it gives 105 reputation, or 135 on a Warsong Gulch weekend. A win also gives 3 Marks of Honor, a loss 1. Concerted Efforts asks for 3 Marks of Honor from Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley, and also gives reputation with the League of Arathor and the Stormpike Guard.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Play on Warsong Gulch weekends: 135 reputation per win instead of 105.
2. Turn in Marks of Honor in threes; with a win's own marks, a win is worth 155 reputation.
3. Keep Marks of Honor from all three battlegrounds for Concerted Efforts.
4. Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation: about 400 wins on a normal week, or about 310 on weekends.
