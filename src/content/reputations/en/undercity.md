Undercity is the faction of the Forsaken capital under the ruins of Lordaeron in Tirisfal Glades, ruled by Sylvanas Windrunner. You reach the city through the ruined throne room and one of three elevators. The reputation is for the Horde only.

## Where you start

Where you start depends on your race. These are the values in the faction data of the Classic Era client, which the database wago.tools reads out of build 1.15.9.

| Your race | Standing at the start |
|---|---|
| Forsaken | Friendly, 1,000 into the standing |
| Orc, Tauren, Troll | Neutral, 500 into the standing |
| Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome | Hated, at the very bottom |

Orcs, Tauren and Trolls begin at Neutral here, a standing lower than the Forsaken themselves, and it runs both ways: the Forsaken begin at Neutral with Orgrimmar, Thunder Bluff and the Darkspear Trolls.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Friendly |
|---|---|---|
| Friendly | 6,000 | 0 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 6,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 18,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 39,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

### Quests

Quests for Undercity give the most reputation. Wowhead points to Tirisfal Glades, Silverpine Forest, Hillsbrad Foothills, the Arathi Highlands, The Hinterlands and the Western Plaguelands. Most quests for one Horde city also give a quarter of that reputation to the other Horde cities.

### Cloth donations

Ralston Farnsley, the Horde Cloth Quartermaster in the Magic Quarter of Undercity, takes cloth. Every quest opens the next one, only for Undercity; Additional Runecloth opens after the four donations.

| Quest | Level | Asks | Reputation | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Donation of Wool | 12 | 60 Wool Cloth | 150 | 650 |
| A Donation of Silk | 26 | 60 Silk Cloth | 150 | 1,650 |
| A Donation of Mageweave | 40 | 60 Mageweave Cloth | 150 | 3,300 |
| A Donation of Runecloth | 50 | 60 Runecloth | 150 | 6,600 |
| Additional Runecloth, repeatable | 50 | 20 Runecloth | 50 | 0 |

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Do the quests in the zones above; the reputation also spills over to the other Horde cities.
2. Turn in the four cloth donations as soon as your level allows: 600 reputation.
3. Then repeat Additional Runecloth. Revered to Exalted alone takes 420 turn-ins, or 8,400 Runecloth.

## Rewards

- **From Honored:** a 10 percent discount at Undercity vendors, on repairs, training and flights.
- **At Exalted:** characters of the other Horde races can buy skeletal horses from Zachariah Post in Brill, Tirisfal Glades.
