Ravenholdt, also called the Assassin's League, is a guild of thieves and assassins that only accepts the very best. It is the sworn enemy of the Syndicate, and most of its quests are for Rogues. Alliance and Horde characters of every class start at Neutral.

## Where Ravenholdt is

At **Ravenholdt Manor** in the Alterac Mountains. The way up starts in the northeast corner of Hillsbrad Foothills.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

| Source | Reputation | Works up to |
|---|---|---|
| A Syndicate humanoid killed | 5 | The end of Honored |
| Syndicate Emblems, repeatable | For every turn-in | Friendly |
| Junkboxes Needed: 5 Heavy Junkbox, repeatable | For every turn-in | Exalted |

- **Syndicate kills:** the Syndicate is in the Alterac Mountains, Hillsbrad Foothills and the Arathi Highlands.
- **Syndicate Emblems:** Rogues pickpocket the emblems from Syndicate humanoids.
- **Junkboxes Needed:** every class can do it, but only a Rogue can pickpocket Heavy Junkboxes. From Revered on it is the only way forward.
- Rogues also have a few normal quests.

Killing Ravenholdt NPCs works the other way: you lose Ravenholdt reputation and gain a little with the Syndicate.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Kill Syndicate humanoids until the end of Honored: from the start of Neutral that is about 4,200 kills.
2. As a Rogue, pickpocket Syndicate Emblems and Heavy Junkboxes on the way.
3. From Revered on, only Junkboxes Needed counts, so keep every Heavy Junkbox.

## Rewards

There are no faction rewards. The guards of Ravenholdt only speak to you with more respect.
