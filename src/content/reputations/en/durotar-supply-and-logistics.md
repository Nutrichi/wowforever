Durotar Supply and Logistics is one of the first two factions that belong to WoW Forever itself. Its own description reads: the Horde's robust supply network fuels its rapid expansion across Kalimdor and beyond. It was added in patch 1.60.1, the build the beta runs on, and its counterpart on the Alliance side is the [Azeroth Commerce Authority](/reputations/azeroth-commerce-authority/).

## Why this reputation is worth it

- **A toy at Honored.** The Advertising License Application starts a quest for the Orcish Tradeskill Sign, a toy that advertises what you craft.
- **The tabard at Exalted.** The Durotar Supply and Logistics Tabard asks for Exalted.
- **It rewards what you already do.** The reputation comes from handing in crates, so the tradeskills you level anyway feed it.

## Which side it is for

Wowhead lists no faction flag on the database page, but every quartermaster stands in a Horde capital and the description speaks of the Horde's supply network. Treat this one as the Horde faction and the Azeroth Commerce Authority as the Alliance faction.

## The standings

The scale is the standard one, so from Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation.

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the bottom of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 30,000 |
| Exalted | | 42,000 |

Below Neutral the scale runs the usual way as well: Hated is 36,000 wide, Hostile and Unfriendly 3,000 each.

## Where the reputation comes from

**Turning in crates.** The Legacy perk Performance Bonus in the Professions tree says it plainly: you have a 5, 10 or 15 percent chance, depending on the rank, to receive 100 percent increased **Merchant's Favor** when you turn in a crate to the Azeroth Commerce Authority or Durotar Supply and Logistics. Merchant's Favor is a currency of its own in the beta, filed under Professions and Tradeskills.

You can see the perk and its three ranks in the [Legacy calculator](/talents/legacy/).

## Where to turn in

Three quartermasters, one in each Horde capital.

| NPC | Where |
|---|---|
| Jornah | Orgrimmar |
| Dokimi | Thunder Bluff |
| Gishah | Undercity |

## What is not known yet

The beta shows the faction, its rewards and its quartermasters, but not the system around it. Still open:

- **Where a new character starts.** The database gives no starting standing, so Neutral is the assumption here and not a confirmed value.
- **How much a crate gives** and whether there is a daily or weekly limit.
- **Where the crates come from:** which tradeskill makes them, what they cost and how many you can carry.
- **What else Merchant's Favor buys** besides reputation.

Players recognise the two names from Season of Discovery, where supply crates worked in a comparable way. Blizzard has not said that the system carries over unchanged, so this guide sticks to what the beta client and the database actually show. It is updated as soon as the beta shows more.
