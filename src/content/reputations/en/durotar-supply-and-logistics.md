Durotar Supply and Logistics is the Horde supply faction of WoW Forever. Its own description reads: the Horde's robust supply network fuels its rapid expansion across Kalimdor and beyond. You loot crates in the world, fill them and hand them in at its camp for Merchant's Favor. The Alliance counterpart is the [Azeroth Commerce Authority](/reputations/azeroth-commerce-authority/).

## Why this reputation is worth it

- **Recipes for seven professions**, bought with Merchant's Favor.
- **The Pack Kodo** mount, and the [Minimule](/guides/pets/minimule/) pet.
- **A title for every profession at 300**, and the Orcish Tradeskill Sign toy at Honored.

## The standings

The scale is the standard one, so from Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation.

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the bottom of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 30,000 |
| Exalted | | 42,000 |

Below Neutral the scale runs the usual way as well: Hated is 36,000 wide, Hostile and Unfriendly 3,000 each.

## Where the reputation comes from

**Craftsman's Writs give the reputation.** A writ is an item that starts a daily quest: you bring a crafted item to an NPC somewhere in Azeroth. A better item gives more reputation. Writs can be traded on the Auction House, so you can pick the ones you can make.

**Crates give Merchant's Favor.** A Waylaid Supply Crate is a random drop in dungeons and in the open world, matching your level and the zone. You fill it with one of the bundles listed on the crate and hand it in to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256386">Dokimi</a>. The Legacy perk Performance Bonus gives a 5, 10 or 15 percent chance on double Merchant's Favor per crate; see the [Legacy calculator](/talents/legacy/).

| Crate | Quality | Merchant's Favor |
|---|---|---|
| Sealed Apprentice Crate | Common (white) | 5 |
| Sealed Apprentice Crate | Uncommon (green) | 10 |
| Sealed Journeyman Crate | Common (white) | 10 |
| Sealed Journeyman Crate | Uncommon (green) | 20 |
