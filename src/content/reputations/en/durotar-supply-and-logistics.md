Durotar Supply and Logistics is the Horde supply faction of WoW Forever. Its own description reads: the Horde's robust supply network fuels its rapid expansion across Kalimdor and beyond. You loot crates in the world, fill them and hand them in at its camp for Merchant's Favor. The Alliance counterpart is the [Azeroth Commerce Authority](/reputations/azeroth-commerce-authority/).

## Why this reputation is worth it

- **A toy at Honored.** The Advertising License Application starts a quest for the Orcish Tradeskill Sign, a toy that advertises what you craft.
- **The tabard at Exalted.** The <a class="wf-wh wf-q1" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=262764"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_tabard_merchanthordec60.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Durotar Supply and Logistics Tabard</a> asks for Exalted.
- **It rewards what you already do.** The reputation comes from handing in crates, so the tradeskills you level anyway feed it.

## The camp outside the Crossroads

The camp stands just outside the Crossroads in The Barrens. The crates go to <a class="wf-wh wf-plain" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=256386">Dokimi</a>.

These are the positions from the NPC pages of Wowhead; its reputation guide gives /way 42 34 for the camp. The Alliance camp at Three Corners has vendors with recipes for seven tradeskills, a mount and titles. Whether the Horde camp sells the same, the guides do not show yet.

## The standings

The scale is the standard one, so from Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation.

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the bottom of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 30,000 |
| Exalted | | 42,000 |

Below Neutral the scale runs the usual way as well: Hated is 36,000 wide, Hostile and Unfriendly 3,000 each.

## Where the reputation comes from

**Turning in crates.** The Legacy perk Performance Bonus in the Professions tree gives a 5, 10 or 15 percent chance, depending on the rank, on 100 percent more **Merchant's Favor** when you turn in a crate. Merchant's Favor is a currency of its own in the beta, filed under Professions and Tradeskills.

You can see the perk and its three ranks in the [Legacy calculator](/talents/legacy/).
