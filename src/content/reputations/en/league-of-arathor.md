The League of Arathor fights the Forsaken of The Defilers for the resources of Arathi Basin. Their camp is Refuge Pointe in the Arathi Highlands. The reputation is for the Alliance only; the Horde earns [The Defilers](/reputations/the-defilers/) in the same battleground. Every character starts at Neutral.

## Where the League of Arathor is

At **Refuge Pointe** in the Arathi Highlands, around coordinates 46, 45, with a flight path, the quest givers and the quartermaster Samuel Hawke.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

### Arathi Basin

Your team earns reputation for every step of resources it gathers. The Warcraft wiki counts 16 reputation for every 260 resources, or 96 reputation for a won battle. On Arathi Basin holiday weekends the step drops to 200 resources, for 128 reputation per win.

A win also gives 3 Marks of Honor for Arathi Basin, a loss 1. The quest Concerted Efforts takes 3 Marks of Honor from each battleground for a larger reputation reward.

### Quests

Field Marshal Oslight at Refuge Pointe has three one-time quests that give reputation.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Play Arathi Basin on holiday weekends: 128 reputation per win instead of 96.
2. Hold as many bases as you can; more resources mean more reputation, even in a battle you lose.
3. Do the quests of Field Marshal Oslight once.
4. Keep Marks of Honor from every battleground for Concerted Efforts.

## Rewards per standing

Samuel Hawke at Refuge Pointe sells:

- **Friendly:** Talisman of Arathor, a trinket, plus potions, bandages and field rations.
- **Honored:** a belt for every armor type.
- **Revered:** boots for every armor type.
- **Exalted:** Ironbark Staff and Sageclaw, shoulders for every armor type, and the Cloak of the Honor Guard.
