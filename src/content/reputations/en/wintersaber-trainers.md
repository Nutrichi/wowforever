The Wintersaber Trainers are two night elves on Frostsaber Rock in Winterspring who raise the white frostsabers of the north. The reputation is for the Alliance only and starts at Neutral. It has one reward, the Reins of the Winterspring Frostsaber, and one road there: the same few quests, many times over.

## Where the Wintersaber Trainers are

Rivern Frostwind stands on top of **Frostsaber Rock** in Winterspring. The first two quests open at level 58, the third at level 60.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

| Quest | Reputation per turn-in |
|---|---|
| Frostsaber Provisions | 50 |
| Winterfall Intrusion | 50 |
| Rampaging Giants | 75 |

Frostsaber Provisions is there from the start. Winterfall Intrusion and Rampaging Giants open later, and Rampaging Giants is a group quest. Humans get 10% more reputation from their racial Diplomacy.

Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation: 840 turn-ins at 50, or 560 at 75.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Start Frostsaber Provisions as soon as your level allows it.
2. Add Winterfall Intrusion when it opens, and do both in the same trip.
3. Do Rampaging Giants whenever you have a group: it gives half again as much as the other two.
4. Plan for a long grind, and spread it over many short sessions.

## Rewards per standing

- **Exalted:** Reins of the Winterspring Frostsaber, an epic mount. Sources disagree on its price.
