The Zandalar Tribe are trolls who came to Stranglethorn Vale to stop the Blood God Hakkar in Zul'Gurub. Their camp is on Yojamba Isle, off the northwest coast of Stranglethorn Vale. Every character starts at Neutral, and the reputation is open to the Alliance and the Horde alike. It comes almost entirely from Zul'Gurub, the raid for 20 players.

## Where the Zandalar Tribe is

On **Yojamba Isle**, west of Zul'Gurub. Exzhal gives the quests, Vinchaxa takes coins and bijous, and Rin'wosho the Trader sells the rewards.

## Where the reputation comes from

### Kills in Zul'Gurub

| Kill | Reputation |
|---|---|
| Normal trash | 5 |
| Speakers among the trash | 15 |
| The boss of the Edge of Madness (Gri'lek, Hazza'rah, Renataki or Wushoolay) | 25 |
| The High Priests and High Priestesses, and Gahz'ranka | 100 |
| Bloodlord Mandokir and Jin'do the Hexxer | 200 |
| Hakkar | 400 |

Icy Veins counts about 2,400 to 3,000 reputation for a full clear. Zul'Gurub resets every three days.

### Coins

Zul'Gurub drops coins of the troll tribes. Vinchaxa takes them in sets of three, in repeatable quests, for reputation and a **Zandalar Honor Token**:

- Gurubashi, Vilebranch and Witherbark Coins
- Zulian, Razzashi and Hakkari Coins
- Sandfury, Skullsplitter and Bloodscalp Coins

### Bijous

Hakkari Bijous also drop in Zul'Gurub. The first quest, A Bijou for Zanza, gives 150 reputation and a Zandalar Honor Token. After that, every bijou used at the Altar of Zanza gives 75 reputation and a token.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Run Zul'Gurub every reset, with the boss of the Edge of Madness when your group can summon it.
2. Keep every coin and bijou, and turn them in with Vinchaxa after each run.
3. A coin set needs three different coins: trade the ones you have double with your raid.
4. Save the Zandalar Honor Tokens for the rewards at Revered and Exalted.

## Rewards per standing

### Head and leg enchants

From Friendly, every class can earn its own enchant for head and legs through a quest on Yojamba Isle, with materials from Zul'Gurub: Animist's Caress (Druid), Falcon's Call (Hunter), Presence of Sight (Mage), Syncretist's Sigil (Paladin), Prophetic Aura (Priest), Death's Embrace (Rogue), Vodouisant's Vigilant Embrace (Shaman), Hoodoo Hex (Warlock) and Presence of Might (Warrior).

### Class sets

Every class has its own set with loot from Zul'Gurub. The pieces are handed in on Yojamba Isle, and the better pieces ask for a higher standing, up to Exalted.
