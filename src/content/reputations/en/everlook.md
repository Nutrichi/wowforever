Everlook is a trading post of the goblins of the Steamwheedle Cartel, at the crossroads of the main trade routes of Winterspring. It belongs to the cartel together with [Booty Bay](/reputations/booty-bay/), [Gadgetzan](/reputations/gadgetzan/) and [Ratchet](/reputations/ratchet/). The reputation is open to Alliance and Horde. Every character starts at Neutral.

## Where Everlook is

In Winterspring, where the trade routes of the zone meet. The quests of Everlook deal with the Timbermaw furbolgs and the dangerous wildlife around the town.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

### Quests

Most reputation with Everlook comes from quests in Winterspring.

### The whole cartel at once

Three repeatable quests raise all four Steamwheedle towns at once, without costing reputation with the Bloodsail Buccaneers: Free Knot! and The Gordok Ogre Suit in Dire Maul, and Again With the Zapped Giants in Feralas.

### Losing reputation

When you lose reputation with Booty Bay by killing its citizens, the other goblin towns, Everlook among them, lose half of that. See [Bloodsail Buccaneers](/reputations/bloodsail-buccaneers/).

### Back from Hated

If you are Hated with Everlook, the repeatable quest Making Amends brings you back to Neutral.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Do every quest in Winterspring.
2. Repeat Free Knot!, The Gordok Ogre Suit and Again With the Zapped Giants; they count for all four towns.
3. Do not kill goblins of the cartel.

## Rewards

There are no standing rewards. From Friendly on, the guards of Everlook help you when someone attacks you, and as with every faction, vendor prices follow your standing.
