The Cenarion Circle is the druid order of Kalimdor. From Cenarion Hold in Silithus it holds the line against the Twilight's Hammer and the silithid of Ahn'Qiraj. Every character starts at Neutral, and the reputation is open to the Alliance and the Horde alike. It matters most for the Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, the raid for 20 players: the class rewards of the Circle are built on items from that raid.

## Where the Cenarion Circle is

**Cenarion Hold** in Silithus is where the turn-ins are and where the reward vendors stand. The Circle also has its home in Moonglade.

## Where the reputation comes from

### The Twilight's Hammer in Silithus

Most cultists of the Twilight's Hammer give 1 reputation per kill, up to Honored. Rare ones, such as the Twilight Prophet and Twilight Lord Everun, give 5, up to Revered.

### Turn-ins at Cenarion Hold

| Turn-in | Reputation |
|---|---|
| Encrypted Twilight Texts | 100 |
| Abyssal Crests | 50 |
| Abyssal Signets | 100 |
| Field Duty | 25 |

The cultists drop Encrypted Twilight Texts. Abyssal Crests and Abyssal Signets come from the elementals that can be summoned at the wind stones of Silithus. Field Duty is a repeatable quest that opens combat, logistics and tactical assignments across the zone.

### The Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj

The trash and bosses of the Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj give Cenarion Circle reputation.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Do the quests of Cenarion Hold as soon as you arrive in Silithus.
2. Farm the Twilight's Hammer camps while kills still count, and keep every Encrypted Twilight Text.
3. Take Field Duty every time you pass Cenarion Hold; the assignments send you where you already are.
4. From Honored on, run the Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj every reset and keep turning in texts.
