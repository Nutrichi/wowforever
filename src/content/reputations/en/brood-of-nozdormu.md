The Brood of Nozdormu is the bronze dragonflight, the guardians of time. Anachronos speaks for them in the Caverns of Time in Tanaris, and their agents wait inside the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj. Every character starts at Hated, the very bottom, which makes this the longest reputation of Classic Era. It is open to the Alliance and the Horde alike and is earned almost entirely in Ahn'Qiraj.

## Where the Brood of Nozdormu is

- **Anachronos** in the Caverns of Time, Tanaris, gives the ring quests.
- Inside the **Temple of Ahn'Qiraj**, Andorgos takes the armor quests for head and shoulders, Kandrostrasz for legs and feet, and Vethsera for the chest.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the bottom of Hated |
|---|---|---|
| Hated | 36,000 | 0 |
| Hostile | 3,000 | 36,000 |
| Unfriendly | 3,000 | 39,000 |
| Neutral | 3,000 | 42,000 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 45,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 51,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 63,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 84,000 |

Hated to Exalted is 84,000 reputation, twice as much as a faction that starts at Neutral.

## Where the reputation comes from

### Kills in Ahn'Qiraj

The trash of the Ruins and the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj gives reputation, but only up to Neutral. From there on, quests carry you. Icy Veins counts 500 reputation for each boss of the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj after Neutral.

### Repeatable quests

| Quest | Turn-in | Reputation |
|---|---|---|
| Secrets of the Qiraji | 1 Ancient Qiraji Artifact | 1,000 |
| Mortal Champions | 1 Qiraji Lord's Insignia | 500 |
| The armor quests in the Temple | Tokens from the bosses of the Temple | 500 |

### One-time quests

The Fall of Ossirian, C'Thun's Legacy and The Savior of Kalimdor also give reputation.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Clear the trash of both raids while it counts: Hated to Neutral alone is 42,000 reputation.
2. Turn in every Ancient Qiraji Artifact and Qiraji Lord's Insignia your raid gets.
3. Do an armor quest as soon as you have its token.
4. Raid the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj every reset: after Neutral, bosses and quests are the only way up.

## Rewards per standing

### Signet Ring of the Bronze Dragonflight

Anachronos gives a ring in three versions: the Protector, the Conqueror and the Invoker. From Neutral on, a quest at every new standing upgrades it: The Path of the Protector, The Path of the Conqueror or The Path of the Invoker. At Exalted the last quest is The Protector of Kalimdor, The Qiraji Conqueror or The Grand Invoker.

### Class sets of the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj

Every class has a set of five pieces from the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj. The pieces are claimed with tokens from the bosses, and the quests ask for a standing between Neutral and Honored, depending on the piece.
