Stormwind is the faction of Stormwind City, the capital of the humans in the northwest of Elwynn Forest. The reputation is for the Alliance only.

## Where you start

Where you start depends on your race. These are the values in the faction data of the Classic Era client, which the database wago.tools reads out of build 1.15.9.

| Your race | Standing at the start |
|---|---|
| Human | Friendly, 1,000 into the standing |
| Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome | Friendly, 100 into the standing |
| Orc, Forsaken, Tauren, Troll | Hated, at the very bottom |

Friendly holds 6,000 reputation, so a character of the city's own race begins 900 ahead of the rest of its side.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Friendly |
|---|---|---|
| Friendly | 6,000 | 0 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 6,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 18,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 39,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

### Quests

Quests for Stormwind give the most reputation. Wowhead points to Elwynn Forest, Westfall, Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Stormwind City, the Arathi Highlands, Dustwallow Marsh, the Stonetalon Mountains and the Western Plaguelands. Most quests for one Alliance city also give a quarter of that reputation to the other Alliance cities.

### Cloth donations

Clavicus Knavingham, the Alliance Cloth Quartermaster in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind City, takes cloth. Every quest opens the next one, only for Stormwind; Additional Runecloth opens after the four donations.

| Quest | Level | Asks | Reputation | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Donation of Wool | 12 | 60 Wool Cloth | 150 | 650 |
| A Donation of Silk | 26 | 60 Silk Cloth | 150 | 1,650 |
| A Donation of Mageweave | 40 | 60 Mageweave Cloth | 150 | 3,300 |
| A Donation of Runecloth | 50 | 60 Runecloth | 150 | 6,600 |
| Additional Runecloth, repeatable | 50 | 20 Runecloth | 50 | 0 |

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Do the quests in the zones above; the reputation also spills over to the other Alliance cities.
2. Turn in the four cloth donations as soon as your level allows: 600 reputation.
3. Then repeat Additional Runecloth. Revered to Exalted alone takes 420 turn-ins, or 8,400 Runecloth.

## Rewards

- **From Honored:** a 10 percent discount at Stormwind vendors, on repairs, training and flights.
- **At Exalted:** characters of the other Alliance races can buy horses from Katie Hunter in Elwynn Forest.
