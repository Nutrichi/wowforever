The Azeroth Commerce Authority is the Alliance supply faction of WoW Forever. You loot crates in the world, fill them and hand them in at its camp for Merchant's Favor. That currency buys recipes, a mount, a pet, a toy and tradeskill titles. The Horde counterpart is [Durotar Supply and Logistics](/reputations/durotar-supply-and-logistics/).

![The vendor window of Huey Sunnydale with the Reins of the Pack Kodo for 6000 Merchant's Favor, a screenshot from the beta of World of Warcraft: Forever](../../../assets/posts/2026-09-23-wow-forever-pack-kodo-vendor.jpg)

## The standings

The scale is the standard one, so from Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation.

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the bottom of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 30,000 |
| Exalted | | 42,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

**Crates.** A crate such as the <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=248549"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_crate_05.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Waylaid Crate</a> is looted in the world, filled and handed in to Marcy Baker. The Legacy perk Performance Bonus gives a 5, 10 or 15 percent chance on double Merchant's Favor per crate; see the [Legacy calculator](/talents/legacy/).

**Craftsman's Writs** also exist, and may be available from level 10, writes Wowhead. What they ask and whether Marcy Baker takes them, is not known yet.
