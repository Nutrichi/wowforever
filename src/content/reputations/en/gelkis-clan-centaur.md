The Gelkis Clan Centaur live in Desolace and are the sworn enemies of the [Magram Clan Centaur](/reputations/magram-clan-centaur/). You can raise only one of the two: what you gain with the Gelkis, you lose with the Magram. Both clans are open to Alliance and Horde.

WoW Forever turns this old rivalry into a real choice. In Classic the reputation stopped below Revered and gave little. Now both clans go to Exalted, with new quests for levels 40 to 43, a quartermaster, twelve recipes, rare weapons and a title.

## Gelkis or Magram?

Pick by your profession. Each quartermaster sells all twelve recipes, but the six of its own clan at Honored for 15 <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=254432"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_vrykul_runestick.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Carved Centaur Totem</a>, and the six of the rival clan only at Exalted for 60. On top of that, three quests at Friendly let you pick one recipe each for the professions of your clan.

| Your profession | Pick |
|---|---|
| Enchanting, Leatherworking, Tailoring | **Gelkis** |
| Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering | [Magram](/reputations/magram-clan-centaur/) |

The last quest, Khan Jehn, gives a choice of three rare items: <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271801"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_axe_17.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Abandoned Ferocity</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271802"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_hammer_08.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Bludgeon of Betrayed Virtues</a>, <a class="wf-wh wf-q3" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=271805"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_orb_01.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Thrice-Damned Effigy</a>. Two of them are melee weapons, and the off-hand has no spell power. Casters do better with the Magram.

## Getting started

Every character starts at Neutral. Two ways lead to Friendly: kill Magram centaurs in Desolace, or hand in a <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=257685"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_enchantedrunecloth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Totem Tote</a> to Molkar with Buying Friendship: Gelkis, which gives 3,000 reputation at once. If you sided with the Magram before, set them to At War in your reputation pane first. At Friendly, Strange Alliance / Gelkis Alliance opens the quest chain.

## The fastest way to Exalted

Do Buying Friendship, then every quest at Friendly and Honored. After that, Economic Warfare is the way to go: 750 reputation for every 5 <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=254432"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_vrykul_runestick.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Carved Centaur Totem</a>. Totems drop from centaurs of every clan in Desolace and do not bind, so you can also buy them on the Auction House.

## The standings

Every character starts at Neutral, 2,000 into the 3,000 of that standing. That value comes from the faction data of the Classic Era client.

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |
