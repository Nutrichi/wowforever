The Gelkis Clan Centaur live in Desolace and are the sworn enemies of the [Magram Clan Centaur](/reputations/magram-clan-centaur/). You can raise only one of the two: what you gain with the Gelkis, you lose with the Magram. Both clans are open to Alliance and Horde.

WoW Forever turns this old rivalry into a real choice. In Classic the reputation stopped below Revered and gave little. Now both clans go to Exalted, each has a quartermaster, and twelve recipes are at stake.

## Gelkis or Magram?

Pick by your profession. Each quartermaster sells all twelve recipes, but the six of its own clan at Honored for 15 <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=254432"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_vrykul_runestick.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Carved Centaur Totem</a>, and the six of the rival clan only at Exalted for 60. One recipe per profession of your clan also comes from a quest that only needs Friendly.

| Your profession | Pick |
|---|---|
| Enchanting, Leatherworking, Tailoring | **Gelkis** |
| Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering | [Magram](/reputations/magram-clan-centaur/) |

The last quest of each chain gives a choice of rare weapons and off-hands. The Gelkis rewards suit melee players best; the off-hand has no spell power for casters. Casters usually prefer the Magram rewards.

## Carved Centaur Totems

<a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=254432"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/trade_archaeology_vrykul_runestick.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Carved Centaur Totem</a> drop from centaurs of every clan in Desolace and stack to 100. Both clans take them as payment, so you collect them while you raise your reputation.

- **Carved Centaur Totems:** a quest that asks for 5 totems, from Captain Pentigast at Nijel's Point for the Alliance or Gurda Wildmane at Ghost Walker Post for the Horde.
- **Buying Friendship: Gelkis:** bring a <a class="wf-wh wf-q2" href="https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=257685"><img class="wf-wh__icon" src="/wh/inv_misc_bag_enchantedrunecloth.jpg" alt="" width="18" height="18" loading="lazy" decoding="async">Totem Tote</a> to Molkar for a big jump in reputation. You need at least Neutral with the Gelkis.

## Where the reputation comes from

- **Killing Magram centaurs** in Desolace. In Classic a kill was worth 20 reputation with the Gelkis and cost 100 with the Magram, according to Warcraft Wiki; the numbers in WoW Forever are not known yet.
- **The quest chain** of Uthek the Wise opens at Friendly: Strange Alliance, Gelkis Alliance, Raid on the Kolkar, Stealing Supplies, Ongeku, Khan Jehn and Khan Hratha.

## The standings

Every character starts at Neutral, 2,000 into the 3,000 of that standing, so 1,000 short of Friendly. That value comes from the faction data of the Classic Era client.

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |
