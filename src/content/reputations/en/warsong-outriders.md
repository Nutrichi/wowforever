The Warsong Outriders are orcs named after the Warsong clan of Grom Hellscream. They fight the Silverwing Sentinels for the logged woods of Ashenvale, in Warsong Gulch. The reputation is for the Horde only; the Alliance earns the [Silverwing Sentinels](/reputations/silverwing-sentinels/) in the same battleground. Every character starts at Neutral.

## Where the Warsong Outriders are

At **Mor'shan Base Camp** in the north of The Barrens, near the border with Ashenvale. The camp has an entrance to Warsong Gulch, the quest givers and the quartermaster Kelm Hargunth. There is no flight path; the closest ones are Splintertree Post in Ashenvale and the Crossroads in The Barrens.

## The standings

| Standing | Reputation in the standing | Needed from the start of Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | 3,000 | 0 |
| Friendly | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Honored | 12,000 | 9,000 |
| Revered | 21,000 | 21,000 |
| Exalted | 1,000 | 42,000 |

## Where the reputation comes from

| Source | Reputation |
|---|---|
| A flag capture | 35 |
| A flag capture on a Warsong Gulch weekend | 45 |
| 3 Warsong Gulch Marks of Honor turned in | 50 |
| For Great Honor, the first time | 150 |
| For Great Honor, every time after | 100 |

A win takes 3 flag captures, so it gives 105 reputation, or 135 on a Warsong Gulch weekend. A win also gives 3 Marks of Honor, a loss 1. For Great Honor asks for 3 Marks of Honor from Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley, and also gives reputation with The Defilers and the Frostwolf Clan.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Play on Warsong Gulch weekends: 135 reputation per win instead of 105.
2. Turn in Marks of Honor in threes; with a win's own marks, a win is worth 155 reputation.
3. Keep Marks of Honor from all three battlegrounds for For Great Honor.
4. Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation: about 400 wins on a normal week, or about 310 on weekends.
