The Frostwolf Clan is an orc clan that made the Alterac Mountains its home after Gul'dan exiled it. When Thrall set sail for Kalimdor, the Frostwolves stayed behind under Drek'Thar. Now they are at war with the dwarves of the Stormpike Guard, who want Alterac Valley for the Alliance. The reputation is for the Horde only; the Alliance earns the [Stormpike Guard](/reputations/stormpike-guard/) in the same battleground. Every character starts at Neutral.

## Where the Frostwolf Clan is

At **The Uplands** in the east of the Alterac Mountains. The base has an entrance to Alterac Valley, the quest givers and the quartermaster Jekyll Flandring. There is no flight path; the closest one is Tarren Mill in Hillsbrad Foothills. Inside Alterac Valley, Grunnda Wolfheart in Frostwolf Keep sells the same items.

## Where the reputation comes from

### During the battle

Reputation for kills is shared by the whole raid and comes in at once.

| Source | Reputation |
|---|---|
| Killing Vanndar Stormpike, the Alliance general | 389 |
| Killing Captain Balinda Stonehearth | 125 |
| Killing Ivus the Forest Lord | 125 |
| Killing an Alliance commander | 12 |
| Killing an Alliance air master | 5 |
| Killing an Alliance guard, up to Honored | 5 |
| Killing an Alliance player | 1 |

### At the end of the battle

| Result | Reputation |
|---|---|
| Vanndar Stormpike killed | 36 |
| Captain Galvangar still alive | 125 |
| Every Alliance bunker destroyed | 24 |
| Every Alliance commander killed | 12 |
| Every tower that is yours or still stands | 12 |
| Every graveyard you hold | 12 |
| Every mine you hold | 12 |

### Quests and Marks of Honor

| Source | Reputation |
|---|---|
| The Battle for Alterac: win a battle, once | 200 |
| The Graveyards of Alterac: assault a graveyard, once | 100 |
| Towers and Bunkers: assault a tower, once | 100 |
| Capture a Mine: once | 100 |
| 3 Alterac Valley Marks of Honor turned in | 100 |
| For Great Honor, the first time | 200 |
| For Great Honor, every time after | 150 |

A win gives 3 Alterac Valley Marks of Honor, a loss 1. For Great Honor asks for 3 Marks of Honor from Alterac Valley, Arathi Basin and Warsong Gulch, and also gives reputation with the Warsong Outriders and The Defilers. The follow-up to The Battle for Alterac, Hero of the Frostwolf, gives a choice of rare weapons.

### The Frostwolf Insignia

The insignia is a trinket that takes you back to Frostwolf Keep inside Alterac Valley. Every quest upgrades it, up to an epic trinket.

| Rank | Quest | Opens at | Reputation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proving Grounds: bring the Frostwolf Banner from Wildpaw Cavern | Neutral | 100 |
| 2 | Rise and Be Recognized | Friendly | 100 |
| 3 | Honored Amongst the Clan | Honored | 100 |
| 4 | Earned Reverence | Revered | 150 |
| 5 | Legendary Heroes | Exalted | 200 |
| 6 | The Eye of Command | The very end of Exalted (999) | |

Some sources of reputation stop at Exalted, so the last points for rank 6 take a little extra work.

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Do the one-time quests first: together they give 500 reputation.
2. Help kill Vanndar Stormpike and Captain Balinda Stonehearth, and keep Captain Galvangar alive; those are the biggest amounts per battle.
3. Turn in Marks of Honor in threes, and keep marks from all three battlegrounds for For Great Honor.
4. Do each Frostwolf Insignia quest as soon as your standing opens it.
5. Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation.
