The Shen'dralar are Highborne who once kept the spell tomes of Queen Azshara. They survived the War of the Ancients in Eldre'Thalas, the city now called Dire Maul. To keep his followers alive, Prince Tortheldrin bound the demon Immol'thar, and later began to sacrifice his own people. The reputation is open to Alliance and Horde. Every character starts at Neutral.

## Where the Shen'dralar are

In the library of **the Athenaeum** in Dire Maul, Feralas. There is no quartermaster: everyone you need is in the library, including Lorekeeper Lydros and the Shen'dralar Provisioner.

You reach the Athenaeum from Dire Maul West, through the northeast exit of Immol'thar's room, or from Dire Maul North, up a ramp near Guard Mol'dar. Both doors need the Crescent Key. Without the key, a Rogue can pick the lock (275 Lockpicking) or pickpocket the key from Guard Mol'dar, an Engineer can use a Powerful Seaforium Charge, and a Blacksmith an Arcanite Skeleton Key.

## Where the reputation comes from

| Source | Reputation |
|---|---|
| The class book of your class, once | 500 |
| The three Quel'Serrar quests, together | 1,000 |
| A libram, repeatable | 200 |
| An Enchanted Thorium Platemail volume, once each | 200 |

### Class books

Enemies in Dire Maul and the books called A Dusty Tome can drop a rare class book. The book starts a short quest to the library; the reward is a version of the Royal Seal of Eldre'Thalas, a trinket with Fire Resistance and stats for your class.

### Quel'Serrar

Warriors and Paladins can earn Quel'Serrar, a one-handed tanking sword. The questline starts with Nostro's Compendium of Dragon Slaying, a rare drop from bosses and A Dusty Tome in Dire Maul, and it also leads through Onyxia's Lair. Every quest goes to Lorekeeper Lydros.

### Librams and Arcanums

First do Elven Legends: Scholar Runethorn in Feathermoon Stronghold gives it to the Alliance, Sage Korolusk in Camp Mojache to the Horde, both in Feralas. You inspect the Skeletal Remains of Telmius Dreamseeker in the Athenaeum. After that, Lorekeeper Lydros takes librams, which drop in Dire Maul.

| Libram | Also needed | Arcanum | Effect on head or legs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Libram of Focus | Pristine Black Diamond, 4 Large Brilliant Shard, 2 Skin of Shadow | Arcanum of Focus | +8 spell damage and healing |
| Libram of Protection | Pristine Black Diamond, 2 Large Brilliant Shard, Frayed Abomination Stitching | Arcanum of Protection | 1% dodge |
| Libram of Rapidity | Pristine Black Diamond, 2 Large Brilliant Shard, 2 Blood of Heroes | Arcanum of Rapidity | 1% melee and ranged haste |

### Enchanted Thorium Platemail

For Armorsmiths: join the two halves of each Advanced Armorsmithing volume and bring the book to Lorekeeper Lydros.

| Volume | Top half | Bottom half | Plans |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | King Gordok, Dire Maul North | Goraluk Anvilcrack, Lower Blackrock Spire | Plans: Enchanted Thorium Breastplate |
| II | Rattlegore, Scholomance | Crimson Hammersmith, Stratholme (living side) | Plans: Enchanted Thorium Leggings |
| III | Urok Doomhowl, Lower Blackrock Spire | Black Guard Swordsmith, Stratholme (undead side) | Plans: Enchanted Thorium Helm |

## The fastest way to Exalted

1. Turn in the book of your class as soon as you find it: 500 at once.
2. Warriors and Paladins: do Quel'Serrar for another 1,000.
3. Armorsmiths: the three Enchanted Thorium volumes give 600.
4. The rest comes from librams at 200 each. Neutral to Exalted is 42,000 reputation, so it takes roughly 200 librams. Some guides mention 500 per libram; that is the amount from a later version of the game.

## What the Shen'dralar offer

There is no list of rewards per standing. What you get comes from the quests above, plus Pattern: Mooncloth Robe, which the Shen'dralar Provisioner in the library sells in limited supply.
