Survival makes the Hunter tougher and better in close combat. Survivalist adds health, Deterrence and Counterattack help when an enemy reaches you, and Lightning Reflexes adds a lot of Agility. In Classic Era it levels slower than Beast Mastery, but it is forgiving, and Wyvern Sting at level 40 puts an enemy to sleep.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- More health, Hit and Agility than the other specs.
- Deterrence and Counterattack save you when an enemy reaches you.
- Monster Slaying and Humanoid Slaying add damage against most enemies you meet while leveling.
- The pet gets no talents, so fights take longer than with Beast Mastery.

## Talents by level

### Level 10 to 30

- Monster Slaying 3/3, Humanoid Slaying 3/3 and Deflection 4/5.
- Deterrence at level 20, then Survivalist 5/5.
- Surefooted 3/3, Savage Strikes 1/2 and Killer Instinct 1/3 at level 30.

### Level 31 to 40

- Killer Instinct 3/3, Counterattack and Savage Strikes 2/2.
- Lightning Reflexes 5/5, then Wyvern Sting at level 40.

### Level 41 to 60

- In Marksmanship: Efficiency 5/5, Lethal Shots 5/5, Aimed Shot and Hawk Eye 3/3.
- Improved Arcane Shot 1/5 and Mortal Shots 5/5, for 20 points in Marksmanship.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Send the pet, then Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting and Arcane Shot, with Auto Shot in between.
- When the enemy reaches you: Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite after a dodge, and Wing Clip to step back and shoot again.
- Keep the pet fed: a happy pet deals full damage.

### Two enemies

- Wyvern Sting from level 40 puts one enemy to sleep. Kill the other one first.
- Lay a Freezing Trap before the pull, or use Feign Death from level 30 to leave combat and lay one then.

### Staying alive

- Deterrence when an enemy hits you hard, and Counterattack after a parry.
- Disengage from level 20 lowers your threat, so the pet takes the enemy back.
- Mend Pet keeps the pet alive, and Feign Death ends the fight.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 1-12 | Dun Morogh, Teldrassil | Durotar, Mulgore |
| 12-20 | Westfall, Loch Modan, Darkshore | The Barrens, Silverpine Forest |
| 20-30 | Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Wetlands | The Barrens, Hillsbrad Foothills, Thousand Needles |
| 30-40 | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace |
| 40-50 | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands |
| 50-60 | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring |

Dungeons worth the detour: Scarlet Monastery from level 30 to 40, Zul'Farrak around 45 and Blackrock Depths from 52.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Agility first, because Lightning Reflexes makes it bigger, then Hit and Crit. Stamina helps in melee.
- A slow ranged weapon, and a two-handed weapon with Agility for Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite.
- Enchants: Agility on the gloves, and a scope on the ranged weapon.
- Tradeskills: Skinning with Leatherworking for leather and, from level 40, mail armor. Or Mining with Engineering for scopes and ammunition.
