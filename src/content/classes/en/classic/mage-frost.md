Frost is the most common way to level a Mage in Classic Era. Frostbolt slows every enemy it hits, and Frost Nova keeps melee enemies away. From the mid-20s, Improved Blizzard lets a Mage pull and kill whole groups of weaker enemies.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Slows and roots keep most enemies from ever landing a hit.
- Blizzard with Improved Blizzard kills groups of lower-level enemies quickly.
- Mana runs out fast, so you drink after most fights.
- Cloth armor punishes a pull that goes wrong.

## Talents by level

### Level 10 to 30

- Improved Frostbolt 5/5 for a faster Frostbolt, and Elemental Precision 3/3 for fewer resists.
- Permafrost 3/3 and Improved Blizzard 3/3, the core of leveling with Blizzard.
- Ice Shards 5/5 for bigger critical strikes with Frost spells.

### Level 31 to 40

- Piercing Ice 3/3, Frost Channeling 3/3 and Arctic Reach 2/2.
- Cold Snap, Ice Block and one point in Improved Cone of Cold, then Ice Barrier at level 40.

### Level 41 to 60

- Improved Cone of Cold 3/3, Shatter 5/5 and Frostbite 3/3, for 41 points in Frost.
- In Arcane: Arcane Subtlety 2/2 and Arcane Focus 3/3, then Arcane Concentration 5/5 for free spells.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Open with Frostbolt at maximum range and cast a second one while the enemy walks toward you.
- Frost Nova when it reaches you, step back and finish with Frostbolt or Fire Blast.
- Finish enemies with low health with a wand to save mana.

### Groups of enemies

- Pull the group, cast Frost Nova, move away and cast Blizzard. Cone of Cold on anything that gets close.
- Improved Blizzard slows the enemies inside the storm. Cold Snap resets Frost Nova for a second root.
- Pull only as many enemies as your mana can kill, and pick groups a few levels below you.

### Staying alive

- Ice Barrier and Ice Block when things go wrong, and Blink to gain distance.
- Polymorph a second enemy that joins the fight.
- Conjure Water and Conjure Food save gold, and Evocation restores mana when you run out.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 1-12 | Elwynn Forest, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil | Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal Glades |
| 12-20 | Westfall, Loch Modan, Darkshore | The Barrens, Silverpine Forest |
| 20-30 | Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Wetlands | The Barrens, Hillsbrad Foothills, Thousand Needles |
| 30-40 | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace |
| 40-50 | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands |
| 50-60 | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring |

Dungeons worth the detour: Scarlet Monastery from level 30 to 40, Zul'Farrak around 45 and Blackrock Depths from 52.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Intellect and Stamina while leveling, and Spirit for less drinking. Spell Damage and Crit matter more at level 60.
- The wand is the most important weapon slot while leveling.
- Enchants: Mana or Stats on the chest and Intellect on the bracers.
- Tradeskills: Tailoring for cloth armor and bags, with Enchanting to craft your own wands.
