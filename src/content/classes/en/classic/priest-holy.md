Holy is the healing spec of the Priest and the one groups ask for in dungeons and raids. Alone it levels slower than Shadow, but a Smite build with Divine Fury and Searing Light works, and Spiritual Guidance turns Spirit into damage and healing. Pick Holy when you mostly level in groups.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- The strongest healer for dungeon groups, so finding a group is easy.
- Divine Fury gives a faster Smite, and Searing Light makes Smite and Holy Fire hit harder.
- Holy Nova damages all enemies around you and heals your group at the same time.
- Slower alone than Shadow, and your mana runs out sooner.

## Talents by level

### Level 10 to 30

- Holy Specialization 5/5 and Divine Fury 5/5.
- Holy Nova, Improved Renew 3/3 and Inspiration 3/3.
- Searing Light 2/2, Holy Reach 1/2 and Spiritual Guidance 1/5 at level 30.

### Level 31 to 40

- Spiritual Guidance 5/5 and Spiritual Healing 5/5.
- Holy Reach 2/2 at level 40, for 31 points in Holy.

### Level 41 to 60

- All points in Discipline: Wand Specialization 5/5, Improved Power Word: Shield 3/3, Improved Power Word: Fortitude 2/2 and Martyrdom 2/2.
- Meditation 3/3 and Inner Focus, then Mental Agility 4/5.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Open with Holy Fire at maximum range, then Shadow Word: Pain.
- Power Word: Shield before the enemy reaches you, so your Smite is not pushed back.
- Smite until the enemy is low, then finish with your wand.

### Dungeon groups

- Renew on the tank before the pull, and Power Word: Shield when damage spikes.
- Heal with lower ranks of Heal to save mana, and keep Flash Heal for emergencies.
- Holy Nova causes no threat, so use it when several players take damage close to you.

### Staying alive

- Power Word: Shield and Renew first, then Heal or Flash Heal.
- Psychic Scream buys time when two enemies hit you.
- Inner Fire for armor, and Fade in a group when enemies turn to you.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 1-12 | Elwynn Forest, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil | Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal Glades |
| 12-20 | Westfall, Loch Modan, Darkshore | The Barrens, Silverpine Forest |
| 20-30 | Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Wetlands | The Barrens, Hillsbrad Foothills, Thousand Needles |
| 30-40 | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace |
| 40-50 | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands |
| 50-60 | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring |

Dungeons worth the detour: Scarlet Monastery from level 30 to 40, Zul'Farrak around 45 and Blackrock Depths from 52.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Spirit for Spiritual Guidance, then Intellect and Stamina. Healing power matters most at level 60.
- The wand finishes enemies while leveling. A staff or a mace with Intellect and Spirit fills the weapon slot.
- Enchants: Mana or Stats on the chest, Spirit or Intellect on the bracers, and Healing Power on the weapon at level 60.
- Tradeskills: Tailoring with Enchanting, or Herbalism with Alchemy for mana potions.
