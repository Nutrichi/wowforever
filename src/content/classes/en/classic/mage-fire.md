Fire deals the most damage per cast of the three Mage specs in Classic Era. Fireball, Pyroblast and Ignite kill single enemies quickly. The price is safety: Fire has no talent slows, so Frost Nova and distance do all the work when an enemy reaches you.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Enemies die in fewer casts, with Ignite adding damage on every critical strike.
- Pyroblast opens a fight with a large hit before the enemy reaches you.
- Blast Wave from the 30s knocks back and slows a whole group.
- Without Frost talents, fights with several enemies are riskier than with Blizzard.

## Talents by level

### Level 10 to 30

- Improved Fireball 5/5 for a faster Fireball, then Ignite 5/5.
- Improved Fire Blast 3/3, Pyroblast and Flame Throwing 2/2 for extra range.
- Incinerate 2/2 and Master of Elements 3/3 for mana back from critical strikes.

### Level 31 to 40

- Critical Mass 3/3 and Blast Wave.
- Fire Power 5/5, then Combustion at level 40.

### Level 41 to 60

- Impact 5/5, Burning Soul 2/2 and Improved Scorch 3/3, for 41 points in Fire.
- In Arcane: Arcane Subtlety 2/2 and Arcane Focus 3/3, then Arcane Concentration 5/5 for free spells.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Open with Pyroblast at maximum range, then Fireball while the enemy walks toward you.
- Fire Blast when it gets close, Frost Nova, step back and finish with Fireball.
- Finish enemies with low health with a wand to save mana.

### Groups of enemies

- Frost Nova, step back, then Blast Wave and Arcane Explosion while the group is rooted.
- Flamestrike on groups that stand still, for example while they attack a tank in a dungeon.
- Keep pulls small: two or three enemies at a time.

### Staying alive

- Frost Nova and Blink when an enemy reaches you.
- Mana Shield absorbs damage when you cannot get away.
- Polymorph a second enemy, and use Evocation when your mana runs out.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 1-12 | Elwynn Forest, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil | Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal Glades |
| 12-20 | Westfall, Loch Modan, Darkshore | The Barrens, Silverpine Forest |
| 20-30 | Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Wetlands | The Barrens, Hillsbrad Foothills, Thousand Needles |
| 30-40 | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace | Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands, Desolace |
| 40-50 | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands | Tanaris, Feralas, The Hinterlands |
| 50-60 | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring | Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Western Plaguelands, Winterspring |

Dungeons worth the detour: Scarlet Monastery from level 30 to 40, Zul'Farrak around 45 and Blackrock Depths from 52.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Intellect and Stamina while leveling, and Spirit for less drinking. Crit feeds Ignite and Master of Elements.
- The wand is the most important weapon slot while leveling.
- Enchants: Mana or Stats on the chest and Intellect on the bracers.
- Tradeskills: Tailoring for cloth armor and bags, with Enchanting to craft your own wands.
