Fury is the Warrior spec with the most damage in Northrend once your weapons are good. Titan's Grip lets you wield two two-handed weapons, Bloodthirst heals you with every use, and Bloodsurge turns Slam into a free instant hit. Whirlwind and Cleave make groups of weaker enemies fall together.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Very high damage with Titan's Grip, Bloodthirst and Whirlwind.
- Bloodthirst and Victory Rush keep your health up without eating.
- Rampage adds critical strike chance for you and your group.
- Takes more damage than Arms, and two weak weapons hurt your damage badly.

## Talents by level

### At level 70

- In Fury: Armored to the Teeth 3/3, Cruelty 5/5, Unbridled Wrath 5/5, Blood Craze 2/3, Dual Wield Specialization 5/5, Enrage 5/5 and Precision 3/3.
- Flurry 5/5, Bloodthirst, Improved Whirlwind 2/2, Improved Berserker Stance 5/5, Rampage, Bloodsurge 3/3, Unending Fury 5/5 and Titan's Grip, for 51 points.
- In Arms: Improved Heroic Strike 3/3, Improved Rend 2/2, Improved Charge 2/2 and Tactical Mastery 3/3.

### Level 71 to 80

- Anger Management, Deep Wounds 3/3 and Impale 2/2.
- Improved Overpower 2/2 and Deflection 2/5, for 20 points in Arms.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Charge in Battle Stance, switch to Berserker Stance, then Bloodthirst and Whirlwind whenever they are ready.
- Slam whenever Bloodsurge makes it instant, and Heroic Strike with spare rage.
- Execute below 20% health, and Victory Rush after every kill.

### Two enemies

- Whirlwind and Cleave hit both enemies at once.
- Demoralizing Shout lowers their damage, and Intimidating Shout frees you from a third.

### Staying alive

- Bloodthirst and Victory Rush heal you, and Enraged Regeneration from level 75 heals you in a pinch.
- Commanding Shout adds health, and Berserker Rage breaks fear.
- Shattering Throw from level 71 breaks a shield or immunity, and Heroic Throw at 80 pulls from range.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Strength first, then Hit, Expertise, Crit and Armor Penetration.
- Two slow two-handed weapons with Titan's Grip.
- Enchants: Berserking on both weapons once gold allows it, Greater Assault on the bracers and Crusher on the gloves.
- Tradeskills: Mining with Blacksmithing for extra sockets, or Mining with Jewelcrafting for your own gems. Skinning pays well in Northrend.
