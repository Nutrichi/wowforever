Discipline is the Priest who hardly ever takes damage. Power Word: Shield absorbs the first hits of every fight, Borrowed Time then speeds up your next spell, and Penance hits an enemy three times or heals you in a pinch. Leveling is slower than Shadow, but you stay ready to heal a dungeon group at any moment.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Power Word: Shield with Reflective Shield and Divine Aegis makes you very hard to kill.
- Penance, Holy Fire and Smite deal decent holy damage.
- You can heal any dungeon along the way without changing talents.
- Slower kills than Shadow, and groups of enemies take a lot of mana.

## Talents by level

### At level 70

- In Discipline: Twin Disciplines 5/5, Improved Inner Fire 3/3, Martyrdom 2/2, Meditation 3/3, Inner Focus, Improved Power Word: Shield 3/3 and Mental Agility 3/3.
- Reflective Shield 2/2, Mental Strength 5/5, Soul Warding, Focused Power 2/2, Enlightenment 3/3, Power Infusion, Focused Will 2/3, Renewed Hope 2/2 and Aspiration 2/2.
- Divine Aegis 3/3, Grace 2/2, Borrowed Time 5/5 and Penance, for 51 points. In Holy: Holy Specialization 5/5 and Divine Fury 5/5.

### Level 71 to 80

- Healing Focus 2/2, Improved Renew 3/3 and Desperate Prayer.
- Searing Light 2/2 and Holy Reach 2/2, for 20 points in Holy.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Power Word: Shield on yourself, then Holy Fire from range.
- Shadow Word: Pain, then Penance while Borrowed Time is active.
- Smite until the enemy dies, and Power Infusion for a strong enemy.

### Groups of enemies

- Shield first, then Holy Nova while the enemies stand around you.
- Mind Sear from level 75, and Psychic Scream to break up a large group.

### Staying alive

- Power Word: Shield before every pull, and Penance or Flash Heal on yourself when needed.
- Desperate Prayer as an instant heal, and Fade and Psychic Scream to get away.
- Shadowfiend and Hymn of Hope when your mana runs low.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 70-72 | Borean Tundra (Valiance Keep), Howling Fjord (Valgarde) | Borean Tundra (Warsong Hold), Howling Fjord (Vengeance Landing) |
| 72-74 | Dragonblight (Wintergarde Keep) | Dragonblight (Agmar's Hammer) |
| 74-75 | Grizzly Hills (Amberpine Lodge) | Grizzly Hills (Conquest Hold) |
| 75-76 | Zul'Drak (Zim'Torga) | Zul'Drak (Zim'Torga) |
| 76-78 | Sholazar Basin (Nesingwary Base Camp) | Sholazar Basin (Nesingwary Base Camp) |
| 78-80 | The Storm Peaks (K3), Icecrown (Argent Vanguard) | The Storm Peaks (K3), Icecrown (Argent Vanguard) |

Dungeons worth the detour: Utgarde Keep and The Nexus from level 70, Azjol-Nerub and Ahn'kahet: The Old Kingdom around 72, and Drak'Tharon Keep and The Violet Hold from 74.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Spell Power first, then Intellect, Haste, Crit and Spirit.
- A staff, or a one-handed weapon with an off-hand item, and a wand with Spell Power.
- Enchants: Mighty Spellpower on the weapon, Exceptional Spellpower on the gloves and Superior Spellpower on the bracers.
- Tradeskills: Tailoring for caster cloth and Lightweave Embroidery, with Enchanting to disenchant what you replace.
