Feral is one of the fastest and most flexible ways to level a Druid. In Cat Form you build combo points with Mangle, Rake and Shred, and Rip and Savage Roar turn them into steady damage. When a pull gets too big, you switch to Bear Form and tank the whole group. Berserk, Survival Instincts and Predatory Strikes' instant heals keep you going with almost no downtime.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Very fast kills in Cat Form, and a solid tank in Bear Form.
- Almost no downtime: no mana needed in forms, and Predatory Strikes gives instant heals.
- Prowl, Dash and Travel Form make it easy to pick your fights.
- Groups are hard in Cat Form before Swipe at level 71, and changing forms costs time.

## Talents by level

### At level 70

- In Feral: Ferocity 5/5, Savage Fury 2/2, Feral Swiftness 2/2, Survival Instincts, Sharpened Claws 3/3, Shredding Attacks 2/2, Predatory Strikes 3/3, Primal Fury 2/2 and Primal Precision 2/2.
- Feral Charge, Heart of the Wild 5/5, Survival of the Fittest 3/3, Leader of the Pack, Improved Leader of the Pack 2/2, Predatory Instincts 3/3, King of the Jungle 3/3, Mangle and Improved Mangle 3/3.
- Rend and Tear 5/5, Primal Gore and Berserk, for 51 points. In Restoration: Furor 5/5 and Naturalist 5/5.

### Level 71 to 80

- Natural Shapeshifter 3/3, Omen of Clarity and Master Shapeshifter 2/2.
- Intensity 3/3 and Improved Mark of the Wild 1/2, for 20 points in Restoration.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Prowl, then Pounce or Ravage to open.
- Mangle (Cat) and Rake, then Shred from behind to build combo points.
- Savage Roar from level 75 with a few points, then Ferocious Bite or Rip at five, and Tiger's Fury for energy.

### Groups of enemies

- Bear Form, then Demoralizing Roar and Swipe (Bear) on the group.
- Swipe (Cat) from level 71 on two or three weaker enemies, and Berserk for a big pull.

### Staying alive

- An instant Regrowth or Healing Touch whenever Predatory Swiftness lights up after a finishing move.
- Survival Instincts and Frenzied Regeneration in Bear Form when a fight goes wrong.
- Barkskin in any form, and Dash or Prowl to get away.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Agility first, then Strength, Armor Penetration, Hit, Expertise and Crit.
- A staff or polearm with as much Agility and Feral Attack Power as possible.
- Enchants: Mongoose or Berserking on the weapon, Crusher on the gloves and Greater Assault on the bracers.
- Tradeskills: Skinning with Leatherworking for leather gear and Fur Lining on the bracers.
