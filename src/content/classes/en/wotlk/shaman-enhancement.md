Enhancement is the Shaman who fights up close with two weapons. Windfury Weapon and Flametongue Weapon add extra hits, Stormstrike and Lava Lash hit hard, and Maelstrom Weapon turns your melee hits into an instant Lightning Bolt or Healing Wave. Feral Spirit calls two wolves that fight and heal you, and Shamanistic Rage takes care of your mana.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Very steady damage, with instant Lightning Bolts from Maelstrom Weapon.
- Maelstrom Weapon also gives instant heals, so you rarely need to stop.
- Shamanistic Rage and Feral Spirit make tough fights much easier.
- Groups of enemies are harder than for Elemental, and you need two good one-handed weapons.

## Talents by level

### At level 70

- In Enhancement: Enhancing Totems 3/3, Ancestral Knowledge 5/5, Thundering Strikes 5/5, Improved Ghost Wolf 2/2, Elemental Weapons 3/3, Shamanistic Focus, Flurry 5/5, Improved Windfury Totem 2/2 and Spirit Weapons.
- Unleashed Rage 3/3, Weapon Mastery 3/3, Dual Wield Specialization 3/3, Dual Wield, Stormstrike, Lava Lash, Improved Stormstrike 2/2, Mental Quickness 3/3 and Shamanistic Rage.
- Maelstrom Weapon 5/5 and Feral Spirit, for 51 points. In Elemental: Convection 5/5 and Concussion 5/5.

### Level 71 to 80

- Call of Flame 3/3, Elemental Devastation 3/3 and Elemental Focus.
- Elemental Warding 3/3, for 20 points in Elemental.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Windfury Weapon on the main hand, Flametongue Weapon on the off hand and Lightning Shield.
- Flame Shock to open, then Stormstrike and Lava Lash on cooldown, and Earth Shock while Stormstrike is active.
- Lightning Bolt at five stacks of Maelstrom Weapon, and Magma Totem or Searing Totem for extra damage.

### Groups of enemies

- Magma Totem and Fire Nova on a group, and Chain Lightning at five stacks of Maelstrom Weapon.
- Feral Spirit for a big pull, and Stormstrike on the strongest enemy.

### Staying alive

- Healing Wave at five stacks of Maelstrom Weapon when your health drops.
- Shamanistic Rage when you take a lot of damage, and Feral Spirit to heal you.
- Frost Shock and Earthbind Totem to slow enemies, and Ghost Wolf to get away.

Dungeons worth the detour: Utgarde Keep and The Nexus from level 70, Azjol-Nerub and Ahn'kahet: The Old Kingdom around 72, and Drak'Tharon Keep and The Violet Hold from 74.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Attack Power and Agility first, then Hit, Expertise, Haste and Crit.
- Two slow one-handed weapons with the same speed, with as much damage as possible.
- Enchants: Berserking on both weapons, Crusher on the gloves and Greater Assault on the bracers.
- Tradeskills: Skinning with Leatherworking for mail gear and Fur Lining on the bracers.
