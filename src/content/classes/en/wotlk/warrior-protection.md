Protection is the tank spec of the Warrior, and in WotLK Classic it can also quest well. Warbringer lets you Charge and Intercept in any stance, Sword and Board turns Devastate into free Shield Slams, and Shockwave stuns every enemy in front of you. Dungeon groups always need a tank, so the spec levels fast through the dungeon finder.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Excellent threat on one and many enemies with Shield Slam, Thunder Clap and Shockwave.
- Very hard to kill with Shield Block, Shield Wall, Last Stand and Critical Block.
- Warbringer and Damage Shield make solo questing workable.
- Lower damage than Arms or Fury when you quest alone.

## Talents by level

### At level 70

- In Protection: Improved Thunder Clap 3/3, Shield Specialization 5/5, Incite 3/3, Last Stand, Improved Revenge 2/2, Shield Mastery 2/2, Puncture 3/3, Concussion Blow and Gag Order 2/2.
- One-Handed Weapon Specialization 5/5, Vigilance, Focused Rage 3/3, Improved Defensive Stance 2/2, Vitality 3/3, Safeguard 2/2, Warbringer, Devastate and Critical Block 3/3.
- Sword and Board 3/3, Damage Shield 1/2, Toughness 3/5 and Shockwave, for 51 points. In Arms: Improved Heroic Strike 3/3, Deflection 5/5 and Improved Charge 2/2.

### Level 71 to 80

- Tactical Mastery 3/3, Anger Management and Deep Wounds 3/3.
- Impale 2/2 and Improved Overpower 1/2, for 20 points in Arms.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Charge in Defensive Stance thanks to Warbringer, then Shield Slam whenever it is ready.
- Revenge whenever it lights up, Devastate as your filler, and Shield Slam again when Sword and Board resets it.
- Heroic Strike with spare rage, and Concussion Blow to stun.

### Dungeon groups

- Thunder Clap and Shockwave on the pull, then Shield Slam and Devastate on the main target and Cleave with spare rage.
- Taunt an enemy that runs to the healer, and Challenging Shout when several do.
- Vigilance on a damage dealer, Intervene to protect a player, and Spell Reflection against casters.

### Staying alive

- Shield Block before heavy hits, Shield Wall and Last Stand when your health drops fast.
- Enraged Regeneration from level 75 heals you in a pinch.
- Shattering Throw from level 71 breaks a shield or immunity, and Heroic Throw at 80 pulls from range.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Stamina first, then Defense until 540 so bosses in heroic dungeons cannot land critical strikes on you, then Dodge, Parry and Block Value.
- A one-handed weapon with a shield that has plenty of armor.
- Enchants: Super Health on the chest, Major Stamina on the bracers and Defense on the shield.
- Tradeskills: Mining with Blacksmithing for extra sockets, or Mining with Jewelcrafting for Stamina gems.
