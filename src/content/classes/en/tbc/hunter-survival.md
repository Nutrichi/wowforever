Survival is the toughest Hunter spec and the one groups like to bring for Expose Weakness, which adds attack power for everyone who hits the enemy. Lightning Reflexes adds a lot of Agility, Deterrence and Counterattack save you when an enemy reaches you, and Readiness resets all your cooldowns at once. It levels a little slower than Beast Mastery, but it is very forgiving.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Survivalist, Deterrence and Readiness make you hard to kill.
- Lightning Reflexes and Expose Weakness give strong scaling with Agility.
- Wyvern Sting and traps give a lot of control.
- The pet gets no talents, so it holds enemies less well than with Beast Mastery.

## Talents by level

### At level 60

- In Survival: Humanoid Slaying 3/3, Savage Strikes 2/2, Deflection 5/5, Survivalist 5/5, Deterrence, Surefooted 3/3, Survival Instincts 2/2 and Killer Instinct 3/3.
- Counterattack, Lightning Reflexes 5/5, Expose Weakness 3/3, Wyvern Sting, Thrill of the Hunt 1/3, Master Tactician 5/5 and Readiness, for 41 points.
- In Marksmanship: Lethal Shots 5/5 and Efficiency 5/5.

### Level 61 to 70

- Aimed Shot, Rapid Killing 2/2 and Go for the Throat 2/2.
- Mortal Shots 5/5, for 20 points in Marksmanship.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Send the pet, cast Hunter's Mark, then Serpent Sting and Arcane Shot.
- Steady Shot from level 62 after each auto shot, and Kill Command from level 66 after a critical strike.
- Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite if the enemy reaches you, then Wing Clip and step back.

### Two enemies

- Wyvern Sting puts one enemy to sleep, so you can kill the other first.
- Freezing Trap on an extra enemy, and Readiness to lay a second trap at once.

### Staying alive

- Deterrence when you are hit hard, and Counterattack after a parry.
- Feign Death ends a fight that goes wrong, and Readiness resets it for a second use.
- Snake Trap from level 68 and Misdirection from level 70 keep enemies on the pet.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 60-62 | Hellfire Peninsula (Honor Hold) | Hellfire Peninsula (Thrallmar) |
| 62-64 | Zangarmarsh (Telredor) | Zangarmarsh (Zabra'jin) |
| 64-65 | Terokkar Forest (Allerian Stronghold) | Terokkar Forest (Stonebreaker Hold) |
| 65-67 | Nagrand (Telaar) | Nagrand (Garadar) |
| 67-68 | Blade's Edge Mountains (Sylvanaar) | Blade's Edge Mountains (Thunderlord Stronghold) |
| 68-70 | Netherstorm (Area 52), Shadowmoon Valley (Wildhammer Stronghold) | Netherstorm (Area 52), Shadowmoon Valley (Shadowmoon Village) |

Dungeons worth the detour: Hellfire Ramparts and The Blood Furnace from level 60, The Slave Pens and The Underbog around 62, and Mana-Tombs and Auchenai Crypts from 64.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Agility first, since Lightning Reflexes and Expose Weakness make it stronger, then Hit and Crit.
- The ranged weapon with the highest damage you can get, and a two-handed weapon with Agility for Raptor Strike.
- Enchants: a scope on the ranged weapon, Superior Agility on the gloves and Assault on the bracers.
- Tradeskills: Skinning with Leatherworking for mail armor and drums, or Mining with Engineering for scopes and ammunition.
