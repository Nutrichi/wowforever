Fury is the fastest Warrior spec in Outland when your weapons are good. Bloodthirst and Whirlwind hit hard, Flurry and Rampage keep the damage climbing during a fight, and Sweeping Strikes turns two enemies into a quick kill. It needs two decent one-handed weapons, so pick up every upgrade that quests offer.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- The highest damage of the Warrior specs with good weapons.
- Sweeping Strikes and Whirlwind handle two or three enemies well.
- Rampage adds attack power in every longer fight.
- Takes more damage than Arms and suffers badly from weak weapons.

## Talents by level

### At level 60

- In Fury: Cruelty 5/5, Unbridled Wrath 5/5, Commanding Presence 5/5, Dual Wield Specialization 5/5 and Enrage 5/5.
- Sweeping Strikes, Weapon Mastery 2/2, Flurry 5/5, Bloodthirst, Precision 3/3, Improved Berserker Stance 3/5 and Rampage, for 41 points.
- In Arms: Improved Heroic Strike 3/3, Deflection 5/5 and Improved Charge 2/2.

### Level 61 to 70

- Anger Management, Deep Wounds 3/3 and Improved Overpower 1/2.
- Impale 2/2 and Improved Thunder Clap 3/3, for 20 points in Arms.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Charge in Battle Stance, switch to Berserker Stance, then Bloodthirst and Whirlwind whenever they are ready.
- Rampage after your first critical strike, and Heroic Strike with spare rage.
- Victory Rush from level 62 after every kill, and Execute below 20% health.

### Two enemies

- Sweeping Strikes, then Whirlwind and Cleave.
- Demoralizing Shout lowers the damage both enemies deal.

### Staying alive

- Victory Rush and bandages keep your health up between pulls.
- Intimidating Shout to break away, and Retaliation in Battle Stance when too many enemies hit you.
- Commanding Shout from level 68 adds health for you and your group.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 60-62 | Hellfire Peninsula (Honor Hold) | Hellfire Peninsula (Thrallmar) |
| 62-64 | Zangarmarsh (Telredor) | Zangarmarsh (Zabra'jin) |
| 64-65 | Terokkar Forest (Allerian Stronghold) | Terokkar Forest (Stonebreaker Hold) |
| 65-67 | Nagrand (Telaar) | Nagrand (Garadar) |
| 67-68 | Blade's Edge Mountains (Sylvanaar) | Blade's Edge Mountains (Thunderlord Stronghold) |
| 68-70 | Netherstorm (Area 52), Shadowmoon Valley (Wildhammer Stronghold) | Netherstorm (Area 52), Shadowmoon Valley (Shadowmoon Village) |

Dungeons worth the detour: Hellfire Ramparts and The Blood Furnace from level 60, The Slave Pens and The Underbog around 62, and Mana-Tombs and Auchenai Crypts from 64.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Strength first, then Hit so your attacks land, then Crit and Expertise.
- Two slow one-handed weapons.
- Enchants: Mongoose or Crusader on both weapons, Brawn on the bracers and Assault on the gloves.
- Tradeskills: Mining with Blacksmithing, or Mining with Jewelcrafting for your own gems. Skinning pays well in Outland.
