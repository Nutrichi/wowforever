Arcane is the strongest Mage spec at level 70, but it is the hardest to level with. Arcane Blast at level 64 deals big damage, Arcane Power and Presence of Mind add burst, and Slow keeps melee enemies from catching you. It needs a lot of mana and decent gear, so it suits players who already have good items at 60 or level through dungeons.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- Very high burst with Arcane Power and Presence of Mind.
- Mind Mastery and Arcane Mind turn Intellect into more damage and mana.
- Icy Veins and Improved Frost Nova from the Frost tree help control a fight.
- Mana runs out quickly, and weak gear makes leveling slow.

## Talents by level

### At level 60

- In Arcane: Arcane Subtlety 2/2, Arcane Focus 3/5, Arcane Concentration 5/5, Arcane Impact 3/3, Arcane Fortitude, Arcane Meditation 3/3 and Improved Mana Shield 2/2.
- Presence of Mind, Arcane Mind 5/5, Arcane Instability 3/3, Arcane Potency 3/3, Arcane Power, Empowered Arcane Missiles 3/3, Mind Mastery 5/5 and Slow, for 41 points.
- In Frost: Improved Frostbolt 5/5, Elemental Precision 3/3 and Improved Frost Nova 2/2.

### Level 61 to 70

- Ice Shards 5/5 and Piercing Ice 3/3.
- Icy Veins and Permafrost 1/3, for 20 points in Frost.

## Rotation

### One enemy

- Frostbolt from maximum range to slow the enemy, then Arcane Missiles.
- From level 64, Arcane Blast two or three times while your mana is high, then Frostbolt to let the stacks fall off.
- Presence of Mind and Arcane Power for a strong enemy, and your wand to save mana.

### Two enemies

- Slow on the first enemy and Polymorph on the second.
- Frost Nova when both reach you, step back, and Arcane Explosion only against weak enemies.

### Staying alive

- Mana Shield and Ice Block, and Blink to get away.
- Icy Veins from level 69 helps you finish a fight faster.
- Evocation and a Mana Emerald from level 68 when your mana is empty.

## Leveling route

| Level | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 60-62 | Hellfire Peninsula (Honor Hold) | Hellfire Peninsula (Thrallmar) |
| 62-64 | Zangarmarsh (Telredor) | Zangarmarsh (Zabra'jin) |
| 64-65 | Terokkar Forest (Allerian Stronghold) | Terokkar Forest (Stonebreaker Hold) |
| 65-67 | Nagrand (Telaar) | Nagrand (Garadar) |
| 67-68 | Blade's Edge Mountains (Sylvanaar) | Blade's Edge Mountains (Thunderlord Stronghold) |
| 68-70 | Netherstorm (Area 52), Shadowmoon Valley (Wildhammer Stronghold) | Netherstorm (Area 52), Shadowmoon Valley (Shadowmoon Village) |

Dungeons worth the detour: Hellfire Ramparts and The Blood Furnace from level 60, The Slave Pens and The Underbog around 62, and Mana-Tombs and Auchenai Crypts from 64.

## Stats, weapons and tradeskills

- Spell Damage and Intellect first, then Crit, Hit and Spirit.
- A staff, or a one-handed weapon with an off-hand item, with Spell Damage and Intellect.
- Enchants: Major Spellpower on the weapon and the gloves, and Spellpower on the bracers.
- Tradeskills: Tailoring for the Spellfire set, with Enchanting to disenchant what you replace.
