Herbalism in WotLK Classic runs from 375 to 450 on the herbs of Northrend. Each herb needs a minimum skill, shown in brackets below. Grizzly Hills has far more Tiger Lily than Howling Fjord or Borean Tundra.

## Route

| Skill | Herbs | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 375-400 | Tiger Lily (375), Goldclover (350) | Grizzly Hills, Howling Fjord, Borean Tundra |
| 400-425 | Adder's Tongue (400), Tiger Lily, Goldclover | Sholazar Basin |
| 425-450 | Lichbloom (425), Icethorn (435) | The Storm Peaks, Icecrown |

## Trainers and tips

- Grand Master Herbalism is taught by Dorothy Egan in Dalaran for both factions, and by herbalism trainers in Howling Fjord and Borean Tundra.
- Frost Lotus is a rare extra on every Northrend herb and grows in Wintergrasp. Alchemists need it for flasks, so it sells well.
- Herbalism also gives Lifeblood, an instant heal over time that you can use every few minutes. Druids can gather straight from Flight Form.
