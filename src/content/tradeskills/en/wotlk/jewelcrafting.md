Jewelcrafting in WotLK Classic runs from 375 to 450 on gems from prospecting Cobalt and Saronite Ore, which makes Mining the natural partner. Learn Grand Master Jewelcrafting at character level 65 as soon as you reach Northrend. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Uncommon Northrend gems | 40 |
| Bloodstone, Huge Citrine or Sun Crystal | 6 |
| Crystallized Earth | 12 |
| Eternal Earth | 46 |
| Earthsiege Diamond or Skyflare Diamond | 30 |

Prospecting Cobalt and Saronite Ore gives the uncommon gems. Earthsiege and Skyflare Diamonds come from prospecting Saronite, from dungeons and from alchemy transmutes. Shadowmight Ring (Eternal Earth and Eternal Shadow) works as well as Stoneguard Band.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 375-395 | Uncommon gem cuts, such as Bold Bloodstone, Solid Chalcedony or Jagged Dark Jade | An uncommon Northrend gem |
| 395-400 | Bloodstone Band, Crystal Citrine Necklace or Sun Rock Ring | Bloodstone, Huge Citrine or Sun Crystal, 2 Crystallized Earth |
| 400-420 | Stoneguard Band | 2 Eternal Earth |
| 420-450 | Meta gem cuts, such as Persistent Earthsiege Diamond or Swift Skyflare Diamond | Earthsiege Diamond or Skyflare Diamond |

## Trainers and tips

- Grand Master Jewelcrafting is taught by Timothy Jones in Dalaran for both factions, by Ounhulo in Howling Fjord and Alestos in Borean Tundra for the Alliance, and by Carter Tiffens in Howling Fjord and Geba'li in Borean Tundra for the Horde.
- Timothy Jones also gives a daily quest that rewards a Dalaran Jewelcrafter's Token. Tiffany Cartier in Dalaran sells designs for those tokens.
- From 425 you can make Icy Prism once every 20 hours, which gives skill up to 450 without spending diamonds.
