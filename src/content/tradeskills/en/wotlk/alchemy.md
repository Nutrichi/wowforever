Alchemy in WotLK Classic runs from 375 to 450 on Northrend herbs, which makes Herbalism the natural partner. Learn Grand Master Alchemy at character level 65 as soon as you reach Northrend. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

Pygmy Suckerfish is fished in Northrend. Enchanted Vials come from the trade supplies vendors, and Frost Lotus is a rare find on every Northrend herb.

## Trainers and tips

- Grand Master Alchemy is taught by Linzy Blackbolt in Dalaran for both factions, by Lanolis Dewdrop in Howling Fjord and Falorn Nightwhisper in Borean Tundra for the Alliance, and by Wilhelmina Renel in Howling Fjord and Arthur Henslowe in Borean Tundra for the Horde.
- Northrend Alchemy Research teaches new recipes and has a long cooldown, so use it whenever it is ready.
- Transmutes need a Philosopher's Stone in your bags. Any flask works for the last step, so pick the one whose herbs are cheapest.
