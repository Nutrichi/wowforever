Tailoring in WotLK Classic runs from 375 to 450 on Frostweave Cloth, which drops from humanoids all over Northrend, so it pairs well with any gathering tradeskill. Learn Grand Master Tailoring at character level 65 as soon as you reach Northrend. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Frostweave Cloth | 3450 |
| Infinite Dust | 340 |
| Moonshroud, Ebonweave or Spellweave | 56 |
| Eternium Thread | 10 |
| Frozen Orb | 10 |

Bolt of Frostweave takes 5 Frostweave Cloth. Make about 170 Bolts of Imbued Frostweave on the 400-405 step, since the bags and the epic pieces need them all.

## Trainers and tips

- Grand Master Tailoring is taught by Charles Worth in Dalaran for both factions, by Benjamin Clegg in Howling Fjord and Darin Goodstitch in Borean Tundra for the Alliance, and by Alexandra McQueen in Howling Fjord and Raenah in Borean Tundra for the Horde.
- Moonshroud, Ebonweave and Spellweave are made from Bolts of Imbued Frostweave and each has a cooldown, so start making them early or buy them.
- Frostweave Bags sell well, which takes some of the cost off the 425-440 step.
