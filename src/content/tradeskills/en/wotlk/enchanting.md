Enchanting in WotLK Classic runs from 375 to 450 on Infinite Dust, Cosmic Essences and Dream Shards from disenchanting Northrend gear. Learn Grand Master Enchanting at character level 65 as soon as you reach Northrend. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Infinite Dust | 580 |
| Greater Cosmic Essence | 27 |
| Dream Shard | 28 |
| Eternal Earth | 134 |
| Crystallized Water | 10 |
| Abyss Crystal | 7 |
| Greater Planar Essence | 6 |
| Arcane Dust | 6 |

The two rods also need an Eternium Rod and a Titanium Rod from a blacksmith. Abyss Crystals come from disenchanting epic Northrend gear.

## Trainers and tips

- Grand Master Enchanting is taught by Enchanter Nalthanis in Dalaran for both factions, and by enchanting trainers in Howling Fjord and Borean Tundra.
- Enchant Cloak - Mighty Armor and Enchant Gloves - Armsman are sold by Vanessa Sellers in Dalaran for Dream Shards.
- Abyssal Shatter turns green near the end, so keep a few extra Abyss Crystals ready for the last points.
