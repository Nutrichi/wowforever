Mining in WotLK Classic runs from 375 to 450 on the veins of Northrend. Each ore needs a minimum skill, shown in brackets below. Sholazar Basin is the best zone from 400, and The Storm Peaks is a good backup when it is busy.

## Route

| Skill | Ore | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 375-400 | Cobalt Deposit (350), Rich Cobalt Deposit (375) | Howling Fjord, Borean Tundra, Dragonblight |
| 400-425 | Saronite Deposit (400), Rich Cobalt Deposit | Sholazar Basin, Zul'Drak |
| 425-450 | Rich Saronite Deposit (425), Titanium Vein (450) | Sholazar Basin, The Storm Peaks, Icecrown |

## Trainers and tips

- Grand Master Mining is taught by Jedidiah Handers in Dalaran for both factions, and by mining trainers in Howling Fjord and Borean Tundra.
- Smelting also gives skill: Cobalt Bar from 350, Saronite Bar from 400, and Titanium Bar and Titansteel Bar from 450. Titansteel Bar has a daily cooldown.
- Titanium Veins are rare and appear in place of Saronite nodes. Mining also gives Toughness, a passive bonus to Stamina, and Crystallized Earth, which crafters buy.
