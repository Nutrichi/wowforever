First Aid in WotLK Classic runs from 375 to 450 on Frostweave Cloth, which drops from humanoids all over Northrend. It costs little, and the bandages help every class between fights. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Frostweave Cloth | 215 |

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 375-400 | Frostweave Bandage | Frostweave Cloth |
| 400-450 | Heavy Frostweave Bandage | 2 Frostweave Cloth |

## Trainers and tips

- Grand Master First Aid is taught by Olisarra the Kind in Dalaran for both factions, by Anchorite Yazmina in Howling Fjord and Brynna Wilson in Borean Tundra for the Alliance, and by Sally Tompkins in Howling Fjord and Nurse Applewood in Borean Tundra for the Horde.
- The Manual: Heavy Frostweave Bandage drops from Rageclaw Primalists and Drakuru Berserkers in Zul'Drak, but only once you have 390 skill.
- Keep the manual in mind early: farm it when you pass through Zul'Drak around level 75.
