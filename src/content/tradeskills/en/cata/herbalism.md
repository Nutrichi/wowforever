Herbalism in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 on the herbs of the Cataclysm zones. Each herb needs a minimum skill, shown in brackets below. You can stay in Mount Hyjal until 500 if Deepholm is busy.

## Route

| Skill | Herbs | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 450-475 | Cinderbloom (425), Stormvine (425), Azshara's Veil (425) | Mount Hyjal, Vashj'ir |
| 475-500 | Heartblossom (475), Cinderbloom | Deepholm, Mount Hyjal |
| 500-525 | Whiptail (500), Twilight Jasmine (525) | Uldum, Twilight Highlands |

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Herbalism is taught by the herbalism trainers in the capital cities, such as Stormwind City and Orgrimmar, from character level 75.
- Gathering herbs also gives Volatile Life, which alchemists and scribes buy.
- Herbalism also gives Lifeblood, an instant heal over time that you can use every few minutes. Druids can gather straight from Flight Form.
