Enchanting in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 on Hypnotic Dust and Celestial Essences from disenchanting Cataclysm gear. Several steps give more than one point per enchant, so the counts are lower than in earlier expansions. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Hypnotic Dust | 470 |
| Lesser Celestial Essence | 10 |
| Greater Celestial Essence | 86 |
| Heavenly Shard | 7 |
| Elixir of Impossible Accuracy | 5 |

The rod also needs an Elementium Rod from a blacksmith and your Runed Titanium Rod from Northrend. Elixir of Impossible Accuracy comes from an alchemist.

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Enchanting is taught by the enchanting trainers in the capital cities, such as Stormwind City and Orgrimmar, from character level 75.
- Formula: Runed Elementium Rod is sold by the enchanting supplies vendors in the capital cities for a Heavenly Shard.
- Heavenly Shards come from disenchanting epic Cataclysm gear, or from breaking Maelstrom Crystals once you have them.
