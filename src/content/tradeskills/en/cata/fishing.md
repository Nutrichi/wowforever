Fishing in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 in Twilight Highlands and Uldum, and the fish feed the Cataclysm cooking recipes. Even with good lures you will catch a lot of junk at first.

## Route

| Skill | Where |
|---|---|
| 450-490 | Twilight Highlands, the lakes and the coast |
| 490-525 | Uldum, along the river |

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Fishing is taught by the fishing trainers in the capital cities. Learn it between 425 and 450.
- The daily fishing quests come from Catherine Leland in Stormwind City for the Alliance and Razgar in Orgrimmar for the Horde. They give skill, gold and sometimes a rare prize.
- Around 450 to 470 about one catch in three is junk. Use lures and fish in pools where you can, since they give no junk.
