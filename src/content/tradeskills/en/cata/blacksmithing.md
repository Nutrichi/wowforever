Blacksmithing in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 on Obsidium and Elementium, which makes Mining the natural partner. Make your Folded Obsidium first, because the next steps use about 85 of them. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Obsidium Bar | 170 |
| Elementium Bar | 325 |
| Hardened Elementium Bar | 2 |
| Volatile Earth | 120 |
| Volatile Fire | 140 |
| Volatile Water | 4 |

Volatile Earth and Volatile Fire come from mining and from elementals. The three Bloodthirsty patterns cost Elementium Bars, and the shoulders cost 2 Hardened Elementium Bars.

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Blacksmithing is taught by the blacksmithing trainers in the capital cities, such as Stormwind City and Orgrimmar, from character level 75.
- The Bloodthirsty patterns are sold by the blacksmithing supplies vendors in the capital cities. The Ornate versions use Volatile Water instead of Volatile Fire.
- Hardened Obsidium Belt, Redsteel Belt and Stormforged Belt give the same skill, so pick the one whose extra materials are cheapest.
