Tailoring in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 on Embersilk Cloth, which drops from humanoids in the Cataclysm zones, so it pairs well with any gathering tradeskill. Bolt of Embersilk Cloth takes 5 Embersilk Cloth. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Embersilk Cloth | 1850 |
| Eternium Thread | 100 |
| Volatile Fire | 70 |
| Volatile Water | 55 |
| Volatile Air | 15 |

Eternium Thread is sold by the trade supplies vendors. Volatiles drop from elementals and come with gathering.

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Tailoring is taught by the tailoring trainers in the capital cities, such as Stormwind City and Orgrimmar, from character level 75.
- The Bloodthirsty patterns are sold by the tailoring supplies vendors in the capital cities.
- Embersilk Bag and Otherworldly Bag can replace Spiritmend Leggings on the 480-485 step, and bags sell well.
