Leatherworking in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 on Savage Leather, which makes Skinning the natural partner. Turn about 600 Savage Leather into Heavy Savage Leather on the 485-490 step: the rest of the route needs roughly 120. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Savage Leather | 910 |
| Eternium Thread | 35 |
| Volatile Earth | 32 |
| Volatile Air | 8 |
| Volatile Water | 28 |
| Volatile Fire | 68 |

Eternium Thread is sold by the trade supplies vendors. Volatiles drop from elementals and come with gathering.

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Leatherworking is taught by the leatherworking trainers in the capital cities, such as Stormwind City and Orgrimmar, from character level 75.
- The Bloodthirsty patterns are sold by the leatherworking supplies vendors in the capital cities. Each turns yellow after about five points, so buy the next one then.
- Cloak of War can replace Cloak of Beasts on the 496-510 step if its materials are cheaper.
