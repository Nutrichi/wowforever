Mining in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 on the veins of the Cataclysm zones. Each ore needs a minimum skill, shown in brackets below. Mount Hyjal has many nodes but also many miners, and Vashj'ir is a quieter choice.

## Route

| Skill | Ore | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 450-475 | Obsidium Deposit (425), Rich Obsidium Deposit (450) | Mount Hyjal, Vashj'ir |
| 475-500 | Elementium Vein (475), Obsidium Deposit | Twilight Highlands, Deepholm |
| 500-525 | Rich Elementium Vein (500), Pyrite Deposit (525) | Twilight Highlands, Uldum |

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Mining is taught by the mining trainers in the capital cities, such as Stormwind City and Orgrimmar, from character level 75.
- Smelting also gives skill: Obsidium Bar from 425, Elementium Bar from 475, Hardened Elementium Bar from 500 and Pyrium Bar from 525.
- Mining also gives Volatile Earth and Toughness, a passive bonus to Stamina. Vashj'ir needs the starter quests there for its underwater mount.
