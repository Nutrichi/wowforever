First Aid in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 on Embersilk Cloth, which drops from humanoids in the Cataclysm zones. It costs little, and the bandages help every class between fights. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Embersilk Cloth | 245 |

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 450-475 | Embersilk Bandage | Embersilk Cloth |
| 475-525 | Heavy Embersilk Bandage | 2 Embersilk Cloth |

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master First Aid is taught by the first aid trainers in the capital cities, such as Stormwind City and Orgrimmar, from character level 75.
- Heavy Embersilk Bandage is taught by the same trainer once you reach 475.
- Keep the Embersilk Cloth you loot while leveling from 80 to 85: that alone covers most of this route.
