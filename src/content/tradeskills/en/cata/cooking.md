Cooking in Cata Classic runs from 450 to 525 with recipes bought for Chef's Awards, which you earn with the daily cooking quest in your capital city. Where a step lists several dishes, pick the one whose fish or meat is cheapest. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Striped Lurker, Lavascale Catfish, Monstrous Claw or Snake Eye | 25 |
| Highland Guppy, Blood Shrimp or Giant Turtle Tongue | 35 |
| Algaefin Rockfish, Crocolisk Tail, Deepsea Sagefish or Dragon Flank | 35 |
| Chef's Award | 12 |

A few recipes also need a spice or a drink from the cooking vendor. Fish comes from Fishing in the Cataclysm zones, meat from their beasts.

## Trainers and tips

- Illustrious Grand Master Cooking is taught by the cooking trainers in the capital cities.
- The daily cooking quest comes from Robby Flay in Stormwind City for the Alliance and Marogg in Orgrimmar for the Horde. You can do one each day, and each gives a Chef's Award.
- Recipes cost 3 Chef's Awards each at Bario Matalli in Stormwind City for the Alliance and Shazdar in Orgrimmar for the Horde.
