Skinning in MoP Classic runs from 525 to 600 by skinning the beasts of Pandaria. The spots below work for both factions, and the creatures come back quickly.

## Route

| Skill | Creatures | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 525-550 | Wyrmhorn Turtle | Valley of the Four Winds, around the small lake |
| 550-590 | Longshadow Mushan, Longshadow Bull | Townlong Steppes, around the Kri'vess tree |
| 590-600 | Stout Shaghorn | Valley of the Four Winds, with a flying mount |

## Trainers and tips

- Zen Master Skinning is taught by the skinning trainers in The Jade Forest and in the capital cities.
- Without a flying mount, the scorpions in the Dread Wastes are a good alternative for the last points.
- Most beasts give Exotic Leather and Sha-Touched Leather, scaled creatures give Prismatic Scale, and Magnificent Hide is a rare extra that leatherworkers pay well for.
