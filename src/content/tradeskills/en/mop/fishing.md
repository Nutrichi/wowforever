Fishing in MoP Classic runs from 525 to 600 in the waters of Pandaria, and the fish feed the Ways of cooking. Skill comes slowly at this point, so the daily quests make a real difference.

## Route

| Skill | Where |
|---|---|
| 525-575 | The Jade Forest and Valley of the Four Winds, the rivers, lakes and pools |
| 575-600 | Krasarang Wilds, around Anglers Wharf |

## Trainers and tips

- Zen Master Fishing is taught by the fishing trainers in the capital cities and in Pandaria. No new training is needed after 525.
- The Anglers at Anglers Wharf in Krasarang Wilds give three daily quests, which can add up to three fishing points a day and build reputation with the faction. Ben of the Booming Voice at Halfhill sends you to a different fishing spot each day.
- Emperor Salmon and Giant Mantis Shrimp from Krasarang Wilds are worth keeping for cooking or selling.
