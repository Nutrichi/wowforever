Enchanting in MoP Classic runs from 525 to 600 on Spirit Dust and Mysterious Essence from disenchanting Pandaria gear. Each enchant gives several points, so the whole route needs only a few dozen materials. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Spirit Dust | 72 |
| Mysterious Essence | 37 |

Any ring enchant works on the 552-556 step. Ethereal Shards and Sha Crystals come from rare and epic gear, but the route does not need them.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 525-552 | Enchant Cloak - Superior Intellect | 3 Spirit Dust, 3 Mysterious Essence |
| 552-556 | Enchant Ring - Greater Intellect | 2 Spirit Dust |
| 556-577 | Enchant Chest - Superior Stamina | 4 Spirit Dust, Mysterious Essence |
| 577-600 | Enchant Chest - Glorious Stats | 2 Spirit Dust, 3 Mysterious Essence |

## Trainers and tips

- Zen Master Enchanting is taught by the enchanting trainers in the capital cities from character level 80, so you do not have to travel to Pandaria for it. Lai the Spellpaw in The Jade Forest also teaches it.
- Ring enchants only work on your own rings, so use them on your own gear.
- Disenchant the green gear you loot while leveling in Pandaria: that covers most of the dust and essences.
