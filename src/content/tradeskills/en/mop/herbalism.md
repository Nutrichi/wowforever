Herbalism in MoP Classic runs from 525 to 600 on the herbs of Pandaria. Herbs here have no skill requirement, so follow the zones below and gather everything you pass.

## Route

| Skill | Herbs | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 525-545 | Green Tea Leaf, Rain Poppy | The Jade Forest |
| 545-575 | Silkweed, Green Tea Leaf | Valley of the Four Winds |
| 575-600 | Snow Lily | Kun-Lai Summit |

## Trainers and tips

- Zen Master Herbalism is taught by the herbalism trainers in the capital cities, and by Han Flowerbloom at the Halfhill Market in Valley of the Four Winds for both factions.
- Fool's Cap grows in Townlong Steppes and the Dread Wastes. Golden Lotus is a rare extra, mostly in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms, and sells well.
- Herbalism also gives Lifeblood, an instant heal over time that you can use every few minutes. Druids can gather straight from Flight Form.
