Blacksmithing in MoP Classic runs from 525 to 600 almost entirely on Ghost Iron Bar, which makes Mining the natural partner. Recipes give several points per craft, so the route is short. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Ghost Iron Bar | 210 |
| Spirit of Harmony | 1 |

Ghost Iron Bar is smelted from Ghost Iron Ore. The Contender's plans cost a Spirit of Harmony, and one plan is enough to reach 600.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 525-542 | Ghost-Forged Belt | 7 Ghost Iron Bar |
| 542-552 | Lightsteel Shield | 9 Ghost Iron Bar |
| 552-557 | Spiritguard Shield | 9 Ghost Iron Bar |
| 557-567 | Ghost-Forged Helm | 12 Ghost Iron Bar |
| 567-575 | Ghost-Forged Shoulders | 7 Ghost Iron Bar |
| 575-600 | A Contender's plate piece from the vendor | 12 Ghost Iron Bar |

## Trainers and tips

- Zen Master Blacksmithing is taught by the blacksmithing trainers in the capital cities from character level 80, so you do not have to travel to Pandaria for it.
- The Contender's plans are sold by the blacksmithing vendors in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms: in the Shrine of Seven Stars for the Alliance and the Shrine of Two Moons for the Horde.
- Ghost-Forged Shoulders turn yellow quickly, so plan a few extra crafts on that step.
