Cooking in MoP Classic splits into six Ways from 525: Grill (Strength), Wok (Agility), Pot (Intellect), Steamer (Spirit), Oven (Stamina) and Brew. Your Cooking skill is your highest Way, so taking one Way to 600 is enough. The route below lists the Grill, Wok, Pot, Steamer and Oven dishes in that order. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Meat or fish for your Way | 90 |
| Vegetables for your Way | 320 |
| Soy Sauce | 5 |

Meat comes from beasts in Pandaria, fish from Fishing, and vegetables from the Halfhill Market or your own farm at Sunsong Ranch. Each banquet uses the meat, fish and vegetables of its Way.

## Trainers and tips

- Each Way is trained at the Halfhill Market in Valley of the Four Winds: Kol Ironpaw (Grill), Anthea Ironpaw (Wok), Mei Mei Ironpaw (Pot), Yan Ironpaw (Steamer), Jian Ironpaw (Oven) and Bobo Ironpaw (Brew). Sungshin Ironpaw gives the quests that unlock them.
- Way of the Brew gives no stats, so pick the Way whose stat you use.
- The banquets from 575 give a main stat buff to the whole raid, so they stay useful once you are done.
