Engineering in MoP Classic runs from 525 to 600 on Ghost Iron Bar, turned into Ghost Iron Bolts and High-Explosive Gunpowder, which makes Mining the natural partner. The route needs about 180 bolts and 30 gunpowder in total. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Ghost Iron Bar | 565 |
| Windwool Cloth | 70 |
| Lapis Lazuli | 60 |
| Spirit of Harmony | 2 |

Windwool Cloth drops from humanoids in Pandaria. Lapis Lazuli comes from prospecting Ghost Iron Ore. Thermal Anvil can replace Mirror Scope if you have gunpowder to spare.

| 595-600 | Mist-Piercing Goggles | 8 Ghost Iron Bolts, 2 Spirit of Harmony |

## Trainers and tips

- Zen Master Engineering is taught by the engineering trainers in the capital cities from character level 80, so you do not have to travel to Pandaria for it.
- Make bolts and gunpowder as you go: making them all at 525 wastes nothing, because they stay useful to the end.
- Lord Blastington's Scope of Doom (Ghost Iron Bolts, Primordial Ruby and a Spirit of Harmony) works as well as Mist-Piercing Goggles for the last points.
