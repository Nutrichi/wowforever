Leatherworking in Classic Era runs from 1 to 300 on leather and hides, which makes Skinning the natural partner. The route below uses trainer patterns until 260, then two patterns that vendors sell in limited supply. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## Trainers and tips

- Apprentice and Journeyman training is available in the capitals, Expert training from Telonis in Darnassus and Una in Thunder Bluff, and Artisan training from Drakk Stonehand in Aerie Peak for the Alliance and Hahrana Ironhide at Camp Mojache in Feralas for the Horde.
- Wicked Leather Gauntlets is sold by Leonard Porter in Western Plaguelands and Werg Thickblade in Tirisfal Glades, Runic Leather Headband by Jase Farlane in Eastern Plaguelands. Both patterns are in limited supply.
- At 225 and character level 40 you can choose Dragonscale, Elemental or Tribal Leatherworking. You do not need any of them to reach 300.
