Tailoring in Classic Era runs from 1 to 300 on the cloth that humanoid enemies drop, so it needs no gathering skill. Enchanting is a good partner, because the items you sew can be disenchanted. Many steps turn cloth into bolts first, so keep your bolts. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## Trainers and tips

- Apprentice and Journeyman training is available in the capitals, Expert training from Georgio Bolero in Stormwind and Josef Gregorian in Undercity, and Artisan training from Timothy Worthington in Theramore for the Alliance and Daryl Stack in Tarren Mill for the Horde.
- Qia at the bank in Everlook sells the patterns for Runecloth Bag and Runecloth Gloves. Her supply is limited, so come back later if she is sold out.
- Bolts of Runecloth and Runecloth Bags sell well at the auction house.
