Skinning in Classic Era runs from 1 to 300 by skinning the beasts you kill, so it fits right into leveling. Keep a Skinning Knife in your bags. Up to skill 100 you can skin creatures up to level (skill / 10) + 10; above that, up to level skill / 5.

## Route

| Skill | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| 1-75 | Dun Morogh, Loch Modan | Durotar, Mulgore |
| 75-155 | Loch Modan, Wetlands, Arathi Highlands | The Barrens, Thousand Needles |
| 155-205 | Arathi Highlands | Thousand Needles |
| 205-280 | Feralas | Feralas |
| 280-300 | Un'Goro Crater | Un'Goro Crater |

## Trainers and tips

- Learn Journeyman, Expert and Artisan from a trainer as soon as you are allowed, before you reach the caps of 75, 150 and 225. Artisan needs character level 35.
- Low creatures give Light Leather, then Medium, Heavy and Thick Leather, and from about level 50 Rugged Leather.
- Enchant Gloves - Skinning adds 5 skill, enough for creatures one level higher.
- Leatherworking uses everything you skin, and leather sells well at the auction house.
