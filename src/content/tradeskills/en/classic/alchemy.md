## Trainers and tips

- Apprentice, Journeyman and Expert training is available in every capital. Artisan training from 225 is only in Feralas for the Alliance and in Swamp of Sorrows for the Horde.
- The Minor Healing Potions from the first step are needed again for Lesser Healing Potions, so keep them.
- Transmute: Arcanite at 275 turns an Arcane Crystal and a Thorium Bar into an Arcanite Bar, which sells well. The transmute has a long cooldown.
- The most valuable recipes at 300, such as the flasks, drop in dungeons and need an alchemy lab to craft.
