Leatherworking in TBC Anniversary runs from 300 to 375 on Knothide Leather, which makes Skinning the natural partner. Learn Master Leatherworking in Hellfire Peninsula or Shattrath City as soon as you arrive. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Knothide Leather | 1510 |
| Rune Thread | 60 |
| Thick Clefthoof Leather | 24 |
| Fel Hide | 24 |

Most Knothide Leather becomes Heavy Knothide Leather, so make about 220 of it in that step. Rune Thread comes from the leatherworking supplies vendors.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 300-325 | Knothide Armor Kit | 4 Knothide Leather |
| 325-335 | Heavy Knothide Leather | 5 Knothide Leather |
| 335-350 | Thick Draenic Vest | 14 Knothide Leather, 3 Rune Thread |
| 350-365 | Heavy Knothide Armor Kit | 3 Heavy Knothide Leather |
| 365-370 | Drums of Battle | 6 Heavy Knothide Leather, 4 Thick Clefthoof Leather |
| 370-375 | Drums of Panic | 6 Heavy Knothide Leather, 4 Fel Hide |

## Trainers and tips

- Master Leatherworking is taught by Brumman in Honor Hold for the Alliance, Barim Spilthoof in Thrallmar for the Horde, and Darmari in Shattrath City for both.
- The Heavy Knothide Leather pattern is sold in limited supply by Cro Threadstrong in Shattrath City.
- Drums of Battle needs Honored with the Sha'tar and is sold by Almaador in Shattrath City. Drums of Panic needs Honored with the Keepers of Time and is sold by Alurmi in the Caverns of Time.
