Tailoring in TBC Anniversary runs from 300 to 375 on Netherweave Cloth from Outland humanoids. The whole route starts by turning cloth into bolts, so save Netherweave Cloth from the moment you arrive. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Netherweave Cloth | 2980 |
| Arcane Dust | 204 |
| Knothide Leather | 10 |
| Rune Thread | 62 |
| Netherweb Spider Silk | 34 |

Make all about 500 Bolts of Netherweave in the first step; the later recipes use them. Rune Thread comes from the tailoring supplies vendors.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 300-325 | Bolt of Netherweave | 6 Netherweave Cloth |
| 325-340 | Bolt of Imbued Netherweave | 3 Bolt of Netherweave, 2 Arcane Dust |
| 340-345 | Netherweave Boots | 6 Bolt of Netherweave, 2 Knothide Leather, Rune Thread |
| 345-360 | Netherweave Tunic | 8 Bolt of Netherweave, 2 Rune Thread |
| 360-375 | Imbued Netherweave Tunic | 6 Bolt of Imbued Netherweave, 2 Netherweb Spider Silk, Rune Thread |

## Trainers and tips

- Master Tailoring is taught by Hama in Honor Hold for the Alliance and Dalinna in Thrallmar for the Horde.
- Eiin in Shattrath City sells Bolt of Imbued Netherweave and Netherweave Tunic; imbued bolts are made at the mana loom next to her. Arrond in Shadowmoon Valley sells Imbued Netherweave Tunic to players who are at least neutral with the Scryers.
- At 350 you can choose Mooncloth, Shadoweave or Spellfire Tailoring through a quest. Each makes its own special cloth with a cooldown.
