Herbalism in TBC Anniversary runs from 300 to 375 on the herbs of Outland. Each herb needs a minimum skill, shown in brackets below, and Felweed and Dreaming Glory give skill all the way to 375, so you can stay in a quiet zone when another is crowded.

## Route

| Skill | Herbs | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 300-315 | Felweed (300) | Hellfire Peninsula |
| 315-325 | Dreaming Glory (315), Felweed | Nagrand, Blade's Edge Mountains |
| 325-350 | Terocone (325), Ragveil (325), Flame Cap (335) | Terokkar Forest, Zangarmarsh |
| 350-375 | Netherbloom (350), Nightmare Vine (365), Mana Thistle (375) | Netherstorm, Shadowmoon Valley |

## Trainers and tips

- Master Herbalism needs character level 50 and 275 skill, and is taught in Hellfire Peninsula by Rorelien in Honor Hold for the Alliance and Ruak Stronghorn in Thrallmar for the Horde.
- Ragveil and Flame Cap grow in Zangarmarsh, Nightmare Vine in Shadowmoon Valley and Netherbloom in Netherstorm.
- Herbs in Outland sometimes give Motes of Life, and Mana Thistle sometimes a Fel Lotus, which sell well.
