Jewelcrafting is new in TBC Anniversary and runs from 1 all the way to 375. It cuts gems for the sockets on Outland gear, and Prospecting turns five ore into gems, which makes Mining the natural partner. Every race can learn it. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## Trainers and tips

- Apprentice to Artisan training is available from jewelcrafting trainers such as Farii in The Exodar and Kalinda in Silvermoon City. Master Jewelcrafting is taught by Tatiana in Honor Hold for the Alliance and Kalaen in Thrallmar for the Horde.
- Design: Pendant of the Agate Shield is sold in limited supply by Neal Allen in Menethil Harbor for the Alliance and Jandia at Freewind Post for the Horde.
- The rare and meta gem designs for the last steps come from reputation vendors, among them the Sha'tar, the Consortium, Lower City and the Keepers of Time.
