Engineering in TBC Anniversary runs from 300 to 375 on Fel Iron, Adamantite and elemental motes, which makes Mining the natural partner. The first steps make bolts, casings and powder that later recipes use again, so keep everything. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Fel Iron Bar | 270 |
| Adamantite Bar | 160 |
| Mote of Fire | 20 |
| Mote of Earth | 40 |
| Primal Earth | 30 |
| Netherweave Cloth | 70 |
| Khorium Bar | 15 |
| Primal Fire | 5 |

Make about 114 Handful of Fel Iron Bolts, 52 Fel Iron Casing and 20 batches of Elemental Blasting Powder in the first step. Your Arclight Spanner and Gyromatic Micro-Adjustor are still needed.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 300-320 | Handful of Fel Iron Bolts / Fel Iron Casing / Elemental Blasting Powder | Fel Iron Bar / 3 Fel Iron Bar / Mote of Fire, 2 Mote of Earth |
| 320-325 | Fel Iron Bomb | Fel Iron Casing, 2 Handful of Fel Iron Bolts, Elemental Blasting Powder |
| 325-335 | Adamantite Frame | 4 Adamantite Bar, Primal Earth |
| 335-355 | White Smoke Flare | Elemental Blasting Powder, Netherweave Cloth |
| 355-360 | Khorium Power Core | 3 Khorium Bar, Primal Fire |
| 360-370 | Adamantite Rifle | 3 Fel Iron Casing, 2 Adamantite Frame, 4 Handful of Fel Iron Bolts |
| 370-375 | Field Repair Bot 110G | 8 Adamantite Bar, 8 Handful of Fel Iron Bolts, Khorium Power Core |

## Trainers and tips

- Master Engineering is taught by Lebowski in Honor Hold and K. Lee Smallfry in Telredor for the Alliance, and by Zebig in Thrallmar and Mack Diver in Zabra'jin for the Horde.
- The schematics for White Smoke Flare and Adamantite Rifle are sold in limited supply, for example by Wind Trader Lathrai and Viggz Shinesparked in Shattrath City.
- The Field Repair Bot 110G schematic drops from Gan'arg Analyzers in Blade's Edge Mountains.
