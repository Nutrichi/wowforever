Skinning in TBC Anniversary runs from 300 to 375 by skinning the beasts of Outland. From level 61 a creature needs five skill per level, so at 360 you can skin every creature in Outland that is not elite.

## Route

| Skill | Creatures | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 300-310 | Starving Helboar, Deranged Helboar | Hellfire Peninsula |
| 310-330 | Ravager, Razorfang Hatchling | Hellfire Peninsula |
| 330-375 | Talbuk, Clefthoof | Nagrand |

## Trainers and tips

- Master Skinning needs character level 50 and 275 skill, and is taught by Jelena Nightsky in Honor Hold for the Alliance, Moorutu in Thrallmar for the Horde, and Seymour in Shattrath City for both.
- Most beasts give Knothide Leather. Clefthoof in Nagrand also give Thick Clefthoof Leather, and you can find Fel Scales, Cobra Scales, Wind Scales and Nether Dragonscales on the right creatures.
- Leatherworkers need Knothide Leather in huge amounts, so it sells well.
