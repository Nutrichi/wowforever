Alchemy in TBC Anniversary runs from 300 to 375 on Outland herbs, which makes Herbalism the natural partner. Learn Master Alchemy in Hellfire Peninsula as soon as you arrive. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Felweed | 40 |
| Golden Sansam | 40 |
| Dreaming Glory | 95 |
| Terocone | 5 |
| Netherbloom | 10 |
| Nightmare Vine | 40 |
| Imbued Vial | 105 |

Imbued Vials come from the alchemy supplies vendors in Outland.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 300-315 | Volatile Healing Potion | Golden Sansam, Felweed |
| 315-330 | Elixir of Healing Power | Golden Sansam, Dreaming Glory |
| 330-335 | Elixir of Draenic Wisdom | Terocone, Felweed |
| 335-340 | Super Healing Potion | 2 Netherbloom, Felweed |
| 340-355 | Super Mana Potion | 2 Dreaming Glory, Felweed |
| 355-375 | Major Dreamless Sleep Potion | Dreaming Glory, Nightmare Vine |

## Trainers and tips

- Master Alchemy is taught by Alchemist Gribble in Honor Hold for the Alliance, Apothecary Antonivich in Thrallmar for the Horde, and Lorokeem in Shattrath City for both.
- Super Mana Potion is sold by Haalrun for the Alliance and Daga Ramba for the Horde. Major Dreamless Sleep Potion comes from Leeli Longhaggle for the Alliance and Daga Ramba for the Horde.
- At 325 and character level 68 you can become an Elixir, Potion or Transmutation Master through a quest, which gives a chance to create extra items.
