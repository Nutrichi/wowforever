Blacksmithing in TBC Anniversary runs from 300 to 375 on Fel Iron and Adamantite, which makes Mining the natural partner. Learn Master Blacksmithing in Hellfire Peninsula as soon as you arrive. Counts are approximate: a recipe can turn green a few points earlier or later.

## What to gather or buy

| Material | About |
|---|---|
| Fel Iron Bar | 165 |
| Netherweave Cloth | 100 |
| Adamantite Bar | 135 |
| Felsteel Bar | 102 |

Felsteel Bar is smelted with Mining from 3 Fel Iron Bar and 2 Eternium Bar.

## Route

| Skill | Craft | Per craft |
|---|---|---|
| 300-305 | Fel Weightstone | Fel Iron Bar, Netherweave Cloth |
| 305-316 | Fel Iron Plate Belt | 4 Fel Iron Bar |
| 316-321 | Fel Iron Chain Gloves | 5 Fel Iron Bar |
| 321-325 | Fel Iron Plate Boots | 6 Fel Iron Bar |
| 325-335 | Lesser Rune of Warding | Adamantite Bar |
| 335-340 | Fel Iron Chain Tunic | 9 Fel Iron Bar |
| 340-350 | Lesser Ward of Shielding | Adamantite Bar |
| 350-360 | Adamantite Weightstone | Adamantite Bar, 2 Netherweave Cloth |
| 360-375 | Felsteel Gloves | 6 Felsteel Bar |

## Trainers and tips

- Master Blacksmithing is taught by Humphry in Honor Hold for the Alliance and Rohok in Thrallmar for the Horde.
- Plans for Lesser Ward of Shielding are sold in limited supply by Mari Stonehand in Shadowmoon Valley for the Alliance and Rohok for the Horde. Adamantite Weightstone needs Honored with the Cenarion Expedition.
- The Felsteel Gloves plans drop in Auchenai Crypts. Other ways to reach 375 are Enchanted Adamantite Belt with the Scryers, Flamebane Gloves with the Aldor, or Khorium Belt from the Murkblood in Nagrand.
